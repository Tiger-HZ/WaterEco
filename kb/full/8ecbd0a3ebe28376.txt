ABSTRACT The shifted (or two‐parameter) exponential distribution is a well‐known flexible probability model for skewed data, such as failure times or survival times due to its two‐parameter representation. Control charts for monitoring a process that is modeled according to a shifted exponential distribution have been studied quite extensively in recent literature.
