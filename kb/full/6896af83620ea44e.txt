Longfin smelt Spirinchus thaleichthys is an imperiled forage fish species in the San Francisco Estuary (SFE), USA, that has declined locally to approximately 1% of historic (pre-1980s) abundances. Turbidity is a water quality parameter that is often critical to the successful feeding and culture of larval fishes and positively correlates with larval abundance in the SFE. To help guide culturing efforts for longfin smelt and inform conservation practices in the SFE, we examined the effects of algal-induced turbidity on survival, growth, and feeding of newly hatched larval longfin smelt.
