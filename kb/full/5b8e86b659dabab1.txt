The ecological health of freshwater systems is strongly influenced by physicochemical parameters, which regulate the distribution, diversity, and structure of aquatic macrophytes. This study assessed key water quality parameters and their relationships with macrophyte distribution in three flowing and three non-flowing water bodies in Gwagwalada Area Council, Nigeria. Water samples were collected and analyzed for pH, total alkalinity (TA), total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS), dissolved oxygen (DO), total hardness (TH), total solids (TS), and biochemical oxygen demand (BOD).
