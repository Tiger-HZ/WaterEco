Under irrigated soil conditions, optimized mineral fertilizer management is necessary to increase sunflower (Helianthus annuus L.) productivity and nutrient use efficiency. This study evaluated the effects of four mineral NPK fertilizer treatments and an unfertilized control on growth, seed yield, seed quality, and nutrient recovery efficiency of sunflower on irrigated grey-brown soils in the Ganja-Dashkasan Economic Zone, Azerbaijan. The experiment was conducted over three consecutive growing seasons (2023–2025) in a randomized complete block design with three replications, comparing N60P60K60, N90P90K90, N120P120K120, and N150P150K150 (kg ha⁻¹) against an unfertilized control.
