Climate change and rising temperatures around the world over the last 50 years have shown that people should use water resources, including groundwater, correctly and efficiently. The fact that many scientists and authorities around the world are now focusing their attention on karri systems shows that karri, an ancient hydrotechnical system, has always been at the service of mankind and will continue to be the most promising land reclamation system in the future. Karizes are nature's most benign system and have no negative impact on ecosystems. They are considered to be the most reliable source of water in arid areas.
