Disaster rescue performance reflects not only hazard severity but also the management systems that organise command authority, inter-organisational coordination, resource allocation, rescue-team standardisation, institutional learning, and the integration of digital information. These functions are usually studied separately, and their joint contribution to observed performance has rarely been tested against outcomes independent of how the functions themselves are coded. This study develops an interdisciplinary framework, CCMR-CL, integrating six management dimensions, and tests it against 38 major disaster events in China (2006&amp;ndash;2025), each coded from official investigation reports and corroborating sources.
