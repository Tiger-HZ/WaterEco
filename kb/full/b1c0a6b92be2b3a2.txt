Abstract Purpose Livestock production is important for rural livelihoods and food supply, but poorly managed manure, wastewater, and contaminated runoff may increase pressure on river systems. This study assessed whether river water-quality patterns in Jombang Regency, East Java, are consistent with livestock-related pollution pressure without using maps, buffer analysis, or spatial modelling. Methods River water-quality monitoring records from 2023 to 2025 were combined with administrative livestock statistics for 2024 and 2025. The dataset included 4,761 East Java monitoring records, of which 90 were from Jombang.
