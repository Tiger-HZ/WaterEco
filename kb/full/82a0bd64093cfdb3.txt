Objective This study aimed to model the effects of physical pretreatments of soymilk and different coagulating agents on the physicochemical, textural, yield, microbial quality, and sensory properties of tofu. Methods Soymilk was subjected to three pretreatments: untreated, thermal pasteurization, and thermal high-intensity ultrasound (thermo-ultrasound). Each soymilk treatment was coagulated using magnesium chloride (MgCl 2 ), calcium chloride (CaCl 2 ), or acetic acid (AA). The resulting tofu types were evaluated based on eight physicochemical and quality variables, together with sensory acceptance using a 9-point hedonic scale.
