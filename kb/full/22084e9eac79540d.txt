This study investigates the application of Six Sigma methodologies, including the DefineMeasure-Analyze-Improve-Control (DMAIC) framework and statistical process control (SPC), to reduce defects and enhance consistency in garri production. Employing a mixed-methods case study design, data were collected from five small scale factories through process observations, microbial analyses, and interviews with 25 personnel. Baseline assessments revealed a 28.4% defect rate (sigma level 2.3), primarily from inconsistent fermentation, poor hygiene, and variable roasting.
