Accurate prediction of droplet impact behavior is essential for applications including spray cooling, coating technologies, additive manufacturing, and inkjet printing. Conventional analytical and empirical models often have limited predictive capability because of the nonlinear interactions among liquid properties, impact conditions, and surface wettability. This study develops machine learning models to predict the maximum spreading coefficient and the critical spreading–splashing threshold velocity using an experimental dataset of more than 700 droplet impacts spanning multiple liquids and hydrophilic, hydrophobic, and superhydrophobic surfaces.
