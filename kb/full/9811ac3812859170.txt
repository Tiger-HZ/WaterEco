Smallholder farmers with less than 2 hectares contribute more than 1/3rd of the world food supply but continue to suffer the challenges of low nutrient use efficiency (30-40%), reduced soil fertility and lack of access to precision agriculture technologies. The aim of the review is to synthesize the evidence of 235 peer-reviewed articles (2010-2025) to assess the precision nutrient management (PNM) technologies, namely, in terms of their applicability, economic feasibility, and adoption capacity within the smallholder farming systems. Systematic literature review according to PRISMA, the analysis of articles on satellite and UAV-based remote sensing, IoT-enabled soil sensors, variable rate application systems, machine learning algorithms, and case studies worldwide were summarized.
