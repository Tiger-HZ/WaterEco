# AMENDMENT CXLIV — SCNAP CLOSED-LOOP BIOLOGICAL WASTE-TO-FOOD CYCLE ## McCaul Compendium | Patent Family 25 | Addendum to Amendment CXLIII **Author:** Justin McCaul, Independent Researcher, Wilburton, Oklahoma **Date:** May 25, 2026 **Depends on:** Amendment CXLIII (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20372163), Amendment LXXX (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20276782) --- ## 1. ABSTRACT Amendment CXLIII established the SOL Coherence Nutritive Assembly Platform (SCNAP) as a device capable of reorganizing CHONPS-containing substrate into biologically coherent food. That amendment assumed soil, water, and atmospheric inputs as the primary substrate source. This amendment establishes and claims a separately patentable application: the use of sterilized human biological waste — urine and feces — as a closed-loop substrate input to the SCNAP system. The closed-loop biological waste cycle removes all external resupply dependency for food production in isolated environments, with specific and significant application to submarine operations, space habitation, polar stations, and extended forward operating bases. The system is designated: **SCNAP-CL** (Closed-Loop variant). --- ## 2. THE SUBSTRATE PROBLEM Amendment CXLIV (144) of McCaul's Law of Coherence compendium. Author: Justin McCaul.
