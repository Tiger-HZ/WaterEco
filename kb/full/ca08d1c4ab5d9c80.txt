Abstract Background Menopause is a major life transition frequently accompanied by vasomotor, psychological, and somatic symptoms that adversely affect women's quality of life (QoL). Due to concerns regarding the long‐term safety of hormone therapy, many women seek complementary and alternative treatments. Homeopathy has gained attention as a potential nonhormonal option for managing menopausal symptoms; however, evidence from randomized controlled trials (RCTs) remains limited and inconsistent. Objectives To systematically review RCTs evaluating the effects of homeopathic interventions on menopausal symptom severity and QoL in menopausal women and to quantitatively synthesize the available evidence through meta‐analysis.
