Existing fractal contact models for normal stiffness suffer from the scale paradox, rely on parameters that are difficult to measure, and yield unstable predictions under thermo-mechanical loads. To overcome these issues, this study systematically extends the ubiquitiform theory to thermo-elastoplastic contact stiffness modeling.
