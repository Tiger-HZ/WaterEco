Microplastics are emerging contaminants of increasing concern in urban aquatic environments, yet information on their occurrence in Cambodia remains extremely limited. This study provides a first baseline assessment of microplastic contamination in six major urban drainage canals in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Wastewater samples were analyzed using wet peroxide oxidation, density separation, filtration, gravimetric analysis, and stereomicroscopic identification. Microplastic abundance was quantified based on mass and particle concentrations, while particle shape, color, and size were characterized.
