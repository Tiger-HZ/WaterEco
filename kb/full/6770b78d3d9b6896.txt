Hunimua Beach is one of several beautiful beaches in the Maluku Province. The beach is located in Liang Village, Salahutu District, Central Maluku Regency, and covers an area of around 88,360 m2 to 890,500 m2. Hunimua Beach is a tourist attraction that is highly popular with the public and frequently overcrowded. Therefore, we need information on water quality to support new ecotourism activities at Hunimua Beach. The aim of this study is to identify water parameters for the diversification of banana boat, boating, and jet ski tourism activities at Hunimua Beach. Data collection in this study was conducted through direct water sampling.
