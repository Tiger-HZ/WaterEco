This study investigates the spatiotemporal variability, spatial consistency, and non-stationarity of meteorological and hydrological droughts in three major water management basins (WMBs) of northern and central Kazakhstan: Nura–Sarysu, Esil, and Tobyl–Torgai. Long-term instrumental observations from meteorological stations and hydrological gauges were used to calculate the Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI), Standardized Precipitation Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI), and Streamflow Drought Index (SDI). Structural changes were detected using the Pettitt, Buishand, and CUSUM tests, while spatial consistency was quantitatively assessed as the proportion of active meteorological stations simultaneously experiencing drought conditions.
