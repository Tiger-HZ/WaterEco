This protocol is adapted from Hotos &amp; Antoniadis (2022). As many filamentous cyanobacterial species resist conventional centrifugation-based pelleting, cells are instead collected by vacuum filtration onto polycarbonate membranes and resuspended in extraction solvent by vortexing. The freeze-thaw regime and extraction solvent have been optimized to improve pigment recovery in Raphidiopsis raciborskii. Refinements were informed by Tanet al. (2020) and Horváthet al. (2013); we recommend consulting both when adapting this protocol to other systems. Successful completion of the protocol will provide yields of phycoerythrin, phycocyanin, and allophycocyanin from each sample.
