A magnitude 6.9 earthquake struck northern Cebu on 30 September 2025, with a shallow hypocenter at ∼5 km depth located offshore about 19 km NE of Bogo City. No active fault had previously been mapped within the epicentral region, raising questions about the earthquake source and the distribution of active deformation in the central Philippines. We investigated the causative fault, coseismic deformation, and associated impacts using seismicity, satellite-derived deformation, high-resolution geomorphic interpretation, and post-earthquake field observations. The datasets indicate rupture of an unmapped NE-trending, right-lateral strike-slip structure, herein referred to as the Bogo Bay Fault.
