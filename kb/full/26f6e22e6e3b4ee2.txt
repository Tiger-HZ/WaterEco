ABSTRACT Intensive aquaculture practices heavily reliant on conventional energy sources have contributed significantly to environmental degradation, primarily through greenhouse gas emissions. The sector faces ongoing challenges, including high energy consumption, land‐use conflicts, and unreliable electricity supply, all of which impede its sustainable development and pose broader risks to both human survival and long‐term ecological balance. The quest for efficient solar energy utilization has led to the emergence of aquavoltaics, a novel integrated model that combines photovoltaic technology with aquaculture.
