Abstract Rare-earth metal catalysts have unique catalytic properties. In order to uncover the critical roles of amine additives and water for Sc catalysts, this study theoretically investigates the trans-diastereoselective [3 + 2] annulation of α,β-unsaturated aldimines with alkenes catalyzed by a C5Me4SiMe3-ligated half-sandwich scandium catalyst. 1-adamantylamine (AdNH2) is found to facilitate the cyclization step by lowering the Gibbs free energy by 3.6 kcal mol–1 and promote catalyst regeneration via 1-adamantylamine-mediated hydrogen exchange. The newly identified 1-adamantylamine-promoted borrowing-hydrogen ring-opening/ring-closing steps enable configuration inversion of intermediate 5 t to its isomer 5 c, thereby establishing a cis-selective pathway with an energy span of 29.4 kcal
