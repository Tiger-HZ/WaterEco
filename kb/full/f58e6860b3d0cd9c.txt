Sesame oil is a valuable edible oil with high nutritional and antioxidant properties, widely used in food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic industries. Its quality can deteriorate during storage due to oxidation, moisture absorption, and exposure to light, making the selection of packaging materials crucial in agro-industrial production. This study examined the effect of different packaging materials on the quality retention of sesame oil, examining the impact of various packaging materials on the retention of quality under agro-industrial storage conditions.
