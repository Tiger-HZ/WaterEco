The article substantiates the theoretical and applied foundations of innovation-oriented management of healthcare institutions under martial law, increasing demand for high-tech medical care and limited managerial resources. The relevance of the research is determined by the fact that digitalization, angiographic care, telemedicine, transplantation capacity, rehabilitation services and service innovations require not only financing, but also high-quality management, evidence-based procurement, trained teams, process integration and staff psychological readiness for change. The concepts of innovation activity of healthcare institutions, management potential, high-tech medical care, managerial gap and staff psychological readiness are clarified.
