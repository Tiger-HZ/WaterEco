Cyanobacteria are photosynthetic prokaryotes commonly found in rice paddy ecosystems. They play a crucial role in the nitrogen cycle and help maintain the balance of agricultural ecosystems. This study aims to analyze the cell structure of cyanobacteria found in rice field water in Karanganyar Regency based on microscopic observations without using any staining treatments. Water samples were taken from a rice field in Karanganyar Regency during the active planting season. The observation was carried out using a light microscope with the wet mount method, without staining, to keep the cells in their natural condition.
