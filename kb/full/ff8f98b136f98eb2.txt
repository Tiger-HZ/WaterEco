Modern water resources management is increasingly challenged by rising water demand and transboundary constraints, with water crises in river basins becoming increasingly systemic in nature. Alongside growing pressures on water resources, there is an increasing need for scien-tifically robust methods of forecasting and risk assessment, making the development and appli-cation of contemporary water management models particularly relevant.
