Abstract This study evaluated the phytochemical composition, antioxidant, antimutagenic, antifungal and antibacterial activities of O. sanctum leaf extracts, with particular focus on sewage water-borne antibiotic-resistant bacteria. Methanolic, ethyl acetate and hexane extracts were prepared and evaluated for phytochemical constituents, total phenolic and flavonoid contents and antioxidant activity. The methanolic extract (OsLME) showed the highest phenolic (43.15 ± 0.52 mg GAE g −1 ) and flavonoid (19.34 ± 0.36 mg RE g −1 ) contents and showed the highest antioxidant activity (89.12 ± 1.8 % inhibition at 4,000 μg mL −1 ).
