Objective To explore the correlation between anxiety status, sleep quality, and quality of life in patients with functional dyspepsia (FD) and to analyze the mediating role of sleep quality between anxiety status and quality of life. Methods A retrospective analysis was performed on 247 patients with FD between January 2023 to December 2025. The assessments included general information, the SAS, PSQI, and SF-36. Using SPSS 26.0 and PROCESS, we built an intermediary model to verify sleep quality as a mediator between anxiety and quality of life in patients with FD. Results SAS scores correlated positively with PSQI scores ( r = 0.543, P &amp;lt; 0.05) and inversely with SF-36 scores ( r = -0.775, P &amp;lt; 0.05).
