The resource utilization of eutrophic cyanobacterial biomass provides a sustainable route for developing biochar-based catalytic materials for dye wastewater treatment. Herein, eutrophic cyanobacteria from Dianchi Lake, Kunming, were used as raw materials to prepare functional catalytic materials via pyrolysis-modification. A trimetallic photocatalyst, CuFeZnXBC400 (XBC = cyanobacterial biochar), was fabricated for methylene blue (MB) degradation without hydrogen peroxide or other external oxidants. The samples were characterized by scanning electron microscopy (SEM), Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) analysis, X-ray diffraction (XRD), X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry (LC–MS), and three-dimensional fluorescence spectroscopy.
