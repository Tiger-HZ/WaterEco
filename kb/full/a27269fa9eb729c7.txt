Abstract Background The Norms and Standards Regulations provide a regulatory foundation for safe, quality care in South African public health facilities. Yet implementation remains inconsistent, with persistent non-compliance reported in Tshwane Municipality. Because healthcare managers translate these regulations into routine practice, their capacity is critical. However, limited evidence exists on how managers themselves understand the role of capacity building in supporting implementation. This study explored healthcare managers’ perspectives on capacity building as a strategic mechanism for implementing the Norms and Standards Regulations and improving quality of care.
