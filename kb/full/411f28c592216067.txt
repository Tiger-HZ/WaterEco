Access to safe drinking water remains a critical public health challenge in indigenous urban settlements across sub-Saharan Africa, where sanitation deficits, aging infrastructure, and poor household water management compound source-level contamination. Isale-Oyo indigenous community in Oyo Town, southwestern Nigeria, exemplifies these vulnerabilities through high population density, traditional compound housing, shared sanitation facilities, and near-total dependence on shallow groundwater.
