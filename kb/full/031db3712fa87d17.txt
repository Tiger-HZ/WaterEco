One of the cognitive challenges suffered by internally displaced persons globally is the quality of their sleep. Among displaced persons, sleep quality is affected by environmental, social and behavioural factors. Till date, the role of personality traits on sleep quality is inconsistent and still questionable. Therefore, this study investigated personality traits on sleep quality among internally displaced persons in Benue North-west. The study adopted cross-sectional survey design where the participants were 384 internally displaced persons comprising of 155 (40.4%) males and 229 (59.6%) females.
