Water is a very important resource, especially for its usage in domestic and irrigation purposes. It is therefore ‎pertinent to evaluate and determine the quality of water in our locality. In this study, five (5) freshwater bodies, ‎namely Obibia River, Ogbunago Stream, Ovia Stream, Otukpuma River, and Diochiogba River, located at Enugu-‎Agidi, Njikoka LGA of Anambra State, were evaluated for their suitability for drinking and other domestic use. ‎Three water samples were collected in-stitu from each stream, making it a total of fifteen water samples from the five ‎sites, and analyzed in the laboratory for their physicochemical and heavy metal content.
