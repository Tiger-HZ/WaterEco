Causal and interpretable fault diagnosis is essential for ensuring the stable operation and efficient maintenance of large-scale microservice systems. However, the existing fault diagnosis approaches confront two significant challenges: (1) existing approaches usually suffer from a lack of causality, which leads to their tendency to attribute faults to superficial phenomena rather than investigating the root causes behind superficial phenomena. (2) existing approaches typically suffer from a lack of interpretability, which makes it difficult to provide effective guidance for subsequent troubleshooting.
