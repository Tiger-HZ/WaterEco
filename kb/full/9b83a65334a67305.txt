Salt-marsh vegetation is jointly influenced by episodic hydrological exposure and the hydrogeomorphic setting in which inundation occurs, yet their roles may differ across temporal and spatial scales. Here, we examined these scale-dependent associations in the Minjiang Estuary, southeastern China, using 1127 fixed sampling sites monitored quarterly from 2018 to 2025. Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical imagery, regional hydroclimatic data, and hydrogeomorphic covariates were integrated to quantify seasonal vegetation anomalies and multi-year spatial variability.
