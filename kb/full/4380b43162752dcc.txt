Habitat quality affects the functioning of ecosystems, and analyzing its evolution characteristics and driving factors is important for regional ecological management. This study, based on multi-source remote sensing data and using methods such as the InVEST model and geographic detectors, explores the spatial and temporal evolution of habitat quality in the rocky desertification control area and quantifies the influence of various driving factors.
