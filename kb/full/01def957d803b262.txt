Access to potable water in oil-producing communities of the Niger Delta remains constrained by decades of hydrocarbon exploration and inadequate environmental management. This study evaluates the physicochemical quality of groundwater and surface water in Otuabagi Community, Ogbia Local Government Area, Bayelsa State, Nigeria. It assesses their suitability for domestic use through the Weighted Arithmetic Water Quality Index (WQI) integrated with Geographic Information System (GIS)-based spatial mapping.
