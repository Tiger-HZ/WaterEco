Abstract A numerical solute transport model was calibrated to a high‐resolution monitoring data set to characterize a multicomponent source of nonaqueous phase liquids (NAPLs) and evaluate the uncertainty of estimated parameters. The dissolution of NAPL mass was simulated using SEAM3D with parameter zones including adjustable NAPL saturations and mass transfer coefficients, representing the heterogenous architecture of the source zone. Source zone parameters were simultaneously estimated using PEST from aqueous‐phase concentrations measured in a multilevel monitoring transect and from mass recovery rates measured at extraction wells during a controlled field experiment. Data‐worth analyses, facilitated by PEST ancillary software, linked maximum aqueous‐phase concentrations of all compounds
