Spasmodic dysphonia (SD) is a vocal pathology characterized by tense, spasmodic, and tremulous voice, affecting the intrinsic laryngeal musculature. It is characterized by a series of involuntary and unpredictable contractions, and two main types have been described: abductor and adductor, with the last one being the most frequent. To date, botulinum toxin is considered the best option for treating this pathology. A study was carried out on 17 patients between 33 and 77 years old, men and women, with a diagnosis of spasmodic dysphonia before and after treatment with botulinum toxin.
