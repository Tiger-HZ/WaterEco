We thank the authors for their thoughtful and constructive comments on our simplified MRI-derived vertebral bone quality score using L1/L2 measurements VBQ L1/2 . We agree with the authors that multicenter studies incorporating heterogeneous scanners and acquisition protocols are necessary before absolute VBQ cutoff values can be broadly applied. Our study was designed to assess whether VBQ measurement could be simplified from L1–4 to L1/L2 without compromising performance, rather than to establish a universal threshold. VBQ L1/2 showed excellent agreement with VBQ L1-4 and comparable discrimination for osteoporosis and impaired bone quality.
