We present the first year‐round estimates of benthic primary production at four contrasting shallow (3–22 m depth) benthic habitats in a southwest Greenland fjord. In situ measurements were performed using the noninvasive aquatic eddy‐correlation (EC) oxygen (O 2 ) flux method. A series of high‐quality multiple‐day EC data sets document the presence of a year‐round productive benthic phototrophic community. The shallow‐water sites were on average autotrophic during the spring and summer months, up to 43.6 mmol O 2 m −2 d −1 , and heterotrophic or close to metabolic balance during the autumn and winter.
