Quality improvement is the application of a systematic method for working through complex problems: it involves the people closest to the issue in discovering new solutions, testing those ideas in a structured way, and learning through the use of data [1]. Data, both qualitative and quantitative, is integral to quality improvement, helping teams learn whether an idea has had its predicted effect [using data at the level of the Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycle], and helping them understand whether the system is improving over time (using data at the project level, presented as outcome, process, and balancing measures on statistical ­process control (SPC) charts) [2].
