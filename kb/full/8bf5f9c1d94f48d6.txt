Abstract Vascular-mobile lncRNAs can function as systemic signals in abiotic stress adaptation. However, their direct molecular mechanisms—particularly the ability to recruit chromatin remodelers for modulating chromatin architecture at target loci—remain largely unexplored in sink tissues, where chromatin accessibility and transcriptional reprogramming are critical for systemic adaptation. Here, we show that the lncRNA QingLattice acts as a mobile signal during iron (Fe) deficiency, moving from shoot to root tissues where it reprograms target gene expression through two synergistic epigenetic pathways.
