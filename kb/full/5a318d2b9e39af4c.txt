Light and temperature strongly influence the vertical distribution of the mysid shrimp, Mysis relicta . We monitored the vertical movements and depth selection behavior of mysids exposed to different light intensities and light–temperature gradients in the laboratory and derived a mysid light preference function in units relevant to mysid vision: “mylux”. Mysids preferred light levels between 10 −8 and 10 −7 mylux (∼10 −6 to 10 −5 lux) and rarely moved into waters of 10 −3 mylux (∼0.1 lux) and greater. A model that assumed equal weight and independence of mysid light and temperature preference functions successfully predicted the proportion of mysids found in two different temperature–light combinations in the laboratory.
