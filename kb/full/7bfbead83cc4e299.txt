Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) require engineers skilled in systems thinking and decision-making under uncertainty; however, traditional pedagogy struggles to simulate dynamic, multi-agent interactions. This mixed-methods study evaluates the efficacy of OpenTTD , an open-source transport simulation game, as a scalable and reproducible game-based learning (GBL) tool in undergraduate ITS education. Quantitative results revealed significantly higher post-test scores in applied and strategic domains for the experimental group, particularly in transport network design and scenario-based problem-solving.
