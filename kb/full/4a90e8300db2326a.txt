ABSTRACT Background Children with disabilities are advised to meet the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans and be as active as possible. A key opportunity for physical activity (PA) is physical education (PE). Methods State laws (50 states and DC) as of December 31, 2023, were compiled from commercial legal databases and double‐coded as part of the National Cancer Institute's CLASS database. Laws were coded for any adapted physical education (APE) provision, APE teacher certification and qualification, and PE exemptions. Results While 49 states addressed the availability of APE, 16 states' laws did not indicate who is eligible.
