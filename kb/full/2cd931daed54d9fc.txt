A total of twenty samples each of five different types of fruits—watermelon, apple, banana, and orange—were collected from vendors and hawkers near the NDDC hostel, hostel H, hostel C, and hostel B. These samples were surface- sterilized using distilled water and analyzed following standard microbiological procedures. The homogenized samples were cultured on bacteriological media using the spread plate technique and then incubated aerobically at suitable temperatures and times. Pure cultures of the resulting microbial isolates were obtained from subcultures of the primary plates.
