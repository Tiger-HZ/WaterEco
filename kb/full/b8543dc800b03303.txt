To address ecological degradation in estuarine coastal wetlands caused by the combined pressures of land–sea interactions and anthropogenic activities, we developed an improved Coastal Wetland Ecological Status Index (CWESI). Using Landsat time-series observations from 1994 to 2024, together with the coefficient of variation (CV), Theil–Sen slope estimator, Mann–Kendall significance test, and Hurst exponent, we quantified the spatiotemporal dynamics and persistence characteristics of ecological quality in the Liaohe Estuary coastal wetland. Furthermore, an optimal parameter-based geographical detector (OPGD) model was employed to investigate the relative contributions and interactive effects of natural and anthropogenic factors on ecological quality heterogeneity.
