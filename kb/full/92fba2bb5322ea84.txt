Abstract Constructed wetlands (CWs) are known to be more sustainable alternatives compared to conventional treatment systems. However, hydraulic performance aspects have not been extensively emphasized within design guidelines. Given that hydraulic performance plays a critical role in determining the efficiency of CWs, detailed investigation into these aspects is essential. In this paper, ANSYS Fluent was used to model the flow in full-scale CW (108 m 2 ) and evaluate the hydraulic performance in the systems. Through validation, R 2 values were obtained between 0.70 and 0.96 indicating that the numerical model results are in good agreement with the experimental residence time distribution curves.
