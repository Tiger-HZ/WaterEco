Introduction Reliable flash flood forecasting is essential for sustainable storm infrastructure planning and hazard mitigation in sensitive dryland landscapes. Loss or infiltration models are central to flash flood prediction in arid watersheds, where runoff is dominated by infiltration excess. Parsimonious models are critical for use in practice. Accurate prediction remains a challenge because calibration data are typically scarce or unavailable. Regionalization can help fill this data gap: model parameters from a physically similar donor basin with available calibration data are transferred to a target watershed with no or limited data.
