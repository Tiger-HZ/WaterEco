A comparison of seven methods for extracting the seasonal component was performed on model data simulating tiltmeter observations in Kamchatka. The seasonal signal was a smoothed meander (period 365 days, duty cycle 0.6), and the noise was flicker noise with exponent α=2.9. The signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) was varied from –25 to +20 dB, with 30 realizations generated for each of the 12 SNR levels. Quality was assessed using RMSE, correlation, standard deviation ratio, and a developed residual periodicity metric. The mean seasonal function (MSF) method consistently outperformed all other methods across all metrics over the entire SNR range.
