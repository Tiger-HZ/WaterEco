Water has played a pivotal role in the evolution of human civilization. It is not merely an essential natural resource for human survival but has also served as the foundation for agriculture, animal husbandry, trade, industry, sanitation, and urban planning. Ancient civilizations typically flourished along riverbanks, as rivers provided the resources necessary for drinking water, irrigation, and agriculture. Among India's ancient civilizations, the Indus Valley Civilization—also known as the Harappan Civilization—is particularly notable for its advancements in water management and urban planning.The Indus Valley Civilization developed primarily around the Indus River and its tributaries.
