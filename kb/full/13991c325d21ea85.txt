Abstract Purpose This study tests the hypothesis that the physicochemical characteristics and reproducible geochemical-mineralogical relationships of urban surface-deposited sediments are fundamentally controlled by the combined effects of the local lithogenic substrate, anthropogenic material inputs, and landscape conditions governing sediment production. To address this, a comparative study was conducted using a consistent sampling and analytical framework across cities with contrasting geological and anthropogenic characteristics. Methods A total of 300 sediment samples were collected from eight cities within the Sverdlovsk Oblast, Russia.
