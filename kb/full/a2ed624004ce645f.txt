Abstract Sea surface height (SSH) retrieval in complex coastal waters using spaceborne Global Navigation Satellite System Reflectometry (GNSS-R) remains challenging due to the intricate coupling between delay-Doppler map (DDM) scattering signatures and ambient environmental factors. To address this issue, this study takes the Beibu Gulf as a case study and proposes CSFMamba (Cross-State Fusion Mamba), a novel deep learning framework that integrates fractional Fourier transform (FrFT) feature enhancement with a dual-branch Mamba architecture. The proposed network constructs a dual-channel FrFT-enhanced DDM representation, extracts spatial delay-Doppler dependencies via Spa-Mamba, models environmental-geometric couplings via Spe-Mamba, and fuses both modalities via F-Mamba for SSH prediction
