We measured accumulation rates of organic carbon (OC) and total nitrogen (TN) and stable carbon (δ 13 C org ) and nitrogen isotopes (δ 15 N) in the sediments of two mountain lakes (Lake Ledvica and Lake Planina, northwest Slovenia). Marked variations of these parameters were observed in both sediments. OC accumulation rates ranged from 4 to 23 g m −2 yr −1 in Lake Ledvica, whereas in Lake Planina they were one order of magnitude higher and increased substantially in the upper sediments. δ 13 C org and δ 15 N decreased upward in Lake Ledvica, from ‐25.5‰ to ‐28.4‰, and from +2.1‰ to ‐3.4‰, respectively. In contrast, substantial variations in the δ 13 C org profile, ranging from ‐30.9‰ to ‐37.4‰, but a quite uniform δ 15 N profile of approximately +1.8‰, was observed in Lake Planina. Element
