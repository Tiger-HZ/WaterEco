Ensiling ground corn after rehydration improves its conservation and increases starch susceptibility to ruminal degradation, potentially replacing costly chemical preservatives. Three complementary experiments were conducted. Intake, ingestive behavior, and blood parameters were evaluated in 32 growing goat kids; nutrient digestibility and N-balance were evaluated in 24 growing goats; and ruminal parameters and in situ degradability were assessed in four rumen-cannulated, castrated male Santa Inês sheep (30.4 ± 6.0 kg) using a 4 × 4 Latin square design, evaluating the replacement of ground corn (GC) with rehydrated GC silage using water (RCSwater), cactus pear cladode mucilage (RCSmucilage), or whey (RCSwhey).
