This study, conducted at the Students’ Demonstration Farm from September to November 2023, evaluated the impact of irrigation strategies on cucumber yield and performance. The results demonstrated that irrigating at 100% of crop evapotranspiration (ETc, treatment W1) with a daily interval produced the peak yield of 121 ton/ha, alongside the highest fruit weight (0.565 g), length (35.5 cm), diameter (5.95 cm) and number of fruits per plant (13). In stark contrast, a severe deficit of 25% ETc (W4) caused drastic declines, which reduced yield by 91% (10.83 ton/ha), fruit weight by 68%, fruit length by 18%, fruit diameter by 14% and fruit number by 69%.
