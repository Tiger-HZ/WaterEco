Depression is associated with persistent negative self-beliefs and altered social information processing. Although considerable research has examined self-perception in depression, less is known about how depressive symptoms relate to beliefs about how romantic partners perceive the self and whether relationship quality moderates these associations. The present study investigated associations between depressive symptoms, self-perceptions, metaperceptions of partner evaluations, and memory for self-referential information across multiple social perspectives.
