The concentration of total Hg was compared among 45 wetlands in three regions of Ontario. Twenty-two of these wetlands were located in the Muskoka-Haliburton Highlands and Temagami - North Bay regions and included bogs, fens, and marshes. Twenty-three were riverine marshes along the St. Lawrence River, near Cornwall, a Great Lakes area of concern, where Hg has been released through industrial activity. Overall, significant but weak negative relationships were found between pH and alkalinity of the surface waters and total water Hg concentrations (r 2 = 0.28-0.30, p &lt; 0.001). A significant positive relationship was found between dissolved organic C and total water Hg (r 2 = 0.30).
