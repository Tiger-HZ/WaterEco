Abstract Recently, ozone (O 3 ) concentrations in urban areas between the Yangtze and Huaihe Rivers (YHR) in China have risen steadily, and the annual onset of O 3 pollution events has advanced. O 3 pollution events could be observed during early spring, yet studies investigating the vertical distribution of ozone during this period remain scarce. To explore the vertical structure of O 3 and meteorological influences, as well as its effects on surface O 3 pollution between the YHR during early spring, multifactor simultaneous vertical observation experiments were conducted in Hefei in April 2022.
