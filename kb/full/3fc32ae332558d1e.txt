Reservoir development in the Niger Delta is affected by geological uncertainties arising from complex stratigraphic architecture, high-frequency sea-level changes, fault-controlled subsidence, and variable sediment supply, which complicate prediction of reservoir geometry, continuity, and quality. This study integrates sequence stratigraphy and petrophysical analysis of the deep-offshore ``BX'' Field, Niger Delta Basin, to define stratigraphic controls on reservoir distribution and quantify properties relevant to hydrocarbon potential and field development. Three-dimensional seismic data and well-log suites from four wells were integrated to interpret sequence boundaries and maximum flooding surfaces and establish Lowstand, Transgressive, and Highstand System Tracts.
