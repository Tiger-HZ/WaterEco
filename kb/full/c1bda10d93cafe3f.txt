This study looks at the eco-friendly synthesis of iron oxide nanoparticles (FeO-NPs) using filamentous freshwater algae, Spirogyra sp. and Zygnema sp. collected from Mokhada Tahasil, District Palghar, Maharashtra. The Algal extracts were used as reducing and stabilizing agents in the synthesis of iron oxide nanoparticles. The produced nanoparticles were characterized using UV-Visible Spectroscopy, Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM), Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR), and X-Ray Diffraction. UV-Visible spectra of the samples showed the formation of nanoparticles with absorption in the range of 290 to 350 nm.
