This study investigates the long-run relationships between aquaculture production (AP), fisheries production (FP), natural resource rents (NRR), economic growth, and CO₂ emissions in China, India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia over the period 1990–2021. Long-run relationships are examined using the LM bootstrap co-integration test, while the Common Correlated Effects Mean Group (CCEMG) estimator is employed for long-run coefficient estimation. The Augmented Mean Group (AMG) estimator and additional sensitivity analyses are also used to assess the robustness of the findings. The baseline CCEMG results do not reveal statistically significant panel-level effects of AP, FP, NRR, or GDP.
