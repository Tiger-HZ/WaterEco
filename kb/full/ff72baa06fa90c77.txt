Sensory qualities that are perceptible to the human senses such as appearance, flavor, texture, scent, taste, and general acceptability of food are commonly used to evaluate the quality of food product. This study was carried out to analyze the sensory properties of blends of aerial yam and soybean flours. The aerial yam bulbs and soybean seeds were processed into flour. The flours were then blended at different ratios, in percentage of (aerial yam flour: soybean flour) 25.00:75.00 (sample A); 37.50:62.50 (sample B); 50.00:50.00 (sample C); 62.50:37.50 (sample D); and75.00:25.00 (sample E), respectively.
