This study is aimed at characterizing groundwater aquifers using electrical resistivity tomography (ERT) in parts of Rivers State, Nigeria. About five (5) vertical electrical soundings (VES) were conducted using the Schlumberger configuration, with current electrode spacing of 200m to 600m across different VES points. ABEM Tarrameter SAS 300B was used for earth probing and the data collected were analyzed using IP 12 WIN software. The results obtained reveled aquifer resistivities and thicknesses ranging from 7.667Ωm – 160,000Ωm and 0.331m – 35.1m respectively. The resistivity values obtained revealed the depth through which people of the area can obtain fresh water ranging from 35m to 50m, with adequate transmission rate.
