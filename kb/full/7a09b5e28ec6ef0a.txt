ABSTRACT Due to its high activity for the hydrogen evolution reaction at low overpotential in alkaline conditions, platinum is a promising cathode catalyst material for next‐generation, high current density alkaline water electrolysis (AWE). However, its implementation in industrially relevant zero‐gap AWE remains poorly understood, particularly regarding catalyst utilization, durability, and impurity tolerance. Here we establish electrode structure‐activity‐stability relationships for a series of Pt cathodes prepared by galvanic displacement, sputtering, annealing, and dealloying, allowing systematic control over microstructure and adhesion.
