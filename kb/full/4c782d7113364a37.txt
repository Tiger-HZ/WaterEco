Electronic archiving systems are designed to manage, preserve, store, and retrieve electronic documents efficiently and effectively. Their primary purpose is to improve document management in digital environments while reducing document accumulation and the difficulties associated with searching for records in traditional paper-based systems. However, acquiring and implementing commercial electronic document management systems has become increasingly expensive for many organizations and institutions. Consequently, many have turned to free open-source solutions that provide effective document management capabilities comparable to, and in some cases exceeding, those of commercial systems.
