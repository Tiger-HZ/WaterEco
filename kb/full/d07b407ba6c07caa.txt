This article identifies the need of electric vehicles (EVs) and analyzes how EVs are eco-friendly for Air Quality Index (AIQ) in Pakistan. It also explores the challenges faced by EV industry to be launched in Pakistan. Being an exploratory study, the qualitative research methodology is used as netnography technique to collect data using the comments of different websites and social media platforms. Those negative and positive reviews are analyzed by using NVivo 20 Software. EVs can reduce greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) and improve air quality. EVs have the potential to play significant monetary improvements in transportation sector because of their innovativeness as compared to the traditional vehicles in Pakistan. Due to technological advancement, this industry has the potential to create new jobs in manufacturing, sales, and maintenance fields. But there are some sustainability challenges to be addressed (i.e., limited resources and lack of infrastructure) for launching EVs in Pakistan. Therefore, government should revise policies and take initiatives for promoting the adoption of EVs. Tax exemptions can be a biggest step for encouraging installation of EV charging stations. Ultimately, it will open avenues for international investors and progress of our country.
