Groundwater is the main sources of water supply in Afghanistan, especially that areas where access to surface water is hard and difficult. Nangarhar Province has significant surface and groundwater resources and depend extensively on these resources for domestic and agricultural usage, including orange orchards, vegetable cultivation &amp; sugarcanes production. Therefore, detecting areas with suitable groundwater potential is essential for sustainable SGRM (groundwater resource management) and well siting. The present study aimed to identify GPZ in Nangarhar Province using RS, GIS, and the AHP.
