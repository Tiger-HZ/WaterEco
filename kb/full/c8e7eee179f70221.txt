In the field of low-light polarisation imaging, it is necessary to establish objective evaluation standards that conform to the subjective perception of the human eye to improve the efficiency of image evaluation and reduce the bias caused by individual differences. In this study, an improved signal-to-noise ratio algorithm without reference images generated under different illuminance (0.001–2.0 lx) is proposed, and compared with traditional evaluation methods such as standard deviation and information entropy, its superiority in the quality evaluation of polarisation images is verified. In this experiment, a time-sharing polarisation imaging system was used to obtain images of polarisation angle, polarisation degree and polarisation intensity by Stokes vector solving, using a WAT-902H2U
