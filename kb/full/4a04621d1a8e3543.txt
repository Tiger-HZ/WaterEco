Abstract Water saturation is an important input parameter in reserve estimation and fluid identification. Many models have been raised to predict water saturation since the Archie's equation was first established in 1942. However, water saturation estimation in tight gas sandstone reservoirs still faces great challenge due to the complicated pore structure and strong heterogeneity at present. In addition, high formation temperature also heavily affects the electrical conductivity of rocks. To accurately predict water saturation in such type of formation, the effect of pore structure and formation temperature to rock resistivity should be considered.
