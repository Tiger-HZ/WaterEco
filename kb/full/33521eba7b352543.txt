The hydrologic cycle describes the continuous movement of water above, on and below the surface of the Earth. The water on the Earth’s surface (surface water) occurs as streams, lakes, and wetlands, as well as bays and oceans. Surface water is any source of water that is open to the atmosphere and is subject to run off from the land. Hence, it is very likely to contain microorganisms that can cause sickness and, in some cases, more serious, even fatal illnesses. The greatest posed of the problem of surface water is its pointed sources of contamination. The pointed sources are mainly from human activities; these pollute the surface water with pollutants such as nutrients, pathogens and chemical elements which in turn have much effect on the environment.
