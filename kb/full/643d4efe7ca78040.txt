ABSTRACT Artificial lagoons are unique structures that serve as key reservoirs for surplus treated wastewater (TWW), presenting unique challenges and opportunities for sustainable urban water management in hyper-arid environments such as Qatar. Despite their important role, policymakers have been cautious with TWW reuse due to perceived public resistance, as public knowledge, awareness, and involvement in lagoon management remain limited, which may impact effective future management of such systems. Public perceptions and knowledge of artificial lagoons are addressed in this study using an online survey.
