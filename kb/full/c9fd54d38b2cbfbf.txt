Coastal eutrophication is a key environmental concern in Finland. A highly indented, well-settled coastline with a myriad of small estuaries means that eutrophication occurs at numerous localities. There is a clear need for general models that predict eutrophication across estuaries. Lake eutrophication has been successfully predicted using a combination of chlorophyll a (Chl) - total phosphorus (TP) regression models and TP mass-balance models. We applied this limnological approach to 19 Finnish estuaries. The Chl-TP regression was highly significant, accounting for 67% of the variation in Chl.
