This study examined the effect of service quality and patients’ satisfaction in hospitals in North-central Nigeria. The mediating role of hospital image specifically, the study x-rayed the individual effects of tangibility, assurance, reliability, responsiveness and empathy on the satisfaction of patients in hospitals in north-Central Nigeria. The study was anchored on Expectancy Disconfirmation Theory (EDT) postulated by Oliver 1980. Cross-sectional survey research design was adopted for the study. A study population of three hundred and eighty four (384) was used for the study. Structural Equation Modelling(SEM) was used to analyze the collected data.
