Functional relationship regression refers to that class of statistical model where a functional relationship is assumed to exist between two arithmetic variables, but the two arithmetic variables can only be viewed with measurement error and (or) natural variability. The goal is to estimate the underlying functional relationship when observing only the variables containing error or natural variability. While statistical details need to be worked out, some general approaches to this class of problem can be recommended. First of all, nonlinear least squares can provide maximum likelihood estimates when the error variance ratio, λ;, is assumed known.
