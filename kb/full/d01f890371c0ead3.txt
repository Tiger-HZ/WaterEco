Thrips tabaci is the main interruption of the cotton production in last ten years among the sucking insect pests of cotton in Sindh Pakistan. In the present study, five varieties of cotton (Koong, IR-3701, MNH-886, Naib-878 and non-Bt CRIS-585, CRIS-613, Saami and Nusrat) were evaluated for their resistance against thrips tabaci under field condition at Tandojam, Sindh, Pakistan during the crop season 2024. Thrips tabaci population was appeared on all Bt and non-Bt cotton varieties after the germination of cotton crop.
