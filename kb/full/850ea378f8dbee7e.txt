The inadequate supply of water poses serious risks to public health, sanitation, and environmental sustainability, undermining efforts to achieve Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6), which aims to ensure the availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all by 2030. This study examined the effects of inadequate water supply on the environmental health of urban residents in Enugu Urban, Enugu State, Nigeria. A descriptive survey research design was adopted, targeting a population of 1,029,400 people across three local governments in Enugu Urban. A sample size of 400 was determined using the Taro Yamane formula, and data were collected through a structured questionnaire.
