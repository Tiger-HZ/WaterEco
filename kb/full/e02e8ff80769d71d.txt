Aim. To evaluate the economic efficiency of cultivating early-maturing potato cultivars using novel seaweed-based growth stimulants and micronutrient fertilizers in combination with the recommended mineral fertilizer rate under the conditions of the southern part of the Western Forest-Steppe of Ukraine. Methods. The study was conducted during 2024–2026 in the fields of ABC-AGRO, a private enterprise located in Khmelnytskyi Oblast, on deep low-humus chernozem.
