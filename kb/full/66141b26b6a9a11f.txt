Abstract The objectives of this study were to determine the effect of adding DL-malate on chemical and nutrient profiles of newly developed pellet products. The DL-malate were added with four different levels, which included: T1 (Control) = 0% DL-malate in blend pellet product; T2 = 17% DL-malate in blend pellet product; T3 = 33% DL-malate in blend pellet product; T4 = 50% DL-malate in blend pellet product. Chemical and nutrient profiles were determined using wet chemistry method. The treatment design was a one-way structure with quantitative treatment. The data was analyzed using Mixed Model Procedure of SAS.
