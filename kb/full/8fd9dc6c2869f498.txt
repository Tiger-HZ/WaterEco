Background: Dental professionals are at high risk of work-related musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs), especially affecting the neck, lower back, shoulders and upper extremities. Exercise-based interventions may help prevent and manage these conditions, but evidence in dental professionals remains limited. Aim: To evaluate the efficacy of exercise-based interventions for the prevention and management of musculoskeletal symptoms among dental professionals. Material and methods: Literature published between 2003 and 2025 was reviewed using international scientific databases, primarily PubMed. Intervention, longitudinal, and cross-sectional studies, as well as systematic reviews and meta-analyses, were considered.
