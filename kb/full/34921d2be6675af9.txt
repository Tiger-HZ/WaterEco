Underground coal extraction alters aquatic ecosystems. Quantitative evaluation of microbial responses serves as an indicator for ecological security. High-throughput amplicon sequencing and structural equation modeling were applied to analyze aquatic bacterial and fungal communities before and after operations at the Talahao coal mine. Mining activities correlated with altered physicochemical water properties and reduced microbial community evenness. Environmental variables, including ion accumulation and suspended solid concentrations, mediated the indirect effects of mining on microbial restructuring based on structural equation models.
