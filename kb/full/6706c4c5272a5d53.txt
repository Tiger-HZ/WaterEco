ABSTRACT River fragmentation through channelization and the construction of dams has strongly altered river ecosystems, leading to declines in native fish species and the proliferation of non‐natives. Knowledge gaps still exist about the effects of fragmentation on the structure of ecological assemblages throughout all habitats in large, fragmented rivers. We aimed to assess the impacts of impoundments on benthic fish assemblages in the Austrian Danube by comparing deep, offshore mid‐channel habitats of impounded and free‐flowing sections, examining seasonal differences, and assessing the use of deep and sheltered habitats connected to fast‐flowing mid‐channel habitats.
