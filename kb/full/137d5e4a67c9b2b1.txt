Background. Prostate cancer is a leading malignancy in men, with incidence expected to increase by 2040. Curative treatments prolong survival, but they are associated with substantial adverse effects, particularly during androgen deprivation therapy (ADT), comprising physical functions and quality of life. Aim. To review the current evidence on the efficacy and safety of exercise as an adjunctive intervention across the prostate cancer care continuum with its potential translation into practical clinical recommendations. Material and methods. A narrative review of randomized controlled trials, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, mechanistic studies, and clinical practice guidelines from 2010–2026 was performed.
