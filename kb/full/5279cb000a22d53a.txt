Mathematical modelling of microalgal growth is a fundamental tool for describing biomass dynamics and supporting the analysis and optimisation of cultivation processes. In this study, the batch growth of the halotolerant microalga Dunaliella sp. was investigated using five classical nonlinear growth models: exponential, logistic, Gompertz, Richards, and Baranyi. The experimental growth profile exhibited a clear sigmoidal pattern characterised by an adaptation phase, rapid biomass accumulation, and a transition to stationary conditions. Model parameters were estimated using nonlinear least-squares regression based on the Levenberg–Marquardt algorithm.
