The management of sludge generated during drinking water treatment poses an environmental challenge due to the large volumes produced and the associated disposal costs. This study evaluates the potential use of sludge from the El Bosque Drinking Water Treatment Plant (Cartagena, Colombia) as a partial replacement of fine aggregate in concrete production. The sludge was characterized through physical, chemical, mineralogical, and microstructural analyses, including particle size distribution, Atterberg limits, specific gravity, X-ray diffraction (XRD), X-ray fluorescence (XRF), scanning electron microscopy coupled with energy-dispersive spectroscopy (SEM-EDS), and petrographic examination.
