Lay Summary Recent research has shown that physiological stage significantly affects nutrient utilization in sows. However, systematic evaluations of energy and nutrient digestibility in soybean meal, DDGS, and rapeseed meal across different physiological stages are still lacking. This study systematically evaluated the digestibility of energy and nutrients from DDGS, soybean meal, and rapeseed meal in multiparous sows across gestation, lactation, and the post-weaning period. Results showed that lactating sows have higher available energy values and digestibility of certain nutrients for DDGS and rapeseed meal, which contain higher fiber content, while gestating sows have higher available energy values and digestibility of certain nutrients for soybean meal.
