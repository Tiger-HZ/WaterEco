Nigerian freshwater ecosystems are subjected to multiple exposures of heavy metals, petroleum hydrocarbons, organochlorine and organophosphate pesticides and microplastics. This co-exposure makes risk assessment of contaminants alone more complex and underestimates the combined ecological exposure and impact on aquatic organisms and human communities. The study objectives are to summarize the available ecotoxicological data from 2020 to 2026, which was obtained from the Scopus, Web of Science, PubMed and Google Scholar databases. Eighty-seven peer-reviewed studies were evaluated that fulfilled the pre-defined inclusion criteria.
