ABSTRACT The graphical abstract illustrates groundwater system fluxes in a sand dam, an in-situ rainwater harvesting system, showing key inflows (recharge from surface runoff) and outflows (evaporation, seepage, water abstraction) and major water uses. Water quality is assessed by comparing influent and effluent concentrations to determine pollutant removal efficiency. Positive removal efficiencies were observed for turbidity, TSS, dissolved oxygen, BOD, COD, ammonia, phosphates, iron, and copper, whereas negative removal efficiencies occurred for electrical conductivity, TDS, total hardness, fluoride, sulphates, nitrates, magnesium, zinc, chromium, and total coliforms.
