The knowledge of soil nutrient status is important for making decisions on measures to be adopted to ensure sustainable crop production. The aim of this study was to assess some physical and chemical properties of selected maize farms in Mokwa Local Government Area (LGA), Niger State. Purposive sampling was used to select three farms of size 1-3 hectares. Six soil samples per farm were taken at depth of 0-15cm (total = 18 samples). The soil samples were prepared and analyzed in the laboratory for pH, organic C (OC), available phosphorus (P), total nitrogen (TN), cation exchange capacity (CEC). The results of the soil analysis revealed that the values of soil pH (5.9), OC (2.6%), TN (0.13%), and available P (13.35 mgkg-1) were within the required level for optimum rice cultivation. Converse
