Microplastics, as a new type of water environment pollutant, have been widely detected in river ecosystems worldwide, and their environmental behavior and ecological risks have attracted significant attention. Based on existing research findings, this paper systematically reviews the basic characteristics and main sources of river microplastics, summarizes the current status of pollution occurrence and ecotoxicological effects in typical watersheds, focuses on analyzing the research progress and application limitations of two mainstream treatment technologies, and explores the key factors affecting treatment effectiveness.
