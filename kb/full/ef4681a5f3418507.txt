This study explores the hydrogeological characteristics of springs in Azerbaijan, focusing on their distribution, water quality, and potential for sustainable use. The country's diverse geological formations, including the Greater Caucasus, Lesser Caucasus, Talish mountain-fold zones, and the Kura-Araz Lowland, significantly influence the quantity and quality of groundwater. Groundwater in Azerbaijan is primarily found in fractureporous and fracture-vein aquifers, with spring flow patterns following radial distribution from the center of the mountainous zones to their peripheries. Hydrogeological factors such as lithology, tectonic structures, and erosion zones play a critical role in determining spring formation, discharge rates, and mineralization levels.
