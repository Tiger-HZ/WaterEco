Safe drinking water remains a challenge to public health issue in the midst of lots of borehole and well waters in our various communities; thus, these further justify the trend that the integrity of such a source of drinking water might not be guaranteed in the end. Access to safe drinking water is a human right; and it is one of the basic requirements for good health. Globally, ground water is the largest and most important source of potable water and being a vital source of drinking water for many communities worldwide, particularly in rural areas where access to treated water supply is limited, the quality of groundwater is however affected significantly by several factors, including geological and hydrological conditions, human activities, and infrastructure.
