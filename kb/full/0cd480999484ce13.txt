Accurate daily runoff prediction is fundamental to understanding runoff dynamics and supporting water resources management. However, runoff prediction in snow-influenced alpine catchments remains challenging because complex hydrometeorological and cryospheric interactions generate strongly seasonal and nonstationary runoff dynamics. To address these challenges, we proposed IWOA-VCBA, a multiscale deep learning model integrating variational mode decomposition (VMD), a convolutional neural network (CNN), bidirectional long short-term memory (BiLSTM), an attention mechanism, and an improved whale optimization algorithm (IWOA), using the Lienz catchment in East Tyrol, Austria, as a case study.
