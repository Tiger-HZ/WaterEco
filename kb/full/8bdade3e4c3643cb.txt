Digital transformation modifies the processes, service channels, and institutional capacities through which local governments provide services to citizens. This study aimed to analyze the impact of digital transformation on the quality of public service in Ecuadorian municipalities, considering the digitization of processes, services, and channels, technological integration, institutional capacities, and citizen-centeredness. A quantitative, non-experimental, cross-sectional, correlational-explanatory study was conducted. Participants included 193 citizens over 18 years of age who used municipal services during the previous 12 months.
