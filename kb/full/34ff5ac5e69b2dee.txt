Integrated nutrient management (INM) can combine mineral, organic and biological nutrient sources to support broad bean production. A field experiment was conducted during the Rabi season of 2025-26 at the Agricultural Research Farm, Department of Horticulture, Raja Balwant Singh College, Bichpuri, Agra (U.P.), to study the effect of integrated nutrient management (INM) on the growth, yield and economics of broad bean (Vicia faba L.) cv. Pusa Udit.
