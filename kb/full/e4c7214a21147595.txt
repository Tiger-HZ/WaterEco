Double-suction centrifugal pumps operating in sediment-laden irrigation districts of the Yellow River Basin are prone to severe sediment-induced wear, while achieving erosion resistance and efficiency enhancement under hydraulic performance constraints remains challenging. This study proposes a multi-objective optimization method integrating theoretical model constraints with Shapley-value-based coordinated evaluation. Flow rate, head, and sediment particle size are used as inputs, while material, rotational speed, and impeller outlet diameter are treated as design variables. An optimization model considering efficiency loss, wear rate, and disk friction loss ratio is established, and dynamic Shapley-value weighting is applied to adaptively select design-variable combinations.
