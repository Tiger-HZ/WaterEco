Cyanobacteria are major contributors to global photosynthesis and are intensively studied for sustainable green H 2 production. Central to this process is the bidirectional [NiFe]-hydrogenase HoxEFUYH, yet its physiological redox partners have remained unresolved. Ferredoxin, NAD(H), and NADP(H) have been proposed as partners, but the lack of active enzyme preparations has prevented a definitive assignment. Here, we purified the intact HoxEFUYH complex from Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 under strictly anaerobic conditions and reveal its function as both a bifurcating and confurcating hydrogenase.
