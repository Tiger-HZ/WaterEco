Introduction: Abnormal semen parameters are a significant cause of male infertility, influenced by various lifestyle, medical, and environmental factors. This study aimed to evaluate the prevalence of abnormal semen parameters and explore potential associated factors among patients undergoing semen analysis in Al Kharj area of Saudi Arabia. Methods: This retrospective observational study was conducted at Prince Sattam University Hospital, Al Kharj, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Medical records of male patients who underwent semen analysis between March 2021 and December 2024 were reviewed. Data on demographics, lifestyle factors, comorbidities, genitourinary history, semen parameters, hormonal profile, and imaging findings were collected and analyzed using Stata 17.
