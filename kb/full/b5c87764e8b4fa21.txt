Abstract Background Deep learning–based reconstruction can accelerate MR image acquisition while potentially preserving image quality. Faster imaging is particularly helpful in pediatric patients, who may be more susceptible to motion-related degradation during prolonged examinations. Objective To compare image quality, reader preference, and acquisition time between Deep Resolve–accelerated and conventional turbo spin-echo (TSE) sequences in pediatric knee MRI and to assess inter-reader agreement for the detection of knee abnormalities. Materials and methods This retrospective, institutional review board–approved study included pediatric patients who underwent clinical knee MRI with both conventional and Deep Resolve–accelerated TSE sequences.
