Background: Rajma (Phaseolus vulgaris L.) is considered a viable crop for enhancing soil health, offering high economic returns to farmers, and functioning as a short-duration catch crop. In this context, the present investigation was conducted to assess the effect of integrated nutrient management on the growth and productivity of rajma in the subtropical humid zone of Assam. Methods: A field experiment on rajma (Phaseolus vulgaris L.) was conducted during two consecutive rabi seasons (2024-2025 and 2025-2026) in three villages of Dibrugarh district, Assam, involving three treatment combinations.
