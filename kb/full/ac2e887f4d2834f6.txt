The GEOGloWS ECMWF Streamflow Service represents a paradigm shift in global hydrological forecasting by implementing Hydrology as a Service (HaaS), providing operational river discharge predictions for every river reach worldwide. This review examines the technical architecture, validation performance, and diverse applications of the GEOGloWS system, which centralizes computational resources at the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) to deliver 15-day ensemble streamflow forecasts and historical simulations since 1979 through open web services. The system addresses critical gaps in hydrological data availability, particularly benefiting hydromet services, local governments, and NGOs in data-scarce regions.
