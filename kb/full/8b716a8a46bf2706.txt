This study investigates the spatial distribution of nutrient concentration in agricultural soil by modeling steady-state diffusion under heterogeneous conditions. A finite difference numerical approach is applied to simulate nutrient dynamics in a two-dimensional domain, incorporating variable soil diffusivity and multiple root uptake zones represented by Gaussian sinks. The results reveal the interplay between soil heterogeneity and root-induced nutrient absorption, providing valuable insights into nutrient mobility and accumulation patterns. This modeling framework offers a foundation for optimizing fertilizer applications and precision farming techniques
