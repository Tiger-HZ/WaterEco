In response to the practical challenges of the lack of objective verification using portable devices and difficulties in participant recruitment for current Schumann wave sleep-aid technology, this study adopted a wearable ring (which collects multi-dimensional physiological data such as sleep status, heart rate, blood oxygen saturation, body temperature, stress index, and blood pressure) and a fixed 7.83 Hz Schumann wave generator. A small-sample self-controlled experiment was conducted with three healthy adults as subjects. The improvement effect of fixed-frequency Schumann waves on sleep quality was verified by collecting physiological data and subjective sleep satisfaction scores of the subjects during the baseline and intervention periods.
