Abstract Tunable infrared absorbers based on phase-change materials are commonly discussed in terms of spectral modulation, whereas the phase-dependent redistribution of electromagnetic loss in metal/PCM multilayer cavities remains less well understood. In this work, we numerically investigate a grating-coupled Ag/GST multilayer cavity for reconfigurable mid-infrared absorption. The TiO2 grating modulates the external coupling and spectral response, while the Ag/GST stack forms a vertically confined resonant cavity. In the amorphous GST state, dual absorption peaks appear at 4.2 and 4.8 μm with absorptances of 94.51% and 81.13%, respectively.
