Abstract Risk-based software testing seeks to allocate CI test-execution effort proportional to estimated defect likelihood, and a widely-adopted substrate for that estimate is a machine-learning classifier trained on repository process metrics. We show that the standard formulation harbours a fatal label-feature circularity: when the file-level label is defined as defect_prone = 1 iff bug_fix_commits &gt;= 1 and bug_fix_commits is simultaneously used as an input feature, the classifier learns to read its own label.
