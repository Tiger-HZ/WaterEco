Agrivoltaic (AV) systems present a promising solution for Dual Land-Use, combining solar energy conversion into Electricity with Agriculture. However, optimizing these systems requires a deep understanding of the complex interactions between Photovoltaic (PV) Modules, Crops, and the Microclimate. This study presents a Multi-Physics Modeling approach for AV systems that explicitly couples Shortwave/Longwave Radiation, Convective Heat-Transfer and Water-Transport. We demonstrate the model's capabilities by simulating various AV configurations, highlighting the trade-off between PV power output and agricultural yield.
