Measuring multidimensional Quality of Life (QoL) increasingly relies on composite indicators to guide sustainable development policy. However, standard linear aggregation frameworks generally assume full compensability, allowing strong performance in one domain to mathematically mask severe deficits in another. For policymakers, this compensatory masking may conceal structural imbalances across sectors, obscuring localized vulnerabilities critical for targeted interventions. To address this diagnostic gap, we introduce the Sustainable Composite Indicator for Policy Evidence (SCIPE), a granular QoL assessment framework that explicitly penalizes horizontal imbalances across SDG-aligned dimensions.
