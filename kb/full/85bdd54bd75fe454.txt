The purpose of this study was to develop a biological treatment process for the degradation of hexahydro‐1,3,5‐trinitro‐1,3,5‐triazine (RDX), a nitramine explosive present in munitions manufacturing and processing wastes. A real waste mixture containing RDX, nitrate and nitrite (NO x ), and several solvents (primarily cyclohexanone and acetone) was used as basic feed for bench‐scale experiments. Pure culture experiments indicated that bacteria can use RDX as a nitrogen source and organic solvents as carbon sources under aerobic conditions. In addition, it has been found that inorganic nitrogen compounds might interfere with the use of RDX as a nitrogen source.
