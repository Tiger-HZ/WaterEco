ABSTRACT As artificial intelligence (AI)‐generated messages increasingly approach human‐level quality, can AI effectively substitute human authors in value‐laden brand communications such as corporate social responsibility (CSR) messaging? This study examines how CSR message source (AI‐generated vs. human‐written) influences consumer perceptions of message authenticity and credibility and subsequently shapes brand attitudes and CSR support intentions. Two experiments test the source effects across two message formats: informational and narrative. Results indicate that human‐written messages consistently generate higher authenticity and more favorable CSR outcomes. Credibility emerges as a significant mediator in informational messages, while machine heuristic serves as a boundary condition
