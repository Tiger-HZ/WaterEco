Access to safe drinking water remains a significant public health concern in developing regions, including Nigeria, where sachet and bottled water have become popular alternatives to unreliable municipal supplies. This study comparatively assessed the microbiological quality of sachet and bottled water sold in Agbor, Delta State, Nigeria. A total of sixteen water samples eight sachet and eight bottled were collected at different locations in Agbor, Delta State and analyzed for heterotrophic bacteria, coliforms and antibiotic susceptibility using standard microbiological techniques. The results revealed variable microbial loads across brands. Sachet water samples exhibited heterotrophic bacterial counts ranging from 10.67 to 41.67 CFU/mL, while bottled water samples ranged from 12.00 to 47.
