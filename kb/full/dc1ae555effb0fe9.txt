The increasing influence of digital technologies has transformed educational practices globally, necessitating a shift in Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) toward technology-driven pedagogy. This study examines how preparing ECCE teachers through the integration of technology and pedagogical practices can enhance quality early learning outcomes in the digital age. Anchored on an analytical framework, the study explores key variables including teacher preparation, technology integration, pedagogical practices, learning environment and support systems, and quality early learning outcomes.
