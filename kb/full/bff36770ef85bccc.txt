Abstarct India is the world’s second-largest rice producer, accounting for ∼ 22% of global production from cultivating over 40 million hectares and exporting more than 15 million tons annually. This makes the country a key player in global food security, but a significant contributor to the global rice methane (CH 4 ) emissions. Current estimates of Indian agricultural CH 4 emissions are highly uncertain, exceeding 50%, and lack spatially explicit details across the region, which poses challenges in implementing sustainable mitigation strategies. This study developed a satellite-based method that resolves seasonal and subnational variability in rice CH 4 emissions across India at 10 m resolution.
