The Integrated Valuation of Ecosystem Services and Trade-offs Habitat Quality (InVEST HQ) model offers a spatially explicit approach for assessing habitat quality utilizing relatively simple, readily available data and an intuitive interface. In this paper, we provide a literature review of the InVEST HQ application based on 61 scientific papers, which have been systematized, analysed, and interpreted using a detailed and structured original template. We identify the main trends and gaps in the model’s applications, as well as its readiness for integration into studies on human-wildlife interactions (HWI).
