Straw return (SR) and biochar application (BA) are widely used organic amendments with potential to improve wheat productivity and resource-use efficiency. However, their responses across environmental and field-management conditions remain poorly synthesized. We conducted a global multilevel meta-analysis of 2,242 yield, 1,239 water productivity (WP), and 1,988 partial factor productivity of nitrogen (PFPN) observation pairs from 385 publications. SR increased wheat yield, WP, and PFPN by 8.31%, 8.76%, and 8.28%, respectively, whereas BA increased them by 10.34%, 11.48%, and 9.96%. Subgroup and Random Forest analyses showed that the magnitude of these benefits was mainly associated with soil properties and field management.
