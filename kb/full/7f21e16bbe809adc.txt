Water quality is threatened by the possibility of seepage of contaminants like radionuclides as well as heavy metals into water bodies and is a concern for human health. This study investigates the pH, gross α and β activity concentration and heavy metals (Cd and Pb) present in available water sources in Otukpo L.G.A. Health risk assessment study was carried out to ascertain the risk level associated with these water samples. The pH of the water samples was determined using HANNA instrument HI 8014, the gross α and β radioactivity levels were determined using the proportional counter, while the level of the heavy metals present in the samples were determined using atomic absorption spectroscopy.
