Abstract Property graphs (PGs) are increasingly adopted to represent interconnected data in several application domains. Recent advances in Large Language Models have considerably improved the translation of natural language (NL) questions into executable Cypher queries (usually referred to as the Text2Cypher task). However, the development and evaluation of new Text2Cypher methodologies remain hindered by the limited availability of high-quality benchmark resources and the lack of a common framework for sharing and comparing datasets, graph snapshots, source code, models, and evaluation artifacts.
