ABSTRACT Schematic graphical abstract of energy-efficient pulsed electrodialysis for oil-refinery wastewater reuse and zero liquid discharge. It shows unipolar pulsed operation at 500 plus or minus 20 Hz, duty cycle s = 2 and current density 5 to 10 mA/cm2 with MK-40 and MA-41 membranes, separating salts, oil products and heavy metals (iron, chromium, nickel) into demineralized water, concentrate and diluate streams, and comparing direct current with pulsed electrodialysis. The indicated outcomes are lower specific energy consumption, faster desalination, improved heavy-metal removal, reduced scaling and pH stabilization.
