This study aimed at evaluating the public-health consequences of sachet bottled water products that are in circulation in the Rivers North Senatorial District, of Rivers State, Nigeria. A random sample of thirty-six sachet-water samples was chosen out of an eclectic mix of sellers in and around the headquarters of the sampled local government area. Ten trace metal constituents, including Chromium, Cadmium, Lead, Mercury, Nickel, Zinc, Iron, Copper, Manganese and Cobalt were determined analytically by Atomic Absorption Spectrometry (AAS) along with measurements of ten physicochemical indices. Bacteriological examination used a membrane filtration procedure to identify and count fecal coliform populations.
