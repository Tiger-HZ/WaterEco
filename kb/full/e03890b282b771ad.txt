Abstract Black Soldier Fly Larvae Meal (BSFLM), a sustainable insect protein source, shows promise as a beneficial alternative ingredient in pet food formulations due to its high protein content and beneficial oil composition. Chitin, an insoluble fiber present in BSFL, may also modulate gut microbiota and production of short-chain fatty acids, although it may slightly reduce nutrient digestibility when added at higher levels. Limited research has evaluated the effects and optimal inclusion levels of BSFL meal (BSFLM) and oil in extruded diets for senior dogs. This study aimed to assess de-fatted BSFLM and BSFL oil as a partial replacement for chicken by-product meal and chicken fat in extruded diets for healthy senior dogs, and its effects on nutrient digestibility and fecal metabolites.
