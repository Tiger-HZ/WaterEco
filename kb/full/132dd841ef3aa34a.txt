Purpose This study aims to investigate the association between positive abnormal audit fees (PAAFs) and financial reporting quality (FRQ). Design/methodology/approach This study uses a multivariate regression analysis to examine a sample consisting of 2,200 bank-year observations from the United States (US) over the 2010–2020 period. Consistent with prior literature, PAAFs are derived using an audit fee estimation model and FRQ is derived using a loan loss provision estimation model. Findings This study provides strong evidence that PAAFs affect FRQ. Findings show an inverted U-shaped association. For lower levels of PAAFs, additional audit fees increase FRQ. By contrast, for higher levels of PAAFs, additional audit fees decrease FRQ. Research limitations/implications The focus on US banks
