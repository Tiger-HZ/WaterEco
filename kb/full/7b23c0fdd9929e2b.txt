Patient-reported service quality can identify priorities for improving inpatient care, but its association with satisfaction must be distinguished from a causal effect. This cross-sectional study examined the five SERVQUAL dimensions and inpatient satisfaction at Doloksanggul Regional General Hospital, North Sumatra, Indonesia. A purposive sample of 245 respondents completed a structured questionnaire. Descriptive statistics, Pearson’s chi-square tests, and Cramér’s V were used, and multiple logistic regression was attempted with all five dimensions entered simultaneously. Men accounted for 50.6% of respondents; intermediate education was the largest educational category (45.3%), and 46.1% had been hospitalized for 2–5 days.
