Performance of a laboratory‐scale sequencing batch reactor using a membrane for effluent filtration was investigated in terms of chemical oxygen demand (COD) removal, nitrogen removal, and membrane permeability during long‐term continuous operation treating synthetic wastewater. The reactor was operated with six 4‐hour cycles per day consisting of 0.5, 2.0, and 1.5 hours for fill, aeration, and effluent filtration–idle, respectively. Minimal solids wasting occurred for the first 10 months of operation followed by an 8‐day solids retention time (SRT) for the final 1.5 months. Membrane fouling was controlled by backwashing with aeration for 10 minutes during each cycle.
