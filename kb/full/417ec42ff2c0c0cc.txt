ABSTRACT Objective To evaluate the acceptability and feasibility of implementing a structured ‘hot debrief’ tool, and its impact on team effectiveness, staff well‐being and identification of system‐level issues following critical events in two EDs. Methods We conducted a multi‐site quality improvement study across two metropolitan Australian EDs from 2023 to 2025. A standardised hot debrief process was implemented following predefined critical events, with senior staff trained as facilitators. Staff perceptions were assessed using anonymous pre‐, post‐ and final‐implementation surveys, with quantitative data analysed descriptively. Completed debrief proformas from two 6‐month trial periods were analysed using reflexive thematic analysis to identify recurring themes related to team function
