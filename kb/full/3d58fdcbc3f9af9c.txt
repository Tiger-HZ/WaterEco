ABSTRACT On-site wastewater treatment systems require reliable polishing processes to reduce organic matter, suspended solids, and nitrogen species in septic tank effluent before discharge or land application. This study evaluated alternative granular biofilter media, including 2A sand, crushed glass, biochar, and a crushed-glass/biochar mixture, using vertical PVC laboratory columns designed to simulate filtration through a discharge-control trench. Primary screened septic tank effluent from a residential onsite wastewater system in Wainui, Akaroa, New Zealand, was applied to columns containing 600 mm of media at a hydraulic loading rate of 25 Lm−2d−1.
