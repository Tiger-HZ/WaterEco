Abstract Because of the influence of various storage zones, solute transport in a stream channel cannot be interpreted by advection and dispersion processes alone. Thus, in recent decades, various models that incorporate storage‐zone effects have been proposed to characterize anomalous storage mechanisms. The validity of these models has been predominantly demonstrated using tracer breakthrough curves measured in surface flow. However, the storage effect is usually less influential on the breakthrough curve behavior than in‐stream flow dynamics. Thus, for model validation, the tracer behavior within the storage zone should be investigated separately.
