Abstract Understanding how soil nutrient composition plays a crucial role in shaping microbial diversity and function, particularly under varying levels of soil nutrient status. This study examines bacterial community dynamics and identifies candidate bacterial bio-indicators influenced by soil nutrient availability in bulk soil samples collected from two distinct agricultural regions. We found a strong association between carbon, nitrogen, potassium, and phosphate availability and enzyme activity with the bacterial genera.
