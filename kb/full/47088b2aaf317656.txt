The study was performed to document the quality of groundwater in the agriculturally intensive Baghpat city of western Uttar Pradesh, India. A total of ten sites were selected strategically in the study area, combining agricultural fields, residential areas, commercial areas, and along the highway joining Meerut and Baghpat cities. A total of 240 groundwater samples were taken using the composite method of sampling and analysed for the selected parameters. The study revealed the values of total dissolved solids (TDS) and total hardness (TH) beyond the standard permissible limit of the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).
