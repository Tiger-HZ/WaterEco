Water Quality Management is an essential competency in Brackish and Marine Fisheries Agribusiness because it requires students to integrate scientific knowledge, technology, quantitative analysis, and problem-solving skills in authentic aquaculture contexts. However, learning activities are still predominantly supported by conventional materials, while the use of digital learning resources remains limited. This study aimed to develop a STEM-based e-module for Water Quality Management and determine its validity, practicality, and effectiveness as a learning resource for vocational high school students.
