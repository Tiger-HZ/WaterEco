ABSTRACT Graphical abstract showing hand-dug wells in Ife-East LGA as a silent reservoir of selected WHO priority pathogens, with a quantitative microbial risk assessment indicating unacceptable infection risk for K. pneumoniae. Groundwater from hand-dug wells is a major source of drinking water in many Nigerian communities but is highly vulnerable to microbial contamination. This study assessed the occurrence, selected virulence attributes, and infection risk associated with third-generation cephalosporin- and carbapenem-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae, Enterobacter spp., Acinetobacter baumannii and Pseudomonas aeruginosa in hand-dug wells in Ife East Local Government Area, southwestern Nigeria.
