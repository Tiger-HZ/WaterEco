The aquaculture sector is expanding rapidly to satisfy the increasing worldwide need for seafood. Ensuring fish well-being is crucial for sustainable farming. Nevertheless, the conventional application of antibiotics for disease management raises significant concerns regarding food safety, antibiotic resistance, and environmental impact. Consequently, immunostimulants are increasingly seen as a valuable option in the management of fish health. In contrast to antibiotics or vaccines, immunostimulants enhance the fish’s immune defences to naturally combat disease. This chapter highlighted the significance of immunostimulants in aquaculture, particularly those derived from natural resources such as plants. Common varieties consist of plant extracts, β-glucans, vitamins, nucleotides, and microbial products. These compounds have demonstrated significant ability to decrease disease outbreaks and enhance fish health, rendering them a valuable resource for safer and more sustainable aquaculture practices.
