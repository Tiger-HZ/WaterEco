Cyanobacteria harmful algal bloom (CHAB) events, often dominated by the cyanobacteria genus Microcystis, are a major water-quality issue in the large, hydrologically complex, Sacramento–San Joaquin Delta (Delta). To improve future CHAB-specific monitoring, we analyzed historical data to characterize spatial and habitat-based patterns in CHAB occurrence.
