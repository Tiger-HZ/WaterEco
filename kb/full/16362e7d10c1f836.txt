A field experiment was conducted during the rabi seasons of 2022–23 and 2023–24 at the Agricultural Research Farm, Rabindranath Tagore University, Raisen, Madhya Pradesh, to assess the effect of integrated nutrient management (INM) on the growth and yield attributes of wheat under Central Indian conditions. The present investigation was undertaken to evaluate the effects of different integrated nutrient management practices involving varying fertiliser levels, applied alone or in combination with FYM, zinc sulphate, and biofertilisers, on the growth and yield attributes of wheat.
