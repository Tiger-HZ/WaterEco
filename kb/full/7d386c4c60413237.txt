Automatic singing voice quality assessment is essential for benchmarking modern singing voice generation and conversion systems, yet subjective listening tests are costly and difficult to scale. This paper presents LMM-Net, a label-masked multi-task network that predicts multi-dimensional perceptual scores under incomplete supervision and emphasizes system-level ranking consistency. LMM-Net combines a self-supervised acoustic encoder with an explicit temporal pitch-contour branch. A gated fusion module injects contour cues in a reliability-aware manner, and attention pooling aggregates frame-level evidence into utterance-level representations for multi-task regression.
