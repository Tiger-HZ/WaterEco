We formulate the problem of estimating hydraulic conductivity from measured hydraulic head data in groundwater flow within the unified framework of parameter estimation using the extended Kalman filter (EKF) and least-squares optimization. We first formulate the EKF and its variants as problems of least-squares optimization. We then show that the successive linear estimator can be interpreted as a variant of the EKF, with its initial estimate obtained through co-Kriging. Furthermore, by showing that the EKF is equivalent to a one-step Newton iteration for solving a least-squares problem, we reformulate the hydraulic conductivity estimation problem as an associated least-squares optimization problem.
