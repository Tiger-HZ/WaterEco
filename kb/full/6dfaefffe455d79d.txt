Electrochemical water treatment offers a versatile approach to contaminant removal and resource recovery, although its practical performance depends on electrode properties, wastewater composition and operating conditions. This review critically examines the principal electrochemical treatment methods, including electrochemical oxidation and reduction, electro-Fenton processes, electrocoagulation, electroflotation, electrodeposition and electrodialysis. Their reaction pathways, treatment capabilities and limitations are discussed, with particular attention to the distinction between pollutant degradation, separation and recovery.
