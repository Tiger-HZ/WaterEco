Abstract Seaweed’s growth and development are influenced by multiple abiotic factors, including light, temperature, and nutrient availability. Red macroalgae store nutrients in the form of pigments such as phycoerythrin and phycocyanin, which support growth during periods of nutrient limitation. Therefore, evaluating growth performance and physiological traits is essential for improving biomass production. In Brazil, macroalgal production is constrained by the limited availability of commercial seedlings and the high cost of nutrients used in laboratory cultivation. This study investigated the biological performance of Gracilariopsis tenuifrons (C.J.Bird &amp; E.C.Oliveira) Fredericq &amp; Hommersand, a red macroalga recognized for its resilience under culture conditions.
