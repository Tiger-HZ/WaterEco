The adverse impacts of coal mining on stream water were assessed. Fifteen stream water samples were collected from different locations around the mine site and two locations away from the mine, which served as the control. To determine the impact on stream water, parameters such as water quality index (WQI), sodium absorption ratio (SAR), soluble sodium percentage (SSP), sodium percentage (Na%), Kelly’s ratio (KR), magnesium hazard (MH), and potential salinity (PS) were calculated and compared with the Federal Ministry of Environment (FMEnv) standards. The analytical results revealed that the water samples in the study area were slightly acidic. All other physicochemical parameters measured in the water samples were within the permissible limits.
