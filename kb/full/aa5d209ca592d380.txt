The increasing demand for reliable marine data to support environmental policies such as the Marine Strategy Framework Directive (MSFD) in Europe requires harmonized, high-quality datasets that are findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable (FAIR). This paper presents a multi-level quality control (QC) protocol applied within the European Marine Observation and Data Network (EMODnet) Chemistry initiative, focused on eutrophication parameters (nutrients, chlorophyll, and oxygen) across European marine regions. The approach combines automated and expert-based validation and includes three consecutive QC steps performed by the data originators (Level 1 QC), the data centers (Level 2 QC), and the regional coordinators (Level 3 QC).
