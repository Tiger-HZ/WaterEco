Abstract This study evaluated microbiological quality of smoked dried fish samples sold in some markets in Port Harcourt metropolis, Rivers State, Nigeria. Three replicates of four common species of smoked dried fish samples namely: Herring Clupea harengus (Sawa). Horse mackerelTrachurus trachurus (Kote). Atlantic mackerel- Scomber scombrus (Titus), and Sphyraena barracuda (Panla) were obtained randomly from the retailers at different sales-point in Choba Junction, Rumuokoro, Mile 1, and Creek Road markets all located in Port Harcourt metropolis of Rivers State, Nigeria. Each sample was transported to the laboratory in a labelled and sterile polythene bags for analysis.
