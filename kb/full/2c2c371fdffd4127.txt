This study examines the spatial and seasonal variability of water quality in glacier-fed river systems within the Karatal River basin, located in the north-western sector of the Zhetysu Alatau (Kazakhstan). The investigation focused on the Kora and Shyzhy rivers, which play a key role in forming the hydrological regime of the Karatal River. Fieldwork was carried out in 2025 and included two sampling campaigns (July and September), during which a total of 30 water samples were collected from the upper, middle, and lower river reaches. Hydro chemical analyses identified the presence of major ions, nutrients, and a range of trace elements (Pb, Cd, Zn, Cu, Cr, Mn, Sb, Sr, Se, Ni, Al, Mo).
