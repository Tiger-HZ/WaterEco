Sustainable aquaculture in inland reservoirs depends on seasonal hydrology, which influences nutrient dynamics and O2 availability. This study aimed to quantify O2 and nutrient-based ecological carrying capacity and seasonal hypoxia risk to support sustainable aquaculture planning. This study evaluated the feasibility and ecological capacity of aquaculture in the Wonorejo Reservoir, Indonesia, using a four-pillar framework: feasibility mapping, ecological assessment, carrying capacity estimation, and risk-based ecological management. Monthly water quality data from 2022–2024, supported by in situ validation in March 2025, were analysed using the Vollenweider phosphorus model, O2 balance calculations, total oxygen demand (TOD), and the ecological balance index (EBI).
