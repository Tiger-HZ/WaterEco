ABSTRACT Since the establishment of the People's Republic of China, the Chinese government has considered water conservation and reservoir management as crucial strategies for economic and social development. Among the initiatives undertaken, reservoir joint operation is a notable innovation in the construction and management of China's water conservation. The Yangtze River Basin, which comprises the largest cascade reservoir system worldwide, has been subjected to over 30 years of practical testing for reservoir joint operation. This has promoted the multi-objective collaborative optimization of China's reservoir group operations, shifting from traditional empirical operations to more modern intelligent operations, demonstrating a high degree of institutional maturity.
