Rapid growth in municipal, agricultural and industrial waste generation has created a dual environmental challenge: waste streams require safe management while water resources require increasingly effective and affordable purification. Waste-derived materials offer a circular pathway in which discarded biomass, food residues, agricultural by-products, industrial wastes, sewage-derived solids and selected plastic wastes are transformed into adsorbents, catalysts, filtration media, ion-exchange materials and multifunctional composites.
