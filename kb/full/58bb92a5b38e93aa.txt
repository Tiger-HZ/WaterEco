Background: To evaluate the effectiveness of water and a liquid soap solution compared with 0.2% chlorhexidine (CHX) mouthwash in reducing microbial load and microbial plaque formation on removable orthodontic appliances. Methods: Forty-two patients aged 9 to 16 years who had newly received acrylic resin removable orthodontic appliances were enrolled and randomized in a triple-blind crossover clinical trial. Thirty participants completed all study phases and were included in the final analysis. Participants were randomly allocated into 3 groups (n=10) according to the initial cleaning protocol: water, water plus liquid soap solution, or 0.2% CHX mouthwash.
