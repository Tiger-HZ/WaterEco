Natural coagulants such as Aloe vera have attracted increasing attention as sustainable alternatives for turbidity removal in water treatment. This paper presents AV-Predict, an open-source, multi-model software designed to predict turbidity reduction in jar test experiments using Aloe vera-based coagulation. The software integrates a publicly available experimental dataset with six machine learning algorithms within a user-friendly computational environment, allowing users to estimate treatment performance, compare predictive models, and explore the influence of operational parameters on clarification efficiency. By providing reproducible workflows and rapid prediction of final turbidity and removal efficiency, AV-Predict reduces the need for extensive laboratory experimentation while supporting research, education, and decision-making. The public availability of both the source code and the experimental dataset further promote transparency, reproducibility, and the adoption of data-driven approaches for the optimization of sustainable water treatment technologies.
