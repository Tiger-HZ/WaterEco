The HexCore HX-9 osmotic engine has zero direct operational emissions (no tailpipe), but its full environmental profile depends on the entire lifecycle, including how the draw solution is regenerated. 1. Well-to-Wheel / Lifecycle Emissions While the engine itself emits nothing, the Regeneration Unit that re-concentrates the brine solution requires energy. Embodied Emissions (Manufacturing & Materials) This is where the osmotic engine has a higher footprint than a simple electric motor: Hydrogel Production: Poly(acrylic acid) is a petroleum-derived polymer, though bio-based alternatives (like cellulose-based hydrogels) can reduce this to near-zero. Membrane Manufacturing: Energy-intensive process (similar to RO membranes). CFRP Casing: High-energy manufacturing, but the hexagonal design minimizes material usage. Salt Production: Industrial salt has a low but non-zero carbon footprint. 4.
