The Journal of Urological Surgery is an open access, scientific, double-blind peer-reviewed journal in the field of Urooncology, Endourology, Pediatric Urology, Andrology, Reconstructive Urology, General Urology, Urogynecology and Functional Urology, Transplantation and Vascular Surgery. No fee is charged from the authors during the submission, evaluation and publication process. It is the official publication of the Society of Urological Surgery and is published quarterly (March, June, September, December).
