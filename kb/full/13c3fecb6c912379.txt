Freshwater degradation in semi-arid regions can simultaneously affect river ecological condition and the reliability of water resources for irrigated food production. This study integrated ecological, irrigation-water, hydroclimatic, and crop-specific assessments across the Euphrates, Shatt Al-Hillah, and Al-Diwaniyah river systems in central Iraq over 5 years (2020–2024). Fifteen stations were monitored seasonally, generating 300 station-season observations. Physicochemical variables, major ions, nutrients, irrigation-water indicators, benthic diatoms, macroinvertebrates, habitat condition, hydroclimatic variables, and crop-specific irrigation constraints were evaluated within a common analytical framework.
