Screen use in the period preceding sleep is common among university students and may impair different dimensions of rest. This integrative review analyzed the evidence on the association between nighttime use of electronic devices and sleep quality in this population. PubMed, the Virtual Health Library, and SciELO were searched for pri-mary studies published from 2016 through August 31, 2026, in Portuguese, English, or Spanish. The search yielded 91 raw records, and 22 studies met the eligibility criteria, representing 19 countries and an unweighted summed sample of 105,101 participants. Cross-sectional designs and self-reported measures predominated, particularly the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index.
