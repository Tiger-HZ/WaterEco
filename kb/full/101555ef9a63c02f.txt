Introduction: Vitamin D has immunomodulatory effects, which may be relevant in autoimmune and immune-mediated diseases. Aim: The aim of this study was to present current data on the role of vitamin D deficiency in the pathogenesis and course of selected diseases. Methods: A narrative literature review was conducted, including mainly publications from 2018–2026 concerning multiple sclerosis, rheumatoid arthritis, autoimmune thyroid diseases, and inflammatory bowel diseases. Results: Vitamin D deficiency is frequently observed in the analyzed diseases. Mechanistic data indicate its influence on dendritic cells, T and B lymphocytes, and the regulation of the inflammatory response.
