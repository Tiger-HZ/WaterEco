Abstract Integrating agricultural statistics with gridded water-use and hydroclimatic products requires harmonized spatial support, units, calendars, missingness rules, and source versions. We constructed a province--year panel for 31 mainland Chinese units during 2010--2022. Irrigation-water productivity (IWP) and hydroclimate were available for 403 observations; agricultural input-carbon productivity (AICP) was available for 310 observations during 2010--2019 because agricultural diesel data were unavailable thereafter. In 2020, reconstructed irrigation withdrawals equaled 95.1% of official agricultural water use and showed strong provincial concordance (Pearson r=0.998; mean absolute percentage error =9.03%).
