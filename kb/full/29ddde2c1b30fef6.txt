Abstract Background Patients with relapsed/refractory pediatric low-grade glioma (R/R pLGG) face considerable long-term morbidity. Most cases are driven by mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) pathway alterations, for which several targeted agents have been evaluated. Systematic literature reviews (SLRs) were performed to deepen understanding of efficacy/effectiveness, safety/tolerability, health-related quality of life (HRQoL), and functional outcomes of systemic drug therapies used in R/R pLGG.
