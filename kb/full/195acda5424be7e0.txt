ABSTRACT Flowchart of experimental and simulation study on chemical clogging in weakly consolidated sandstone during geothermal tailwater recharge, including artificial core reinjection tests, X-ray diffraction, ion concentration measurements, PHREEQC simulation, and resulting clogging reaction pathways and permeability evolution. Clogging in weakly consolidated sandstone reservoirs hinders geothermal tailwater recharge, with chemical clogging being particularly complex due to multiple influencing factors and in reservoir reactions. This study conducts experimental and simulation research on this issue. Artificial cores made from natural rock samples are used in recharge experiments.
