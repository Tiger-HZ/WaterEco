LHAASO J2108+5157 is one of the most intriguing Galactic ultra-high-energy γ-ray sources. It is detected up to hundreds of TeV but lacks a clear counterpart at lower frequencies. -LAT observations have revealed a compact GeV source spatially coincident with the PeV emission, while VERITAS and LST-1 impose stringent upper limits in the intermediate TeV band, producing a pronounced spectral suppression between the GeV and TeV domains. Fermi We investigate a time-dependent hadronic scenario in which a past episode of microquasar jet activity injected relativistic protons that are now observed in a relic phase.
