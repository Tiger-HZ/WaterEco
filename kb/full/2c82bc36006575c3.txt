ABSTRACT The co‐occurrence of heavy metal contamination and biodegradable microplastic (polylactic acid, PLA) pollution poses increasing risks to terrestrial plant communities and soil functioning, yet species‐specific responses to combined stress remain poorly understood. Cd and microplastics frequently co‐occur in agricultural soils, where microplastics can alter cadmium mobility, bioavailability, and transport pathways, potentially modifying metal toxicity and plant stress responses compared with single‐pollutant exposure.
