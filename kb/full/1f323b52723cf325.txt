Purpose. Carbonate reservoirs account for more than half of global conventional oil reserves, yet their inherently oil-wet surfaces and complex pore architecture present persistent challenges to conventional waterflooding. This study investigates the combined application of smart water (SW) and SiO2 nanoparticles as a hybrid enhanced oil recovery (EOR) strategy to improve displacement efficiency in carbonate rock. Methods. Four injection scenarios were evaluated at reservoir temperature (85°C) with carbonate core samples: formation brine (FB), smart water (SW), SiO2 nanofluid (NF) at 0.05 wt.%, and a hybrid NF-SW formulation.
