Improving nitrogen use efficiency (NUE) while sustaining crop productivity is a major challenge for sustainable maize production. Plasma-activated water (PAW), enriched with reactive oxygen and nitrogen species, has emerged as a promising biostimulant for enhancing nutrient acquisition and plant performance. This study evaluated the effects of PAW on root architecture, rhizosphere metabolism, nitrogen acquisition, agronomic nitrogen use efficiency (ANUE), and grain yield (GY) under four nitrogen application rates (0, 50, 75, and 100% of the recommended dose) in maize grown under controlled polyhouse conditions.
