Groundwater is the principal source of domestic and agricultural water supply in Kibiya Town and its environs, northwestern Nigeria. However, heavy metal contamination poses potential risks to human health. This study assessed the concentrations, spatial distribution, and pollution status of selected heavy metals in groundwater from local Basement Complex aquifers. Thirty groundwater samples from boreholes and hand-dug wells were analyzed for Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu, Pb, Cd, Cr, and F using Microwave Plasma–Atomic Emission Spectrometry (MP-AES). Groundwater contamination was evaluated using the Heavy Metal Pollution Index (HPI), Heavy Metal Evaluation Index (HEI), and Contamination Degree (Cd).
