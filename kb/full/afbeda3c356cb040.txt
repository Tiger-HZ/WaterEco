ABSTRACT Wastewater is a potential reservoir for pathogens, and poor management increases the risks of diseases such as rotavirus-associated diarrhea. This study assessed community awareness, hygiene practices and socio-demographic predictors of rotavirus and wastewater-related health risk in Morogoro Municipality and Mvomero District, Tanzania. A cross-sectional survey was conducted between November 2024 and February 2025 among 244 adults residing near wastewater treatment facilities. Data were collected using a structured questionnaire and analysed using descriptive statistics, chi-square tests, and logistic regression.
