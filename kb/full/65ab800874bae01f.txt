Abstract Oilseed fermentation is a traditional biological process with a long history. In recent years, due to its potential in enhancing nutritional value, reducing anti‐nutritional factors, and generating bioactive compounds, this technology has regained attention. This article systematically reviews the historical development, common types, microorganisms, and fermentation techniques of oilseed fermentation, and elaborates in detail on the regulatory mechanisms of macronutrients, micronutrients, and anti‐nutritional factors during the fermentation process.
