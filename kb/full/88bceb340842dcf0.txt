Introduction: Antimicrobial resistance is a global public health problem that threatens the treatment of infections caused by microorganisms because of their increasing resistance. This study determined the resistance patterns and the potential of bacteria isolated from selected surface waters to produce certain enzymes. Methods: Surface water samples were collected from the Asa River and the University of Ilorin River, and bacteria from the composite samples were isolated and identified using standard microbiological techniques. Resistance patterns of the bacteria to conventional antibiotics were determined using the Kirby-Bauer disc diffusion method, and the results were compared with Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI) guidelines.
