Despite the importance of groundwater as a source of drinking and domestic water, limited information is available on groundwater quality in the Chehelsoton area. The lack of localized data on key physicochemical parameters limits assessment of potential health risks and sustainable groundwater use. Therefore, systematic evaluation of groundwater quality is needed. This study aimed to evaluate groundwater quality in the Chehelsoton area by analyzing selected physicochemical parameters and comparing the results with international drinking water standards. We collected five groundwater samples from the study area and analyzed six key chemical parameters for water quality assessment.
