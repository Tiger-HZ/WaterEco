Abstract This study presents an integrated remediation strategy and human health risk assessment for multimetal-contaminated industrial soil. The sequential treatment combined mechanical screening, high-intensity magnetic separation (HIMS), and chemical extraction using ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid (EDTA). Initial characterization revealed extreme contamination levels, with cadmium and thallium posing significant noncarcinogenic risks, particularly for children (hazard index = 169.4). Physical separation proved highly effective: Mechanical screening recovered 79% of the soil mass as a cleaner coarse fraction, while HIMS achieved metal removal efficiencies between 73 and 93% for lead, arsenic, and antimony.
