These datasets contain data on 1) perch (Perca fluviatilis) total length and age at catch of individual fish, 2) Catch Per Unit Effort (CPUE) of perch and other fish species and average annual water temperature, 3) three-spined stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) estimated densities, and 4) distance from the open sea of several locations spread along the Swedish Baltic Sea coast.Data in (1) and (2) were retrieved from the Swedish national database for coastal fish monitoring (slu.se/kul) [2025-05-15] and come from fish monitoring surveys that are conducted between mid-July and late August, using multi-mesh gillnets. Average annual water temperatures were calculated from daily mean sea surface temperatures from satellite data (E.U. Copernicus Baltic Sea L4 dataset (https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00156) [2025-05-15]). Three-spined stickleback densities (3) were estimated following the approach outlined in Olin et al. (Nature Communications 2024) using data from the Baltic International Acoustic Survey, which runs annually in October and combines hydroacoustic data with trawl hauls to estimate densities.
