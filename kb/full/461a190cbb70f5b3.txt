Abstract Deran Lake, located within Hutovo Blato Nature Park in Bosnia and Herzegovina, is a shallow wetland ecosystem shaped by complex hydrological connectivity, meteorological variability, and anthropogenic pressures. This study applies and adapts a spatial multicriteria decision-making framework, originally developed for water quality modelling in a different lake system, to assess eutrophication susceptibility in Deran Lake. The main objective was to evaluate the flexibility and robustness of the framework under different hydrodynamic and ecological conditions. Monitoring was conducted from June to October 2025 and included 76 in situ measurements of water temperature (WT), dissolved oxygen (DO), electrical conductivity (EC), and turbidity.
