Health problems have made it necessary to look for new sources of biocompounds for the food industry. Cyanobacteria offer a solution due to the compounds inside their biomass, such as pigments and protein. Rice husk aqueous extract was evaluated as an innovative culture medium using four concentrations (16, 32, 48 and 64 g/L, respectively) for the biomass production of Limnothrix sp. Rice husk aqueous extract at a concentration of 48 g/L showed the best results for Limnothrix sp. biomass production as well as phycocyanin and protein contents. Furthermore, cultivation of Limnothrix sp. was carried out in bubble column photobioreactors, evaluating three volumetric air flows (157, 180 and 570 mL/min, respectively).
