Abstract This study evaluated the effects of dietary carbon‐to‐nitrogen (C/N) conditions, generated by combining Dunaliella salina with sugar beet raffinate, on the growth, survival, reproductive performance, biochemical composition, fatty‐acid profile, digestive enzyme activity, microbial community, and biomass production of Artemia parthenogenitica cultured in biofloc systems. Eight treatments were evaluated for 21 days. The control treatment consisted of 25% algae and 75% yeast and was designated as C/N 3.5.
