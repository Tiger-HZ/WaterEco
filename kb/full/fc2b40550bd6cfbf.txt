Mining, urban wastewater discharge, and intensive agriculture have degraded freshwater quality across the Andean Altiplano, yet integrated multi-parameter assessments remain scarce for high-altitude tributaries of Lake Titicaca. This study evaluated the spatial and seasonal distribution of seven heavy metals (As, Cd, Cr, Cu, Hg, Pb, and Zn) in surface water and sediments, along with physicochemical and microbiological parameters, across 11 stations in the Coata River Basin (Puno, Peru). Dissolved arsenic exceeded the WHO guideline (0.01 mg L?¹) at 10 of 11 stations. In sediments, mean Zn (337.9-400.5 mg kg?¹), Pb (67.7-81.2 mg kg?¹), and As (41.9-43.3 mg kg?¹) surpassed the Threshold Effect Level at every site.
