MXenes are widely recognized for battery applications due to their rapid ion-intercalation capability and excellent cycling stability. However, conventional synthesis methods often involve hazardous HF, prolonged reaction times, and complex procedures, limiting their practical implementation. Here, we present an efficient and sustainable ammonia-assisted hydrothermal strategy for synthesizing Nb 2 CT x MXene from the Nb 2 AlC MAX phase using a mild etchant, providing a safe, cost-effective, and scalable route. The as-prepared MXene was further delaminated via probe sonication, yielding an expanded interlayer spacing of ∼9.7 Å and optimized surface functionalities.
