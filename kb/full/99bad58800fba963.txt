Long-term nutrient management plays an important role in maintaining soil micronutrient fertility under continuous cropping. The present study evaluated the effect of long-term fertiliser and manure application on the vertical distribution of DTPA-extractable Zn, Cu, Mn and Fe in a Vertisol under a soybean-wheat cropping system during 2024-25 at the AICRP-LTFE experimental farm, JNKVV, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, India. The long-term experiment was initiated in 1972 and comprised nine nutrient-management treatments, including graded NPK levels, nutrient omission treatments, NPK + Zn, NPK + FYM, sulphur-free NPK and an unfertilised control, and was arranged in a randomised block design with three replications.
