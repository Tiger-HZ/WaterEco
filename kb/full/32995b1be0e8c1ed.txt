Traditional ethnic foods are integral to the food systems of tribal communities and are closely linked to locally available resources and cultural practices. This study analysed the nutrient composition of three traditional recipes associated with the Paniya, Mullu Kurumas and Kattunayakar communities of Wayanad, Kerala, and evaluated the sensory acceptability of nutritionally enriched variants. Bamboo rice porridge, ash gourd leaves stir-fry and cooked lesser yam were analysed for proximate composition, selected micronutrients and functional characteristics. Five enriched treatments were developed for each recipe using locally available nutrient-dense ingredients, including sprouted pulses, tubers, green leafy vegetables and other vegetables.
