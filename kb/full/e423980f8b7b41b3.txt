Abstract Unmanaged floating waste in rivers threatens aquatic ecosystems and sustainable water resource management. This study presents the design and experimental evaluation of a self powered floating waste collection system that operates exclusively using river flow energy. The proposed device converts hydrodynamic energy into mechanical power through a rotating wheel driving a conveyor based collection mechanism. The effects of blade geometry and number, conveyor type and angle and guiding arm configuration were evaluated under real river conditions using representative floating debris and selected polymer wastes.
