The primary purpose of studying any facies analysis is to give more detail information of any formation in order to explore and produce petroleum. Petroleum is form in an anoxic environment within the source rock, reserve in a good quality reservoir and trapped. For a reservoir to be good in quality must have a good porosity and permeability. The aim of this research is to determine and interpret the various lithofacies of the sediments of the Gombe formation along Wuro Biriji stream section, through establishing a lithostratigraphic section and granulometric analysis.
