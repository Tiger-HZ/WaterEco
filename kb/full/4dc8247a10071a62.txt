Background: Nostocales is one of the most diverse orders of cyanobacteria with 1,905 species currently recognised. Particularly, in Campeche only seven benthic marine species have been recorded. Questions and/or Hypotheses: This study represents a first approach to the knowledge of the benthic marine diversity of Nostocales from Campeche, which, given its environmental heterogeneity, will be composed previously unrecorded species. Our aim is to expand the richness of this group, as well as provide detailed descriptions and illustrations for each species. Site and years of study: Cyanobacteria collections were carried out during 2024, along three locations on the Campeche coast. Methods: Semi-permanent preparations were made from fresh samples, microphotographs were taken, and morphometric measurements were recorded to determine taxonomic identification, comparing with specilized literatured. Results: A total of 17 species of Nostocales were obtained, of which nine correspond with new records from Campeche, representing 52.96 % of its diversity. Taxonomic notes were added to highlight morphological differences that do not match between our specimens and descriptions of species identified from specialised literature. Rivulariaceae is the most represented family. Conclusions: The current study has allowed a greater appreciation of the state of knowledge of marine benthic Nostocales tin the area, however, the diversity and specific richness of the group is currently widely underestimated, so more research focused on cyanoprokaryotes is needed.
