Abstract The water quality assessment has tremendous significance for water pollution prevention. First, the DO (dissolved oxygen), permanganate index, TN (total nitrogen), TP (total phosphorus) as well as NH 3- N are adopted as the evidence body. Their basic probability is determined using the cloud theory. Secondly, the synthetic probability of different sections is calculated based on the evidence theory.
