Climate fluctuations can result in long periods of drought interspersed with short but rapid and intense rainfall events. This feasibility study includes verification of the planning documents for a medium-sized city of Katrineholm (including historical and problematic sites) and Land-use land-cover modeling (LULC). Aim was to identify the best Nature-based Solution (NBS) that could be incorporated into the local adaptation plan for reducing surface runoff and improving retention. In SCALGO, the data for modeling of surface runoff are based on topography, LULC and soil data.
