Hydraulic support linkage production lines require high real-time response and stable coordination among machining, welding, inspection, transfer, and quality verification units. However, heterogeneous data from sensors, visual inspection devices, equipment states, control commands, and quality records are difficult to integrate effectively, while centralized processing may cause delayed control decisions. To address these problems, this paper proposes a multi-source data fusion and collaborative control method based on edge computing. The method performs time alignment, feature extraction, attention-based fusion, dynamic state prediction, and graph-based collaborative control at the edge side.
