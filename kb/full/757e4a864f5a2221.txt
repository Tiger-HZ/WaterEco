This study investigates the relationship between curriculum leadership and teachers’ self-reported instructional quality by integrating mediation and moderation perspectives within a unified framework. Drawing on data from 711 teachers, it examines whether curriculum leadership is indirectly associated with instructional quality through teacher agency and whether this association varies depending on the quality of leader–member exchange (LMX). The findings indicate that curriculum leadership is both directly and indirectly associated with instructional quality, with teacher agency statistically accounting for part of this association.
