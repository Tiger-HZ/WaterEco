Abstract The development of cyanobacteria blooms is of increasing concern in many lakes worldwide, and as a result, modeling their predictors is vital for understanding where and why they occur. In this study, we developed and analyzed a 640‐lake data set that spans Canada and 12 ecozones to identify the drivers of cyanobacteria biomass and of several key toxin‐ and bloom‐forming genera ( Microcystis , Aphanizomenon , and Dolichospermum ). The database consisted of an exhaustive list of potential predictors ( n = 55), including water chemistry, land‐use, and zooplankton variables.
