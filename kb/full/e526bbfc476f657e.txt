Submarine groundwater discharge (SGWD) and associated nutrient fluxes at Hasaki Beach along the Kashima coast in Japan were investigated through field measurements and numerical simulations. The field data indicate that (1) groundwater has higher concentrations of land‐derived nutrients than seawater and river water; (2) microbial activity near the shoreline is likely to induce mineralization, reduction, and oxidation of nitrogen and phosphorus; and (3) in the portion of the aquifer underlying coastal forest, nitrate is appreciably reduced. A numerical model incorporating effects of water table and tidal fluctuations was developed to evaluate SGWD.
