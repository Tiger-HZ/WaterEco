The objective of this study was to evaluate the effect of foliar and soil application of molybdenum (Mo) fertilizer on the biological efficiency, competitive ability, and nutrient uptake of the sorghum–cowpea intercropping system. The experimental design was a randomized complete block design with four replications and nine cropping systems: sorghum–cowpea intercropping with foliar Mo application; sorghum–cowpea intercropping with soil Mo application; sorghum–cowpea intercropping without Mo application; sole sorghum with foliar Mo application; sole sorghum with soil Mo application; sole sorghum without Mo application; sole cowpea with foliar Mo application; sole cowpea with soil Mo application; and sole cowpea without Mo application.
