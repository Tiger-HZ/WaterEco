Abstract Understanding how cascaded reservoirs alter nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) dynamics is essential for safeguarding water quality in inland river basins. Using the upstream Heihe River Basin (Gansu, China) as a representative cascaded system, we monitored five reservoirs and five adjacent river reaches from August 2023 to January 2024 across ice-covered (frozen) and open-water (non-frozen) periods. We measured physicochemical parameters (e.g., WT, pH, EC, TDS, Salinity, DO) and nutrient/organic indices (TN, TP, NO - 2 -N, NH 3 –N, COD Cr , COD Mn ) and assessed trophic status using the eutrophication index (EI) and N:P stoichiometry.
