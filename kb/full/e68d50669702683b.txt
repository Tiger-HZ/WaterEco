Abstract Desalination is limited by challenges such as high energy consumption and brine management. In this work, a modeling platform integrating water treatment and brine concentration models with solar energy models is employed to conduct a techno-economic analysis (TEA) of key desalination applications for two case studies: brine desalination at the kay bailey hutchison desalination plant (KBHDP) and produced water in the Permian Basin. This study presents a TEA of both potential state-of-the-art technologies at these sites and proposed treatment trains powered by solar energy technologies using the simulation and optimization tool WaterTAP-REFLO.
