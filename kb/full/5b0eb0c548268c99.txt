The rapid expansion of coastal aquaculture has raised concerns about pollution from improperly managed practices. Anthropogenic activities, particularly the release of dissolved organic matter (DOM) from fish feed, are a significant contributor to coastal DOM pools. However, the influence of this DOM on coastal eutrophication and overall ecosystem health remains poorly understood. This study investigates the underlying mechanisms by examining the relationship between DOM dynamics and coastal water quality through year-long monitoring of 36 fish culture zones and their adjacent waters in Hong Kong.
