This study examines the effect of technical qualification on the quality of service delivery in Federal Ministries in Cross River State, Nigeria. The increasing demand for efficient public service has heightened the need for competent employees with the requisite expertise, educational qualifications, professional certifications, and practical skills. However, concerns persist regarding poor service delivery resulting from inadequate technical competence, inappropriate staff placement, and insufficient professional development. Guided by Max Weber's Bureaucratic Theory, the study investigates how employee expertise, educational qualification, professional certification, and skills influence quality service delivery in selected Federal Ministries.
