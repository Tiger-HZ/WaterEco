Abstract Mycetinis scorodonius is a garlic-scented mushroom from the family Omphalotaceae that can be used as a seasoning mushroom. γ -Glutamylmarasmin is considered the aroma precursor, which is converted into marasmicin through enzymatic activity. Marasmicin itself is unstable and breaks down into several other flavor compounds, such as methyl dithioformate, 2,3,5-trithiahexane and 2,4,5,7-tetrathiaoctane. This study examined the differences in aroma between fresh and dried fruiting bodies, as well as activation with water. It was found that fresh fruiting bodies activated with water had the highest aroma content, while dry fruiting bodies without the activation of water released almost no aroma compounds.
