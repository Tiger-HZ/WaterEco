Objectives The Assessment of SpondyloArthritis international Society recently defined early axial spondyloarthritis (axSpA) as a duration ≤2 years of axial symptoms. Previous studies have shown no significant difference in short‐term outcomes between early and established axSpA patients treated with biologic/targeted synthetic disease‐modifying anti‐rheumatic drugs. In order to evaluate long‐term outcomes, we compared spinal radiographic progression between early and established axSpA patients followed under standard of care treatment in a large Swiss cohort.
