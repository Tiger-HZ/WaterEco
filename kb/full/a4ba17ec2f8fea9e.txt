Abstract Offshore carbonate drilling in Malaysia involves drilling through karst features that often lead to severe loss circulation, impacting approximately one third of the wells drilled. These losses typically occur near the carbonate top, rendering conventional circulation ineffective and increasing well control exposure during tripping, particularly in high-angle development wells. The Downhole Isolation Valve (DIV) provides a reliable downhole isolation mechanism that enables safe and predictable operations under losses conditions while improving completion efficiency. The DIV is a flapper-type downhole isolation valve installed within the 9-5/8″ tieback casing, providing full-bore access and operated via dual encapsulated control lines connected to a surface control unit.
