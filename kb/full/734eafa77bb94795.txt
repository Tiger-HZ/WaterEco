ABSTRACT The study examines the industry‐level impact of Indian Accounting Standards (Ind‐AS), largely converged with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), on the matching quality of expenses with revenues. Using matching‐quality attributes, the study employs both a two‐stage regression framework and a single‐stage model, covering 31 industries (807 firms) and 45 industries (869 firms), respectively. The study evaluates changes in matching attributes over the initial post–Ind‐AS period (2017–2019) and the later post–Ind‐AS period (2019–2022), controlling for the COVID year (2020–2021).
