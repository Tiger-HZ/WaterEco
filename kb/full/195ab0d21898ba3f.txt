We evaluated the seasonal variation of denitrification, anaerobic ammonium oxidation (anammox), and dissimilatory nitrate reduction to ammonium (DNRA) rates in the sediments and the integrative N (and O) isotopic signatures of dissolved inorganic nitrogen (DIN) compounds in the overlying water column of the monomictic Lake Lugano south basin. Denitrification was the dominant NO − 3 reduction pathway, whereas the contribution of anammox and DNRA to total benthic NO − 3 reduction was &lt; 6% and &lt; 12%, respectively. Sedimentary denitrification rates were highest (up to 57.2 ± 16.8 µmol N m −2 h −1 ) during fully oxic bottom water conditions.
