This study investigates the magneto-thermo-bioconvective sheared hybrid nanofluid (SWCNT-MWCNT/H 2 O) flow within a porous cavity containing oxytactic microorganisms. The enclosure is left-side heated and right-side cooled with adiabatic tops and bottom. In addition, the upper wall moves horizontally ([Formula: see text]-direction), and other walls are stationary. Finite-difference discretization is applied to nondimensional equations, and the resulting linear equations are efficiently solved through iterative techniques.
