Abstract Foliar nutrient application plays an important role in citrus production, as nutrient availability influences fruit development, yield, juice quality, and biochemical composition. The present study evaluated the effect of foliar application of macro- and micronutrients on fruit yield and quality of sweet orange cv. Malta under West Bengal conditions. Thirteen nutrient treatments comprising K 2 SO 4 , KNO 3 , Ca(NO 3 ) 2 , ZnSO 4 , H 3 BO 3 and NPK (19:19:19) at different concentrations were evaluated. Significant variation was observed in fruit physical, biochemical, yield, and antioxidant attributes.
