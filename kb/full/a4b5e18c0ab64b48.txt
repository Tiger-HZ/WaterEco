Manganese-based lithium ion-sieves (LIS) are promising adsorbents for lithium extraction from salt lake brines. Their powder form, however, hinders industrial application because it disperses poorly, is difficult to recover, and causes excessive pressure drop in fixed-bed columns. Here we address this powder-forming challenge through porous spherical granulation, using solid paraffin as a mesoporous porogen. Co-doped precursors were prepared by hydrothermal calcination. XPS showed that Co doping raised the Mn4+ ratio from 65.77% to 72.16%, which suppresses the Jahn-Teller effect. The optimal powder reached 33.36 mg/g in LiCl solution and retained 89.0% of this capacity after five cycles.
