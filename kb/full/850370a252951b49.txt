The article presents estimation of the ecological state of soil phototrophic microbe complex in forest and meadow ecosystems in the vicinity of the former chemical weapons destruction facility “Maradykovskiy” (CWDF). Currently, a production and technical complex “Ecotechnopark “Mirnyy” (PTK) for the disposal of wastes of the 1st and 2nd hazard classes is being created on its territory. Natural groups of algae and cyanobacteria (CB) has a set of physiological-biochemical and ecological features and are widely used in soil state ecological assessing. Nowadays the algocyanoflora communities’ structure within the site of PTC is typical for the forest zone.
