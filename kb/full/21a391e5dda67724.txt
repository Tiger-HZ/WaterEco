Abstract Background Malt barley is an important crop cultivated for both home consumption and market purposes. However, its productivity and grain quality are mainly constrained by low-yielding varieties, a lack of appropriate time of N fertilizer application, and N fertilizer rate recommendations in the study area. Result Thus, this study was carried out under farmer’s field at central Ethiopian highlands during the 2023–2024 main cropping season to assess the effect of varieties, time of nitrogen application, and nitrogen fertilizer rate on yield and quality of malt barley.
