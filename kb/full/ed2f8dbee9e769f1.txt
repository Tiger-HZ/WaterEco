Corn farming occupies a substantial portion of agricultural land in the United States, yet the potential impacts of agrivoltaic systems on commodity crop yields remain poorly quantified at a national scale. Building upon our previous county-level modeling framework, we extended our approach to analyze the top 1000 corn-producing counties in the U.S. For each county, the model was calibrated by comparing 20 years of USDA-reported yield data with model predictions to reflect local growing conditions, management practices, and genetic improvements. Our analysis quantified the trade-offs between crop yield loss due to shading from sparse solar arrays and potential yield gains resulting from increased soil moisture retention under partial shading.
