ABSTRACT Mango ( Mangifera indica L.) is one of the most widely cultivated tropical fruits, generating large quantities of by‐products during processing, with mango seed representing 20%–25% of the whole fruit weight. Traditionally discarded as waste, mango seeds are now being recognized as a sustainable source of nutrients, bioactive compounds, and value‐added products. The seed kernel is rich in carbohydrates, proteins, and dietary fiber, whereas the oil fraction contains nutritionally important fatty acids and phytosterols. In addition, mango seed is a reservoir of bioactive molecules, including polyphenols, flavonoids, tannins, tocopherols, and carotenoids, which exhibit diverse functional properties such as antioxidant, antimicrobial, antidiabetic, and hypocholesterolemic activities.
