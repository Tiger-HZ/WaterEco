An integrative taxonomy approach of the Indo-Pacific deep-water genus Vermeijius Kantor, Fedosov, M.A. Snyder &amp;#38; Bouchet, 2018 (Neogastropoda: Buccinoidea: Fasciolariidae) revealed a significant radiation, with molecular (cox1 barcode fragment) and morphological data supporting species delimitation and the status of the genus. A total of 40 species is recognized, of which 27 are described as new, namely V. abditus sp. nov., V. admirabilis sp. nov., V. albulus sp. nov., V. antea sp. nov., V. atimovatae sp. nov., V. bellatulus sp. nov., V. brevicostatus sp. nov., V. celatus sp. nov., V. chinoi sp. nov., V. comorensis sp. nov., V. corbariae sp. nov., V. costuliferus sp. nov., V. cracens sp. nov., V. fijiensis sp. nov., V. geminatus sp. nov., V. macdonaldensis sp. nov., V. mainbaza sp.
