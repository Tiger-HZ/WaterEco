Finger millet (Eleusine coracana L.) is a climate-resistant crop, which is high in calcium and dietary fibre. However, its significance in terms of food security is declining, as decreasing yields discourage farmers from cultivating it. The field experiment was conducted at Shri Guru Ram Rai (SGRR) University, Dehradun during 2023–24 and 2024–25, using eight genotypes and five nutrient management treatments. The study was conducted in randomised block design with 8 varieties and 5 treatments to evaluate seed quality and growth performance and yield. The treatments included farmyard manure, humic acid, biofertiliser, nano-fertilisers and locally prepared liquid formulations known as Jeevamrut and Neemastra.
