Abstract Municipal solid wastes pose environmental challenges of seeping of leachates containing toxic heavy metals through soil to well water of nearby residential buildings. This contamination route is experienced by people residing in the vicinity of open dumpsite at Lapite in Ibadan. This objective of this study was to assess the impact of heavy metals arising from combustion process on soil and underground water in the vicinity of Lapite dumpsite in Ibadan of Nigeria.
