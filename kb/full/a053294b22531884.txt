Accurate mapping of surface water bodies is essential for dam monitoring, irrigation planning, and sustainable water resource management. Conventional methods based on spectral indices, such as NDWI, can face challenges in complex environments due to spectral ambiguity, seasonal variability, and urban or vegetated surfaces. Deep learning approaches such as U-Net and DeepLabv3+ have improved water-body segmentation; however, accurately delineating fine boundaries and detecting small water bodies remain challenging. To address these limitations, this study proposes WaterFormer-MS, a multi-stream deep learning framework for surface water segmentation from Sentinel-2 imagery.
