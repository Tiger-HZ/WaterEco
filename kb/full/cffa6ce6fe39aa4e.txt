Soil physicochemical heterogeneity and rhizosphere microbial communities jointly influence plant nutrient acquisition and crop performance, yet their relative associations with strawberry nutritional status and productivity remain poorly understood in acidic agricultural soils. In this study, soil physicochemical properties, enzyme activities, bacterial community diversity and composition, leaf mineral status, agronomic traits, and fruit quality were systematically characterized across strawberry cultivation sites. Mantel tests, redundancy analysis (RDA), and partial least squares path modeling (PLS-PM) were used to examine the relationships among soil conditions, microbial communities, plant mineral nutrition, and strawberry performance.
