Scarcity of protein source ingredients such as soyabean is a limiting factor for aqua feed availability. Growth and nutrient utilisation in the African catfish Heterobranchus bidorsalis fingerlings fed Lablab purpureus seed meal was investigated. Six iso-nitrogenous (40 % crude protein) diets in which L. purpureus seed meal replaced soyabean meal at 0 % (control diet), 10 %, 20 %, 30 %, 40 %, and 50 % were formulated for the trial. A total of 126 H. bidorsalis fingerlings with an average weight (± SD) of 12.25 ± 0.02 g, were randomly distributed into 18 plastic aquaria (measuring 48 × 28 × 28 cm) in groups of 7 per aquaria and three replicate aquaria were allocated for each of the six diets.
