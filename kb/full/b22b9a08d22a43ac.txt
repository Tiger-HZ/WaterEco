Purpose: The aim of this study is therefore to estimate the level of fluoride exposure from groundwater consumption at Olbanita wellfield boreholes and associated potential non-carcinogenic risks to children and adults. Methodology: Water samples were collected from six productive boreholes (BB2, BB3, BB4, BB5, BB6 and BB7) during dry season of the study area for three months and analyzed for fluoride concentrations using spectrophotometric method. Fluoride exposure levels and potential non carcinogenic health risk were evaluated using the United States Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA) method by calculating Chronic Daily Intake (CDI) and Hazard Quotient (HQ) for adults and children.
