Abstract Background Nitrogen fertilization in sandy soils often results reducing nitrogen use efficiency and posing environmental risks. Biochar has been proposed as a soil amendment to improve nutrient retention and crop quality. However, the combined effect of biochar and graded nitrogen rates on the physiology and rhizome quality of turmeric (curcuma longa L.) remain poorly understand. Methods A factorial pot experiment was conducted using four nitrogen rates (0, 70, 150, and 250 kg N ha⁻ 1 ) and two biochar rates (0 and 15 t ha⁻ 1 ) in a greenhouse.
