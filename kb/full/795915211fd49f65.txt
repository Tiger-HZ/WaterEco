Buffalo meat shami kebab is a traditional comminuted meat product whose processing quality may be modified by fibre-rich plant ingredients. This study evaluated cooked mashed elephant foot yam (EFY) as a partial meat replacer in buffalo meat shami kebab. A control without EFY and treatments containing 5%, 10%, and 15% EFY were prepared in six independent batches. Physicochemical characteristics, proximate composition, and sensory attributes were evaluated. EFY incorporation increased product yield from 91.85% in the control to 94.35% at 10%, while moisture retention rose from 56.85% to 60.15%.
