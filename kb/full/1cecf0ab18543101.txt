Improving soil carbon quality and biological fertility through long-term nutrient management is essential for sustaining productivity, reducing input dependence and enhancing climate resilience in rainfed Vertisols. This study evaluated the effects of nine treatments including control, inorganic fertilisation (50 % RDF, 100 % RDF, RDF + Zn), organic amendments [farmyard manure (FYM) (50 % NFYM, sole FYM), crop residues (CR) (50 % NCR)] and integrated nutrient management (INMFYM, INMCR) on soil carbon pools, oxidisable carbon fractions, carbon management indices (CMI) and enzyme activities in a permanent manurial experiment.
