The balance between fed and extractive compartments is critical in recirculating multitrophic aquaponic systems (RMAS). This study evaluated the effects of Pacific oyster (Crassostrea gigas) stocking level on water quality, gilthead seabream (Sparus aurata) performance, oyster growth, glasswort (Salicornia europaea) production, and whole-system fresh-product output. Nine independent RMAS received no oysters (O-00), 30 oysters (O-30), or 60 oysters (O-60). Ninety juvenile seabream (79.7 ± 13.8 g) were reared for 134 days with salicornia, while oysters received no supplemental feed. Oyster inclusion was associated with lower nitrate and phosphate concentrations, whereas other water-quality variables showed no treatment-related differences.
