[“Quality: Judge Literary and Artistic Merit” explains that judging literary merit is a necessary critical move. While ever-changing perspectives allow us to find value in nearly everything we read, watch, and experience, we certainly don&s;t have time to consume everything. We have to make a choice, and our choice is often based on our sense of quality. As theorists of value, we may argue with others about the qualities and functions of a “well-crafted” text, and as critics who judge value, we argue with others about whether or not a particular text has the proper qualities or valued functions, and those are two different kinds of debates.]
