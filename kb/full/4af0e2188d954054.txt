Abstract Temperate meromictic deep lakes are characterized by oxygen (O 2 ) depletion below the oxycline due to respiration and oxidation rates exceeding O 2 diffusion. The effects of meromixis on nutrient biogeochemistry are generally studied in the water column, whereas those produced in the benthic compartment are less explored. We expect marked effects of anoxia on benthic nitrogen (N) cycling as nitrification, the main source of nitrate (NO 3 − ) within sediments supporting denitrification and dissimilatory nitrate reduction to ammonium (DNRA), is suppressed.
