Surface water bodies provide essential ecosystem services, but their quality is increasingly being affected by human activities. In Mexico, information on the impact of leachate generated by open-air municipal landfills on surface water bodies remains limited. The objective of this study was to assess the water quality of the Alseseca River in Tlapacoyan, Veracruz, Mexico, at a point located approximately 200 m from a municipal landfill lacking leachate control infrastructure. The hypothesis was that proximity to the landfill negatively affects water quality by increasing levels of microbiological contaminants and heavy metals.
