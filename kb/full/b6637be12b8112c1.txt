The goal of this research was to assess the effect of humic acid (HA) applied to the soil and foliar sprayed with proline (Pro) on the growth and yield of sweet peppers, Capsicum annuum L., cv. Carisma F1, and soil fertility (N-P-K) at 2 different irrigation water salinities (1.5 and 3.5 dS m⁻¹) in a field study conducted in a plastic greenhouse in the fall of 2024 using randomized complete block design (RCBD) and 3 replications of each treatment. The experimental treatments consisted of 3 levels of HA (0, 8 and 16 kg ha⁻¹), 3 concentrations of Pro (0, 50 and 100 mg L⁻¹) and 2 irrigation water salinity levels (1.5 and 3.5 dS m⁻¹).
