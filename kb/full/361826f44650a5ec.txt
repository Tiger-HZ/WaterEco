Anaerobic reactors and algal sludge conversion have become potential technologies of sustainable bioenergy generation and waste management. Anaerobic digestion can be defined as a biological process where the microorganisms decompose the organic matter to produce biogas with high levels of methane and carbon- dioxide. The algal sludge is formed as a result of wastewater treatment, as well as the harvesting of algal biomass, which is a useful substrate because it contains a large amount of organic and nutrient matters. The process is improved and stabilized by the next generation reactor designs, such as and anaerobic sludge blanket and anaerobic membrane bioreactors. Performance is among the factors that are greatly affected by operational factors such as temperature, retention time and substrate composition. The hybrid solution ensures a decrease of environmental pollution, greenhouse gas emissions, and circular bioeconomy objectives.
