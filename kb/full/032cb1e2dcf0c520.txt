A field experiment was conducted from November 2021 to March 2022 at the Experimental Farm of the Department of Plantation, Spices, Medicinal and Aromatic Crops, Faculty of Horticulture, Uttar Banga Krishi Vishwavidyalaya, Pundibari, Cooch Behar, West Bengal, to evaluate the effect of integrated nitrogen management on the growth, quality, and post-harvest soil nitrogen status of coriander (Coriandrum sativum L.). The experiment was laid out in a randomised block design (RBD) with eight treatments and three replications. Different combinations of inorganic nitrogen, farmyard manure (FYM), poultry manure (PM), and recommended phosphorus and potassium (PK) fertilisers were evaluated.
