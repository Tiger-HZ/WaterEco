Abstract Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) optical fiber methane sensors provide promising routes for gas monitoring, yet most existing anti‐resonant hollow‐core fiber (ARHCF) SPR schemes lack multi‐parameter collaborative analysis and detailed polarization‐dependent mechanism interpretation. In this paper, we computationally investigate an SPR methane sensor based on inner‐gold‐coated ARHCF using the finite‐element method. Three critical structural parameters, including gold‐film thickness, methane‐sensitive cryptophane‐A film thickness, and anti‐resonant tube wall thickness, are comprehensively studied.
