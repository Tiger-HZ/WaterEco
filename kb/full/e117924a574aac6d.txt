Two new taxa of the water mite genus Hygrobates Koch, 1837 are described from Cyprus: H. dorflingeri n. sp. and H. ponticus cyprioticus n. ssp. Both belong to the H. calliger complex, whose members are common components of water mite assemblages in low-, middle-, and high-order streams. Phylogenetic analysis and ASAP species delimitation based on COI barcoding support their recognition as distinct lineages. Their morphological and genetic similarity to close relatives from mainland Anatolia suggests potential dispersal pathways for ancestral lineages during the colonization of Cyprus.
