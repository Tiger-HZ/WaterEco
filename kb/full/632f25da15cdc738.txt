Introduction Cadmium (Cd) and arsenic (As) co-contamination in paddy soils poses a persistent challenge for rice safety due to their distinct responses to soil redox conditions. Although organic amendments have been widely used to regulate metal(loid) availability, their effects on Cd/As behavior in soil-rice systems under contrasting water regimes remain unclear. Methods A pot experiment was conducted under flooding-moisture alternation (FM) and continuous flooding (F) to evaluate the effects of fulvic acid (FA), humic acid (HA), and woody peat (PT) on Cd/As availability, Cd/As in iron plaque (IP), and grain Cd/As accumulation.
