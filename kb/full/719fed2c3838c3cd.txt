Recurrent algal blooms in park lakes, urban reservoirs, and decorative ponds are not a natural inevitability but the visible result of a nutrient mass imbalance. When phosphorus exceeds 0.1 mg/L and nitrogen exceeds 1 mg/L, water temperature holds in the 24-28 °C range, and circulation is weak, microalgae and cyanobacteria grow rapidly: cell density reaches 10^6-10^8 per milliliter, chlorophyll-a rises above 100 µg/L, and transparency falls below 0.5-1 m. Conventional interventions - algaecides, peroxides, copper compounds, coagulants, aeration, and sand filtration - kill or settle biomass that has already grown but remove no dissolved phosphorus or nitrogen; within 3-6 weeks the nutrient medium is restored and the bloom returns. Seasonal chemical management of a water body of about 1 km2 costs $200,000-500,000 per year, and a recreational closure of 20-30 days can produce direct losses of $1-3 million per season. This paper presents ARBOPOOL, a closed-loop treatment platform built on vacuum phase separation carried out at ambient temperature. Rather than dosing the water, ARBOPOOL evaporates it under vacuum, carrying nitrogenous compounds and dissolved gases away with the cold vapor and denying microflora their substrate. The process uses no membranes, no chlorine, and no reagents, and it operates in full recirculation with zero discharge. Water is returned to a WHO-standard quality in which fish can live, while the concentrate leaves the system as a dry, odorless organic solid suitable as tree fertilizer. A case study of the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool (~15,000 m3; 619 x 51 m; ~0.5 m deep) demonstrates that four ARBOPOOL-200 units deliver 800 m3/day, achieving full turnover roughly every 19 days at 0.7 kWh/m3 - 560 kWh/day, 204 MWh/year, and an average draw of about 23 kW - for an operating cost of $0.084/m3, approximately $67/day or $24,500/year. A 24/7, GSM-managed quality e-lab monitors nitrogenous compounds, pH, and bacteriology with instant alerts, giving operators a warning before the water turns green rather than after. ARBOPOOL reframes bloom control from a seasonal chemical battle into a continuous, quantifiable engineering service. A pilot can be deployed in 8 weeks.
