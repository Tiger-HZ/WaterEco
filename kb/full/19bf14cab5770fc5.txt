Microplastics (MPs; plastic particles smaller than 5 mm) are widely reported in the surface water and sediment of the Vietnamese Mekong Delta, but their ecological hazard has not been synthesised across the Delta. Because polymer types differ by orders of magnitude in the toxicity of their constituent monomers and additives, particle abundance alone is an incomplete measure of hazard. We compiled published MP records for the Delta and, for context, for other Vietnamese river and coastal systems (12 records from 11 studies), and evaluated them with two separate indicators: the pollution load index (PLI), which scales abundance against a reference, and the polymer hazard index (PHI), which weights the polymer assemblage by chemical hazard score.
