This article presents data on the distribution of various forms of mineral nitrogen and phosphorus in water sources of Southeastern Kazakhstan for the period from 2014 to 2023. It was found that the content and ratio of mineral nitrogen forms varied. Among nitrogen compounds, nitrates predominated, accounting for 82–86% of the total nitrogen. However, no exceedances of the maximum permissible concentrations (MPC) for water bodies of fishery importance were detected. Ammonium and nitrite nitrogen accounted for 12–17% and 0.8–2.1% of the total nitrogen, respectively. Nevertheless, exceedances of their MPC by 1.2 to 3.0 times were observed in almost all seasons.
