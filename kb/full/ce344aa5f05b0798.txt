Phycobiostimulants derived from macroalgae, microalgae, and cyanobacteria are increasingly used to improve crop performance. However, their effects vary across products, crops, and environments. In this review, we propose a biomass–process–composition–mechanism–efficacy (BPCME) framework to systematically trace this variability. Biomass provenance, including taxonomic identity, cultivation conditions, physiological state, and harvest timing, defines the initial biochemical potential, whereas processing and formulation determine the molecular profile delivered to the plant.
