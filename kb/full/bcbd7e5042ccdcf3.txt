Stimuli-responsive metal–organic frameworks (MOFs) offer opportunities to couple structural transformations with switchable electronic states, yet reproducing spin-crossover (SCO) behaviour across different frameworks and metal centres remains challenging. Here, we demonstrate a conserved watergated switching mechanism in a family of flexible tetrazolate MOFs comprising Fe(II), Co(II), and mixed Fe/Co frameworks. Dehydration induces framework contraction, metal–N bond shortening, ligand immobilization, and a shift toward low-spin states, while rehydration restores the initial structure and physical properties.
