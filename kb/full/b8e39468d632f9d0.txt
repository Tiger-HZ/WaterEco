Abstract Objective To explore changes in health-related quality of life (HRQoL) measured using EQ-5D-5L among participants of a digital education and exercise therapy for knee and hip osteoarthritis (OA). Methods A descriptive longitudinal registry-based study among participants aged 40 + years enrolled in the digital program with responses to EQ-5D-5L at enrolment and at least one follow-up at 3- [ n = 15147] or 12-months [ n = 4586]. Changes in EQ-5D-5L profile from enrolment were examined using the Paretian classification of health change (PCHC). Results At enrolment, selfcare and pain/discomfort were the EQ-5D-5L dimensions with the least and most reported problems, respectively.
