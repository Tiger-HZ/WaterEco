Although dissolved rare earth elements (REEs) are increasingly utilized to explore riverine geochemical processes, their seasonal responses and source controls remain poorly constrained. This study investigated the sources of dissolved REEs in the Mun River and evaluated their responses to monsoon-driven hydrological processes. ΣREE ranged from 16.41 to 467.01 ng/L. Most river waters displayed negative Ce anomalies and positive Eu anomalies, whereas anthropogenic Gd enrichment was localized. ΣREE concentrations showed no significant seasonal difference in upper reaches but were significantly higher during wet season in the middle-lower reaches. Light REEs (LREE) and Middle REEs (MREE) generally exhibited stronger wet-season responses than Heavy REEs (HREE) in the middle and lower reaches.
