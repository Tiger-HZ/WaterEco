Purpose Drawing on the network embeddedness perspective, this study aims to investigate how supplier network stability affects two distinct facets of green innovation – diversity and quality – and how these relationships are moderated by absorptive capacity. Design/methodology/approach The proposed relationships are tested using ordinary least squares regression based on a sample of 1,655 firm-year observations from 577 Chinese publicly listed firms over the period 2009–2023. To address potential endogeneity and selection bias, we conduct a series of analyses. Findings We find that supplier network stability is detrimental to both green innovation diversity and green innovation quality.
