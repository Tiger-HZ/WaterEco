A pilot-scale engineered floating wetland raft system was developed to mitigate the eutrophication problem for the Baltic Sea. The preliminary batch experiments demonstrated effective nutrient removal, with PO43- removal efficiencies reaching up to 63% in the Bolboschoenus maritimus planted system. NH4+ removal was highly efficient in all planted systems, reaching 99.9% and 96.9% for Bolboschoenus maritimus and Schoenoplectus tabernaemontani, respectively. Temporal nitrogen dynamics showed initial NO3- reduction followed by accumulation after day 7, with evidence of nitrification and incomplete oxidation. In the field-scale vertECO® raft prototype, maximum PO43- removal efficiency reached 87%, although performance varied widely (-56% to 25%) due to biomass washout and internal release proc
