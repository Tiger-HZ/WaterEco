In the framework of soil erosion processes at different spatial and temporal scales, the concept of connectivity is applied to express the physical link among hillslopes and the channel network concerning flow motion and associated sediments. In this paper, basin sediment connectivity was used in the framework of the Sediment Delivery Distributed (SEDD) model together with sediment yield measurements performed at the W2 experimental basin (Calabria, Italy) in the period 1978–1994. The frequency distribution of the travel time was used to estimate the coefficient βL of the structural component of the sediment delivery ratio of each morphological unit.
