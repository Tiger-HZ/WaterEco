Results of studies on eutrophication of surface waters as one of the negative phenomena of the functioning of aquatic ecosystems are summarized in the article. It is noted that eutrophication is a complex process caused by the influence of a number of factors, primarily an increased content of nutrients, in particular nitrogen and phosphorus compounds. The criteria for assessing the trophic state of water bodies and watercourses using chemical, biological and physical parameters, as well as complex indices of trophic state based on them, are presented. External and internal sources of nutrient input to the aquatic environment are considered.
