This study examines how land-use change alters soil erosion in the Bolango Watershed, Gorontalo Province, Indonesia, through an integrated framework that couples the revised universal soil loss equation (RUSLE) with agent-based modelling (ABM). Across the 52,494 ha watershed, marked transformations were projected between 2013 and 2033, including a 14.25% decline in forest cover, a 42.37% expansion of dryland agriculture, and a 122.24% increase in settlements. Spatial analyses implemented in geographic information system (GIS) software showed that mean soil loss decreased from 59.08 Mg∙ha−1∙y−1 in 2013 to 49.22 Mg∙ha−1∙y−1 in 2023, before rising slightly to 50.17 Mg∙ha−1∙y−1 in 2033.
