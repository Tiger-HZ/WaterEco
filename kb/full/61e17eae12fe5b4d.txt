Explicit modeling of process variability in the dynamics of fisheries is motivated by a desire to incorporate more realism into stock assessment models, and much recent research effort has been devoted to the computational features of fitting state-space models for this purpose. Here, we extend the Bayesian application of nonlinear state-space modeling to sequential population analysis of age-structured data using a model formulation that allows for unreported catches and incidental fishing mortality. It is shown that, once a familiarity with the general-purpose Bayesian software BUGS is acquired, implementing a state-space model is a relatively simple task.
