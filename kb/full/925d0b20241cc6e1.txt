The engineering planning and design of Small Water Impounding Projects (SWIPs) necessitates proper site selection to ensure technical viability and sustainability. This study developed localized criteria for assessing the site suitability of SWIPs in the whole province of Bukidnon by integrating national engineering standards with local experts’ opinions using the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). Pairwise comparison of the formulated criteria was performed by selected local experts to derive their relative weights, with consistency ratios checked against Saaty’s thresholds to validate their judgments.
