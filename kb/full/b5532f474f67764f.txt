The aeromagnetic data over parts of the southern bida basin, Nigeria, were interpreted by source parameter imaging (SPI) and Pdepth techniques in order to determine the sediment thickness of the area. The data were analyzed and interpreted using Oasis Montaj 8.4. The regional fields were separated from the total magnetic intensity (TMI) fields to obtain the residual fields using first order polynomial fitting. The result of the SPI interpretation revealed depth ranged from 151.6m (shallow magnetic bodies) to 1608.2 m (deep lying magnetic bodies). The depth estimates obtained from the Pdepth technique shows a depth for contact ranged from -81.7 to -1717.8m and dike depth ranged from -270.7 to 1890.2m.
