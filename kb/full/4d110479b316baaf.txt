ABSTRACT Background Hospitalized older adults are at high risk for hospital‐associated disability, but one‐to‐one physical therapy (PT) models often cannot meet demand for skilled mobility progression. Group‐based PT is effective in outpatient and rehabilitation settings, but its feasibility in acute inpatient care is unknown. Methods We conducted a single‐site, prospective quality improvement implementation of acute care group‐based PT (AC‐GBPT) on a 39‐bed medical‐surgical geriatrics unit at an academic medical center. Sessions were offered weekly during routine clinical operations without additional staffing.
