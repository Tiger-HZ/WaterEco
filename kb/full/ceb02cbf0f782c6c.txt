Abstract Insulator defect detection is essential for reliable power transmission. In unconstrained UAV inspection, extreme aspect ratios, complex backgrounds, and tiny low-contrast defects make precise detection challenging. To reduce missed detections and false alarms, we propose SGMP-DEIM, a real-time detector integrating Semantic-Grouped Mamba and Multi-Granularity Diffusion. In the backbone, we build a Semantic Grouped Mamba Stage (SGM-Stage) where semantic grouping guides an approximate state space model (ASSM) to capture long-range dependencies along insulator strings with linear complexity. In the neck, we develop a Multi-Granularity Diffusion Pyramid Network (MGDP-Net) with a High-Frequency Dual-View Feature Aggregation (HDFA) module. By introducing a high-resolution P3 level and performing bidirectional diffusion across scales, MGDP-Net strengthens high-frequency details and improves localization of tiny defects. We further propose a Clustering-Enhanced Meta Encoder (CEM-Encoder) that uses clustering-driven attention to learn robust semantic prototypes and suppress background interference. Experiments show SGMP-DEIM improves the baseline by 7.2% in 〖AP〗_50 and 6.9% in 〖AP〗_(50:95), outperforming strong real-time detectors such as RT-DETR and YOLO variants. To evaluate robustness in extreme conditions, we also build a challenging test benchmark with diverse weather disturbances using an AIGC-based augmentation framework for testing only. Overall, SGMP-DEIM provides an effective solution for real-time, high-precision insulator defect detection.
