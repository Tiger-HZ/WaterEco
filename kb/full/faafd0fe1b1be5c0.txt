Abstract Accurately assessing ecological condition is crucial for ecosystem conservation. The Remote Sensing Ecological Index (RSEI), which incorporates soil moisture (WET), was widely used to assess regional ecological condition. However, in coastal wetlands, the performance of the WET index is often undermined due to high soil salinity caused by seawater intrusion. Therefore, a Water-Salt Stress (WSS) index was developed by integrating WET and salinity (SI) to quantify the combined water-salt stress on vegetation. Replacing WET with WSS, an improved index, Water-Salt Stress Remote Sensing Ecological Index (WSRSEI), was established for ecological condition assessment.
