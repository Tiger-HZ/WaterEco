Abstract Eutrophication and cyanobacterial blooms have increased worldwide due to intensified nutrient inputs from agricultural and urban activities. Conceptual frameworks integrating environmental indicators are essential to support reservoirs management. In this study, the Pressure–State–Response (PSR) framework was applied to identify eutrophication drivers in the Nova Ponte hydroelectric reservoir (Minas Gerais, Brazil). Long-term water quality data (1995–2020), land-use maps, and environmental indicators were integrated to construct a causal network linking anthropogenic pressures, environmental conditions, and management responses.
