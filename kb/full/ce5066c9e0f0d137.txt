The national water network is a component of China’s infrastructure, and coordinating ecological security with economic development is important for its construction. Taking the Chao’er River to West Liao River Water Diversion Project, part of the three-horizontal and three-vertical Inner Mongolia water network, as a case study, this paper analyzes the main receiving areas, Tongliao and Xing’an League. Using panel data from 2019–2025, a matching degree model incorporating water resource, ecological, and economic dimensions was constructed. The Gini coefficient and coupling coordination degree were combined to assess spatial matching and its evolution before and after project operation, and optimization scenarios for current water allocation are proposed.
