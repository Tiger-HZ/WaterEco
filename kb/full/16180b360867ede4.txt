Introduction Polygonatum odoratum ( P. odoratum ) is the dried rhizome of plant Polygonatum odoratum (Mill.) Druce, and it is a well-known medicinal and edible plant extensively utilized in Asia, but its commercial products, sliced or strip forms, limiting its wider applications. The powdered form of P. odoratum offers greater convenience for modern food applications. This study aims to develop efficient processing strategies for high-quality P. odoratum powder. Methods This study used fresh P. odoratum as raw material to investigate the quality of powder produced by vacuum microwave drying combined with extrusion cooking (combined approach) and compared it with powders obtained by hot-air drying and vacuum microwave drying alone.
