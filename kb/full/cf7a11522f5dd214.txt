Abstract This study investigates the determinants of economic agents' perceptions of water quality and water resource management in the Oueme delta, Benin, using primary survey data collected in April 2021 from 3,075 economic agents aged 16 and above, spanning fishing, agriculture, livestock rearing, water transport and household users. Because a formal test rejects the proportional-odds assumption underlying the standard ordered probit model conventionally used in this literature, and further diagnostics reveal significant heteroskedasticity, the perception model is estimated as a heteroskedastic ordered probit with standard errors clustered by commune.
