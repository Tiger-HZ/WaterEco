Ta3N5 is a promising material for overall water splitting due to its suitable band positions and high theoretical photocurrent (12.9 mA cm−2). However, pristine Ta3N5 suffers from significant optical, recombination, and resistive losses, resulting in photocurrents and efficiencies far below theoretical expectations. To address these limitations, this work focuses on enhancing photocurrent and overall performance through structural and morphological engineering and surface functionalization.
