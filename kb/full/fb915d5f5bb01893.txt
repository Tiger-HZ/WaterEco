To investigate the eutrophication status of Dianshan Lake, which is located in Qingpu district, Shanghai, and to evaluate any health risks associated with water exposure, we evaluated the effects of eutrophication of water bodies and algal toxins on liver and kidney function in the local population. We reviewed past monitoring and evaluated the water quality improvement and health impact on the population after implementing water quality protection measures for Dianshan Lake. We monitored the source water of Dianshan Lake monthly throughout the year and analysed the water quality, eutrophication, and algal toxin pollution status and health risks of the local population, including liver and kidney function.
