We examine the relationship between observed tsunami water levels and tsunami currents in Humboldt Bay, California at different locations and events (27 February 2010; 11 March 2011; 29 July 2021; 15 January 2022). Water levels and currents were highly correlated for all four Humboldt Bay locations/events and dominated by oscillations with a period of roughly 57 min, consistent with previous analysis of water levels. Phase lags between water level and current were generally small (typically 0-30°). Tsunami current time series were shifted in time relative to tsunami water level time series, based on the observed lag; strong linear relationships were observed across all datasets.
