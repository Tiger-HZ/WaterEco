This study assessed selected physico-chemical parameters and bacterial loads in a migratory bird-inhabited lake at the Jahangirnagar University (JU) campus from July to December 2025. Water samples were collected monthly from three depth strata—surface, middle, and bottom, along with bottom mud samples from three locations of the lake. Temperature, pH, and DO were significantly higher in surface water than in the middle and bottom layers (p &lt; 0.05), whereas EC, TDS, and total hardness tended to be higher in bottom water across the study period. Bacterial abundance varied significantly among water depths and sampling months, with bottom water showing significantly higher bacterial loads than surface and middle waters, indicating that lake sediments function as a major microbial reservoir
