Groundwater continues to be a significant source of drinking water in the Asaba metropolis. However, its quality might be compromised given the increasing rate of urbanization and unsustainable anthropogenic activities. This paper therefore assesses the concentrations of selected heavy metals: Cu, Zn, Fe, Cr and Ni in borehole water from ten locations across the study area. In addition, it evaluates the non-carcinogenic health risks associated with exposure through consumption by adults. Water samples were collected using standard methods and analyzed according to the guidelines of APHA and WHO.
