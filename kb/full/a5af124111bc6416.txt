This study focuses on the quality of drinking water in the neighborhoods of the municipality of Kassa.The lack of an adequate drinking water supply has a significant impact on the residents’ way of life and health. Our objective is to determine the potability of the water used by the island’s population by analyzing its physicochemical and bacteriological qualities. To this end, we conducted a field survey to identify potential sources of pollution, and then collected water samples for on-site and laboratory analysis. In the field, across the ten areas visited, samples were collected from thirteen wells, two boreholes, and one spring, due to the presence of water and their heavy use by the local population.
