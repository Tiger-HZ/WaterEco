Purpose This paper investigates whether a governance threshold exists in the biofuel production–food security nexus. Design/methodology/approach This paper employs panel threshold regression estimation technique to identify the threshold of governance using a sample of 99 countries for the period from 2012 to 2024. Findings Utilizing an advanced estimation technique of panel threshold regression, this paper identifies the threshold level of governance and determines whether biofuel production has a positive or negative impact on food security given the threshold value of governance. The results reveal that a single threshold value of governance exists in the biofuel production–food security nexus.
