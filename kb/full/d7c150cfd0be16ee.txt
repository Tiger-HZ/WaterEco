Fibroblast migration contributes to the spatial organization of tissue repair and extracellular matrix remodeling, but the transcriptional mechanisms regulating this process under physiologically relevant mechanical conditions remain incompletely understood. We investigated whether GATA4 regulates fibroblast migration in a tissue-of-origin-dependent manner. Cardiac fibroblasts (CFs), lung fibroblasts (LFs), and tail-tip fibroblasts (TTFs) were isolated from neonatal male and female Gata4 fl/fl mice and cultured on 8-kPa polydimethylsiloxane substrates. CF behavior on 8-kPa substrates was first compared with that on conventional tissue-culture plastic.
