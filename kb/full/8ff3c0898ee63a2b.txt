More than 33 skate species have been registered in the Southwestern Atlantic Ocean (SAO) 34°S, where hotspots of the endemic threatened marine chondrichthyans with high scientific and conservation priorities have been detected. The lack of species-specific landing data highlights the importance of biological data-based methods to determine the vulnerability and productivity of skate species. In this work, we estimated the demographic parameters for the skates Atlantoraja castelnaui, Rioraja agassizi, Sympterygia bonapartii, and Zearaja brevicaudata in SAO, by implementing Leslie matrix models.
