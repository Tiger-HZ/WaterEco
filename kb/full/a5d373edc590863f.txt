ABSTRACT The presence of Giardia spp. cysts and Cryptosporidium spp. oocysts in drinking water remains a major public health concern due to their low infectious dose and resistance to chlorination. This study presents a systematic review of dissolved air flotation (DAF), sedimentation, and ultraviolet (UV) disinfection performance. Following PRISMA 2020 guidelines, 40 peer-reviewed articles (1995–2021) were analyzed, yielding 237 datasets for qualitative synthesis. Results indicate that DAF achieves log10 reduction values (LRV) of 1.79–2.8 for Giardia and 1.42–3.7 for Cryptosporidium, heavily influenced by coagulant selection and operating pressure (400–600 kPa).
