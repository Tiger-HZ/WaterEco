Marine plastic pollution has become a major environmental challenge affecting marine biodiversity, ecosystem stability, environmental quality, and human well-being across coastal and oceanic ecosystems. Increasing plastic production, ineffective waste management, urbanization, and transboundary transport processes have accelerated the accumulation of macroplastics, microplastics, and nanoplastics in aquatic environments. This review critically evaluates the major sources, transport pathways, environmental distribution, ecological impacts, socio-economic implications, and mitigation strategies associated with marine plastic contamination.
