Freshwater is a vital resource whose availability, quality, and accessibility have been affected by variability and climate change, population growth, land-use change, social inequality, and socio-environmental conflicts. In the face of these challenges, intersectoral collaboration is required to plan, implement, and monitor actions that ensure water security in Mexico. The aim of this study was to analyze historical water availability and projected surface water trends in Mexico, based on official concession data, hydrological and climate models, as well as spatially explicit maps to support the development of public policies in the country.
