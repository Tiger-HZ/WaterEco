Abstract Synchrotron X-ray diffraction under extreme pressure requires X-ray beams with high positional stability, high photon flux, and beam sizes and photon energies matched to a wide range of experimental conditions. To meet these requirements, a stability-oriented multi-scale X-ray focusing system has been developed for the ID31 high-pressure beamline at the High Energy Photon Source (HEPS). The optical system combines a liquid-nitrogen-cooled pseudo-channel-cut monochromator, compound refractive lenses (CRLs), and Kirkpatrick–Baez (KB) mirrors to provide beam sizes ranging from several tens of micrometers to below 200 nm over a photon-energy range of 20–75 keV.
