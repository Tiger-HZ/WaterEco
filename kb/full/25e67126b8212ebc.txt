Wheat (Triticum aestivum L.) is an important grain and staple food crop, which plays an important role in the economy of Pakistan. The attack of insect pests has a significant impact on plant life and grain yields in wheat crop resulting in a considerable loss of yields. The most important approach to reducing these losses is through production of pest resistant/tolerant varieties. Among the insect pests the aphid Rhopalosiphum padi is a main destructive pests of wheat crop which cause a considerable damage to wheat production in Sindh Pakistan. In the present investigation, various varieties of wheat resistance to R. padi under field condition were studied.
