This scoping review examines environmental pressure clusters and monitoring gaps in the Northwestern Federal District of the Russian Federation during 2014–2025. It asks how persistent industrial and urban pressures can be assessed when post-2022 restrictions on data exchange have weakened transboundary verification. The review follows Arksey and O’Malley’s scoping-review framework, Levac et al.‘s methodological refinement and PRISMA-ScR reporting principles.
