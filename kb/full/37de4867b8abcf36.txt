Abstract The massive algal blooms caused by water body eutrophication pose a serious threat to important surface water bodies such as lakes and reservoirs. Assessing the potential for algal growth in water bodies and monitoring the changes in dissolved oxygen are crucial for preventing such algal blooms. This study has developed an algal bloom warning model by estimating the algae growth potential and monitoring the changes in dissolved oxygen. This model has achieved good predictive results in a certain lake in southern China. The approximate time of algal mass blooms was predicted by calculating the algae growth potential, accurately simulating the algal growth trend, and monitoring changes in dissolved oxygen. Finally, the study summarized the advantages and disadvantages of the model and envisioned its combination with odorant detection, providing a new approach for subsequent odorant monitoring.
