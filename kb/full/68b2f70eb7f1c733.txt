Anthropogenic impacts increasingly involve multi‐factor changes to resource pools and flows, whose outcomes on ecosystem function are difficult to predict. Here, we factorially manipulated drought with eutrophication (nitrogen fertilization) in C 3 and C 4 grassland, testing for impacts on aboveground community‐ and species‐level plant biomass. Drought and eutrophication may be especially detrimental to plant production, with elevated water demand by drought‐stressed and fertilized plants hypothesized to limit growth while potentially triggering insect outbreaks given changes to foliar‐nitrogen (N).
