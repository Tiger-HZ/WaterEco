The Kubuqi Desert, China’s seventh-largest desert, is characterized by a severe water supply–demand imbalance and ecological fragility. This study conducted a multi-dimensional dynamic evaluation of the water resources carrying capacity (WRCC) of the desert and its subordinate banners using the TOPSIS model with game theory-based combined weighting. The main obstacle factors were subsequently identified via the obstacle degree model. The results indicated that the comprehensive WRCC index increased from 0.351 in 2010 to 0.584 in 2023, showing a stepwise upward trend. The WRCC grade gradually shifted from Grade II in 2010 to Grade III in 2023.
