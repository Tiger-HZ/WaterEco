ABSTRACT Graphical summary of the study. Four anaerobic digestion configurations - conventional anaerobic digestion, thermal hydrolysis combined with conventional digestion, an anaerobic membrane bioreactor configuration, and the fully integrated configuration - are evaluated by plant-wide simulation across food-waste mixing ratios. Four indicators (specific methane production, sidestream load to mainstream, greenhouse gas emissions and energy footprint) are normalised and combined into a single composite Sustainability WWTP Index.
