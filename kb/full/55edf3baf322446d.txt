Super hybrid rice is widely cultivated for its high yield potential. However, systematic knowledge of how its grain quality has evolved alongside genetic yield improvement, and how it responds to variety factors, nitrogen (N) management, and climatic conditions, remains limited. Two-year field experiments were conducted at two subtropical sites using three representative super hybrid rice varieties representing different breeding phases and grown under four N application rates. In addition, historical grain quality data from all 136 super hybrid rice varieties released between 2005 and 2023 were analyzed to assess long-term trends associated with breeding progress.
