Introduction Water resources play a crucial role in ecosystem functioning, as they are critical for domestic use, agriculture, and economic development in South Africa. An increase in the frequency and intensity of extreme weather events is one of the commonly anticipated and acknowledged effects of climate change and the El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO), which significantly affects Southern Africa. These extreme weather events can include floods, droughts, and heatwaves, each of which can affect water quality, quantity, and infrastructure. Methodology This review thus examines the extent to which extreme weather events affect water resources through a detailed literature review using the PRISMA method, appraised by a three-tier system.
