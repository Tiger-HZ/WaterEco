ABSTRACT Water environmental governance is assessed using technical indicators, but these do not show whether implementation is visible or credible to stakeholders. This study examines relationships between stakeholder perceptions and objective indicators in China's Yangtze River Economic Belt. A 2023 survey of 551 respondents in 11 jurisdictions was analysed using structural equation modelling. Perceived policy alignment, implementation credibility, and administrative capacity were positively associated with stakeholder-perceived implementation effectiveness, jointly explaining 56.0% of its variance; implementation credibility had the largest estimated direct coefficient.
