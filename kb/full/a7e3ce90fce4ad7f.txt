As the proportion of renewable energy generation continues to increase, the uncertainty and volatility of power system operation have significantly intensified. Traditional load regulation effectiveness evaluation methods based on single indicators cannot fully capture the comprehensive impact of regulation measures on system operating conditions. To address the above issues, this paper proposes a risk-aware intelligent evaluation method for load regulation effectiveness based on multi-source data fusion. First, a multi-source power data fusion framework is constructed to achieve unified modeling of load data, dispatch operation data, and electricity market data.
