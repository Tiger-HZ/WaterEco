Morocco's semi-arid environment, diverse geography, and extensive agricultural practices present serious water erosion problems for the Ouljet Esoultan watershed, a sub-basin of the Beht River. This study evaluates the qualitative and quantitative risks of water erosion using the Erosion Potential Model and the PAP/CAR standards. According to the PAP/CAR technique, high erosion classes make up 11% of the watershed, whereas low to very low erosion classes predominate, making up roughly 73%. The predominant erosion forms (sheet, rill, and gully erosion) collectively affect 76% of the area within the Ouljet Esoultan watershed.
