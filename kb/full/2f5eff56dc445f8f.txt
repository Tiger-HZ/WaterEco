Introduction Groundwater which is the last assumed resort for safe and potable water is now at great risk to contamination due to anthropogenic activities that aids industrialization. In Nigeria, the endless dependent on petroleum products for automobiles, fuel, power generation and the nation’s economy has geometrically proliferated the siting of filling stations all over the countrywhere products sold are stored and channeled through underground storage tanks and pipe networks into the dispenser which lies above the water level. Hence, this study compared the ground water quality in Ogbomoso north and south metropolis.
