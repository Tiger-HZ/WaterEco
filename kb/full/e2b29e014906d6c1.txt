This study evaluated the bioactive, nutritional and sensory attributes of pasta developed from composite flours of wheat and germinated brown rice (GBR). Pasta was prepared using wheat-GBR flour blends at 70:30 and 50:50 ratios, with GBR germinated for 12, 24 and 36 hours and 100% wheat pasta as the control sample. Analyses included total phenolic content (TPC), total flavonoid content (TFC), gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), proximate composition, vitamins, minerals and sensory attributes using standard AOAC and spectrophotometric methods. Results for the bioactive compounds such as TPC (254.58- 338.50 mg/GAE/100 g), TFC (233.56-275.46 mg/QUE/100 g) and GABA (2.38-4.27 mg/100 g), increased significantly (p &lt; 0.05) with germination time.
