Eutrophication, resulting from the environmental impacts of aquaculture activities and the presence of excess nutrients, is particularly significant for aquatic ecosystems. The present study aims to evaluate the eutrophication and trophic status of an aquaculture zone along the southern coast of the Black Sea by employing various indices and comparing the findings with established threshold levels for coastal waters. Water quality parameters-including chlorophyll-a, dissolved oxygen, dissolved inorganic nitrogen, and total phosphorus-were measured at 16 aquaculture production sites located along the southern Black Sea coast.
