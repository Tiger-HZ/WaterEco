Abstract Identifying the environmental factors influencing biotic patterns in large rivers will assist with extrapolating biological monitoring results to broader scale conclusions about river condition. In the present study, we collected macroinvertebrates and physico‐chemical data at 47 shallow‐water (&lt;1‐m deep) sites, including nine sites at major tributary junctions, during summer along the lower Waikato River, North Island, New Zealand. Macroinvertebrate communities were dominated by a few relatively abundant and widespread taxa. Upper site samples were characterised by high relative abundances of Diptera, but the significance of this group declined further downstream where Crustacea became more dominant.
