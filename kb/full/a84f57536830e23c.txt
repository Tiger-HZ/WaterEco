Continuous cultures can provide refined insights on the response of phytoplankton communities to small changes in nutrient flux, however are logistically more challenging to perform at sea than batch cultures. Here we describe the design and successful testing of a new continuous culture system for shipboard experiments using natural phytoplankton communities. Using this system, we studied the effects of nitrate amendments in coastal waters of the Pacific Northwest, and low‐level iron additions in High Nitrate, Low Chlorophyll (HNLC) waters of the subarctic Pacific.
