Tasks related to relational understanding are useful for arguing about basic mathematical structures and relationships, and for encouraging long-term mathematical thinking. It has been determined that learners in primary school have considerable difficulties arguing about these relationships. Based on these findings, a study was conducted in which the written argumentations of tasks targeting relational understanding provided by 72 learners aged ten to thirteen were evaluated. The tasks covered various mathematical topics and were divided into tasks involving pattern continuation, explorative argumentation and persuasive argumentation.
