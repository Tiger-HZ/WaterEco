Accurate hydraulic model calibration is fundamental to the reliable operation of Water Distribution Networks (WDNs). However, simultaneously estimating quasi-static (e.g. pipe roughness) and dynamic (e.g. nodal demands) parameters remains challenging. This study proposes a two-stage hybrid calibration framework based on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs). Offline, a Graph Attention Network (GAT) infers pipe roughness coefficients from sparse data. Online, a Spatio-Temporal Graph Attention Network (STGAT) estimates real-time demand dynamics. Simulation-based validation using a real WDN topology and sensor noise demonstrated high accuracy. During offline calibration, 74.8% of pipes achieved errors below 5. For online calibration, network-level R2 values for nodal-demand estimation remained above 0.93 across evaluated scenarios, including summer peak loads. By separately static and dynamic parameters according to temporal characteristics while partially mitigating their coupling, the framework improves estimation accuracy and responsiveness for smart water systems and digital twins.
