This study examined whether fairness perceptions statistically mediated the curvilinear associations between perceived parental differential affection and control and sibling relationship quality and whether Big Five personality traits moderated these pathways. Participants were 555 undergraduates (54% women; ages 18–25, M = 20.66, SD = 1.66) who reported on parental differential treatment, fairness perceptions, personality, and sibling relationship quality. Fairness perceptions mediated the associations between both forms of differential treatment and sibling relationship quality, with relative equality generally associated with better relationships.
