This study investigated the impact of water to binder ratios on the strength properties of Anadara granosa shells incorporated concrete. Concrete cubes, cylinders and beams were casted, cured for 7, 14 and 28 days and were tested. Sand's fineness modulus (FM) of 3.61, uniformity curvature (UC) of 2.78 and uniformity coefficient (UC) of 4.3; the coarse aggregate's FM of 4.69, UC of 1.4 and UC of 3.33. The FM, UC and UC for AGS were 4.5, 3.33 and 3.11 with specific gravity of 2.6, 2.9 and 2.5 for sand, cement and AGS. The concrete's workability was ascertained via Slump test. The density result of 2641.98kg/m3 shows that the concrete is a lightweight concrete.
