In the majority of lumped water balance models of river basins, groundwater is represented by a single reservoir, and groundwater discharge is described by a single‐ valued function of storage in the reservoir. It is demonstrated that single‐valued storage‐ discharge functions are often incapable of representing the actual storage‐discharge characteristics of a river basin. An alternative discharge function is proposed which is derived from a solution to the one‐dimensional saturated flow equation for an idealized hillslope with spatially varying physical properties. The saturated flow in the whole river basin is, however, generally better described by a two‐dimensional flow equation.
