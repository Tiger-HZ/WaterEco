The Mekong Delta of Vietnam is one of Southeast Asia’s most important food-producing regions, supporting intensive rice cultivation, globally significant aquaculture, and diverse inland and rice-field fisheries that contribute to food security, rural livelihoods, and ecosystem functioning. However, hydraulic engineering, agricultural intensification, aquaculture expansion, urbanization, upstream river development, and climate-related pressures have progressively altered the hydrological and ecological processes sustaining these interconnected systems.
