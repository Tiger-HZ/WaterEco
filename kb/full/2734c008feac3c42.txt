This study examined long-term changes in floristic composition and hydrological conditions following reforestation in the Yangju Forest Watershed Experimental Catchment, Republic of Korea. The analysis integrated repeated floristic surveys spanning approximately 50 years’ hydrological record from the same experimental catchment. Floristic surveys conducted at 9, 19, 26, 40, and 50 years after reforestation were analyzed using taxonomic and functional-group richness, Sørensen similarity, and zeta diversity, while streamflow records from 1984–2019 were evaluated using flow-duration curves (FDCs) and hydrological indices.
