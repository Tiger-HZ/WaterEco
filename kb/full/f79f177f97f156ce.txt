ABSTRACT Microbial diversity affects marine biogeochemical cycles and sustainability. However, the processes and feedbacks that control the spatial and temporal trends in species diversity and functional gene diversity remain largely unknown at the global marine scale. We analysed 1582 marine metagenomic samples and 19 variables related to marine climate and microbial community dynamics. Over the past 12 years, marine microbial species diversity has significantly decreased across the surface ocean, especially in the Pacific. The greatest decrease reached 24.29% in the central region of the North Pacific Ocean (NPO).
