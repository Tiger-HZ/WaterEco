Eutrophication triggered by excessive nutrient loading has become a major driver of lake degradation in many parts of the world. Lake Rawapening is one of Indonesia’s national priority lakes for environmental restoration and has experienced eutrophic conditions progressing toward a hypertrophic state due to high phosphorus loads (P-load) and total phosphorus concentrations (TP-C). Despite the implementation of lake rehabilitation programs, their effectiveness in improving trophic status has not been quantitatively evaluated, limiting stakeholders’ understanding of the outcomes. This study evaluates the effectiveness of rehabilitation interventions in controlling the trophic state of Lake Rawapening using a calibrated SWAT hydrological model.
