X-band rainfall radar systems offer advantages such as high spatial resolution, flexible deployment options, and strong near-surface detection capabilities. However, due to the short electromagnetic wavelength in this band, the radar base data are highly susceptible to multiple coupled factors—including clutter from mountain vegetation, tall buildings, and other terrain features; electromagnetic interference from surrounding radio-frequency equipment; beam obstruction caused by complex topography; and attenuation of rainfall intensity along the precipitation path—resulting in pronounced distortion of raw echoes.
