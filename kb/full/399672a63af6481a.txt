Water scarcity and soil salinization constrain sustainable alfalfa (Medicago sativa L.) production in arid Xinjiang, China. Although drip irrigation and microbial inoculants improve soil quality and crop productivity, their synergistic effects on rhizosphere ecosystems remain unclear. This study investigated the effects of drip irrigation combined with composite microbial inoculants on rhizosphere soil properties, fungal communities, and alfalfa yield. A two-factor randomized block experiment was conducted with four irrigation levels (6450, 7050, 7650, and 8250 m3/ha) and three inoculant rates (0, 0.5, and 1.0 m3/ha), with conventional irrigation (9750 m3/ha) as the control.
