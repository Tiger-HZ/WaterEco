Abstract This study evaluated the potential of Gliricidia sepium (GS) hay to replace cottonseed cake (CSC) as a dietary protein source for growing Bunaji bulls by assessing blood metabolites, rumen fermentation characteristics, nutrient digestibility and nitrogen balance. Twenty growing Bunaji bulls (110.5 ± 2.0 kg) were randomly assigned to four dietary treatments in which GS replaced CSC at 0, 25, 50 and 75% of the protein source. The experiment lasted 56 days, comprising a 14-day adaptation period and a 42-day feeding trial, followed by a digestibility and nitrogen balance study. Blood samples were collected on days 0, 28 and 56, while rumen fluid was sampled before feeding and at 4 and 8 h post-feeding.
