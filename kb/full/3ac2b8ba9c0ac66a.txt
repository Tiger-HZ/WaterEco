This Research investigated the pollution indices of river Onyongo in both wet and dry seasons (2023 to 2024). Physicochemical properties and microbial quality content of the water were investigated at three different points namely Ogbala, Oyobi and Ubeke respectively using standard laboratory procedures and heavy metal were analysed using Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer (AAS). Physicochemical properties varied significantly (P&lt;0.05) with season and locations and were within permissible limits except Phosphate (0.65mg/ml) and Nitrate (11.1mg/L) that exceeded permissible limits (0.3 and 3.0mg/L).
