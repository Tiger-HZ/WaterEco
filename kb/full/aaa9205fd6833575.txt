Portfolio assessment can provide authentic documentary evidence of pre-service teachers’ development, but limited evidence explains how reflective growth, learning quality, and professional readiness are manifested together in Bachelor of Technology and Livelihood Education (BTLED) portfolios in the Philippine context. This study employed qualitative document analysis to examine the completed teaching portfolios of seven fourth-year BTLED pre-service teachers from Ifugao State University–Potia Campus. A researcher-adapted coding matrix organized documentary evidence into three dimensions reflective growth, learning quality, and professional readiness using four indicators per dimension and a four-level evidence scale from Emerging (E1) to Advanced (E4).
