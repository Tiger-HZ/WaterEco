Bioaccumulative substances challenge conventional aquatic life water quality criteria because their ecological risks are driven primarily by internal tissue residues and food-web transfer rather than dissolved concentrations in water. This review synthesizes current understanding of their bioaccumulation mechanisms, tissue-specific distribution, and trophic transfer behavior in aquatic ecosystems. On this basis, we critically review existing tissue-residue–based water quality criteria approaches, including tissue-based criteria (TBC), tissue residue guidelines (TRGs), and environmental quality standards in biota (EQSbiota), with emphasis on their conceptual frameworks, data requirements, and regulatory applications.
