Abstract Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) indicator 6.4.2 reports water stress for almost every country yet compares countries sitting at very different points of its range. This study asks whether a standard drought index moves the indicator the same way in all of them. Two nested country panels are used, and estimation proceeds nonparametrically, with parametric fixed effects kept only as a reference point. The relationship is found to depend on the scale of a country. In larger countries it is negative and significant, consistent with drought raising water stress. In the smallest countries, and particularly small island states, it is weak, inconsistently signed, or absent. Land area and total population cannot be separated as the operative dimension.
