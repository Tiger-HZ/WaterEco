This study assesses the spatio-temporal evolution of the quality of groundwater intended for irrigation in the coastal aquifer of the Bay of Algiers (northern Algeria) during three sampling campaigns conducted in 2006, 2020 and 2025. An integrated approach combining physicochemical parameters, the main irrigation water quality indices, hydrochemical diagrams, multivariate statistical analysis and geographic information systems was used to characterise the controlling hydrochemical processes and assess the suitability of the water for irrigation. The results reveal a progressive deterioration in water quality, marked by increases in electrical conductivity, total dissolved solids, sodium and chloride.
