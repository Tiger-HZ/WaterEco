The study assesses the food hygiene practices among food vendors at the Tsohuwar Kasuwa market in Azare, Katagum Local Government Area, Bauchi State. Food hygiene is a measure that ensures the safety and quality of food throughout its handling process. These practices prevent food poisoning. It is associated with the prevention and preservation of health and healthy living. A descriptive survey was adopted; the target population for the study comprises all food vendors in Tsohuwar Kasuwa market, which is 100. The entire population was used as a sample, and the instrument for data collection was a structured questionnaire. The instrument used was a structured questionnaire developed by the researcher. The instrument was validated and established by the two experts, and errors were corrected
