This study examined the effect of eye health challenges on audit quality performance in Nigeria. The study was motivated by the growing use of digital technologies in auditing, which requires auditors to spend long hours working with computers, financial statements, spreadsheets, electronic records, and other digital materials. The study focused on digital eye strain, visual fatigue, uncorrected vision problems, eye discomfort, and eye-care practices and their implications for audit quality. A quantitative survey design was adopted. Out of 520 questionnaires administered to auditors and accounting professionals, 400 were returned.
