In this paper we examine the potential role of light limitation in the regulation of phytoplankton standing crop in Florida's largest river, the St. Johns. We hypothesized that spatial and temporal patterns in standing crops of phytoplankton are strongly affected by variations in light availability in the mixed layer, particularly as they relate to river basin morphology and changes in water color, which reach high levels in the St. Johns River.
