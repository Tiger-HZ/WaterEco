Nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) influx into receiving water bodies leads to cultural eutrophication and, therefore, poses a major environmental challenge. Consequently, increasingly stringent discharge limits have been established, necessitating advanced wastewater treatment methods. In addition, phosphorus is a finite resource, with current reserves projected to last less than a century. The principles of the circular economy therefore emphasize not only the removal of N and P from wastewater, but also their recovery and reuse as fertilizers.
