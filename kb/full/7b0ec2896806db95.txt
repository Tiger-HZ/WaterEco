Air pollution is an emerging environmental health concern in rapidly urbanizing regions of developing countries. This study assessed ambient air pollution levels and associated health risks in selected communities of Abia State, southeastern Nigeria. Field monitoring was conducted across multiple sampling locations representing residential, commercial, and traffic-dominated environments. Concentrations of major atmospheric pollutants including particulate matter (PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀), carbon monoxide (CO), carbon dioxide (CO₂), nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), hydrogen sulphide (H₂S), and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) were Introduction measured using portable air quality monitoring devices.
