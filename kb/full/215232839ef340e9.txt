Abstract With rapid urbanization, population growth, and excessive water withdrawal from groundwater, the issue of groundwater depletion has become a critical concern in Lahore, Pakistan. This study evaluates groundwater depletion at the local scale using satellite observations and ground-based data. The methodology employed included well water data from 220 sites over the period 2000 to 2019, obtained from the Water and Sanitation Agency (WASA); spatial interpolation with Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW) using ArcGIS; and Mann-Kendall trend analysis. International Centre for Global Earth Models (ICGEM) satellite data from the Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment (GRACE) were also used to understand groundwater variations.
