Abstract The Araguaia River Basin (ARB), located in Brazil and positioned at the transition between the Cerrado and Amazon biomes, stands out for its socioeconomic, environmental, and cultural importance. The region contains extensive irrigated agricultural areas and numerous communities that depend directly on its water resources. This study evaluated the Water Stress Index (WSI) in the ARB by harmonizing the distinct water-allocation criteria adopted by the different states that comprise the basin, integrating heterogeneous definitions of allowable withdrawal limits and regulatory thresholds into a unified analytical framework.
