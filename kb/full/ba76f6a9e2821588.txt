Plant-assisted synthesis offers a lower-hazard route to zinc oxide (ZnO) nanomaterials, yet the amount of plant extract can simultaneously alter nucleation, crystal growth, surface passivation, aggregation, and optical response. In this study, ZnO powders were prepared by alkaline precipitation from zinc acetate using 0, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 mL of Aloe vera inner-leaf gel extract, followed by drying and calcination at 350 °C. The extract is interpreted principally as a phytochemical complexing, growth-regulating, and surface-capping medium rather than as a conventional reducing reagent for Zn(II).
