Abstract O 3 generation in atmospheric air and O 2 plasmas poses a significant hindrance to the design and development of atmospheric plasma devices used for the preparation of plasma activated water (PAW). Notably, these devices do not operate in a sealed chamber. In this study, water is activated by N 2 -based instead of O 2 -based spark discharge over water to generate PAW at atmospheric pressure. Our analysis demonstrates that the biological reactivity of N 2 nonthermal PAW (N 2 -PAW) is much higher than that of O 2 nonthermal PAW (O 2 -PAW). E. coli, with their concentration of 1.8 × 10 6 CFU ml −1 , are completely inactivated in N 2 -PAW following a plasma treatment time of ⩾40 s.
