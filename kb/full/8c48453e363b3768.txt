Background YouTube is one of the most widely accessed digital platforms for health-related information, particularly among children exposed to animated content. However, the educational quality of cartoon-based videos on children’s tooth brushing remains uncertain. This study evaluated the educational value and content characteristics of YouTube cartoon videos related to children’s tooth brushing. Methods A cross-sectional content analysis was conducted on the first 200 videos retrieved on December 28, 2025, using the keywords “children,” “tooth brushing,” and “cartoon.” After applying inclusion and exclusion criteria, 109 videos were included.
