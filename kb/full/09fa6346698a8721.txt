The study evaluated sweet potato varieties to identify better cultivars for cultivation and its position regarding food security in the area. The varieties used were (Local-Kpukunduku, mother’s delight, king J and Tis 87/0087). They were planted in a land area of 500m2 on experimental units of 5mx5m. It was laid out in a Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD) with three replicates. Parameters used for evaluation are vine length, number of nodes and leaves collected at two weeks intervals from ten plants on each experimental unit and yield measured after harvest. Data collected were subjected to statistical analysis using analysis of variance (ANOVA) and means separated using Duncan multiple range test (DMRT) at (p&lt; 0.05).
