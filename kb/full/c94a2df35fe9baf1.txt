ABSTRACT Water pollution, intensified by anthropogenic activities, compromises aquatic ecosystems, especially through eutrophication, which favors the proliferation of microcystin‐LR‐producing cyanobacteria (MC‐LR). We conducted a systematic and narrative literature review involving 72 studies on the effects of MC‐LR on fish and 105 on remediation methods, aiming to understand the ecotoxicological impacts of this cyanotoxin and evaluate mitigation strategies. Our results show that most research is conducted under laboratory conditions, mainly with Danio rerio .
