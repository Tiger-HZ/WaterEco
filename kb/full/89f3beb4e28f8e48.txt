Abstract The effects of dairy product fatty acids on human health, especially cardiovascular diseases, have come to light in recent decades. On the hand, as is well known, fermented milk products such as yoghurt offer several nutritional and health benefits, which has led to an increase in their consumption worldwide. Therefore, this work was established to study the starter activity, fatty acids composition and lipid health quality indices of Greek yogurt made from different types of milk using a probiotic starter culture and transglutaminase enzyme. Five Greek yoghurt treatments were prepared using milk from cows, buffalo, camels, goats, and sheep that had been fortified with 8% milk protein concentrate.
