Background Viral infections and sleep disturbance may be associated with immune dysregulation, but their relationship with baseline immune status in treatment-naïve breast cancer remains unclear. We investigated associations of viral infection history, including cutaneous warts, and sleep quality with peripheral lymphocyte counts. Methods This cross-sectional study included 100 women with pathologically confirmed breast cancer who had received no antitumor therapy. Sleep quality was assessed using the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. Data on psychological status, systemic viral infections, cutaneous warts, and absolute lymphocyte counts were collected.
