Water hardness is an important water-quality parameter that can affect domestic water use and household activities, particularly in rapidly urbanising areas. This study assessed residents’ perceptions of the impacts of water hardness and anthropogenic activities contributing to hardness in selected riverine communities of Port Harcourt Metropolis, Rivers State, Nigeria. A structured questionnaire covering socio-demographic characteristics, domestic water use, perceived challenges of hard water, and awareness of human activities affecting water quality was administered to residents in 15 communities along three selected rivers. A total of 300 questionnaires were distributed, with 20 administered randomly in each community, and 277 were successfully retrieved, giving a 92.33% response rate.
