This study compared Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometry (AAS) and X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) for determining trace heavy metals (Pb, Fe, Co, Ag, Ti, Cr, Cu, V, Zn, Mn, Ni) in Oguta Lake, Nigeria, during dry and rainy seasons. A total of 18 water samples (3 sites × 2 seasons × 3 replicates) were analyzed. AAS exhibited superior sensitivity and reproducibility, while XRF offered faster throughput and lower costs. A significant discrepancy was observed for Cu, where XRF reported inflated values due to matrix effects. Concentrations of Pb, Fe, and Zn measured by AAS exceeded WHO permissible limits for drinking water, indicating ecological and health risks.
