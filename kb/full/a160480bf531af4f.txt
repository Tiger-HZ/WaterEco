Climate change and population growth are driving the increasing demand for alternative water sources in agriculture. Nutrient-rich treated municipal wastewater emerges as a potential alternative for agriculture production. Therefore, a pot experiment using zeolite as the growing substrate to simulate hydroponic conditions was conducted to assess the impacts of fully treated municipal wastewater and partially treated wastewater that undergone only mechanical treatment step on basil ( Ocimum basilicum cv. Edwina) production, aiming to explore the potential of nutrient recovery from these wastewater sources.
