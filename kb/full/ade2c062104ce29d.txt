Water resource management has become an increasingly critical issue due to rapid urbanization, population growth, climate variability, and industrial expansion. Ensuring a sustainable water supply requires not only effective distribution but also accurate prediction of future water demand and continuous monitoring of water quality. In this study, we explore the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) models to predict water consumption patterns based on historical data and influential factors such as population dynamics, temperature fluctuations, and rainfall variability.
