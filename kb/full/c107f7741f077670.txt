Background: Access to microbiologically safe water is essential for the prevention of waterborne diseases. In many communities, wells and rivers remain important sources of domestic water, but these sources may be vulnerable to microbial contamination.Objective: This study assessed the microbial contamination and public-health quality of well and river water sources in North Bank, Makurdi, Benue State, Nigeria.Methods: A total of 200 water samples were examined, comprising 100 samples each from well and river sources. Samples were assessed for microbial contamination, bacterial isolates, and faecal indicator organisms.
