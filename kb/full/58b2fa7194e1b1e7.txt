Purpose: The objective of this study was to report alcohol-triggered periocular flushing during treatment with tacrolimus 0.1% ophthalmic solution instilled into the conjunctival sac for Sjögren-related ocular surface disease. Methods: A 46-year-old woman with severe dry eye disease refractory to standard therapy was treated with tacrolimus 0.1% ophthalmic solution, instilled twice daily, into the inferior conjunctival fornix of both eyes. Clinical response, onset of periocular symptoms, and their temporal relationship with alcohol ingestion were evaluated, excluding alternative causes of flushing.
