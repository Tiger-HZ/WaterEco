This study investigates the reservoir properties and source–reservoir system types of the Upper Permian Dalong Formation shale in Western Hubei (Middle Yangtze region) through core observations and systematic experiments, including total organic carbon (TOC) analysis, X-ray diffraction (XRD), field emission scanning electron microscopy (FE-SEM), low-pressure gas adsorption, and methane isothermal adsorption. The Dalong Formation shale is dominated by organic-rich siliceous facies, with average quartz and TOC content of approximately 60% and 6.16%, respectively. Organic matter (OM) pores are the most abundant pore type, and the average porosity is 2.75%.
