Background: No studies have examined the effect of artificial intelligence (AI)-generated hospital courses on discharge summary documentation time. Objectives: To evaluate the effect of an AI-generated hospital course tool on discharge summary edit time, quality, and clinician perceptions. Methods: Observational study between a “Pre-AI period” (March 16, 2025 to November 19, 2025) and a “Post-AI period” (November 20, 2025 to February 11, 2026). An AI hospital course tool using a GPT 4.1 model to generate a draft hospital course integrated into mandatory discharge summary templates on November 20, 2025.
