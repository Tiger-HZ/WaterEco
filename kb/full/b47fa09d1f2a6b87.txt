Fluoride contamination in surface and groundwater was a major environmental issue in various parts of the world. Fluorosis is caused by consumption of contaminated water; hence, it is essential to treat fluoride from contaminated water sources. This study involves the development of nano-alumina functionalized carbon composite adsorbent (NAAC) nanocomposite under controlled atmosphere for adsorption of fluoride from water. NAAC was synthesized using carbonization process at controlled nitrogen atmosphere. The X-ray diffraction (XRD), (High Resolution Transmission Electron Microscopy) HR-TEM coupled with (Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy) EDS and (Selected Area Electron Diffraction) SAED were used for characterization of the NAAC.
