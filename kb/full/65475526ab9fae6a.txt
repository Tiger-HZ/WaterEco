ABSTRACT The dried rhizome of Polygonati Rhizoma, known as Huangjing, is commonly used clinically in both its raw and wine‐processed forms. However, the distinctions in quality between them remain unclear, and the methods for identifying them are considerably intricate. This study aimed to analyze the differences in color and changes in key components between raw and wine‐processed Polygonati Rhizoma, to reveal the correlation between the color and chemical components of Polygonati Rhizoma before and after processing, and to provide a reference for the identification and quality evaluation of Polygonati Rhizoma processed products.
