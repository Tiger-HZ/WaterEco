Water quality indices are generally developed for specific water uses, but direct comparisons of indices designed for public water supply and irrigation using the same monitoring dataset remain limited. This study evaluated surface water quality in the Federal District (FD), Brazil, assessing its suitability for public drinking water supply and irrigation under different land uses. Eighteen sampling sites across rural, urban, and natural hydrographic units were monitored bimonthly from December 2017 to October 2019. A total of 12 sampling campaigns were conducted, resulting in 12 samples per site and 216 samples overall.
