Effective management of degraded urban water bodies requires spatially dense, low cost, timely water quality monitoring and decision making system. Yet conventional laboratory monitoring remains too costly and time consuming for most cities. This study develops and validates a Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI) framework integrating consumer smartphone multi-color-space imaging with Random Forest regression, SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) explainability, Random Forest Spatial Interpolation (RFSI), and a governance centered Decision Error Analysis for urban lake water quality mapping.
