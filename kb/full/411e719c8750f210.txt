The present investigation was conducted during June to February for two consecutive years i.e., 2024–25 and 2025–26, in the Department of Fruit Science, Chandra Shekhar Azad University of Agriculture and Technology, Kanpur, to evaluate the influence of integrated nutrient management (INM) on soil nutrient status and economics of guava (Psidium guajava L.) cv. L-49.
