Nitrogen runoff from agricultural lands has emerged as a critical environmental and public health issue, contributing to widespread eutrophication, hypoxic zones, and contaminated drinking water. The Midwestern United States—home to extensive corn and soybean production—relies heavily on nitrogen-based fertilizers, yet over half of the applied nitrogen is lost to runoff. Addressing this challenge, we present the Nitrogen Effluence Separation and Treatment (NEST) system: a modular, dual-phase filtration technology designed for retrofitting into existing tile drainage infrastructure.
