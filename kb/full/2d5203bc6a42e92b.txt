Fish are increasingly threatened by multiple anthropogenic stressors, with eutrophication representing a particularly important driver of community change. Nevertheless, fish community responses to eutrophication remain poorly understood in highly urbanized drinking‐water reservoirs of southern China. Traditional survey methods based on morphological identification are time‐consuming, inefficient, and challenging for large‐scale monitoring. Environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding offers a rapid and reliable alternative for aquatic biodiversity assessment. In this study, eDNA metabarcoding was used to investigate fish communities in 27 drinking‐water reservoirs in southern China during the dry and wet seasons of year 2021–2022.
