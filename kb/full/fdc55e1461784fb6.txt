Abstract Burosumab is effective in improving rickets in children with X-linked hypophosphatemia (XLH). Predictors of health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in pediatric XLH patients treated with burosumab are unknown. In this cross-sectional analysis of a prospective binational observational study, we investigated HRQoL in 64 pediatric XLH patients (36 female) on burosumab treatment, using the KIDSCREEN-52 questionnaire and qualitative interviews. Associations between HRQoL and clinical findings were analysed using multiple regression and receiver operating characteristics (ROC) curve analyses.
