The authors present research results on the Polezhaevka River water chemical composition of January 2026. They show variations in dissolved solid concentrations, due to significant differences in the groundwater, wastewater, water from deteriorated water supply, and wastewater systems chemical composition. It has been found that the waters composition is hydrocarbonate-calcium, revealed increased mineralization values, dominance of chlorides over sulfates, and water pollution with nitrite and ammonium nitrogen.
