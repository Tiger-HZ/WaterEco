This study investigated the effect of waste management on surface water quality in the Cosine and Chinda rivers, located in the Obio/Akpor Local Government Area of Rivers State, Nigeria. The objective was to examine the types of solid waste generated, evaluate the disposal techniques employed by the local water authority, and identify the implications for water quality in both rivers. A mixed-methods approach combining structured questionnaires and statistical analysis was adopted, and 400 respondents were surveyed. Descriptive statistics revealed that both rivers had similar solid waste compositions, with organic waste being the most dominant, followed by plastics, paper, metals, and hazardous materials.
