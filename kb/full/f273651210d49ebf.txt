A successful transition to a circular economy of water requires the integration of infrastructure modernization, wastewater reuse, resource recovery, governance coordination, economic incentives and public acceptance. This review assesses the status of circular economy policies and implementation barriers in Romania’s water sector, with emphasis on wastewater reuse and the transition from linear water management to circular water systems. We apply a structured qualitative policy review and document-analysis approach, complemented by descriptive quantitative indicators, cross-country comparison and illustrative scenario calculations.
