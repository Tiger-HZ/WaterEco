Analyzing the spatial distribution characteristics of water environmental quality and its driving mechanisms is essential for developing effective watershed management strategies, which is also a key application of intelligent earth observation in remote sensing. This study leverages advanced Artificial Intelligence technology (AI), specifically the Geographically Weighted Random Forest (GWRF) model, to refine the estimation of water quality by integrating monitoring data from national and provincial control sections with geographic information. A high-resolution (30m) water quality map of Sichuan Province was generated, focusing on total phosphorus (TP) and permanganate index (CODMn). Results show TP is dominated by Class II (66.2%) water bodies, while CODMn is primarily Class I (51.3%). TP concentrations are high in the Minjiang and Tuojiang River basins, influenced by temperature, elevation, and cropland proportion, while CODMn is concentrated in the central Sichuan Basin, driven by cropland and elevation. These findings offer valuable insights for optimizing watershed pollution control and precise management strategies.
