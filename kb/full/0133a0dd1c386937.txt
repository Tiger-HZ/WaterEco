Introduction Loneliness in later life is closely linked to older adults' living environments and psychosocial resources. Although digital inclusion has received increasing attention in aging societies, less is known about whether perceived healthy community environments remain relevant to loneliness among older adults. This study examined whether community belongingness (CB) and multi-source received social support (MRSS) formed indirect psychosocial pathways linking perceived healthy community environment (PHCE) to loneliness among older adults in China. Methods Data were drawn from a face-to-face cross-sectional survey conducted across 21 communities in Wuxi, China. The analytical sample included 469 adults aged 60 years or older.
