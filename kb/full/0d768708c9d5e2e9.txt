Abstract To address the challenges of signal attenuation, source signal mixing, and inaccurate localization in multi-source partial discharge (PD) monitoring of gas-insulated switchgear, a distributed fluorescent optical fiber sensing method based on dual-end sensing, signal separation, and a region-wise localization model is proposed. First, a dual-end optical coupling strategy is developed to improve signal transmission efficiency and provide multi-port sensing information for subsequent localization. Then, a signal separation method is employed to decouple overlapping PD signals under dual-source discharge conditions.
