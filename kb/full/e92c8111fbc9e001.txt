This chapter discusses performance indicators, evaluation models, and digital security functions influencing quality in smart city-centered systems from a Quality 4.0 viewpoint. It discusses how the traditional static, compliance-focused quality metrics are being supplanted by data-driven dynamic systems. This chapter contributes to a systemic narrative where evaluation is framed as a dynamic, data-intensive activity that connects monitoring, predictive analytics, and adaptive governance mechanisms. Special emphasis is put on the importance of data governance, digital security, and transparency as essential determinants of systemic performance and public trust in urban settings relying heavily on data. This analysis highlights a crucial interdependence among data quality, decision-making processes, and general smart city system efficacy in an increasingly complex world. The uniqueness of this chapter can be found in framing smart city technologies as a support system for data-driven quality management, where reliability, quality, and governance of data directly affect the quality of management. Results indicate that the effectiveness of Quality 4.0 systems is contingent not only on technological capabilities but also on structural features concerning security, transparency, and trust within urban governance generally.
