The surrounding rock-support system of an ultra-deep shaft exhibits pronounced time-dependent mechanical behavior under conditions of high in situ stress and groundwater pressure. To investigate the stability of the surrounding rock under the combined effects of non-hydrostatic in situ stress and groundwater pressure, a mechanical model of the surrounding rock-support system of an ultra-deep shaft was established by considering the creep behavior and installation timing of the support structure, and the corresponding viscoelastic analytical solution was derived. The validity of the proposed solution was verified through numerical calculations, and the effects of the key parameters on the mechanical response of the system were further analyzed.
