Climate change, rising demand, pollution, and ecosystem degradation are intensifying pressures on water resources and increasing interest in nature-based solutions (NbS). This systematic literature review examines water-related NbS and implementation actors, drivers, and barriers. A Scopus search covered English- and Spanish-language publications from 2010 to 2024, with reporting informed by the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA). Of 186 records initially retrieved, 53 publications from 2017 to 2024 were retained. Descriptive bibliometric analysis and qualitative categorization, supported by VantagePoint version 15.2, identified 12 thematic categories, six driver dimensions, and seven barrier dimensions.
