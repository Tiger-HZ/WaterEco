Abstract Late gestational nutrient restriction of beef females causes body store mobilization and decreases colostrum production, independent of whether fetal growth is reduced. Despite this, effects of fetal genetic growth potential on nutrient partitioning to the gravid uterus and mammary gland in nutritionally-stressed beef cows are unknown.
