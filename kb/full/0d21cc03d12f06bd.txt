Abstract Straw returning to the field enhances crop yield and cultivated land quality while presenting risks of water quality degradation, particularly during the soaking period. In this study, a barrel planting experiment was used, three treatments (CK: no straw returning, ST: straw returning, SO: straw returning + micro–nano aeration irrigation) were set up to study the dynamic changes of surface water, soil solution (0–10, 10–20, and 20–30 cm), and key water quality indices [total nitrogen (TN), ammonia nitrogen ( NH 4 + - N ), nitrate nitrogen ( NO 3 − - N ), total phosphorus (TP), chemical oxygen demand (COD), dissolved oxygen (DO), pH] in the rice soaking period.
