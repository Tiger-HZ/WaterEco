This study aimed to provide basic data needed for the development of beauty and health care behavior programs with the goal of improving the QoL of modern adults. Specifically, it examined the effects of beauty and health care behaviors on quality of life, with PPA serving as a mediating factor. For the purpose of this study, questionnaire data were collected from adult men and women nationwide using both online and offline survey methods. In total, 385 completed questionnaires were retained and analyzed in the final stage of the study. The results are as follows: Beauty and health care behaviors and PPA were found to significantly contribute to QoL. In the relationship between beauty and health care behaviors and QoL, PPA showed a partial mediating effect. These findings indicate that beauty and health care behaviors are variables that influence QoL both directly and indirectly. The above results have academic significance and are expected to provide basic data needed for the development of beauty and health care behavior programs aimed at enhancing QoL.
