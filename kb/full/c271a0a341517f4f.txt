Abstract. Eutrophication poses a profound threat to the provisioning of ecosystem services in lakes worldwide. Understanding how eutrophication, alongside other biotic and abiotic factors, drives sediment nitrogen removal is essential for maintaining the intrinsic purification capacity of ecosystems and safeguarding water security. To address this, we conducted a systematic investigation of 17 representative lakes across the Eastern China Plain using 15N stable isotope tracing, 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing, and metagenomics profiling. This study aims to quantify the contribution of the anammox to nitrogen removal, elucidate the impacts of eutrophication on overall nitrogen removal, and identify the critical biotic and abiotic factors driving these processes.
