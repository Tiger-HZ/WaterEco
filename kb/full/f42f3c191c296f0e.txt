Abstract Global warming threatens the viability of tropical coral reefs and associated marine calcifiers, including symbiont‐bearing larger benthic foraminifera (LBF). The impacts of current climate change on LBF are debated because they were particularly diverse and abundant during past warm periods. Studies on the responses of selected LBF species to changing environmental conditions reveal varying results. Based on a comprehensive review of the scientific literature on LBF species occurrences, we applied species distribution modeling using Maxent to estimate present‐day and future species richness patterns on a global scale for the time periods 2040–2050 and 2090–2100.
