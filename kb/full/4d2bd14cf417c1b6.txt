Abstract In many cases, shallow coastal lagoons are, on the one hand, vulnerable habitats for birds and marine ecosystems and, on the other hand, threatened by discharging nutrient-laden surface waters and groundwater. In particular, the localization and quantification of submarine groundwater discharge (SGD) is of key concern in this regard. The presented study aimed at investigating SGD into a vulnerable coastal lagoon that is strongly impacted by evaporation applying a multi-tracer approach. The joint application of radionuclides ( 222 Rn, 223 Ra, 224 Ra), stable water isotopes (δ 18 O, δ 2 H) and the water salinity as environmental water tracers allowed evaluating the suitability of the individual parameters in this specific type of environment.
