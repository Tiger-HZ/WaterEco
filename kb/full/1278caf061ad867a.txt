ABSTRACT This study was conducted in the Mula‐Mutha River Basin (MMRB), located in the northern Western Ghats of Maharashtra, India, to assess whether monsoon‐driven floods (seasonal hydrological variability) and anthropogenic footprints (spatial environmental gradients) exert stronger control on diatom assemblages. To achieve this, diatom and water samples were collected across three hydrological seasons (pre‐monsoon, monsoon and post‐monsoon) over 2 years (2022 and 2023).
