Eastern Mediterranean region is characterized by water resources scarcity while climate change is expected to has adverse impact on them. Shortage of water resources requires their rational management to meet the water needs. The water resources in the island of Crete, Greece are studied in light of climate change analyzing their supply and the use of nonconventional water resources. The increase in tourism industry and the decrease in precipitation due to climate change in the future is going to affect the water supply and demand in the island. The study has been conducted reviewing the existing literature. It is indicated that Crete under normal meteorological conditions is water sufficient while water consumption per capita is significantly higher compared to other Mediterranean islands
