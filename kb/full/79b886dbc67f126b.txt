Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), often described as ‘forever chemicals,’ are persistent contaminants that resist many conventional water-treatment processes. This qualitative documentary study examines electrochemical degradation and defluorination as approaches for sustainable PFAS remediation. An interpretivist and exploratory design was used to synthesize evidence from peer-reviewed research, review articles, authoritative environmental reports, and relevant institutional and regulatory documents.
