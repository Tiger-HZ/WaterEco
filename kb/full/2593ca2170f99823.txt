The objective of this study was to formulate dark chocolate using commercial cocoa powder and replacing cocoa butter with vegetable fat powder (Allanblackia seed oil base). The physicochemical properties of the cocoa and vegetable fat powder (Colour, pH, Degree of fineness, Moisture, Fat, Ash, Protein and Carbohydrates) and the characteristic quality of dark chocolate (Moisture content, Melting point, Hardness and Color) were evaluated using standard methods.
