Global climate change is reshaping the hydrological regimes in arid Central Asia, where water resources depend heavily on glacier melt, snowmelt, and rainfall. Compared with well-studied high-mountain regions such as the Himalaya and the Alps, hydrological responses in Central Asia remain insufficiently synthesized. This review summarizes recent advances in climate change, moisture transport, glacier and snow dynamics, runoff changes, and related risks under continued warming. During 1960–2024, air temperature rose by ~0.33 °C per decade, while precipitation increased modestly by ~2.3 mm per decade.
