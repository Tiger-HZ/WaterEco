Quality assurance in higher education is not just a technical process of assessing standards in the contemporary education system, but a governance process linked to the impact of government capacity, policy implementation, and institutional coordination. In Afghanistan, despite the existence of formal policies, standards, and evaluations for quality assurance, there is still a significant gap between these mechanisms and the actual quality of higher education. This study aims to assess the role of government capacity and institutional coordination in the effectiveness of quality assurance in higher education institutions in Nangarhar province.
