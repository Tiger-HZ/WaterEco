Introduced salmonids support recreational fisheries but can threaten native freshwater biodiversity, requiring reproductive evidence to inform management. We compared seasonal reproductive patterns and length at maturity of the introduced rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) and brown trout (Salmo trutta) in Lake Galletué, Chile. We examined 250 trout collected during 10 sampling campaigns from March 2018 to May 2019 using biological measurements, macroscopic maturity staging, and complementary ovarian histology. Seasonal patterns and limnological associations were tested using multivariate permutation methods. Salmo trutta dominated catches (68.4%) and was generally larger than O. mykiss.
