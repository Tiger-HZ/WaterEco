Sludge generated in wastewater treatment plants is subjected to a dewatering process prior to disposal or reuse due to its high water content. The determination of the sludge’s dry matter content is generally carried out in laboratory environments using gravimetric methods; however, these methods are time-consuming and labor-intensive processes. In this study, videos of the sludge loading process into trucks were recorded through a camera system integrated into the treatment plant, and the behavior exhibited by the sludge at different dry matter levels was analyzed using image processing techniques.
