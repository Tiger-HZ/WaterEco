The paper presents the results of an assessment of the hydrological and hydraulic conditions of the inflows to the Buków and Racibórz polders from the Oder and Olza rivers during the September 2024 flood, as well as the transformation of the flood wave within these polders. The hydrological analysis was supported by a two-dimensional hydraulic model, which was used to verify stage–discharge relationships and estimate peak discharges at the key gauging stations: Chałupki on the Oder River and Łaziska on the Olza River. The study evaluates the extent to which the Buków and Racibórz polders contributed to reducing the flood wave on the Oder River. It also discusses operational challenges associated with forecasting inflows to the polders, highlighting the main sources of uncertainty, including errors in high-flow hydrometric measurements, riverbed erosion at gauging cross-sections, and the influence of riparian vegetation.
