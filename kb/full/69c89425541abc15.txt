Weed control is challenging in organic vegetable farming because weeds compete with crops for water, nutrients, sunlight and growing space; however, chemical herbicides are generally not used in organic production. This study was conducted as a field experiment over two seasons, 2022–23 and 2023–24, at the AICRP on Weed Management Block, College Farm, Professor Jayashankar Telangana Agricultural University, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, to evaluate eco-friendly weed-management techniques for enhancing fruit yield and nutrient uptake in watermelon. The experiment was conducted using a randomised block design with three replications. The nine treatments were groundnut-shell mulch, rice-husk mulch, polyethene mulch + intra-row hand weeding, rice-straw mulch + hand weeding, hoeing twice, fenugreek
