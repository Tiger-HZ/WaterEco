This study examines the challenges and Opportunities for Farmers in Dairy Farming Business in Plateau State. This study employed a multi-stage sampling technique within a descriptive and predictive research design to examine the determinants of modern adoption among dairy farmers in Plateau State, Nigeria. The sample size was determined using Cochran’s formula for an unknown population, yielding 384 respondents across three purposively selected Local Government Areas: Jos South, Riyom, and Barkin Ladi. Instrument validity was confirmed by a strong Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin (KMO) value of 0.851 and a significant Bartlett's Test of Sphericity (p = .003).
