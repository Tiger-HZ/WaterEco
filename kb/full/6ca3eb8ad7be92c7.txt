We performed three experiments in indoor streams and one experiment in a natural stream to investigate the effects of growth factors on δd 13 C levels in benthic microalgae. In the indoor streams, algae grown under conditions of high light and high phosphorus had δd 13 C values that were 16% higher than those in algae grown under conditions of low light and low phosphorus. Light effects were much stronger than phosphorus effects. The effects of both factors increased in strength as algal biomass accrued, and by the end of the experiments, algal δd 13 C and biomass were highly correlated. In the natural stream, algae exposed to direct sunlight were enriched 15% over shaded algae, corroborating the strong effect of light in the indoor streams.
