Abstract Agricultural production is closely related to the natural environment and can cause impact when managed inadequately. Agroforestry systems (AFSs), on the other hand, constitute a sustainable alternative, which can reconcile the production with soil conservation and the ecosystem maintenance. This study aimed to evaluate soil quality aspects in a chronosequence of AFs and compare it with soil under native vegetation (NV) and under monoculture (MONO). Five AFSs were evaluated: AFS0, AFS1, AFS2, AFS3, and AFS4, with zero, one, two, three, and four years of implementation, respectively. The present investigation was conducted in a coastal–Caatinga biome transition zone in the semi-arid region, Brazil, between August 2020 and July 2021.
