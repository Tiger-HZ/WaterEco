Accurate characterization of three-dimensional aquifer heterogeneity is essential for contaminant transport modeling, yet direct inversion of grid-scale hydraulic conductivity fields is often ill-posed and computationally expensive. This study proposes a variational autoencoder-least squares generative adversarial network (VA-LSGAN) for compact latent-space parameterization of three-dimensional heterogeneous hydraulic conductivity fields and applies it to perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) inverse modeling at a contaminated field site. The VA-LSGAN combines a three-dimensional convolutional variational autoencoder with a least-squares adversarial loss, retaining the encoder-decoder structure required for inversion while improving the preservation of spatial contrasts and continuity.
