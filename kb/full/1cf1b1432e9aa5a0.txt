Background Fast-growing poplar ( Populus spp. ) and willow ( Salix spp. ) trees are frequently cultivated in agroforestry systems in temperate latitudes, and the foliage from these trees is widely used as feed for sheep and cattle. However, little is known about their feed value for goats and their impact on the oxidative and metabolic stress situation during the lambing period. The first aim was to determine the macronutrient and energy digestibility and the usability of minerals of poplar and willow foliage in goat wethers. The second aim was to examine whether supplementing the diet with poplar foliage would improve antioxidant defense status, calcium and metabolic homeostasis during the periparturient period in dairy goats.
