Abstract Ensuring access to clean water and sanitation, regardless of living environment, is a central aim of Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 6. However, household water use practices remain underexplored in geographically isolated communities in Cambodia. This exploratory study investigated household water use conditions in a remote island village in Cambodia through water quality analyses, surface swab tests, and field interviews involving 30 households. Bathing water samples were collected from eight households, drinking water samples from nine households, and surface swab tests were conducted on household water-use utensils.
