The present work explores further a class of analytical solutions of moisture movement in unsaturated porous media, characterized by an exponential dependence of the hydraulic conductivity on water pressure. The Green's function method is used to derive a general analytical model pertaining to multidimensional steady infiltration to a shallow water table with arbitrary boundary conditions and arbitrary root‐uptake forcing functions and for various simple source geometry. The general solution is expressed in integral form from which particular analytical solutions pertaining to cases of surface and subsurface irrigation, evaporation, and root‐uptake in the presence of a shallow water table can be easily deduced from the general analytical model.
