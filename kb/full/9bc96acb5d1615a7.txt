BACKGROUND Postoperative inflammation after joint injury or surgery contributes to pain, edema, and delayed functional recovery. Cryotherapy is commonly used to alleviate pain and modulate inflammatory responses; however, the effectiveness of various cooling modalities in reducing temperatures in deeper periarticular and intra-articular tissues remains uncertain. AIM To compare the cooling performance of a convection-based cryotherapy system with two circulating-pad cooling systems in reducing temperature at various tissue depths. METHODS A cadaveric porcine knee model was used to assess comparative thermal transfer in periarticular tissues and the joint space.
