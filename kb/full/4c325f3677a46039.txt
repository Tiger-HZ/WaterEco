Abstract Purpose Non-life insurance is considered to have an advantage over traditional life insurance for some situations because it covers a variety of risks such as fire, theft, and casualty-related losses. However, poor government policy may negate the tendency for non-life insurance policy subscriptions. Considering this issue, the authors investigate the relationship between bureaucratic quality and nonlife insurance penetration. Institutional quality, innovation, and insurance align with the timeline of Sustainable Development Goals, SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), and SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth).
