1. Seasonal dynamics, spatial distribution and population size of the phantom midge Chaoborus flavicans in different parts of the eutrophic Lake Hiidenvesi (30.3 km 2 ) were studied. 2. Density of larvae was low in the shallow, most eutrophic parts of the lake, while the deep Kiihkelyksenselkä basin was inhabited by a dense population. In the deepest part of Kiihkelyksenselkä (33 m) density was 13 989 ± 3542 m –2 in May, declined to 1102 ± 274 m –2 in July and recovered to 7225 ± 1314 m –2 by October. In spring and autumn the majority of larvae were benthic while, during high summer, few larvae were found in the sediment. 3. Horizontal distribution fluctuated seasonally.
