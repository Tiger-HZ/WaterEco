Nitrate toxicosis from contaminated drinking water is a com­mon cause of acute death in cattle. The objective of this study was to determine if commercially available semi-quantitative colorimetric test strips designed for human drinking water can be used as a screening test to identify elevated nitrate con­centrations in cattle drinking water. Six test strip brands were used to measure nitrate concentrations in water samples with known values. Sample concentrations were prepared and veri­fied via ion chromatography. Ten volunteers were instructed to test water samples and record nitrate levels according to the test strip manufacturer’s instructions. There was no sig­nificant effect of evaluator or evaluator sex.
