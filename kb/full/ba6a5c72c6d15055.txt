To meet the practical demands for accelerometers in bridge vibration monitoring, including multidimensional sensing, broad working bandwidth, and miniaturization, a three-dimensional (3D) fiber Bragg grating (FBG) accelerometer based on elliptical flexible hinges is proposed. The sensor integrates an X–Y accelerometer featuring a biaxial elliptical flexible hinge and a Z-direction accelerometer with a uniaxial elliptical flexible hinge. The structural parameters are optimized via theoretical modeling and finite element simulations to achieve an optimal balance between resonant frequency and sensitivity.
