ABSTRACT Synthesis of lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO 4 , LFP), a dominant cathode material for lithium‐ion batteries, typically relies on multi‐precursor systems involving phosphoric acid, whose production is sulfuric‐acid‐intensive and associated with environmental burden and supply‐chain volatility. Here, a greener single‐source method is introduced using lithium phosphate (Li 3 PO 4 , LPO) as the sole Li‐P precursor for hydrothermal LFP synthesis. Because LPO remains largely solid during hydrothermal processing, its physical characteristics directly influence LFP formation.
