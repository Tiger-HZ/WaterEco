Background: At present, the business processes of hospital medical waste classification management are mainly completed by manual work. The related business procedures are complex, time-consuming, and laborious, the digitization level of classification business is low, and data collection is difficult. These problems have seriously affected the daily management of hospital medical waste. Objective: Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology provides new ideas for solving these problems. The research purpose of this article is to use RFID technology to change the business operation mode and data collection management method of medical waste classification, and improve the digitization level of medical waste classification management.
