Abstract DOM is a key substance driving the biogeochemical cycle of aquatic ecosystems. Based on field sampling data collected during the wet and dry seasons from the mainstem and tributaries of the Dongjiang River, this study utilized EEM-PARAFAC to characterize differences in FDOM components, their relative abundances, and source-associated signatures between the wet and dry seasons. Simultaneously, by integrating conventional water quality parameters and land-use proportions, the response relationships between DOM and hydrological seasonal variations alongside other environmental drivers were quantified.
