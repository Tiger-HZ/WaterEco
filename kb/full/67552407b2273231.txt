Background: This study aimed to evaluate multidimensional postoperative changes in nutrient intake, body composition, biochemical parameters, diet quality, and eating behavior during the first 6 months following sleeve gastrectomy (SG). Methods: This prospective observational study included 55 adults who underwent SG at a specialized obesity clinic in Ankara, Turkey. Eating behavior (TFEQ-TR21), diet quality (HEI-2010), dietary intake, anthropometric measurements, and biochemical parameters were assessed preoperatively and at 6 months postoperatively. Results: Six months after SG, total energy intake decreased markedly, with significant reductions in carbohydrate and fiber intake, while the proportion of energy from protein increased ( p &lt; 0.001).
