This study highlights the impact of urbanization, agricultural runoff, and invasive species on freshwater ecosystems and aquatic biodiversity. It presents a comparative ecological assessment of two freshwater lakes in Vellore District, Tamil Nadu, India: Katpadi Lake (urban) and Saina Palayam Lake (rural). A total of 15 fish species were documented across both lakes, with Oreochromis niloticus identified as an exotic invasive species. The fish community was dominated by Cypriniformes (60%), demonstrating ecological adaptability. Diversity indices such as Shannon (H′) and Simpson (1–D) showed slight variations between the lakes.
