During the 2025 season, an experiment was conducted in the nursery of the private farm Alaejaylat in Libya to assess the impact of extracted Moringa oleifera and amino acids on the development and quality of olive trees of the "Koroneiki" variety. Ten treatments (control, Moringa extracts at 4, 8, and 12 g/l), amino acids at 50, 75, and 100 mg/l, and mixtures MLAE 4 g/l + Amino acid 50 mg/l, MLAE 8 g/l + Amino acid 75 mg/l, and MLAE 12 g/l + Amino acid 100 mg/l) were used in three replicates.
