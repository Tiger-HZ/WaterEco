Abstract The O.E. Meinzer Award of the Geological Society of America's Hydrogeology Division has been presented 61 times between 1965 and 2026, honoring 68 individuals. Because each award recognizes a specific publication or body of work, the winner list constitutes a 60‐year record of what the hydrogeological community has considered its frontier. This note assembles a structured dataset of all recipients and the 177 representative papers listed for them on the Division's website, and analyzes the age structure of the winners, the co‐authorship and thematic content of the cited works, the journals in which they appeared, and the geographic and institutional distribution of the winners.
