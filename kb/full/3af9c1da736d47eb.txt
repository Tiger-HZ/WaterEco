Petroleum sludge contamination remains a pressing challenge in oil-producing regions, posing risks to soil health, ecosystem and public safety due to the high concentrations of hydrocarbons such as Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) and Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs). This study investigated the remediation potential of two organic waste materials, Plantain Peel Biochar (PPB) and Sterilized Cow Dung (SCD), applied individually and in combination to stimulate indigenous microbial communities for enhanced biodegradation of the contaminated soil.
