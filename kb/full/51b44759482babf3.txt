Eutrophication is one of the main anthropogenic pressures experienced by marine ecosystems. It is mainly linked to the use of fertilizers in agriculture and can result in hypoxia, increased turbidity or change in pH. These effects can negatively impact marine species and affect the functioning of ecosystems. Life cycle assessment (LCA) is a tool that can be used to quantify such impacts on the environment of a given product. Part of the process of conducting an LCA is translating environmental flows associated with a product, such as nutrient emissions, to impacts through so-called characterization factors, a component of which are effect factors .
