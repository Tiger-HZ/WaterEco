Studies on the nutrient, antinutrient and associated mycoflora of two varieties of cocoyam (Xanthosoma sagififolium and Colocasia esculenta) corms were carried out in the Department of Plant Science and Biotechnology, Rivers State University. The method of Association of Official Analytical Chemists (AOAC) was adopted for determining proximate and antinutrient components of healthy cocoyam varieties while the cultural laboratory technique was adopted for the isolation of spoilage fungi associated with spoilt cocoyam.
