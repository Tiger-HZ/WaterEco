Abstract. Coastal ecosystems play a major role in global biogeochemical cycles, which are challenging for ocean color satellite observations due to independent variability in the dynamics of phytoplankton, non-algal particles (NAP), and colored dissolved organic matter (CDOM). Field measurements and match-ups with satellite data are required to assess the validity of multi-sensor satellite products and optimize (or develop new) bio-optical inversion algorithms. For this purpose, the water reflectance, light absorption and backscattering properties of coastal waters must be measured together with the concentration, composition and size of the optically-active water constituents.
