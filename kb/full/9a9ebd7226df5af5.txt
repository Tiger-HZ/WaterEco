Offshore Freshened Groundwater (OFG) represents a potential unconventional water resource for water-stressed coastal regions. However, its development is hindered by a lack of systematic, cost-effective methods for regional-scale screening and prioritization, which are necessary before expensive site-specific surveys can be justified. This study addresses this gap by developing and applying an integrated, two-step data-driven framework. First, we present a novel, infrastructure-based prioritization for Euro-Mediterranean OFG sites, ranking them based on regional water-stress and proximity to existing desalination plants and oil and gas platforms.
