Abstract Antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) have profoundly exacerbated environmental risks to public health, evolving into a pressing ecological dilemma. Distinct from traditional pathways, outer membrane vesicles (OMVs) are emerging as important vectors for horizontal gene transfer (HGT) in aquatic environments, yet their role in inter-phylum ARG dissemination remains unclear. In this study, we demonstrate that OMVs mediate bidirectional HGT of ARGs between the cyanobacterium Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 and Escherichia coli DH5α. Co-incubation experiments confirmed that OMVs derived from either strain could associate with the other, accompanied by successful functional expression of transferred resistance genes.
