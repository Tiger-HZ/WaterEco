Groundwater-level forecasting with recurrent networks presupposes a multi-year record and a model trained per well. Pretrained time-series foundation models forecast without training on the well, from its record and the expected weather. Three wells of the Korean national monitoring network with twenty-year records were forecast ten days ahead throughout 2015–2024, each block re-anchored on the last observation, under one protocol for TimesFM-3, Chronos-2, Moirai-2 and per-well GRU, LSTM, gradient-boosted, feed-forward and linear models, using 2 observed weather as the forecast. Trained on ten years, tuned recurrent networks and zero-shot TimesFM-3 halve the error of persistence, TimesFM-3 being ahead on two wells and level on one; simpler models trail.
