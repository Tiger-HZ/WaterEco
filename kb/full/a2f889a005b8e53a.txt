This study explores how institutional quality and ICT influence financial inclusion in OIC countries. This setting is particularly relevant because many member states show relatively low levels of financial inclusion and face significant disparities in institutional capacity, digital infrastructure, and financial system development. Using panel data for 20 OIC countries from 2016 to 2023, the study constructs composite indices of financial inclusion, institutional quality, and ICT through Principal Component Analysis and estimates a random-effects model selected through the Chow, Hausman, and Lagrange multiplier tests.
