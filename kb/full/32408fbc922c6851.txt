Abstract Shale gas reservoirs represent important energy resources, and the injection of unseparated flue gas has been proposed as a potential strategy for coupling geological carbon storage with enhanced shale gas recovery. In this study, density functional theory calculations were employed to systematically investigate facet-dependent adsorption behaviors and underlying surface chemistry of CO 2 , N 2 , H 2 O, and CH 4 on two representative MnCO 3 crystal facets, (1 0 4) and (1 1 0). The gas–solid interfacial interactions were elucidated through adsorption configurations, adsorption energies, charge density redistribution, and electronic structure characteristics.
