Food quality is becoming a major and global concern with increasing demand for safe and healthy food. Thus, techniques for verifying food freshness in real time are very important for improving public health and safety. This study provides an easy-to-use solution to monitor food quality. A sample of food is placed within the chamber monitored by sensors that evaluates diverse parameters related to food spoilage. These data points are processed by the Arduino Uno and sent by wireless communication to ThingSpeak or Blynk platforms for data storage and visualization. The consumer gets timely information about the food spoilage. The study signifies the efficiency of assorted sensors to display real-time data on food quality parameters. Thus, the system helps to reduce the risk of consuming spoiled food. As a result, the consumers can make informed decisions regarding the quality and safety of their food.
