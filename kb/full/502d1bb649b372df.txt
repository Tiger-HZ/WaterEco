Abstract It is well known that the Lehr and Lowe Price Indexes will have difficulties measuring constant-quality price change in the face of rapid inflation rates. The reverse is also true: the indexes will have difficulties when inflation rates are falling rapidly. Price indexes for IT goods typically show very rapid declines. Using data for three consumer IT segments, this note calculates Lehr and Lowe price indexes and compares them to four other quality adjusted unit value indexes that do not have this problem. The gaps in the two types of indexes are substantial in our samples, suggesting that this problem can be numerically important for IT segments. JEL Codes: C43, E31, D12, L63
