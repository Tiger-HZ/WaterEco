Background Entrectinib is effective for ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), but its postmarketing safety profile remains incompletely characterized outside clinical trials. We conducted a dual-database pharmacovigilance study to characterize real-world reporting patterns, identify cross-database replicated adverse event signals, explore age- and sex-related reporting heterogeneity, and evaluate time-to-onset patterns. Methods Reports were retrieved from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS; 2020Q1–2025Q4; 520 patients; 1,573 preferred-term [PT] events) and the Japanese Adverse Drug Event Report database (JADER; 2020Q1–2025Q3; 254 patients; 393 PT events).
