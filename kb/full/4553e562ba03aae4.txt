Coarse cereals are indispensable to both national food security and the implementation of the Healthy China Initiative, underscoring the urgent need to promote their green and low-carbon production. Using panel data from Shanxi Province spanning 2010–2020, this study first employed water footprint (WF) and carbon footprint (CF) accounting methods, together with a coupling coordination degree (CCD) model, to characterize the spatio-temporal dynamics of WF, CF, and their CCD for six major coarse cereal crops across the province. On this basis, the entropy-weighted TOPSIS model was applied to assess the spatio-temporal patterns of the resource-environment performance index (REPI), while exploratory spatial data analysis (ESDA) was used to detect spatial autocorrelation.
