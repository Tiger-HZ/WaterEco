Water User Organizations (WUOs) and Local Administrative Organizations (LAOs) in Thailand have legal responsibilities for local water resource management and for submitting water-related projects to the Thai Water Plan (TWP) system. However, many local organizations face limitations in baseline data, digital tools, and technical capacity required for effective water planning. This study developed &amp;ldquo;Waterplan RRU&amp;rdquo; a digital platform designed to support water resource planning and decision-making and implemented it in four subdistricts of Chachoengsao Province: Samet Tai and Samet Nuea in Bang Khla District, Plaeng Yao in Plaeng Yao District, and Khlong Khut in Ban Pho District. The study involved four LAOs and six registered WUOs and employed participatory action research
