Author Correction: Biotic interactions shape the realized 
niche of toxic cyanobacteria
Pinelopi Ntetsika   , Stefanie Eyring   , Ewa Merz   , Marta Reyes, Benno Käch, 
Stephan B. Munch   , Stuart R. Dennis, Marco Baity-Jesi    & Francesco Pomati   
In the version of this article initially published, in Fig. 1f, the y-axis label “Cryptophyta” incor-
rectly appeared in bold instead of the “Chrysophyta” label; in Fig. 2a, b, the current y-axis labels 
“Correlation coefficient (ρ)” appeared originally as “Coefficient (ρ)”; and in Fig. 3a, the y-axis 
label now reading “Interaction coefficient” appeared originally as “Coefficient (ρ). ” In Extended 
Data Figs. 5 and 6, the images and legends were earlier, incorrect versions and are now updated. 
The figures have been amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
