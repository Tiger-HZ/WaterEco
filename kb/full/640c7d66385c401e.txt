Water is an essential natural resource for the survival of living beings and the development of societies; however, increasing natural and anthropogenic pressures have contributed to the progressive deterioration of its quality. In this context, the objective of this study was to evaluate the water quality of the Chone River by applying the National Sanitation Foundation Water Quality Index (ICA-NSF), with the aim of determining its level of contamination and establishing its potential implications for the various uses of this water resource. To this end, five sampling points (P1–P5) located in the watershed associated with Chone, Bolívar, Tosagua, and Junín were analyzed, considering two sampling depths (30 and 60 cm).
