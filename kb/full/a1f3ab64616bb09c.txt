In the original version of this Article, the name of the type strain of Pseudomonas onubensis sp. nov and the 
deposit number from CECT were incorrect, where
“The type strain is L1 T (= MIS C2T = CECT 31157T = LMG 34132T) and was isolated from the rhizosphere of 
Medicago spp. plants. ”
now reads:
“The type strain is L1 T (= MIS 1 T = CECT 31092T = LMG 34132T) and was isolated from the rhizosphere of 
Medicago spp. plants. ”
Description of Pseudomonas onubensis sp. nov.
Pseudomonas onubensis (o.nu.ben’sis. L. fem. adj. onubensis, of or belonging to Onuba, the ancient Latin name of 
Huelva, a city in Spain, where the type strain was isolated).
Cells are individual and motile Gram-negative rods. Colonies are light-yellow, convex, irregular, smooth, and 
wet with undulate edges on TSA plates (pH = 7) at 30 °C for 24 h (optimal conditions). The growth ranges are 
pH 6–9, 5–40 °C, and 0–0.5 M NaCl.
Catalase- and oxidase-positive. According to the API 20NE test, it can assimilate D-glucose, L-arabinose, 
D-mannose, D-mannitol, N-acetyl-glucosamine, potassium gluconate, capric acid, malic acid, and trisodium 
citrate. Nitrate reduction, indole formation, D-glucose fermentation, aesculin and gelatin hydrolysis, and 
assimilation of D-maltose, adipic acid, and phenylacetic acid are negative. Strong enzymatic activity of arginine 
dihydrolase and no activity of urease or β-galactosidase are observed.
The genome of the type strain L1 T has 6,653,686  bp assembled into 383 contigs, with a mean coverage of 
121.3 × and an N50 value of 50,308 bp. The G + C content is 59.9%.
The type strain is L1 T (= MIS 1 T = CECT 31092 T = LMG 34132T) and was isolated from the rhizosphere of 
Medicago  spp. plants. The GenBank accession number for the 16S rRNA gene sequence is OM397092. The 
GenBank/EMBL/DDBJ accession number for the draft genome is CBFHAZ01.
The original Article has been corrected.
