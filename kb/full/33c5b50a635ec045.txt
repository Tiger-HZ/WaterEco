The increasing need for nutrient-dense functional foods for the elderly has stimulated interest in the utilization of locally available food resources. This study evaluated the nutrient and proximate composition of composite delicacies developed from plantain, almond, and oat for elderly consumers in Umudike, Ikwuano Local Government Area, Abia State, Nigeria. The purpose of the study was to develop innovative food products from plantain, oat, and almond nuts as a means of promoting diversification and enhancing their effective utilization. The study adopted research and development (R&amp;D) design. research work was carried out in Umudike in South-East geopolitical zone Nigeria.
