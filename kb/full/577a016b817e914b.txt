Summary Globally, a small proportion of sewage treatment plants have surface-covered treatment tanks, or are constructed underground (enclosed). This provides a significant opportunity for nitrous oxide (N2O) quantification directly from the collected off-gas, an advantage over plants with surfaces open to the atmosphere. This chapter aims to provide guidelines for quantifying N2O emissions from enclosed and surface covered wastewater treatment plants. It outlines how covered treatment processes – ranging from partially or fully structurally covered tanks to fully underground facilities – enable effective odor control and create controlled environments for monitoring gaseous emissions.
