Biosolids are nutrient-rich organic materials derived from domestic wastewater treatment plants, which have gained increasing recognition as valuable resources due to their diverse organic and inorganic constituents. These constituents find beneficial applications across agriculture, civil engineering and energy recovery sectors. Since the late 1990s, Indah Water Konsortium (IWK) has led research and innovation in converting sewage sludge into nutrient-rich biosolids. These biosolids serve as effective fertilisers, significantly enhancing agricultural productivity and soil health while minimizing reliance on chemical fertilisers thereby promoting environmental conservation through the reuse and recycling of organic waste.
