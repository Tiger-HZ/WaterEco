Soil-applied inorganic fertilizers remain the foundation of modern rice production, yet their efficiency is often constrained by nutrient losses through leaching, volatilization, and fixation, ultimately limiting plant uptake and yield potential. Seaweed-based foliar fertilizers have attracted increasing attention because they provide nutrients that can stimulate plant growth and nutrient assimilation. However, their effectiveness when integrated with conventional soil fertilization under field conditions remains insufficiently understood.
