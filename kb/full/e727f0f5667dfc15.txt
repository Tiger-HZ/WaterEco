ABSTRACT Water demand from an exponentially growing population and rapid industrialization increases pressure on freshwater sources, drawing global attention to the development of promising water treatment technologies. Membrane distillation (MD) offers an effective solution for desalinating seawater and treating wastewater. MD systems with various configurations and membrane modules can achieve high rejection rates (&gt;99%) of salts and toxic contaminants. Recent advancements, including novel modules, configurations, membrane materials, and modifications, have revolutionized MD technology, steering it toward sustainable solutions for water scarcity.
