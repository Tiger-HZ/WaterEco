Abstract Background. The National Allergy Centre (NAC) in Islamabad is the only public-sector allergy immunotherapy facility in Pakistan and sees several hundred patients a day. Before this project it operated entirely on paper, with no appointment or triage system, long queues, fragmented and duplicated records, and clinical staff diverted to manual data entry. Documented public-sector experience of electronic medical record (EMR) implementation in Pakistan and the wider region is scarce. We describe a healthcare-manager-led EMR implementation and the change-management approach used to enable adoption. Methods.
