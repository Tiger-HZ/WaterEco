ABSTRACT Effective water resource management is critical in humid tropical regions like Kerala, India, where intense monsoonal rainfall often leads to high runoff and seasonal water scarcity. This study developed a decision-support framework integrating the Soil and Water Assessment Tool (SWAT) with GIS-based multi-criteria evaluation (MCE) for sustainable water conservation planning in the 140.94 km2 Manali watershed. The SWAT model, calibrated and validated with satisfactory performance (R2 = 0.785, NSE = 0.785), indicated that nearly 78% of annual rainfall contributes to streamflow, suggesting significant scope for water retention and groundwater recharge.
