Abstract School based WASH services are critical for child health, educational performance, and equitable learning environments, yet context-specific evidence from rapidly urbanizing Ethiopian cities remains scarce. This study investigated WASH services and drinking water quality across schools in Bahir Dar City. A cross-sectional survey was conducted involving 117 randomly selected schools to assess WASH services alongside onsite physicochemical testing and membrane filtration for E. coli detection. Additionally, multivariable logistic regression was used to identify predictors of service coverage.
