This research involves developing an environmental and territorial diagnosis aimed at assessing ecosystem potential and limitations. The goal is to guide decision-making regarding environmental sustainability by studying territorial environmental variables such as water, sanitation, waste, vulnerability conditions, criticality, areas of ecological interest, infrastructure, urban services, transportation, equipment, growth, land use patterns, behaviors, land use behavior, and conflicts, in line with the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, with a focus on achieving resilient and sustainable cities and human settlements.
