Arctic–Boreal terrestrial ecosystems are rapidly changing under amplified high-latitude warming, including widespread expansion of shrubs, with consequences for regional carbon and energy balances. Yet, high-latitude vegetation diversity and vegetation–climate interactions remain under-represented in many global land surface models. In ORCHIDEE, the land surface component of the IPSL Earth system model, high-latitude vegetation is represented primarily as boreal trees or grasslands, omitting explicit shrubs. Here, we implement three high-latitude shrub plant functional types (PFTs) (tall deciduous, low deciduous, and evergreen dwarf shrubs) in ORCHIDEE (revision 9269).
