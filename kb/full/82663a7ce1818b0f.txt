Oxbow lakes (baors) are semi-closed freshwater ecosystems that contribute substantially to inland fisheries and livelihoods in southwestern Bangladesh, yet their ecological productivity remains insufficiently explored. This study aimed to assess the physicochemical conditions, plankton diversity and abundance, and fish community structure of three baors—Bukbhora, Jhapa, and Khedapara—from July 2023 to June 2024.
