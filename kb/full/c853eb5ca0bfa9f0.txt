The study is based on simultaneous measurements of surface ozone concentrations and meteorological parameters over a period of about two years in mountainous and coastal regions in Bulgaria. The average O3 at the mountain station (Rozhen) is 48 ppb, and at the coastal station (Ahtopol), 41 ppb. It is found that extremely low and high ozone concentrations are less common in the mountainous area than in the coastal area. The diurnal cycle of O3 at Rozhen shows small amplitude, with maximum values recorded at night, while at Ahtopol the diurnal cycle is distinct, with a maximum in the late afternoon and a minimum in the morning.
