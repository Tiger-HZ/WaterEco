Rainfall erosion significantly affects nutrient transport in plantation forests, but the internal nutrient-driven mechanism remains unclear. In this study, three types of plantation forests in the Xishuangbanna were selected to examine soil nutrient contents in erosion and deposition zones, nutrient concentration in runoff, and nutrient contents in litter, while the relationship among them was analyzed using the RDA method, aiming at exploring the internal driving mechanism of nutrient transport following erosion caused by the rainy season. The results show that, after the rainy season, the nutrient contents in the soil of all three forest types decreased in the erosion zone, with the most severe changes at the bottom of the slope.
