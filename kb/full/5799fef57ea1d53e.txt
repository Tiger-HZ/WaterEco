This research investigates sustainable water treatment by evaluating competitive adsorption of Zn, Cd, and Hg using nitrogen-doped and nitrogen co-doped carbon cryogels. The synthesized materials exhibited high specific surface areas (up to 1530 m2/g) and a maximum multicomponent adsorption capacity of 1.03 mmol/g for the CCNS3 sample. The practical applicability of these adsorbents was successfully validated in a real water sample, achieving up to 93% removal efficiency for the investigated metals despite the presence of background ions.
