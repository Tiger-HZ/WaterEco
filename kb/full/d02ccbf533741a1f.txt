Water potential gradients drive water flow within and between soils and plants, and the internal plant water potential controls a wide range of physiological processes including photosynthesis, growth, and mortality. Notwithstanding this clear relevance for many critical aspects of ecosystem function, water potential data have historically been relatively inaccessible and unnetworked. The absence of a centralized repository for plant water potential time series limits our ability to integrate a wealth of ecophysiological information from other networks and from remote sensing. Closing this gap is necessary to address unresolved questions about plant responses to drought and heat stress, and to make confident predictions about plant and ecosystem function in a warming world.
