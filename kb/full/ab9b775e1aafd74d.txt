Excessive fluoride (F − ) in groundwater poses a serious threat to human health, necessitating the development of efficient defluorination materials. In this study, a novel composite hydrogel bead (La-SP/SA) was fabricated by incorporating shale powder (SP) into sodium alginate (SA) matrix followed by crosslinking with lanthanum chloride. The synthesis conditions were determined as SP dosage 4.0%, La 3+ concentration 0.2 mol L −1 , and crosslinking time 12 h. Under adsorbent dosage of 0.7 g L −1 , initial F − concentration of 9.0 mg L −1 , and pH of 5.0, La-SP/SA achieved an excellent removal efficiency of 94.33% (12.18 mg g −1 ), with a residual F − concentration meeting both the WHO guideline and Chinese standard.
