Abstract Groundwater is a vital resource for household, commercial, and agricultural uses in the Himalayan foothills. This study uses geophysical, geochemical, and statistical techniques to assess the groundwater potential and quality in the Sahaspur Block, Dehradun District, Uttarakhand, India. The Schlumberger array was used to perform 58 Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES) measurements in order to detect aquifer zones and define subsurface lithology. The analyzed resistivity data showed multilayered subsurface formations made up of saturated aquifers, worn strata, and topsoil, suggesting generally good groundwater potential throughout the study area.
