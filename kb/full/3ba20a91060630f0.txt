ABSTRACT This paper describes and analyzes recharge practices in Bács‐Kiskun County, Hungary, within what can be categorized as the farmer domain. Such locally embedded aquifer recharge practices remain underexplored in Hungary. Current research and funding largely focus on large‐scale Managed Aquifer Recharge (MAR) interventions emphasizing intended recharge. This study uses qualitative methods, combining field observations, in‐depth and semi‐structured interviews, and document analysis carried out from March 2024 to December 2024. Sixteen interviews were carried out with key stakeholders and smallholder farmers involved in MAR implementation projects at the local or national level.
