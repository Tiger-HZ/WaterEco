Abstract Previous research has demonstrated benefits of nutrient-enriched late-gestation diets for improving sow micronutrient status and farrowing outcomes; however, the optimal timing of implementation and evaluation under commercial conditions in different genetic lines remain unclear. The objective of this study was to evaluate the effects of timing of a nutrient-enriched late-gestation diet on reproductive performance and sow mortality in commercial sows. A total of 1,237 AcuFast sows (parity 0–9; average parity 2) were allotted by parity to one of four dietary treatments in late gestation.
