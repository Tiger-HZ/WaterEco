To remove metals that cause water pollution in the local environment, it may be more cost-effective to use materials such as chicken feathers, which are produced in large quantities, readily available, and affordable. In this study, chicken feathers were placed in a chromatography column and treated with contaminated stream water samples prepared at three different pH levels, with a view to their use in contaminated stream water treatment. Contaminated stream water samples taken from the lower part of the chromatography column and the chicken feathers in the column were incinerated in a microwave oven and analysed using an ICP-MS instrument.
