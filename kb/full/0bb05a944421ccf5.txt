This article presents the results of the development and quality evaluation of an active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) based on the association of two strains of lactic acid bacteria - Lactobacillus fermentum 30 and Lactobacillus cellobiosus 36. The relevance of the study is determined by the need to create domestic probiotic drugs for the treatment of acute intestinal infections and the correction of dysbiosis in the Republic of Kazakhstan. During the study, the biomass accumulation process was scaled up and subsequent lyophilization was carried out using an optimized protective environment.
