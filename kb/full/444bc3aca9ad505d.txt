This research was conducted to determine the fatty acids, macronutrients, and micronutrients of almond genotypes. It was determined that the genotypes possessed high-quality fatty acids, such as low linoleic acid and high oleic acid content. The oleic acid content of kernels was found to be between 54.60% and 71.33%. In addition, the linoleic acid was found to be between 11.39% and 14.54%. In terms of macronutrient content, the highest amount was found in the genotypes to be N (5.35%-1.71%), followed by K (0.61%-1.78%) and P (0.27%-1.04%). The highest amount of micronutrients was Zn (19.07 mg kg-1 to 47.77 mg kg-1), followed by Fe (17.79 mg kg-1 to 47.85 mg kg-1) and Mn (16.15 mg kg-1 to 27.31 mg kg-1).
