EkoLakay container-based sanitation (CBS) with weekly waste collection and treatment is one of the only household-level safely managed sanitation options in northern Haiti. Despite a low monthly fee of 350 HTG (less than 3 USD), some households still struggle to pay for the service, indicating that targeted subsidies could expand coverage to the poorest and most vulnerable households. To inform targeted subsidy design, we conducted surveys with 603 households in four neighborhoods of Cap Haitien to investigate willingness to pay (WTP) for EkoLakay CBS services and to determine the socio-economic, behavioral, and contextual factors associated with WTP.
