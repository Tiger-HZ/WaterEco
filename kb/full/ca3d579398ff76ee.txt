This work focuses on the development of LC-MS/MS method for the determination of bisphenol A (BPA) in water samples. BPA is one of the endocrine disruptors with negative health effects and poses a significant risk to humans and ecosystem. An MRM method was developed and was applied to the analysis of water before and after UV treatment under various conditions. The efficiency was evaluated using the removal efficiency factor. The results showed that UV radiation in combination with catalyst can reduce content of BPA in water.
