The present study investigated the adsorption behavior of crystal violet (CV) from aqueous solution using poly(acrylic acid-co-acrylamide) [P(AAc-co-AAm)] hydrogel as an adsorbent. Batch adsorption experiments were conducted at 30°C using different initial CV concentrations to evaluate adsorption kinetics and equilibrium behavior. The hydrogel exhibited rapid CV uptake during the initial contact period, followed by a gradual increase in adsorption until equilibrium was approached, indicating progressive occupation of available adsorption sites. Kinetic analysis was performed using first- and second-order models, while equilibrium data were evaluated using Langmuir, Freundlich, and Temkin isotherms.
