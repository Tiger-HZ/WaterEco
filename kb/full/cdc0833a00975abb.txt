ABSTRACT Chitin is a structural polysaccharide in arthropod exoskeletons and fungal cell walls and is introduced into aquafeeds through ingredients such as insects, krill, shrimp, and yeast. This study applied a meta‐analytical approach to evaluate the effects of dietary chitin‐containing ingredients and dietary chitin content on fish growth performance and nutrient digestibility, and to develop regression models identifying factors influencing fish responses to chitin. The pooled effects indicated that the dietary inclusion of krill, shrimp, and yeast improved fish growth without affecting feed intake, whereas insect‐based diets reduced growth, and insects and yeast reduced nutrient digestibility.
