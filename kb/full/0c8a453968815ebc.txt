This study addresses key methodological gaps in prior T6 heat-treatment research on A356 aluminum alloy, namely the ambiguity of treatment-temperature definitions, reliance on perceived (non-quantitative) porosity assessment, incomplete statistical testing across mechanical responses, and the use of single-factor discrete temperature screening instead of a continuous optimization design. A two-factor Central Composite Design (aging temperature 162-183 °C; aging time 2.3-13.7 h) following a fixed solution treatment (540 °C/6 h, water quench) was employed, comprising 13 experimental runs.
