Soil freeze-thaw cycles are common in high-latitude regions. These cycles alter soil physical properties. They also influence microbial activity and ecological functions. However, the response of fungal community functions to freeze– thaw events remains poorly understood. This study focused on typical black soil in Northeast China. We employed highthroughput sequencing of the fungal ITS region. Fungal functional guilds were predicted using the FUNGuild tool. The aim was to comparatively analyze the effects of freeze-thaw processes on fungal community functions. The results revealed significant differences across freeze-thaw stages.
