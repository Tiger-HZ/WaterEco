ABSTRACT Constructed wetlands (CWs) are low‐cost, low‐energy nature‐based solutions for wastewater treatment and are commonly regarded as robust; however, this perception is often based on removal efficiencies or mean effluent concentrations, without accounting for effluent variability or the probability of maintaining concentrations below regulatory limits. This study applied the reliability coefficient (RC) to 34 vertical‐flow constructed wetland (VFCW), horizontal‐flow constructed wetland (HFCW), and hybrid constructed wetland (HCW) systems treating domestic wastewater identified through a systematic literature review.
