Introduction This study evaluated the short-term effects of adding a 4-week radial extracorporeal shock wave therapy (rESWT) program to standardized physiotherapy on spasticity, walking performance, lower-extremity motor function, and health-related quality of life in chronic stroke patients with plantar flexor spasticity. Methods In this prospective, single-blind, sham-controlled trial, 85 chronic stroke survivors were randomized to active rESWT (n = 43; 0.10–0.20 mJ/mm 2 , 4000–6000 pulses/session, 3 sessions/week) plus physiotherapy or sham rESWT plus physiotherapy for 4 weeks. Outcomes were assessed at baseline and Week 4. The primary outcome was the Modified Ashworth Scale (MAS).
