Benthic organisms have relatively limited mobility and long life cycles, enabling them to comprehensively reflect long-term changes in water quality, sediment conditions, and hydrological conditions. They are therefore important indicators for assessing the ecological health of lakes. This study integrates survey data on benthic animals in Dongting Lake from 2001 to 2025, with a particular focus on reviewing publicly available data from 2016 to 2025, and conducts stage-based visualization of three categories of indicators: phytoplankton, zooplankton, and benthic animals. The results show that the decline in total phosphorus concentrations and the improvement in water quality in Dongting Lake have provided a foundation for ecological restoration.
