Abstract Background Systematic reviews, as one of the most credible types of scientific studies, play a crucial role in research and practical decision-making. Despite their importance, the criteria and quality indicators of search strategies in systematic reviews have received less attention. Objectives This study aimed to develop a system of indicators to assess the quality of search strategies in systematic reviews. Methods This research was conducted in two phases. In the first phase, search strategy quality indicators were extracted and classified through a review of documents, guidelines, and relevant studies.
