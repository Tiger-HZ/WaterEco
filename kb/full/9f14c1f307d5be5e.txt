Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is a major global public health problem, with a particular focus on end-stage kidney disease patients on continuous haemodialysis. Malnutrition is widespread in patients on haemodialysis and is closely associated with increased morbidity and mortality. The study aimed to assess the nutritional status and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) of patients on maintenance haemodialysis in selected dialysis centres in Chattogram, Bangladesh. The study was conducted as a descriptive, cross-sectional survey of 186 adult patients on maintenance hemodialysis. The patients were recruited from four Chattogram dialysis centres using a non-probability sampling technique.
