Abstract Purpose Calcareous soils restrict nutrient availability and crop productivity due to high pH and calcium carbonate. This study evaluated the effects of mineral nitrogen (NH 4 NO 3 ) and vermicompost on leek ( Allium ampeloprasum L.) biomass, vitamin C, and nutritional status under calcareous soil conditions.
