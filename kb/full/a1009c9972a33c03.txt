The application of exogenous nutrient bags (ENBs) is critical for achieving high yields in morel mushroom cultivation; however, the high cost and lack of locally adapted formulations have become major constraints for the sustainable expansion of this industry. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of ten treatments, including a conventional formulation control, incorporating locally available agricultural by-products (rapeseed straw, organic fertilizer, alfalfa, and oats), on soil fertility, enzyme activity, microbial community structure, and ultimately the yield and quality of cultivated Morchella sextelata in Jianzha County, Qinghai.
