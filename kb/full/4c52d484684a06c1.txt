Abstract Synthetic data generation through document rewriting has emerged as a promising technique for improving language model pretraining. A central open question in this line of work is whether rewriting can compensate for low-quality source material or whether higher-quality inputs systematically yield better models after rewriting. Recent studies have begun to characterize this interaction, but they typically compare across corpora of different perceived quality levels rather than controlling quality within a single annotated corpus, and focus primarily on English pretraining from scratch.
