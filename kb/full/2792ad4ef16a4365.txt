ABSTRACT Target variable selection is often predefined in machine learning (ML)–based surface water pollution prediction without systematically considering dataset characteristics. This study developed a target variable selection unit (TVSU) integrating Gini feature importance and model pre‐evaluation for same‐period, cross‐station, and cross‐variable reconstruction of surface water quality and associated pollution‐management decision support. Results showed that the scenario adhering to TVSU achieved the best predictive performance in both shallow and deep learning models (nRMSE values of 0.418 and 0.295/relative MAE values of 36.4% and 30.4%), whereas scenarios disregarding TVSU exhibited substantially higher prediction errors.
