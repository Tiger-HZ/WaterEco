Food-borne illnesses linked to poultry meat and its processed products remain a significant public health concern. This study assessed the microbiological quality of poultry products from three farms—Di Life, Mechis, and Ngozi &amp; Ibeka—located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. A total of 152 samples, including water, feed, air, faeces, meat, cloaca, gizzard, and egg, were randomly and aseptically collected monthly over a three-month period.
