This study was conducted in 2023 and 2024 to reveal the differences in yield, quality, and nutrient content of meadows in Bingöl province (Türkiye) semi-arid ecological conditions according to different cutting times. In the study, meadows were harvested at 15-day intervals starting in May and four cuttings were performed. Yield characteristics included green forage yield and dry matter yield; quality characteristics included crude protein content, crude protein yield, acid detergent fiber (ADF), neutral detergent fiber (NDF), digestible dry matter (DDM), and relative feed value (RFV); and nutrient elements included phosphorus (P), potassium (K), calcium (Ca), and magnesium (Mg) were examined.
