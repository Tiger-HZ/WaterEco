Objective We aim to improve the quality control of Rhodiola rosea , Astragalus membranaceus , and Carthamus tinctorius capsules (RAC capsule) from the aspects of thin-layer chromatography (TLC) and high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), respectively, and the established quality control methods are suitable for their popularization and application in large-scale production. Methods A TLC method was established for the identification of R. rosea , A. membranaceus , and C. tinctorius in RAC capsules. A HPLC method with gradient elution and UV detection was established for simultaneous determination of salidroside, tyrosol, hydroxysafflor yellow A (HSYA), anhydrosafflor yellow B (ASYB), and rhodiolin.
