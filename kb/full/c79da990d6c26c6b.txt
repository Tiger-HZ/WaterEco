The groundwater genus Metacrangonyx (Crustacea: Amphipoda: Metacrangonyctidae) is highly diversified in Morocco. Two new species from wells of the South High Atlas are described. Metacrangonyx messoulii sp. n. is distinguished by four dorso-distal robust setae on the peduncle of the third uropod, while M. nicoleae sp. n. has no armature on this peduncle.
