The ecological status of the two nautical mile (2 nm) coastal waters of Bulgaria (BG) and Romania (RO) is evaluated in terms of eutrophication, focusing on various environmental and chemical indicators related to nutrient enrichment and its effects. Applying the Blue2 Modelling Framework (Blue2MF) Black Sea model, we simulate diverse combinations of nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) reduction. By integrating atmospheric and marine conditions, hydrology and environmental dynamics, the model provides a comprehensive framework for analysing the impact of external pressures on the marine environment and can be used to set regional sustainability goals.
