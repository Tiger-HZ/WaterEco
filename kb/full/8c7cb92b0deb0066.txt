Motives: Soil quality is a fundamental but often under-integrated component of regional land-use planning and sustainable land management in rapidly developing tropical environments. This study examines the spatial drivers of soil quality variability across selected states in the South-South region of Nigeria using multivariate and regression modelling techniques. Understanding the dominant drivers of soil quality variability is therefore essential for developing effective land-use management strategies in the region.
