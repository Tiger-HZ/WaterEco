Abstract Reconciling crop productivity with limited water availability remains a central challenge for sustainable cotton production in arid Xinjiang, China. Although deficit irrigation (DI) and planting density are recognized as key management levers, the physiological mechanisms through which their interaction influences canopy photosynthetic capacity and yield maintenance are incompletely understood.
