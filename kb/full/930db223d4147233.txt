Abstract Applied psychology micro-major programs, as emerging interdisciplinary education models, face challenges in student learning quality assessment including complex evaluation dimensions and heterogeneous learning behavior data. Addressing the problem that existing assessment models struggle to capture deep temporal dependencies and key influencing factors in students’ learning processes, this study constructs a deep learning assessment framework integrating Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory networks and multi-head attention mechanisms.
