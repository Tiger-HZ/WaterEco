Abstract Wheat productivity among smallholders in semi-arid sub-Saharan Africa remains low and highly variable, with yields often below attainable levels. While previous studies have identified numerous determinants of yield, most treat irrigation as an exogenous input, providing limited insight into how household, institutional, and farm-management factors influence productivity through supplementary irrigation. This study applied a mediation framework to examine whether supplementary irrigation mediates the relationship between these factors and wheat productivity among 451 smallholder households in the Eritrean highlands.
