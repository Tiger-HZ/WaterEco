Objective To develop an evidence-based quality evaluation index system for Nutrition Nursing Clinics (NNCs) grounded in the Donabedian Structure-Process-Outcome model. Methods A preliminary indicator system was developed through a systematic literature review, expert panel discussions, and a national survey. Utilizing the Delphi method, 17 experts participated in two rounds of consultation. Questionnaires with a 100% effective response rate in both rounds were used to collect expert opinions on the importance of indicators. Indicators were screened based on an average importance score ≥3.5 and a coefficient of variation &amp;lt;0.25.
