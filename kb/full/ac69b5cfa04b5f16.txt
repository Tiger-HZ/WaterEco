Purpose: The main purpose of the study was to determine the concentration of heavy metals (Fe, Zn, Cu, Pb, Cr, and Cd) in the sachet water sample of the Rivers North Senatorial District of Rivers State, Nigeria, and to assess the health hazards. Procedure: Descriptive and experimental design was used whereby thirty-six sachet water samples were purposely selected among the popular brands in eight local government areas. The laboratory of the Nigerian Institute of Marine Pollution and Control, University of Port Harcourt was used to analyze the samples in terms of heavy metals and physicochemical parameters. Also, microbial contamination was determined and Hazard Index (HI) of non-carcinogenic effects was determined by Estimated Daily Intake (EDI) and Target Hazard Quotients (THQ). Findings
