Accurate estimation of carbon gas (CO2 and CH4) emissions from lakes is crucial for understanding the global carbon budget. Carbon gas emissions have been considered greatest in small lakes, however, this conclusion may be biased due to limited samples of diverse lake types and sizes. Remote sensing would allow detailed mapping of regional emissions but has hitherto not been developed. Here, we demonstrate the high accuracy, continuity, and large-scale observation capabilities of optical satellite data in mapping CO2 and CH4 dynamics. Using this innovative approach, we investigated 113 meso-eutrophic lakes in eastern China and estimated diffusive carbon gas fluxes.
