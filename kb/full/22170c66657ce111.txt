Land degradation is a major environmental challenge threatening the productivity and sustainability of dryland ecosystems globally, particularly in sub-Saharan Africa. In Nigeria’s semi-arid regions, declining soil fertility, desert encroachment, and reduced vegetation cover have intensified the vulnerability of rural livelihoods. This study assessed the effectiveness of the half-moon water harvesting technique as a low-cost, sustainable land restoration strategy. The technique involves constructing semi-circular micro-catchments designed to capture and retain surface runoff, thereby improving soil moisture, reducing erosion, and promoting vegetation recovery.
