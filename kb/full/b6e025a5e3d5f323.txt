Abstract A spatial-environmental assessment of the quality of treated drinking water at Al-Baradiyah Water Treatment Plant, Basra (Iraq), in response to increasing salinity intrusion into the Shatt al-Arab River Data-driven based integration methodology were investigated by employing Water Quality Index (WQI), removal efficiency (RE) analysis, GIS-based spatial mapping and artificial neural network (ANN) modelling. Monthly water samples from both raw and treated sources were collected over 2024–2025 for major physicochemical parameters analysis.
