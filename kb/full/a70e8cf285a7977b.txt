Eutrophication, usually considered an overproduction of biomass in water bodies due to an enrichment of nutrients such as phosphorus (P) and nitrogen (N), is a common thread to riverine ecosystems. In Europe, chlorophyll a (Chl-a) concentrations, an indicator for algae biomass, have been successfully reduced from the 1980s by limiting phosphorus input into rivers. However, elevated Chl-a levels in European rivers are still found until today.
