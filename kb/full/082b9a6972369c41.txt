Continuous, real-time monitoring of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in surface water is critical for environmental protection, yet conventional laboratory Gas Chromatography–Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) suffers from analyte loss during sample transport. This study field-validates a portable Membrane Inlet Mass Spectrometry (MIMS) system for direct, on-site monitoring of seven target VOCs (benzene, toluene, xylenes, chlorobenzene, 1,2-dichloroethane, trichloroethylene, and tetrachloroethylene). Laboratory validation established limits of detection between 4 and 8 µg/L, linearity (R2 &gt; 0.98), and acceptable precision and accuracy per AOAC guidelines, benchmarked against headspace GC-MS.
