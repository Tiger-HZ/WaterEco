Abstract High-density nitrogen-vacancy (NV) ensembles are promising platforms for solid-state quantum sensing, but their performance is limited by dipolar interactions and inhomogeneous dephasing. Periodic decoupling sequences such as Waugh-Huber-Haeberlen (WAHUHA) can extend the observed stroboscopic decay time, but a longer fitted lifetime does not necessarily imply improved magnetic-field sensitivity. Here, we experimentally demonstrate this disconnect experimentally. WAHUHA increases the fitted stroboscopic decay time from $T_{2}^{*}$ T 2 ∗ of 0.9 μs to $T_{2,\mathit{eff}}^{*}$ T 2 , eff ∗ of 31 μs, while producing little improvement in dc magnetic-field sensitivity.
