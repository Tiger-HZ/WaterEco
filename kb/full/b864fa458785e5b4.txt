The ModelSEED Biochemistry Database (https://modelseed.org) supplies foundational mass- and charge-balanced reaction networks for metabolic reconstructions. Here we present an update on the biochemistry where the database has been expanded to ∼ 46,000 compounds, and ∼ 56,000 reactions, featuring ∼ 37,000 metabolic structures. We have expanded our approach for handling thermodynamic data, enabling multiple sources of data to be derived, integrated, and presented to the wider research community. We now publish predictions of pKa, reaction energy (and respective uncertainties), and estimates of reaction direction from multiple independent sources.
