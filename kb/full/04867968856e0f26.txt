Propagation plugs remain with the root system after transplant, but whether plug effects persist through crop development is not well defined. ‘Red Velvet’ microtomato was sown in five commercial substrates and finished under common greenhouse nutrient film technique (NFT) conditions. Final germination differed among substrates (60%–100%; P = 0.0027). Coir had the lowest germination, yet surviving coir and Preforma plants were among the largest at transplant and reached reproductive stages earliest. OASIS ® had 100% germination, but smaller transplants and later flowering and fruit maturity. Preforma reached first mature fruit 14 days earlier than Oasis.
