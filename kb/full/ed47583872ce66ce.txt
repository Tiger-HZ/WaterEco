The study investigated the multilayer mineral composition of the snacks produced from pumpkin, sweet potatoes, dates, tiger nuts and native bananas. The organoleptic/sensory evaluation was carried out on the samples. A nine-point Hedonic scale and Analysis of variance (ANOVA) was used (see appendix I). The simple random sampling technique was used to select a sample size of 15 academic and non-academic staff of the Department of Nutrition and Dietetics of the Imo State University, Owerri as judges.
