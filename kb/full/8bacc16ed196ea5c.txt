Groundwater contamination by 90Sr remains an important component of the long-term environmental impact of the Chornobyl accident, while the spatial coverage of monitoring observations limits direct assessment of contamination patterns across the Chornobyl Exclusion Zone (CEZ). This study evaluates the applicability of machine-learning methods for regional assessment of 90Sr groundwater contamination and identification of its principal controlling factors. Random Forest (RF) and XGBoost models were developed using groundwater 90Sr observations for 2015-2020 and source-related, hydrogeological, geochemical, topographic, and monitoring-well predictors.
