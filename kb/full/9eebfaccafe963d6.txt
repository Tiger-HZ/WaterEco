Abstract On-surface synthesis enables the fabrication of molecular complexes, which cannot be obtained through traditional solution chemistry. Generally, metal substrates have been used due to their catalytic properties. Here, a tripod-like magnetic molecular complex, (C60)3–Co, has been created on an inert nonmetal substrate, graphite, and characterized with the scanning tunneling microscopy through on-surface synthesis. By deposition of Co atoms onto a double layer of C60 supported on a graphite substrate, the Co atoms get trapped within the room temperature C60 layers before the formation of (C60)3–Co via a totally self-organized reaction at 543 K.
