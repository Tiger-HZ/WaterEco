Open water access promotes natural water-related behaviors of ducks but too much water exposure may lead to higher litter wetness and hygiene problems in semi-dry housing systems. The present study was conducted to evaluate the effects of graded water-contact areas in trays on water use behavior, floor condition, plumage cleanliness, foot health, and growth performance of Co ducks. The four treatments were defined as follows: T1 – drinking water only, with no tray-based water-contact area (0 cm²/duck); T2 – drinking water plus a tray-based water-contact area of 25 cm²/duck; T3 – drinking water plus a tray-based water-contact area of 50 cm²/duck; and T4 – drinking water plus a tray-based water-contact area of 100 cm²/duck.
