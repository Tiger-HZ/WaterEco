Abstract Phytoplankton communities are sensitive to global change drivers, yet the combined effects of warming and nutrient enrichment remain insufficiently explored in tropical freshwater ecosystems. Here, we tested the individual and interactive effects of increased temperature and eutrophication on phytoplankton structure using outdoor mesocosms in a tropical region. We hypothesized that both stressors would influence community responses, promoting higher phytoplankton density, increased chlorophyll- a , reduced species richness, and shifts toward tolerant Chlorophyta and Cyanobacteria, with stronger effects expected when warming and nutrient enrichment occurred together.
