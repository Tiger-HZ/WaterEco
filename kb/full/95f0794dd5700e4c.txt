In the frame of Subtask A of the SHC Task 69: Solar Hot Water for 2030 best practice examples as well as common systems in different regions worldwide have been collected to provide an overview of the status quo of solar thermosyphon systems as well as PV hot water systems. The regions considered were chosen according to the classification used in the IEA SHC Solar Heat Worldwide report [1], namely Asia excluding China1, China, Europe, Latin America, Middle East and North Africa, North America, Oceania and Sub-Saharan Africa.
