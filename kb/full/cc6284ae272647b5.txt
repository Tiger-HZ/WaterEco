The middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River are a major grain and oilseed production region in China, where crop planting structure and phenological timing are closely related to agricultural water demand and regional water security. This study developed a multi-source remote sensing framework to map crop types, planting areas, and key phenological stages from 2018 to 2024. Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, and Landsat-8/9 data were integrated to construct optical, radar, vegetation, water, and yellowness time-series features. Time-weighted dynamic time warping was used to identify rice, wheat, and maize, while a rule-based sample generation and one-class classification method was applied to map rapeseed.
