The current study evaluated the genetic characterization and differentiation of oil families in the units of the Yamama, Nahr Umr, and Mishrif reservoirs of the Ratawi oil field, south of Iraq. The combination of bulk properties, stable carbon isotopes, and molecular biomarkers reveals a distinct pattern of hydrocarbon generation, migration, and accumulation, influenced by salt-induced tectonics in the studyarea.
