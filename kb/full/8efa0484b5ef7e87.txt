Abstract High-resolution measurements of near-surface temperature are essential for understanding environmental responses to extreme weather events, yet are rarely available from dense, regularly spaced in situ measurement grids. Here, we analyse temperature measurements derived from a dense network of over 3,000 nodal seismometers deployed across a 6 km² area in North Yorkshire, UK, during the July 2022 heatwave, using data from embedded microcontroller sensors.
