ABSTRACT With shrinking arable land, environmental degradation, and water scarcity threatening traditional agriculture, soilless farming emerges as a sustainable alternative. This study examines the impact of various nutrient media on the growth, yield, and nutritional composition of Sorrel (Hibiscus sabdariffa) and Malabar Spinach (Basella alba) grown in EcoPeat (cocopeat). Four nutrient media were tested: municipal water (control), hydroponic nutrient solution (N1), vermiwash (N2), and vegetable wash (N3). Growth indicators such as root and shoot length were assessed over four weeks, while nutritional parameters (moisture, ash, vitamins, and minerals) were analyzed using standardized laboratory techniques.
