In real-world industrial manufacturing, surface defect segmentation is severely challenged by the extreme scarcity of annotated defect samples, which renders exhaustively supervised approaches impractical. Although meta-learning based few-shot segmentation paradigms have made initial progress, existing methods still suffer from two prominent limitations. First, the limited number of support set samples leads to insufficient feature diversity, rendering the model prone to overfitting during training. Second, feature extraction restricted to a single hierarchical level and lacking contextual guidance constrains the representational capacity for complex and multi-scale defects.
