Introduction. Sleep disturbances in children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis remain an insufficiently studied issue. The Child and Adolescent Sleep Checklist (CASC) is a tool used to assess sleep in children; however, evidence regarding its use in patients with juvenile idiopathic arthritis remains limited. Clarifying the relationship between disease activity, other clinical parameters, and CASC scores may contribute to a more personalized approach to the management of these patients. The aim of this study is to assess sleep quality in children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis in the Komi Republic and to examine the associations between disease activity, demographic characteristics, and the total CASC score.
