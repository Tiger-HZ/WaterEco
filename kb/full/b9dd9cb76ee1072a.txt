I-sharing (i.e., the perception that one shares the same subjective experience) with a romantic partner positively predicts relationship satisfaction and perceptions that one’s partner knows one’s true self (i.e., perceived partner true self knowledge. The current work extends the research on I-sharing in romantic relationships by investigating whether daily fluctuations in perceptions of I-sharing with a romantic partner positively relate to daily romantic relationship outcomes such as relationship quality (i.e., relationship flourishing and satisfaction), perceived partner true self knowledge (PTSK), and perceived compassionate acts towards one’s romantic partner.
