Cadmium is a highly toxic and non-biodegradable heavy metal that poses serious environmental and public health risks due to its persistence, bioaccumulation, and widespread industrial usage. Rapid, sensitive, and simple detection of cadmium (Cd 2+ ) contamination remains an important challenge for environmental monitoring. The present work describes a green-synthesized, nano-plasmonic sensing platform incorporating gold nanoparticles (GNPs) synthesized from Annona squamosa leaf extract (ASLE) for the visual and UV–visible detection of Cd 2+ ions in aqueous medium. The synthesis of ASLE-derived gold nanoparticles (ASLE-GNPs) was optimized by varying solution-pH and precursor-to-extract ratio, and were well-characterize with several analytical techniques.
