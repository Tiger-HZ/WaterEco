Packaged drinking water plants depend on synchronized filling, capping, date coding, labelling, and shrink- wrapping operations to maintain production rate, product quality, and dispatch readiness. This study presents a case-based discrete-event simulation and virtual commissioning study of the 500 mL packaged drinking water packaging section at Tashi Beverages Limited, Bhutan. Operational data collected from the plant, including station processing times, weekly downtime estimates, rework assumptions, production target, rated capacity, buffer capacity, and effective operating time, were used to parameterize a Siemens Tecnomatix Plant Simulation model.
