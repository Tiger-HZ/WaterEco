This study was conducted to evaluate the impact of various irrigation treatments on the performance and efficiency of a drip irrigation system used in tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) cultivation. The research focused on the effects of emitter clogging, emission uniformity (EU), and Christiansen's coefficient of uniformity (CU) on system reliability and crop productivity. Six different treatments were assessed, and the results demonstrated highly significant differences among them. Treatments T3 and T5 consistently showed superior performance, maintaining high EU and CU values with manageable clogging levels, resulting in significantly higher yields and water use efficiency.
