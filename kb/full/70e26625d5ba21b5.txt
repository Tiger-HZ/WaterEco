Abstract Purpose This study aimed to quantify the losses of water, soil, and nutrients through surface runoff during natural rainfall events and to evaluate different soil management systems for garlic cultivation. Methods The experiment was conducted using a clayey Cambisol in 2023 and 2024. The crop sequence included cover crops before garlic during autumn/winter, followed by common beans in summer. The treatments included were as follows: (i) CT: conventional tillage with soil disturbance and no cover crops before garlic; (ii) BO: garlic over black oat residue; (iii) FR: garlic over forage radish; and (iv) WP: garlic over winter pea, with the three cover crops cultivated under a no-tillage (NT) system.
