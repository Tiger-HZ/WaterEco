Abstract Using panel data from various provinces in the western region from 2010 to 2024 as the sample, this paper constructs evaluation indicator systems for the development levels of new-quality productive forces and integrated urban-rural development at the provincial level, respectively. The entropy method is employed to measure the development levels of new-quality productive forces, and a coupling and coordination model is used to evaluate the degree of coupling between these two systems. This paper also examines the spatial clustering of the “new-quality productive forces” and integrated development systems through spatial autocorrelation analysis.
