Abstract Seaweed-based biostimulants have emerged as promising sustainable agricultural inputs due to their potential to enhance crop productivity, improve nutrient use efficiency, and increase tolerance to abiotic stresses, thereby reducing dependence on synthetic fertilizers. However, the environmental performance of their production systems remains insufficiently quantified. This study presents a cradle-to-gate life cycle assessment (LCA) of a formulated seaweed-based biostimulant derived from Kappaphycus alvarezii, based on representation of a real-world industrial-scale production system in India.
