Short Communication
Development of a Novel Oil–Water Emulsion 
Formulation Utilizing Polyglyceryl-10 
(Behenate/Eicosadioate)
Kazuki Matsuo1*, Yui Sugiura2, Nobuhide Tsuji2, Kaoru Maeyama2
1 Technology and Development Department, Premier Anti-Aging Co., Ltd., 
34F, Toranomon Hills Station Tower, 2–6–1, Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo, 105–5534, JAPAN
2 Mikimoto Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., 1425, Kurose-cho, Ise, Mie, 516–0018, JAPAN
A novel oil –water (O –W) emulsion formulation using polyglyceryl-10 (behenate/eicosadio -
ate) (PGBE) was developed and its emulsiﬁcation mechanism was investigated. Stable emul-
sions were obtained with 5 wt% PGBE even at a high internal oil phase ratio of 60%. Polarized 
optical microscopy revealed optical anisotropy in both emulsion droplets and dried ﬁlms. X-ray 
scattering and scanning electron microscopy indicated that hydrated PGBE crystals adsorb at the 
O–W interface of the emulsion droplets. Bridging interactions among the crystals formed a net -
work structure in the continuous phase, enabling low-ﬂuidity emulsions without thickeners. The 
formulation also showed a tendency to increase stratum corneum moisture content, suggesting 
its potential for cosmetic formulation design.
Key words : emulsion, crystal, X-ray scattering, diﬀerential scanning calorimetry, skincare, 
rheology, polyglycerol, scanning electron microscope, polymorphism, formulation, moisturizing
