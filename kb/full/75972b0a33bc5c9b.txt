To support water resource management, flood control, disaster mitigation, and environmental protection in the middle reaches of the Jinsha River, precipitation and runoff data from five monitoring stations—Shigu, A’hai, Zhongjiang, Jin’anqiao, and Panzhihua—were jointly used as multivariate inputs to forecast runoff at Panzhihua Station. To obtain well-separated intrinsic mode functions (IMFs) at different frequencies while avoiding the use of future information during feature construction, SVMD was implemented in a rolling manner, with only the historical observations available up to each forecasting origin used for decomposition.
