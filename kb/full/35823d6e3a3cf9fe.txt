Sedimentation stability and gel strength are critical performance metrics of water-based testing fluids, yet their responses to multiple downhole factors have not been systematically compared within a single fluid system. Focusing on the Dengying Formation gas reservoir in the Sichuan Basin, this study systematically investigates the individual effects of temperature, pH, additives (viscosifier and dispersant), solid-phase composition (bentonite, sand, barite), and acid contamination via controlled-variable experiments. Temperature exhibited a pronounced influence on the testing fluid, with viscosity and gel strength decreasing as temperature increased, particularly above 150 °C.
