Controlled nuclear fusion remains a primary objective in advanced energy research, traditionally requiring massive external energy inputs for plasma heating and confinement. This study presents an empirical investigation into Tomoiu Internal Confinement Fusion (TICF), a hybrid chemical-thermonuclear approach designed to generate and confine transient, high-temperature microplasmas within a localized hydrogen-air-water environment using internally self-generated acoustic, magnetic and electromagnetic energies without requiring externally supplied electrical power.
