A multi-faceted, multi-factorial interaction of technical, financial, social, and institutional factors limits the sustainability of community-managed water systems (CMWS) in Nepal over the long run. This paper goes beyond the descriptive analysis to utilise a structural equation modelling (SEM) framework as an empirical test to quantify the association between these key determinants. The sample size used was 360 households and 36 water committee members in three different ecological regions of Nepal which were chosen through a stratified random survey.
