A permeable shrinking sheet is a surface with time-dependent permeability and the sheet shrinks with time. In this study, the slip and heat sink/source effects are considered as an integrated medium which can model trihybrid nanofluids (THNFs) in a cohesive manner. Base fluid is water ( H 2 O ) with dispersed nanoparticles of aluminum oxide ( A l 2 O 3 ), copper (Cu), and copper oxide (CuO). Three different methods are used: (i) A numerical study to explore the mathematical model, which shows the presence of dual solutions; (ii) A stability study to find stability nature of the dual solutions; and (iii) A statistical optimization to discover the maximum heat transfer rates by applying Response Surface Methodology (RSM) and Grey Relational Analysis (GRA) for only stable solution.
