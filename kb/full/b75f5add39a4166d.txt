Abstract. Global average temperatures and the frequency of extreme climatic events, including heat waves, are increasing due to the anthropogenic rise in greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. Shallow aquatic areas can potentially buffer some anthropogenic alterations, acting as net sinks for nutrients and GHGs such as CO2, CH4 and N2O.
