Abstract The response of terrestrial climate to future warming remains debated. The Pliocene, with CO 2 levels similar to today, is an important analog for the future. The East Asian monsoon marginal region is one of the most climate sensitive regions on Earth. Based on contemporaneous aeolian sediments and aquatic‐influenced alluvial sediments from the Weihe Basin, we quantitatively reconstructed land surface temperatures using lipid biomarkers during the Pliocene‐Pleistocene. A source‐affinity classifier model of alluvial‐fan sediments demonstrates that sedimentary changes do not obscure the reconstructed paleotemperature trend.
