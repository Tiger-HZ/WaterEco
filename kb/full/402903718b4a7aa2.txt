Background Deep-inspiration breath-hold (DIBH) reduces cardiac and pulmonary radiation exposure during radiotherapy for left-sided breast cancer. Surface-guided radiotherapy (SGRT) and optical surface monitoring systems (OSMS) can support patient positioning and breath-hold monitoring, but evidence specific to SGRT/OSMS-assisted DIBH workflows remains fragmented. Methods PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, and Cochrane CENTRAL were searched from inception to May 21, 2026. Studies using SGRT/OSMS-assisted DIBH and reporting extractable dosimetric or image-verified setup outcomes were eligible. Effects were expressed as mean differences (MDs) with 95% confidence intervals (CIs); dosimetric MDs were defined as SGRT/OSMS-assisted DIBH minus FB, with negative values indicating lower doses.
