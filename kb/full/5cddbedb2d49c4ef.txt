Abstract Trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) is a metabolite of e.g., pesticides, refrigerants and propellants, and it is considered a ‘non-relevant metabolite’ of pesticides under the EU Drinking Water Directive (DWD) as current exposures have not been found to provide a consumer health risk. Since an EU-harmonised concept for the derivation of drinking water guidance values is unavailable, various guidance values have been proposed or implemented on the Member State level for TFA in drinking water. This causes uncertainty with respect to the adequacy of such values. To address this uncertainty, the scientific rationale underlying the different guidance values is reviewed here.
