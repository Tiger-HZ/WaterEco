As the centenary of Alajos Hauszmann's death is commemorated in 2026, his legacy continues to shape the understanding of Hungarian architectural history at the turn of the century. This study presents and analyzes a previously unpublished manuscript by architect Dr. Jenő Kismarty-Lechner, written in 1947 to mark the centenary of Hauszmann's birth. Serving as a newly discovered primary source, this document fundamentally nuances the post-1945 perception of Hauszmann's oeuvre. Kismarty-Lechner, a former student and associate, challenges the strictly historicizing canon surrounding his master by identifying distinct Secessionist (Art Nouveau) elements in Hauszmann's late works, specifically within the interior spaces of the Royal Palace.
