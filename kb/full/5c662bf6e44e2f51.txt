Industrialization in rapidly growing urban centers has intensified environmental concerns, particularly regarding the discharge of untreated or poorly treated effluents into aquatic systems. This study evaluates the seasonal dynamics and pollution load of industrial wastewater within Kano metropolis, Nigeria, with emphasis on physicochemical characteristics and associated health risk implications. Wastewater samples were collected from twelve industrial discharge sites during both wet and dry seasons, covering influent, effluent, upstream, and downstream points.
