Distributed land-surface models require parameters at every grid cell, yet calibration data are unevenly informative, streamflow is measured directly but only at sparse gauges, while satellite evapotranspiration and soil moisture cover the domain indirectly. We present SCALE, a three-stage framework for learning, refining, and regionalizing parameters across continental domains . Stage A trains multivariable differentiable parameter learning (MdPL) from attributes to parameters through a frozen neural surrogate, jointly constrained by evapotranspiration, soil moisture, and streamflow and anchored to a pedotransfer prior.
