Densities of hydropsychids (Trichoptera) on different-sized stones were compared before and after a winter flood, and the effects of rock movement (a likely form of disturbance during floods) on these organisms were tested. Before the flood, the density of hydropsychids was an order of magnitude higher on large than on small stones; after the flood, densities on larger stones had fallen to levels similar to those on small stones. In a four-week colonization experiment over the period during which the flood occurred, densities of hydropsychids were compared on bricks of two sizes, which were either fixed to the bed of the stream so that they could not move, or unfixed and able to move with changes in flow.
