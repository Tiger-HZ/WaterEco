Harmful algal blooms (HABs) are recurring problems in New Jersey (NJ). Some HABs produce organic phycotoxins that are detrimental to both ecosystem organisms and human health. While most studies on HABs and phycotoxins focus on freshwater in NJ, comparative measurements of phycotoxins in marine coastal environments are understudied. In the summer of 2025, surface water from 11 freshwater, 3 brackish rivers, and 10 coastal sites in northern and central NJ was collected and analyzed for 12 dissolved phycotoxins using a combination of solid phase extraction and liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry.
