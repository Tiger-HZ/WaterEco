ABSTRACT The development of stable, durable cathodes with high oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) activity remains a critical challenge in solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) research. This work employs a Sn‐In co‐doping strategy to synthesize a cathode material with the nominal composition Ba(Co 0.7 Fe 0.3 ) 0.8 (Sn 0.5 In 0.5 ) 0.2 O 3‐δ . The co‑doping strategy transforms the material structure into a more symmetric cubic phase (Pm‐3m) and significantly enhances its oxygen vacancy concentration along with ORR catalytic activity.
