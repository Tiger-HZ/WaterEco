Abstract Fermented fertilizers are widely used in morel cultivation, yet their effects on soil nutrient status and bacterial-fungal community responses remain unclear. This study compared one unfertilized control (CK) and six representative fermented fertilizer treatments (Ms1–Ms6) in Morchella sextelata cultivation. Cultivation performance was evaluated using fruiting time, yield-related traits, and nutritional quality, while soil physicochemical properties, bacterial and fungal diversity, community composition, and their relationships with environmental factors were analyzed. Comprehensive evaluation ranked the treatments as Ms1 &gt; Ms2 &gt; Ms4 &gt; Ms3 &gt; Ms5 &gt; Ms6 &gt; CK, with Ms1 and Ms2 showing better overall performance.
