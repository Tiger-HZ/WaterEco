A comprehensive analysis of Landolphia owariensis fruit using standard methods examined both its nutritional composition and anti-nutritional factors. Nutritionally, the fruit contains low levels of protein (0.55%) and fat (0.66%), and its crude fiber (1.20%) which contributes modestly to dietary needs. The carbohydrate content was measured at 15.64%, offering approximately 70.70 kcal per serving, which positions the fruit as a moderate energy source while maintaining a relatively low caloric count. The ash content of 1.57% suggests the presence of minerals, albeit in moderate quantities, underscoring the fruit's overall nutritional profile.
