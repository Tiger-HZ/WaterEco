Abstract Burial in sediments as organic carbon (OC) is the key pathway for C reduction. However, the C fixation mechanisms in fishpond sediments remain unclear. In this study, sediments were taken from fishponds raising different fish species. Different OC fractions including microbial biomass C (MBC), enzymatic activities, and C uptake indices including microbial C use efficiency (CUE) were obtained. The microbial communities were identified using 16S rRNA high‐throughput sequencing. The genes, cbbL and cbbM, encoding Calvin‐Benson‐Bassham (CBB) cycle were analyzed to decipher the community assembly regulation of C‐fixing bacteria.
