ABSTRACT Background: The Pathway to Excellence (Pathway) program recognizes healthcare organizations that support positive nurse work environments, yet the impact of Pathway designation and engagement in the Pathway journey before designation on outcomes remain understudied. Purpose: To examine nurse job outcomes, nurses' assessments of the work environment, and patient satisfaction in Pathway-designated hospitals and hospitals pursuing Pathway designation. Methods: This cross-sectional study integrated data from the 2021 RN4CAST Study, the American Hospital Association Annual Survey, and Hospital Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems.
