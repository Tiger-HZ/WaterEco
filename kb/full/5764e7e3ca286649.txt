Purpose The purpose of this study is to analyze the influence of spatial encroachments in coastal area on the quality of life (QoL) of residents in the Dehiwala coastal belt, Western province in Sri Lanka. Design/methodology/approach A mixed-methods approach was used, incorporating both, quantitative and qualitative analyses. Primary data was collected through questionnaire surveys and in-depth interviews, respectively, with residents and professionals to capture diverse perspectives. Three key variables of infrastructural, social and ecological were used to analyze the encroachments, and the psychological domain was used to describe the QoL.
