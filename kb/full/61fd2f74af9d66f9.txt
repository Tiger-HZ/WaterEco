Large optics have become mandatory with the new generation of large telescopes. In this work, we aim to refine the optical surface estimation of a mirror. By using partial surface measurements, as in a standard sub-aperture sampling approach, we populate our surface using a continuously refined reconstructor that is optimal in a least-squares sense, converging to a stable solution that ensures surface continuity. Simultaneously, we estimate both the cavity and alignment errors for each individual measurement of the partial optical surface. The basis chosen for the estimation consists of the Zernike modes by Noll. Optical surfaces are estimated together with cavity and alignment parameters in a single, unified minimization process.
