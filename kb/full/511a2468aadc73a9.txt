Additional information is needed to simultaneously document bat spatiotemporal occurrence at caves, rivers, and standing water. We estimated simultaneous probabilities of occurrence of eight bat species of conservation concern at 14 sites in three habitat types (i.e., caves, a large river, and ponds) from June to September in 2022 and 2023. We used acoustic data and generalized linear mixed-effects models to document bat occurrence by day of year and habitat type. Our detectors operated 1,983 detector-nights. We used 5,423 nightly species occurrences, and all bat species occurred in all the habitats we sampled.
