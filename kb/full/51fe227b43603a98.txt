Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) in urban aquatic environments is an emerging public health concern, particularly in rapidly growing cities with inadequate wastewater and solid-waste management. This study assessed the occurrence, distribution and potential dissemination pathways of AMR across diverse water environments in Chennai, South India. Samples were collected from sewage treatment plants and associated upstream and downstream locations, an industrial wastewater detention tank, groundwater near a dumpsite, and community-managed water systems.
