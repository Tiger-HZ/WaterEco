Abstract The updated version of ISO 7218:2024, titled “Microbiology of the food chain—General requirements and guidance for microbiological examinations”, serves as a significant horizontal standard that underpins microbiological testing throughout the food chain. The 2024 update not only involves editorial refinements but also incorporates a clearer risk-based approach to sample management, contamination prevention, internal quality assurance, quantitative result handling, and laboratory reporting. These modifications are particularly important for official control microbiology laboratories, where analytical outcomes must be scientifically sound, operationally traceable, and sufficiently reliable to support regulatory decision-making.
