Abstract Although it is well established that microalgae take up metals and other contaminants from water and it has been suggested that algae may play a significant role in mobilizing sediment bound contaminants, there has been little research on the uptake of sediment‐associated contaminants by microalgae. This may be important for microphytobenthos, which is closely associated with sediments. We report on laboratory experiments investigating the uptake of cadmium (Cd) from sediment and water by Cylindrotheca closterium over 96 h. The role of microalgae in the partitioning of Cd between sediment and water was also investigated.
