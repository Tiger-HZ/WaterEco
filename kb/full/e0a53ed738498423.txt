The Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) satellite altimetry mission has provided unprecedented global, highly resolved measurements of ocean topography and Terrestrial Water Storage anomalies (TWS). The latter can provide invaluable information about hydrology and land hydroclimate. Yet, the spatial and temporal heterogeneity and irregularity of the SWOT-based TWS data can hinder their use in practice in different disciplines. We address this limitation by presenting a novel, machine learning-based methodology named SWOT-Net. SWOT-Net utilizes probabilistic spatiotemporal graph convolutional recurrent neural networks to (i) model the irregularly sampled TWS data, (ii) consider the spatiotemporal dynamics of TWS, and (iii) quantify the modeling uncertainty.
