Abstract Lacustrine sediments preserved in the Himalayan region constitute valuable archives for reconstructing Quaternary climate variability, monsoon dynamics, neotectonic activity, and landscape evolution. However, palaeolimnological investigations in the Indian Himalaya have largely relied on direct sediment coring without prior geophysical characterization of lake basins, increasing the risk of recovering disturbed, discontinuous, or incomplete sedimentary sequences. This study evaluates the potential of integrated multi-frequency water-borne Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) for simultaneous lake bathymetry, sub-bottom stratigraphic imaging, and identification of optimal sediment coring location points in the Lesser Himalayan lakes-Bhimtal and Sattal lakes in the Nainital District, Utta
