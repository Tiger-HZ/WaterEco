Optimizing fertilization strategies is key to increasing peanut yields and maintaining soil health, but it is still unclear how the proportion of basal fertilizer (applied before sowing) and topdressing (applied through drip irrigation during growth) systematically affects the “soil microbe plant” system under integrated drip irrigation and water–fertilizer management.
