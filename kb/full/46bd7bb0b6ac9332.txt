This systematic study summarizes empirical science from hydrogeology, hydrogeochemistry, climatology, and agricultural engineering to assess how climatic extremes, unregulated groundwater extraction, and infrastructure breakdowns may have stressed groundwater in District Shaheed Benazirabad(SB) in the Province of Sindh in Pakistan. A onfined to non-confined district along the left bank of the Indus River is characterized by a hyper-arid to semi-arid climate, low relief (12.5 cm/km slope), and a shallow unconfined alluvial aquifer that is recharged predominantly through seepage from unlined canals.
