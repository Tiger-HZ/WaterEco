Water scarcity and water pollution are among the most critical global challenges of the twenty-first century. Rapid urbanization, population growth, industrialization, and climate change have significantly increased the demand for clean, safe, and sustainable water resources. These growing concerns underscore the urgent need for innovative and efficient water treatment technologies capable of addressing complex environmental issues while supporting sustainable development. This book provides a comprehensive exploration of contemporary advancements in water treatment, with a particular focus on chemical technologies and innovative materials developed to overcome present and future water-related challenges. It highlights the latest research, emerging methodologies, and sustainable practices that are reshaping the field of water purification and wastewater management. Designed for researchers, environmental scientists, engineers, academicians, students, and policymakers, this volume presents a multidisciplinary perspective by integrating concepts from chemistry, environmental science, engineering, and sustainability studies. It explores cutting-edge developments including green chemistry, nanotechnology, carbon-based materials, metal-organic frameworks (MOFs), advanced oxidation processes, membrane bioreactors, microbial fuel cells, and smart water management systems. These technologies demonstrate significant potential for improving treatment efficiency, reducing environmental impacts, and ensuring long-term water security. The chapters provide a balanced discussion of both conventional and advanced water treatment techniques, covering topics such as wastewater treatment processes, adsorption technologies, photocatalytic degradation, membrane-based systems, and emerging digital solutions for intelligent water management. Special emphasis is placed on the application of zeolites, carbon nanomaterials, and photo-nanocatalysts for the effective removal of contaminants, alongside detailed explanations of the chemical mechanisms that govern these treatment processes. Beyond technological innovation, the book also addresses critical environmental and infrastructural issues, including heavy metal contamination, aging water distribution systems, resource recovery, and the sustainability of treatment operations. It offers practical insights into integrating environmentally responsible practices with modern technologies to develop resilient and efficient water management solutions capable of meeting global demands. By adopting an interdisciplinary approach, each chapter enhances the reader's understanding of the chemical principles, engineering concepts, and emerging innovations that are driving the future of water treatment. The content is intended to support both academic learning and practical applications while encouraging continued research and collaboration in the pursuit of sustainable water resources.
