AbstractThis article presents the results of a comprehensive study on the occurrence, epizootological characteristics, diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of chilodonellosis caused by Chilodonella cyprini in carp fish (Cyprinus carpio) cultivated in artificial water bodies of the Samarkand region, Republic of Uzbekistan. The research focuses on the identification of the main causative agents, including Chilodonella cyprini, Chilodonella piscicola, and Chilodonella hexasticha, their biological characteristics, and their influence on the health condition of fish populations. The study analyzes the major factors contributing to the development and spread of infection, including unfavorable environmental conditions, changes in water temperature, deterioration of hydrochemical parameters, organic pollution of ponds, high stocking density, and weakened physiological state of fish during winter periods. Clinical symptoms and pathological changes associated with the disease were investigated through external examination and microscopic analysis of samples collected from the skin, fins, and gills of infected fish. The effectiveness of therapeutic measures using sodium chloride and malachite green solutions was evaluated, demonstrating their role in controlling parasitic infestation. The obtained results confirm that early diagnosis, appropriate treatment, and systematic preventive measures are essential for reducing fish mortality, improving fish health, and ensuring sustainable development of carp aquaculture in the Samarkand region and similar aquatic farming environments.
