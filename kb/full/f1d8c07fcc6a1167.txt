Efficient manipulation of spoof surface plasmon polaritons (SSPPs) is of considerable interest for integrated plasmonic systems. However, the strong momentum mismatch between highly confined SSPP modes and free-space radiation fundamentally limits controllable out-coupling and wavefront engineering. Here, we propose and experimentally demonstrate a reconfigurable SSPP radiation platform based on a tunable phase-gradient metasurface. The device integrates a broadband coplanar waveguide-to-SSPP transition with a metasurface composed of rotationally anisotropic unit cells.
