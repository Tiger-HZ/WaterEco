Artisanal chocolate manufacturing has grown rapidly in West Java, Indonesia, where food safety data remain scarce. West Java's volcanic soils contain naturally elevated cadmium, which cacao absorbs into the cotyledon regardless of processing, and micro-enterprises lacking validated HACCP systems and laboratory access raise microbiological and regulatory concerns. Sixteen chocolate bars (eight dark, eight white) from eight BPOM-registered West Java producers were analysed in triplicate (48 observations per parameter) between May and December 2025. TVC, TYMC, and Salmonella spp. were assessed by ISO methods, Pb and Cd by ICP-MS, and all producers roasted at ≥120°C for ≥5 minutes as their control point.
