Abstract Microwave imaging (MWI) has emerged as a promising non-ionizing, cost-effective technique for breast cancer detection. However, the influence of key system design choices remains poorly characterized. This study aims to systematically investigate how antenna array geometry, tumor characteristics, and tissue dielectric properties influence radar-based image reconstruction using a robust computational pipeline. A total of 575 different scenarios were simulated and analyzed, combining 25 distinct breast models with varying tumor configurations and 23 antenna configurations (including 20 randomly selected antenna subsets and 3 uniform arrays).
