The Sea of Marmara is a semi-enclosed basin undergoing rapid ecological change driven by eutrophication, recurrent mucilage events, and large-scale macroalgal proliferation. In such systems, benthic grazers play an important role in shaping habitat structure and ecosystem resilience. This study provides the first integrated assessment of population structure, growth, reproductive dynamics, and exploitation status of the sea urchin Paracentrotus lividus in the northeastern Sea of Marmara, Monthly sampling was conducted at four coastal stations between November 2023 to October 2024, where 2,400 individuals were analyzed using morphometrics, gonadosomatic index (GSI), histology, and length-frequency based growth and mortality models.
