Increasing competition in the culinary industry requires restaurants not only to attract new customers but also to retain existing customers through satisfaction and repeat purchases. This study aims to analyze the effects of brand image, product quality, and loyalty card on customer satisfaction and repeat order, and to examine the mediating role of customer satisfaction among regular customers of Tsukamie Noodle Bar Bandung. This quantitative study used a survey method. The sample consisted of 51 regular customers who held loyalty cards and were selected using saturated sampling. Data were collected through a Likert-scale questionnaire and analyzed using Structural Equation Modeling–Partial Least Squares (SEM-PLS) with SmartPLS 3.0.
