Wetlands in Bangladesh are crucial for aquatic biodiversity and small-scale fisheries, yet they are increasingly vulnerable due to declining fisheries resources and socio-economic challenges affecting dependent communities. This study aimed to document the livelihood conditions of fishing communities with the physical features, biodiversity, and resource-use characteristics of two important fisheries-dependent wetlands in northern Bangladesh: Choroi Beel (Rajshahi) and Singhimari Beel (Nilphamari). A mixed-methods approach was employed, including household surveys (n = 57), focus group discussions, key informant interviews, and field observations.
