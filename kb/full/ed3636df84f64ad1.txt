BACKGROUND Short-video platforms have become important sources of health information for patients and the public. However, the quality and reliability of online information on glioma remain unclear. OBJECTIVE This study aimed to evaluate the content characteristics, quality, and reliability of glioma-related videos on TikTok and Bilibili. METHODS A cross-sectional analysis was conducted by searching for glioma-related videos on TikTok and Bilibili. Relevant videos were screened according to predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria. Data on video duration, engagement metrics, uploader identity, and content domains were collected.
