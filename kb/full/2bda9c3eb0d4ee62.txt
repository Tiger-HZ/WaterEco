Deployed underwater acoustic target recognition (UATR) classifiers will meet vessel types they never trained on, yet how badly standard pipelines cope lacks controlled measurement. We report a six-protocol open-set recognition (OSR) pilot on four recording-disjoint corpora: four intra-dataset class-holdout protocols plus two cross-library protocols (one corpus known, another's test classes all unknown), extending our zero-transfer finding to OSR. A ResNet-18 classifier trained with cross-entropy (CE) or the entropic open-set loss (EOS) is scored with maximum softmax probability (MSP), the energy score, and OpenMax (3 seeds).
