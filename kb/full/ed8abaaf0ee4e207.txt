The study was conducted to determine the food safety knowledge, attitude and practices of road-side vendors of ready to eat grilled catfish in Awka metropolis, Anambra state, Nigeria. Cross-sectional survey was adopted in selecting a sample of vendors. This sample was derived from 20 entities randomly selected from four predetermined towns that were considered to have high concentration of these operators. Thereafter, a structured questionnaire comprised of items on socio-demographic factors of vendors, their food safety knowledge, attitude and practices was used in collecting data for the study.
