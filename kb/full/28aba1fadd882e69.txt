Abstract The Isfahan-Borkhar plain, located within the Sanandaj-Sirjan structural zone and mainly composed of fine-grained sediments, has experienced significant land subsidence in recent years. This study evaluates land subsidence potential by integrating conventional weighting methods, fuzzy logic models, and artificial intelligence techniques. Seven conditioning factors were considered: aquifer media, land use, groundwater pumping rate, recharge, alluvium thickness, distance from faults, and groundwater table decline. An initial land subsidence potential (LSP) map was generated using the simple additive weighting framework (SAWF), followed by an AHP-based LSP map using previously reported analytic hierarchy process (AHP) weights.
