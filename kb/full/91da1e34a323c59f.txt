Quality management has undergone significant advances and transformations driven by digital technology, leading to the emergence of Quality 5.0 (Q5.0). In this sense, organizations require comprehensive guidance to adapt their processes and incorporate more human-centric, sustainable, and resilient values. This chapter aims to propose drivers for the development and improvement of Q5.0, with the objective of supporting the modernization of quality management within organizations in light of Industry 5.0. To develop this proposal, a content analysis of relevant, recent, and reliable scientific literature on this topic was conducted.
