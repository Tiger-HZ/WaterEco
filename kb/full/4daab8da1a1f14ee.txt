The water column of Lake Baikal is extremely weakly#x2014;but permanently—stratified below 250 m. Despite the thickness of this relatively stagnant water mass of more than 1000 m, the water age (time since last contact with the atmosphere) is only slightly more than a decade, indicating large‐scale advective exchange. In the stratified deep water, the fate of water constituents is determined by the combined action of advective processes (deep‐water intrusions) and small‐scale turbulent vertical diffusion. Here, vertical diffusivity is addressed through the analysis of 25 temperature microstructure profiles collected in the three major basins of Lake Baikal to a depth of 600 m.
