Abstract When talking about natural calamities; earthquake stands out to be the most dangerous and life threatening one. It disrupts and damages almost all of the structures. During this seismic event and afterwards; there might be eruptions of fire; human and animal life can undergo psychological problems. To overcome all this; water is required. For providing an unwavering supply of water, elevated water tanks come as a relief. These tanks should therefore remain operative within the duration of earthquake and after it also. These tanks are constructed over the head and therefore named as overhead tanks. These are supported upon a tower or a combination of columns and bracings.
