ABSTRACT Graphical overview of an oxbow lake used as a Nature-based flood-retention system in a tropical watershed. The illustration shows floodwater entering the oxbow lake, temporary storage, controlled spillway release, and reduced downstream peak discharge. The system achieves approximately 11% peak-discharge reduction while supporting hydrological resilience and potential water-resource and ecosystem co-benefits. Flood mitigation in tropical watersheds increasingly requires sustainable approaches capable of maintaining effective flood-control functions under variable hydrological conditions.
