Hydroponic systems substitute soil with nutrient solutions, making water quality a primary production concern. Because treated municipal water is often too expensive for commercial-scale production, alternative water sources such as industrial process effluents are being explored as cost-effective alternatives. Sugarbeet processing effluent represents one such underexplored source that offers potential for reuse at scale. This study compared three sugarbeet processing effluents with a tap water control in a deep water culture (DWC) system and assessed how two nutrient management strategies influenced the yield, water use efficiency (WUE), and nutrient use efficiency (NUE) of lettuce ( Lactuca sativa L.).
