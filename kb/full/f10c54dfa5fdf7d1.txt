Indoor environmental quality (IEQ) plays a crucial role in occupants’ well-being and daily performance. Understanding this relationship is important in educational buildings, as students spend significant time in classrooms. This study integrated environmental measurements – operative temperature, illuminance, CO 2 concentration and noise – with 972 student responses collected across 23 classrooms at a university in the humid subtropical climate of Southern Brazil. The dataset was structured into 100 classroom sessions and analysed using a combined framework of multiple linear regression, K-means clustering and discriminant analysis.
