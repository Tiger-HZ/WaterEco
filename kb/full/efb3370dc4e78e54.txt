Abstract This paper reviews the deployment of a novel 6.125-inch single‑cone hybrid drill bit in a challenging lateral section targeting thin carbonate reservoirs in a major Middle East oil field. The objectives were to reduce non‑productive time, enhance logging‑while‑drilling (LWD) density image quality, and deliver an above‑average rate of penetration (ROP) in formations characterized by high‑density anhydrite and severe vibration tendencies. A detailed offset‑well analysis was performed before the run, including performance review, dull grading, and vibration-signature evaluation.
