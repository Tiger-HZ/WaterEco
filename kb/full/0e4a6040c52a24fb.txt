The aim of this paper was to assess the socio-economic impacts of refugees' life standard on the host communities in Itang special wareda the cast of Kule refugees' camp.To achieve the objectives, data were collected by using both primary and secondary sources and qualitative and quantitative approaches were employed.Explanatory and descriptive research design were used and the 156 sample were selected by using purposive and systematic samplings techniques and instruments like interviewee, focus group discussions, and observation and survey questionnaires were used to collected data.Finally thematic analysis and SPSS V 20 were utilized to analyze the qualitative and quantitative data collected respectively.The study found out that the presence of refugees in the region has both negative and positive implications.
