Abstract High-resolution soil property maps have a wide range of applications, e.g. policymaking and precision agriculture. Previously published maps covering the EU-27 plus the UK range in resolution from 250 to 30 metres depending on the soil property. In this work, we produced 10 m resolution maps for soil texture (clay, silt, sand), coarse fragments, pH and bulk density. They are the products of a machine-learning workflow trained on LUCAS soil datasets and using AlphaEarth Foundations satellite embeddings as predictors. We performed hyperparameter tuning and training on Random Forests (RF) and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN). The best-performing models were ANN, with R 2 values ranging from 0.21 for coarse to 0.69 for pH. The best-performing ANN hyperparameters for each soil property
