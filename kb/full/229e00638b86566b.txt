This paper presents a novel modelling framework that integrates artificial intelligence (AI) tools with deficit irrigation (DI) management. Different levels of DI were applied to summer and winter crops, with six scenarios assessed for each season. Four metaheuristic algorithms – LSHADE (Success-History based Adaptive Differential Evolution with Linear Population), PSO (Particle Swarm Optimisation), GWO (Grey Wolf Optimiser), and NRO (Nuclear Reaction Optimisation) – were implemented and automated using Python-based models to identify optimal solutions. The optimisation model aims to maximise the project’s total net profit by determining the most efficient allocation of land and water resources under DI conditions.
