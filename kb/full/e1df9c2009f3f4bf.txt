This study systematically investigates the physical constraints and environmental applicability of near-field geoacoustic inversion using a horizontal line array (HLA) in shallow-water environments. While near-field HLA configurations offer advantages for localized sensing and mobile platforms , their performance is strictly governed by the underlying propagation physics. Using the SCOOTER wavenumber integration model to ensure the inclusion of continuous spectra and leaky virtual modes, the study evaluates inversion robustness through Bartlett matched-field processing and Monte Carlo analysis.
