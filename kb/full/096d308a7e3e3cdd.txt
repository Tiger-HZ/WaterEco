Background. Skeletal muscle is the main site of insulin-mediated glucose disposal, and its loss aggravates insulin resistance in type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). Aim. To synthesise evidence on the mechanisms, efficacy, comparative and combined effects, training parameters, safety and prescription of resistance training for glycaemic control in adults with T2DM. Material and methods. A narrative review with a structured search of PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus and the Cochrane Library (2007-2026), prioritising randomised trials, systematic reviews, meta-analyses and guidelines. Results. Resistance training improves glycaemic control by increasing or preserving muscle mass, muscle glucose uptake and insulin sensitivity.
