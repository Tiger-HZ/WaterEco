Understanding habitat selection is essential for predicting species distributions and developing effective conservation strategies, particularly for semi-aquatic mammals. However, habitat requirements of the Eurasian water shrew (Neomys fodiens, EWS) remain poorly understood in northeastern China. We investigated habitat use by comparing 44 used and 44 control plots in the Lesser Khingan Mountains during winter and spring. Aquatic and terrestrial environmental characteristics were evaluated separately using generalized linear models and multimodel inference, and supported predictors were subsequently combined in an integrated model.
