Introduction Inorganic fertilizers (IF) remain the most common nutrient source in agriculture due to their high nutrient availability to crops and efficiency. Circular economy strategies through waste recovery can complement them. However, the use of wastes remains challenging because they contain multiple nutrients and potential pollutants. This study aims to demonstrate that using waste-derived soil amendments can reduce the reliance on IF. Methods We compared different treatments: (1) Control (no-fertilized); (2) formulated residue-based amendment (RBA); (3) inorganic fertilizer (IF); and (4) their combination, RBA + IF (50:50), as an integrated nutrient management (INM) strategy.
