Introduction Microbial amendments are increasingly used in sustainable crop production, but it remains unclear whether their application is accompanied by coordinated changes in fruit traits, rhizosphere soil conditions, and resident bacterial communities in blueberry cultivation. This study examined whether microbial culture treatment was associated with concurrent changes in blueberry fruit performance, rhizosphere soil chemical properties, and bacterial community characteristics. Methods Highbush blueberry ( Vaccinium corymbosum L. cv. Duke) plants receiving no microbial treatment (NT) were compared with plants receiving a microbial culture treatment (BT) consisting of Lactobacillus casei , Saccharomyces cerevisiae , and Lactobacillus plantarum through drip irrigation.
