Nutrients play a fundamental role in plant growth, soil fertility, and overall agricultural productivity. However, inefficient fertilizer application in conventional farming systems often leads to nutrient imbalance, reduced soil quality, and increased environmental degradation. The emergence of precision agriculture and artificial intelligence (AI) has introduced new opportunities for improving nutrient management through data-driven decision-making. This study investigates an AI-driven nutrient optimization system designed to enhance fertilizer efficiency while reducing nutrient runoff and improving soil health.
