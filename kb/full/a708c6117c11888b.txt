Abstract Genomes provide the fundamental basis for understanding biological systems. Owing to rapid advances in sequencing technologies and assembly methods, the number of sequenced genomes has increased substantially. A high‐quality genome should have high continuity, completeness, and accuracy in its assembly, along with comprehensive and precise annotations of protein‐coding genes and other functional genomic elements. However, it is still difficult to fully achieve these standards due to technical limitations and the inherent genome complexity, especially for plant genomes.
