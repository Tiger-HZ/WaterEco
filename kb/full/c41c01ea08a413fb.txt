Background/Objectives: Breast cancer (BC) survivors frequently experience persistent psychological and physical symptoms and reduced health-related quality of life (HRQoL). Yoga has emerged as a promising supportive intervention in this context; however, evidence regarding remotely delivered yoga programs remains limited. This systematic review aimed primarily to evaluate the effects of remotely delivered yoga interventions on HRQoL among women with BC. Secondary outcomes included psychological and physical symptoms, as well as feasibility, adherence, and safety.
