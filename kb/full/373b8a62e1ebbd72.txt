Climate variations in the Legal Amazon cause changes in this biome that oscillate between droughts, increases in precipitation rates, among others. However, information about them is not yet frequent, and this gap still needs to be filled, which motivated this research, whose objective is to understand the main consequences arising from climate change. To carry out this research, the investigative method was used in past data published between 2016 and 2025, in order to verify the evolution of this information and whether it was related to the main fact: climate change. The data obtained and analyzed indicated that, among the five consequences, floods (4.0 ± 4.2) are mentioned in the 49 selected articles, followed by precipitation (3.5 ± 3.5). Droughts had a lower incidence (1.0 ± 1.0). These data indicate that traditional communities, especially those allocated as family farms, must be attentive to the modes of production and human well-being and the quality of life and the negative effects caused by climate change.
