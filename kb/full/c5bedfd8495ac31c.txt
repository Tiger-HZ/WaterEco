Sustainable irrigation for smallholders requires strategies that minimize and optimize land, water, and energy use while remaining resilient to climate change. The study uses the Land-Water-Energy (LWE) nexus and long-term climate trends to assess smallholder irrigation performance. The Land-Water-Energy Nexus Index (LWENI) assesses the sustainability of four smallholder irrigation systems in Vhembe District. Six normalized indicators for the availability and accessibility of land, water, and energy resources were scored on a 0–1 scale and then aggregated with equal weighting. The standardized composite LWENI scores ranged from 0.53 (Rambuda) to 0.74 (Mphaila), indicating performance ranging from moderately unsustainable to moderately sustainable.
