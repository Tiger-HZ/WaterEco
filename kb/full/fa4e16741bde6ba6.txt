Abstract The quality of life which depends on the surrounding environment has been greatly affected by increased pollution level over the decades. Among them, heavy metals cause severe health and environmental impacts once they enter the water stream through industries. The conventional methods of treatment are either cost intensive or energy intensive. Electrocoagulation is one of the robust and techno-economical technology for treating heavy metals in water and wastewater. In particular, the removal of hexavalent chromium released from electroplating industries, which is toxic even at low concentrations has been addressed by electrocoagulation in batch and continuous mode operations.
