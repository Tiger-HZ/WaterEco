Abstract In conventional analysis of sedimentation in open channel flow, suspended sediment transport is regarded as an advection diffusion process. A series of theories based on Brownian motion are developed for describing the diffusion process of sediment particles attributed to turbulence. However, difficulties remain because these models cannot represent the time coherence of turbulence structures that affect instantaneous local sediment concentrations. Focusing on the impact of two flow structures in the near‐wall region on suspended sediment transport, ejection, and sweep events, this study proposes a new stochastic Lagrangian model to simulate sediment particle trajectory.
