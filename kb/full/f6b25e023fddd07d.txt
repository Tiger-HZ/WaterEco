Metro tile roofing sheet contains heavy metals as part of its composition. They are often released when the roofs are exposed in sun and rain. They are deposited on the surface of nearby soil as sink and are in turn infiltrating into underlying soils. To investigate the extent to which metro tile roofs degrade nearby soil, a total number of twenty samples of soil from 0 cm to 15cm and 15 cm to 30 cm depths during the dry and wet season respectively were sampled. Metro tile roof runoff was collected during the wet season. Both soils and water samples were analyzed for Cu²⁺, Cr³⁺, Cd²⁺, Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, and Al³⁺.
