This study evaluates the effectiveness of non-structural watershed management measures for flood risk reduction, with a focus on the Mirusha River catchment and the urban area of Malisheva. The research investigates the application of retention ponds integrated with contour-aligned channels (swales), following the principles of Keyline Design, as a strategy to control surface runoff, enhance infiltration, and mitigate urban flooding. The methodology combines long-term climatic and hydrological data analysis, runoff estimation using multiple hydrological methods, and GIS-based spatial analysis to identify optimal locations for retention ponds.
