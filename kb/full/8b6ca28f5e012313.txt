The core factor determining spectral radiometric calibration performance for optical remote sensing instruments lies in the performance of spectral radiometric calibration light sources. Tunable calibration light sources developed based on spectral tunability technology possess extensive application value in fields such as spectral radiometry and metrology. However, existing DMD-based spectrally tunable calibration light sources introduce significant spectral stray light that is difficult to avoid attributed to the inherent diffraction effect of DMD, which severely degrades the accuracy of the calibration light sources and makes them unable to meet the practical calibration requirements of large-aperture, high-precision optical remote sensing instruments.
