Purpose This paper proposes a model based on analytic hierarchy process (AHP), 2-tuple fuzzy linguistic representation and failure mode and effect analysis (FMEA) to measure risks associated with suppliers of critical items. Design/methodology/approach In the proposed model, AHP measures the relative importance of failure modes and 2-tuple aggregates quantitative and qualitative risk evaluations to calculate RPN values for each supplier. A test application of the model was conducted at a philanthropic hospital, where Risk Priority Number (RPN) values for three suppliers of surgical gloves were calculated.
