Objective: As a quality improvement initiative, dynamic point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) assessments were introduced to our large nonprofit academic medical center to improve adherence with society resuscitation guidelines. Sonography-guided volume resuscitation decisions were based on dynamic assessments and integration into workflow. Materials and Methods: The medical team independently assessed volume status using existing methods (eg, physical examination, static POCUS) in hypo-perfusing or hypotensive patients (N = 50). A wearable Doppler POCUS device was used to perform a dynamic assessment of fluid responsiveness, specifically measuring the change in stroke volume surrogate after preload challenge.
