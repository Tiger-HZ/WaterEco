Abstract The efficient recovery of natural gas from complex reservoirs, particularly in heterogeneous geological environments, remains a persistent challenge in the energy sector. Suboptimal production, driven by water intrusion, coning, and premature breakthrough, significantly curtails recovery factors and escalates operational expenditures. This paper introduces a novel Artificial Intelligence (AI)-driven Smart Reservoir Gas Water Shut-Off System (SRGWSS) designed to autonomously optimize gas recovery. The SRGWSS integrates real-time sensor data analytics, predictive machine learning models, and automated control mechanisms for proactive water management.
