The effective use of digital technology in MSMEs requires adequate human resource capabilities to create value in service delivery and customer interactions. This study examines the relationships between digital literacy, work motivation, and consumer behavior through service quality, while also assessing the moderating role of employee competence. An explanatory quantitative design was applied to MSMEs using the MP Store platform in Surabaya. Data were obtained through questionnaires and analyzed using PLS-SEM. A population of 2,879 users. The sample size was determined using Slovin's formula with a 5% error rate, resulting in a minimum sample requirement of 352 MSME units.
