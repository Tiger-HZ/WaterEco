The study aimed to assess the impact of agroecological conditions in the slope agricultural landscape of the foreststeppe zone of the Central Black Earth Region on the yield and grain quality of the Suzdalets spring barley variety, depending on cultivation technology. The work was carried out in the Kursk region in 2024-2025. The experimental plot, with a total area of 21.06 hectares, is located on a watershed plateau (0-1°) and northern and southern slopes with gentle (2-3°) and steep (&gt;3°) slopes. The compared technologies were resource-saving (no-till), biologized, and intensive. Under the dry conditions of 2024, the grain yield on the watershed plateau (2.52 t/ha) exceeded this indicator on the steep southern and northern slopes by 37% and 39%, respectively.
