Abstract Kenikir plants are one of the plants with high potential for cultivation, but soil fertility is a limiting factor. This study investigates the impact of solid organic fertilizer (bokashi and chicken manure) fertilizer and plant spacing on the growth, yield, and nutrient uptake of kenikir in dryland conditions. A factorial randomized block design with four dose (bokashi and chicken manure) rates (0, 3, 6, and 9 t ha -1 ) and three spacing treatments (30×30 cm, 30×35 cm, 30×40 cm) was used. Results showed that bokashi significantly improved plant height, leaf number, stem diameter, and branching.
