Sustainable water resource management in semi-arid Mediterranean regions is critically dependent on reliable spring discharge forecasting and on quantifying the long-term impacts of climate change on groundwater resources. This study develops and evaluates a suite of non-autoregressive machine learning architectures, MLP, CNN, LSTM, and a hybrid CNN–LSTM, for predicting the monthly discharge of major springs supplying Palermo, Italy.
