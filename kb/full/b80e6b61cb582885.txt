Allanblackia floribunda and soybean oil were blended in ratio 40:60 (Allanblackia: Soybean) and used in the formulation of bakery fat. Physicochemical properties of the individual oils and as well as the formulated bakery fat were evaluated and compared with a commercial counterpart. Result revealed that moisture content, smoke point, flash point, density and melting point of A. floribunda and soybean oil were, respectively, 0.49 and 0.25% , 194.0 and 189.0℃, 266.5 and 249.0℃, 0.888 and 0.915 g/cm3 , 36.5 and -20.50℃. Similarly, oil colour, viscosity and refractive index were, 20.2Y, 3.2R and 20.0Y, 4.2R, 35.0 and 25.0 Cst, 1.46 and 1.47°C.
