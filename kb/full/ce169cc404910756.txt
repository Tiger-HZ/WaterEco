Abstract Background Nutrient resorption is an important nutrient conservation strategy in mangroves; however, its variation with respect to salt regulation strategy, season and leaf anatomical traits remains unclear. We investigated 10 cooccurring mangrove species in Dongzhaigang National Nature Reserve, Hainan, China, during the dry and wet seasons. Soil nutrients, green and senesced leaf concentrations of 12 elements, and six leaf anatomical traits were measured to quantify nutrient resorption efficiency (NuRE) and identify its main drivers. NuRE was strongly element specific.
