This study evaluated the effects of insect protein sources and dietary inclusion levels on nutrient digestibility in dogs and examined the structure of the underlying evidence and research collaboration. Nine in vivo feeding trials involving black soldier fly, cricket, and silkworm pupae were synthesized using network meta-analysis. Low-level silkworm pupae inclusion (&lt;10%) significantly improved dry matter (+6.77 percentage points) and organic matter (+5.06 percentage points) digestibility relative to controls, whereas high-level cricket inclusion (&gt;20%) significantly reduced dry matter, organic matter, and crude fat digestibility.
