Abstract Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) provides water surface elevation (WSE) that can constrain river hydraulics through data assimilation (DA), but basin‐scale impacts of assimilating real observations remain uncertain under heterogeneous availability and regulation. We assimilate node‐level SWOT WSE into a large‐scale hydrodynamic model over the Ohio River Basin using an ensemble Kalman filter and test river‐network‐aware localization. Assimilation improves discharge skill at most of the 66 USGS gages, including sites without direct SWOT overpasses, with ∼60% showing higher KGE and ~3% degrading.
