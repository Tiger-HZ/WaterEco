Abstract Phosphorus management has become a major challenge in wastewater treatment because phosphorus is both a key driver of eutrophication and a finite nutrient resource that should be recovered and reused. This review examines recent advances in phosphorus removal and recovery, with particular emphasis on emerging adsorption-based materials, membrane processes, and electro-assisted hybrid technologies. Conventional approaches, including chemical precipitation and enhanced biological phosphorus removal, are first considered to establish their performance and limitations as a basis for comparison with emerging treatment pathways.
