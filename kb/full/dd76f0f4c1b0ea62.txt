Global environmental challenges, necessitate innovative resource recovery strategies. This chapter explores the “stress-induced biorefinery” paradigm, which leverages the heterogeneous nature of wastewater as a tunable metabolic signal for microalgae. By manipulating environmental stressors such as nutrient limitation, light intensity, and salinity, microalgal metabolism can be “programmed” to accumulate high-value compounds, including triacylglycerols for biofuels, carotenoids for nutraceuticals, and carbohydrates for biopolymers. Integrated systems utilizing algal-bacterial consortia offer energy-efficient treatment by replacing mechanical aeration with photosynthetic oxygenation. Furthermore, the coupling of nutrient recovery with carbon sequestration provides a pathway for industrial carbon management. This chapter synthesizes biological mechanisms, engineering strategies for diverse effluent streams, and cascading valorization routes to transform wastewater treatment from a cost-intensive disposal process into a sustainable, value-generating manufacturing platform.
