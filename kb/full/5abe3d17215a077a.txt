Abstract Objectives/Scope This study introduces the deployment of an Online Surface Network Model (SNM), developed to provide continuous visibility and optimization features for a brownfield surface production system. The primary aim of this trial was to assess the feasibility, technological readiness, and potential value of incorporating live field data into a hydraulic surface network model. The goal was to improve decision-making processes, pinpoint flow constraints, and foster production optimization through a digital twin representation of the surface network. Methods, Procedures, Process The project focused on modeling a critical section of the Marmul surface network, incorporating wells, flowlines, manifolds, and key infrastructure using a hydraulic simulation tool.
