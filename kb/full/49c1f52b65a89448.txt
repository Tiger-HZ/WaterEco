Multimodal remote sensing (RS) image interpretation has received significant attention from researchers, but it still faces challenges such as high computational cost, redundant features and suboptimal cross-modal fusion strategies, especially in terms of linear complexity reasoning, bridging modal heterogeneity and constructing fair fusion strategies. There are limitations in these aspects. Therefore, this paper proposes a novel classification framework that combines the dynamic Mamba mechanism and multi-head attention. This framework consists of two core modules: (1) the progressive dynamic spatial-spectral Mamba module, which gradually compresses the spatial dimension and enhances the channel representation through dynamic feature extraction, achieving hierarchical feature learning, reducing redundancy and overhead. (2) The learnable multi-head attention fusion module, which retains spatial structure information through position labels and enhances heterogeneous feature interaction through cross-modal attention to generate high-quality fusion representations. Experimental results on three multimodal remote sensing datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
