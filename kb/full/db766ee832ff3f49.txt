Accurate representation of terrestrial Essential Climate Variables (ECVs) is fundamental for understanding the hydroclimate system and informing policy decisions. Despite progress in hydrological modelling, coordinated multi-model intercomparisons across diverse regions at kilometre-scale resolution remain limited. This study presents a multi-scale analysis of six state-of-the-art models (CLM, GEOframe, mHM, PCR-GLOBWB, TETIS, and wflow$\_$sbm) using unified meteorological and observational datasets, benchmarking their model performance and flux consistency against a common reference. This study assessed the models in three hydroclimatically contrasting basins (the Rhine, Po, and Tugela) at multiple spatial resolutions.
