Freshwater ecosystems in the Niger Delta region are increasingly threatened by industrial pollution, which can disrupt aquatic biodiversity and ecosystem functions. This study aimed to assess the abundance and distribution of benthic macroinvertebrates and plankton in the Okulu Aleto River, Eleme, Rivers State, Nigeria, to evaluate the ecological impacts of varying pollution levels. Using an experimental research design, samples were collected from six strategically selected locations including: upstream, midstream, downstream, effluent discharge site, and two control sites, representing a gradient of anthropogenic influence.
