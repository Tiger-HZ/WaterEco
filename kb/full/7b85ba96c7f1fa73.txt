This article examines the long-term trends in the formation, accumulation, and disposal of industrial and municipal waste in the Republic of Belarus. The rates of waste generation, the overall increase in the amount of waste produced, the share of waste recovered, and the structure of accumulated waste were explored. Using the data on selected waste storage and disposal facilities, their impacts on the levels of groundwater pollution were investigated, along with the conditions and duration of waste storage, the subgrade systems for waste placement, and the systems for collection and drainage of atmospheric water.
