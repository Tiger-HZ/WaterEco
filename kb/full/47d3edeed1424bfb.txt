Forest ecosystems play an important role in maintaining soil fertility through continuous organic matter inputs and nutrient cycling. The present study was conducted to assess temporal changes in soil nutrient availability across five forest locations in Telangana State between May 2023 and April 2024. The mean available nitrogen content increased from 266 to 295 kg ha⁻¹ between May 2023 and April 2024. Available phosphorus increased from 46.8 to 50.1 kg ha⁻¹, while potassium increased from 413 to 425 kg ha⁻¹. The highest concentrations of available N, P, and K were recorded at Adilabad during both sampling periods.
