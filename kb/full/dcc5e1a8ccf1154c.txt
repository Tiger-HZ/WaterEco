This study was conducted to assess the effect extrusion process parameters (barrel temperature, screw speed, and feed moisture) on the hydrogen cyanide and oxalate contents of extruded aerial yam-soybean flour blends, using response surface methodology. Design Expert (version 11.0.1) was used in the experimental design, adopting a three-factor experimental set up at five levels each. The aerial yam bulbs and soybean seeds were separately processed into flour using standard laboratory procedures. The aerial yam flour (AYF) and soybean flour (SBF) were blended at the ratio of 25% AYF:75% SBF, and extruded using a single-screw extruder (SCE).
