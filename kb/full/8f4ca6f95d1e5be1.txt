Background: Human serum albumin (HSA) is a critical therapeutic protein with widespread clinical applications. Despite standardised manufacturing protocols, quality variations between HSA preparations are poorly characterised. Objective: To comprehensively characterise five commercial plasma-derived albumin preparations (VIALEBEX®, ALBUNORM®, ALBUREX®, FLEXBUMIN®, ALBUTEIN®) using multiple analytical approaches to assess structural integrity, post-translational modifications, functional properties and purity.
