Aquatic ecosystems are increasingly threatened by invasive aquatic weeds and harmful algal blooms (HABs), driven by eutrophication, climate change and anthropogenic disturbances. These proliferations degrade water quality, disrupt ecosystem functioning, reduce biodiversity and cause major economic losses in fisheries and water management. Conventional control strategies, including chemical, mechanical and biological methods, often provide limited effectiveness and may generate secondary environmental impacts. Ultraviolet–C (UV-C) radiation has emerged as a promising non-chemical alternative for sustainable aquatic ecosystem management.
