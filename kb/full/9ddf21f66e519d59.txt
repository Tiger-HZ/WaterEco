Abstract This research investigates the spatiotemporal characteristics and influencing factors of eutrophication in Xiangshan Bay from 2000 to 2024. By integrating long-term monitoring data and statistical analyses, the study elucidates the evolutionary trends, spatial distribution patterns, and underlying driving mechanisms of eutrophication in the bay. Evaluations using the Eutrophication Index ( EI ) and the Trophic Index ( TRIX ) reveal that the water quality in Xiangshan Bay has remained in a eutrophic state for at least the past 25 years, exhibiting an overall trend of initial increase followed by a decline.
