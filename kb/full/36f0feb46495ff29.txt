Objective: This study consisted of three water sampling campaigns for water intended for human consumption in households in Marabá. Method: A total of 115 samples were collected. In all, 28 water quality parameters were analyzed. Three different home testing kits were used to analyze the samples: the first kit allowed for the analysis of 16 quality parameters (15 chemical parameters and 1 physicochemical parameter) and was used in campaigns 1 and 3; the second kit allowed for the analysis of 10 parameters, which were different heavy metals, and was used in campaign 2. Kit 3 is a single-parameter kit for analyzing sodium chloride in water.
