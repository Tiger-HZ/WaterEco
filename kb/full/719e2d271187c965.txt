ABSTRACT Saposhnikovia divaricata (SD, Chinese name: Fangfeng) is a commonly used herbal medicine in traditional Chinese medicine (TCM), yet unclear chemical profiling and remarkable quality heterogeneity among commercial batches restrict its standardized quality supervision. This study established an integrated strategy combining high‐performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) fingerprinting and multivariate chemometrics to quantify bioactive compounds and discriminate geographical origins of SD. Eight bioactive markers including four chromones and four coumarins were unambiguously assigned among 18 shared characteristic fingerprint peaks.
