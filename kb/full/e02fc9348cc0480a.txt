Abstract In topographically complex water distribution networks (WDNs), maintaining uniform water quality and optimal hydraulic pressures presents a significant operational challenge. This paper proposes an integrated algorithmic framework combining automated Reverse Osmosis (RO) permeate blending with pressure-dependent Variable Speed Drive (VSD) pump optimization. By dynamically adjusting the blending ratio of RO permeate and raw groundwater using real-time Electrical Conductivity (EC) feedback, the target mineralization and pH balance are maintained without unnecessary remineralization overhead.
