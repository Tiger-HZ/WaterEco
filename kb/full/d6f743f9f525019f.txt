Groundwater is one of the most important sources of water for agriculture, domestic consumption, and industrial activities, yet its availability is strongly influenced by rainfall, recharge, temperature, evapotranspiration, hydrogeological characteristics, agricultural activity, irrigation demand, and human extraction. Conventional groundwater-prediction systems mainly forecast a future water-level value and give limited insight into the drivers behind that value or the effect of possible interventions. This paper reviews the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), time-series forecasting, explainable AI, and decision-support techniques for groundwater-level prediction and management.
