Images from geophysical surveys and data inversion can be valuable information for hydrogeologic site characterization and monitoring. This paper focuses on solving nonlinear electrical resistance tomography (ERT) inverse problems; the methods are applicable to other inverse problems, such as groundwater flow. The total variation (TV) method is used to constrain ERT inverse solutions. The classic TV functions are modified to obtain differentiability with respect to parameters, which allows the use of gradient search optimization methods. The modified total variation (mTV) method constrains ERT inverse solutions effectively. Sometimes the mTV constraints cause important parameter features to be smoothed out.
