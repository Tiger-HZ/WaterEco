This study examines the contribution of sustainable tourism to increasing Local Own-Source Revenue (LOSR) in Klaten Regency, Indonesia. The analysis is based on four key dimensions of sustainable tourism: economic, social, environmental, and institutional. A mixed-methods approach was employed, combining quantitative data collected from 100 respondents with qualitative insights obtained through in-depth interviews with key stakeholders. The quantitative analysis was conducted using Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) to examine the relationships among the variables. The findings indicate that the economic and social dimensions have a positive and significant effect on LOSR, whereas the environmental and institutional dimensions do not have a significant direct effect.
