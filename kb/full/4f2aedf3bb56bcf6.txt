Severe sedimentation in the Sengguruh Reservoir has reduced its effective storage capacity to approximately 24% of the original value, creating persistent dredging and spoil bank management problems. This study aimed to evaluate the potential reuse of dredged spoil bank sediment as sustainable fill material through lime stabilisation. Natural sediments and sediments treated with 10% and 15% calcium oxide on a dry weight basis were evaluated experimentally.
