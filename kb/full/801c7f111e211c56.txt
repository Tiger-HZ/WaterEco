Background Although the embolic stroke of undetermined source (ESUS) construct was proposed as a unifying concept for embolic‐appearing strokes without an identified cause, it includes patients with diverse and often unrecognized underlying mechanisms. This study investigates the differential rate of recurrent stroke according to the completeness of neurodiagnostic testing in a diverse multicenter cohort. Methods This was a retrospective analysis of a multicenter observational cohort study (n=27 sites). ESUS was categorized into 3 groups: (1) established mechanism after further workup (ESUS‐E), (2) comprehensive diagnostic workup without an identified cause (ESUS 2.0), and (3) no advanced workup.
