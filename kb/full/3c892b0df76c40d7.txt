Accurate water level observations are essential for flood forecasting, hydrological analysis, and water resource management; however, sensor malfunctions and telemetry errors frequently introduce anomalous observations that compromise data quality. This study proposes a reconstruction-oriented Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) Autoencoder for river water level anomaly correction and compares its performance with conventional first-, second-, and third-order polynomial and exponential regression models.
