This study was carried out in Kahramanmaraş conditions in the 2019-20 and 2020-21 growing seasons in order to investigate the grain yield and some quality traits of some advanced oat genotypes in terms of suitability for registration. The research was set up with three replications in randomized completeblock design. In the experiment, 8 advanced lines and 8 varieties (Kırklar, Kahraman, Küçükyayla, Halkalı, Yeniçeri, Sebat, Elmas and Aslanbey) in total 16 oat genotypes were used. In investigated traits, 1000 grain weight (TGW), test weight (TW), sieve analysis of grains (&gt;2.2, 2.2-2.0, 2.0-1.5 mm), oil ratio (OR) and protein ratio (PR) were evaluated.
