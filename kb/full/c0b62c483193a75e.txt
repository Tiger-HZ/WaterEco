Background. Barodontalgia is dental or oral pain triggered by changes in ambient pressure. In scuba divers, this condition is clinically relevant because it may indicate previously undetected dental pathology and may affect comfort, concentration and underwater safety. Aim. The aim of this narrative review is to summarize current knowledge on barodontalgia in scuba divers from a dental perspective, with emphasis on risk factors, clinical presentation, diagnosis, management and prevention. Results. The literature indicates that barodontalgia is usually associated with pre-existing oral pathology, including dental caries, defective restorations, pulpitis, pulp necrosis, apical periodontitis, periodontal disease, residual air spaces, impacted teeth and maxillary sinus disorders.
