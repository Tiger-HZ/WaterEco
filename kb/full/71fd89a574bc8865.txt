This study evaluated the effects of textile effluent—an increasingly common irrigation source in Bangladesh’s garment-industry regions—on nutrient acquisition, heavy-metal uptake, and associated health risks in rice (Oryza sativa L.). Six irrigation and fertiliser treatments were applied: T1 (control), T2 (recommended fertiliser dose with fresh water), T3 (50% RDF + 25% effluent), T4 (50% RDF + 50% effluent), T5 (50% RDF + 75% effluent), and T6 (50% RDF + 100% effluent). Rice grains from T2 exhibited the highest nutrient concentrations and uptake, whereas T4—representing 50% textile effluent—produced comparable values across most nutrients.
