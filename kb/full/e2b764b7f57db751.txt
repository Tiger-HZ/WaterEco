This study evaluated the effects of nitrogen fertilization and soaking temperature on the technological quality of parboiled rice. Four nitrogen rates (0, 50, 100, and 150 kg ha⁻¹) and four soaking temperatures (60, 70, 80, and 90 °C) were tested. Nitrogen fertilization affected thousand-grain weight, milling yield, and the percentage of broken grains, while soaking temperature influenced hectoliter weight, electrical conductivity, milling yield, and grain breakage. The highest thousand-grain weights were observed at N rates of 0 and 50 kg ha⁻¹; hectoliter weight decreased as temperature increased, and electrical conductivity was lower at temperatures of 60 and 70 °C.
