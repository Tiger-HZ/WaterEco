Abstract Wet markets are recognised as important non-domestic sources of organic rich sullage. However, information on their discharge characteristics and temporal variability remains limited. This study presents a discharge profiling and pollution loading assessment of Bangi Wet Market and Mantin Wet Market, located within the Sg. Semenyih and Sg. Beranang catchments, respectively. A structured sampling programme was conducted over a full operational cycle to capture temporal variations associated with market activities. Key physicochemical and microbiological parameters analysed include pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen (DO), biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), suspended solids (SS), ammoniacal nitrogen (NH 3 -N), oil and grease, and faecal coliform.
