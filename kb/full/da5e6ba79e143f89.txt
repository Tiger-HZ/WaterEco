The excessive application of conventional fertilisers often results in rapid nutrient loss, low nutrient-use efficiency, and adverse environmental impacts. To address these challenges, phosphate-based glass fertilisers were synthesised using the conventional melt-quenching technique as environmentally sustainable slow-release nutrient sources. The glass compositions were melted at 750–800 °C with a soaking period of 30 min and rapidly quenched to produce homogeneous amorphous glass fertilisers.
