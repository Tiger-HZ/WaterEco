Lay Summary Sheep exhibit a nocturnal peak in growth hormone secretion with regular circadian fluctuations, making the study of feeding time effects on their physiological functions highly important and practical. This study assigned twelve Dorper×Hu crossbred rams to daytime and nighttime feeding groups to explore how feeding patterns affect their digestive metabolism, blood parameters, intestinal microflora and metabolites. Results showed sheep had greater digestive utilization efficiency of crude fat, crude protein and nitrogen in the daytime. At nighttime, their muscle had more beneficial fatty acids, blood levels of melatonin, glucagon-like peptide-1 and other hormones rose, and antioxidant and immune activity were enhanced.
