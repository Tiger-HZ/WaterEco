In southeast Texas, groundwater extraction has caused irreversible aquifer compaction that results in land subsidence. As this region continues to experience population growth and increased water demand, the evaluation of projected subsidence impacts is crucial for effective water resource planning. This study presents a scenario-based framework for investigating future flood hazards and quantifying economic damages associated with inland subsidence in the Spring Creek Watershed, Texas, United States (US).
