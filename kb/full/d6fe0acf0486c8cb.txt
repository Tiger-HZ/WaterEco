This study evaluated the effects of dietary curcumin powder supplementation on growth performance, nutrient intake and digestibility, nitrogen utilization, rumen fermentation, blood urea nitrogen (BUN), and plasma antioxidant activity in meat goats. Twenty-four crossbred male goats (Thai native × Anglo-Nubian; initial body weight, 25.80 ± 2.06 kg) were assigned to four dietary treatments containing 0, 0.5, 1.0, or 1.5% curcumin powder in the concentrate. Dietary curcumin powder supplementation significantly affected final body weight and nutrient intake and produced dose-dependent responses in apparent nutrient digestibility and nitrogen utilization.
