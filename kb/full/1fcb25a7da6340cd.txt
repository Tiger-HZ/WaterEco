Abstract A rapidly warming Arctic Ocean and associated sea‐ice decline is resulting in changing sea‐ice protist communities, affecting productivity of under‐ice, pelagic, and benthic fauna. Quantifying such effects is hampered by a lack of biomarkers suitable for tracing specific basal resources (primary producers and microorganisms) through food webs. We investigate the potential of δ 13 C values of essential amino acids (EAAs) (δ 13 C EAA values) to estimate the proportional use of diverse basal resources by organisms from the under‐ice ( Apherusa glacialis ), pelagic ( Calanus hyperboreus ) and benthic habitats (sponges, sea cucumber), and the cryo‐pelagic fish Boreogadus saida .
