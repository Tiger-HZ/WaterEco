Abstract In recent years characterization models have incorporated site-dependent conditions for non-global impact categories in Life Cycle Analysis (LCA). However, regional impact characterization factors (CFs) have not been developed for all parts of the world. Ecosystems can be described according to nutrient supplies that limit growth and this can be considered the cornerstone of eutrophication research. The terrestrial eutrophication model is largely absent despite its importance for assessing agricultural commodity systems or land use activities.
