Industrial growth in battery supply chains is increasing mineral processing by-products (MPBs), which pose environmental risks but also offer resource recovery potential. This thesis develops a life cycle assessment (LCA) framework tailored to MPB recycling, addressing gaps in current research. Three case studies evaluate copper slag in cemented paste backfill, delithiated β-spodumene in concrete, and nickel slag across multiple applications. Results show that appropriate reuse pathways can significantly reduce environmental impacts to a great extent. Overall, the study demonstrates that MPB recycling can enhance sustainability and supports its integration into circular economy strategies for battery minerals.
