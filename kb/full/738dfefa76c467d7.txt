Background. While physical activity is widely acknowledged for its physiological benefits, its role as a non-pharmacological intervention for mental health stabilization across diverse populations demands reevaluation. Exercise and structured sport participation serve as key systemic regulators of neuroplasticity, mood elevation, and stress resilience. Aim. This review aimed to synthesize literature concerning the impact of physical activity and sport on depression, anxiety, cognitive function, and psychological well-being, focusing on neurobiological pathways, psychosocial dynamics, and optimal exercise dosing strategies.
