Eutrophication, which is usually caused by the disposal of wastewater containing nutrients such as those present in hydroponic effluent, is known to have a negative effect on the quality and balance of water. Therefore, the optimization of the treatment of wastewater in an economical manner is necessary. Phosphorus and nitrogen are nutrients which can be extracted and recovered from wastewater with the help of bacterial and microalgal consortia. This is in accordance with the concept of the circular bio-economy, which is the production of goods from waste. The concept of the use of microalgae-bacteria consortia in the bioremediation of wastewater will be presented briefly in this chapter. The principle of symbiotic connections states that the use of microalgae-bacteria consortia is better than the use of single species in the bioremediation of wastewater. These consortia collect lipids, proteins, and carbohydrates which can be converted into biofuel and other beneficial products, aside from the removal of contaminants from wastewater.
