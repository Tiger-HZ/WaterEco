As an important military optoelectronic equipment, laser target designation (LTD) plays a key role in the terminal guidance weapon system. Aiming at the problems existing in the current LTD manufacturing process, such as low efficiency of manual inspection, isolated quality inspection data, and large error of manual interpretation, we firstly construct a quality control system covering the whole manufacturing process of LTD. We clarify the inspection objects, inspection contents, inspection standards and inspection timings for the three typical scenarios: incoming inspection, in-process inspection and final inspection.
