Sugar beet (Beta vulgaris L.) is an economically important industrial crop, and its productivity and sugar accumulation depend heavily on effective nutrient management. This study investigated how mineral fertilization combined with foliar applications of boron (B) and zinc (Zn) influences plant growth, photosynthetic performance, nutrient uptake, yield, and sugar production under irrigated alkaline soil conditions in southeastern Kazakhstan during the 2024 and 2025 growing seasons. The field experiment was conducted using a randomized complete block design with three replications and five treatments: (1) control (CK), (2) N300P150K300 (NPK), (3) NPK + B, (4) NPK + Zn, and (5) NPK + B + Zn.
