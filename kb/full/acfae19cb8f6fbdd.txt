Abstract Aiming at simultaneously settling the problem of waste resource recycling, oily wastewater pollution/health risk, flammability, and durability of traditional oil–water separation materials, this work developed the superhydrophobic/superoleophilic sponge materials (P/APP@ZIF-8@PDA@PU), which were inspired by natural lotus leaves and mussels. The results suggested that the water contact angle (WCA) and oil contact angle (OCA) of P/APP@ZIF-8@PDA@PU reached 154.3 ± 1.1° and ∼0°, respectively. The joint action of PDA/APP@ZIF-8 and PDMS in manufacturing hierarchical roughness and reducing the surface energy of sponges was confirmed.
