The quantity of water derivable from groundwater could be affected by overexploitation, which also promotes negative impact on the quality. This study investigated groundwater exploitation, treatment methods, and domestic water supply challenges in Yenagoa metropolis, Bayelsa State, Nigeria. A survey of 350 households was conducted to assess sources of water, facility ownership, treatment practices, maintenance frequency, and supply-related challenges. Results showed an overwhelming reliance on groundwater (95.4%), primarily managed by landlords (67.7%), with filtration (42.9%) being the most common treatment method.
