Integrated assessments combining soil compactness, carbon storage, and structural vulnerability remain scarce in tropical soils of the Colombian Orinoquia. Soil physical condition was compared across natural forest (n = 21), secondary vegetation (n = 27), agricultural use (n = 12), and improved pastures (n = 67) in Arauca, Colombia, using stratified purposive sampling. The 127 samples from the 0–30 cm layer were analyzed for pH, soil organic carbon (SOC), bulk density, and particle-size distribution. Texture-adjusted packing density, fixed-depth SOC stock, and the Pieri index were calculated.
