This chapter provides an overview of advanced technologies for removing heavy metals from water and wastewater systems. It discusses a range of treatment approaches, including adsorbent-based methods employing materials such as activated carbon, chitosan, and metal-organic frameworks (MOFs), as well as conventional chemical precipitation techniques and emerging electrochemical methods, including electrocoagulation and electrodeposition. This chapter also examines membrane-based separation processes, highlighting the roles of micro-, ultra-, and nano-filtration in the effective removal of heavy metals. In addition, pressure-driven membrane technologies such as forward and reverse osmosis are examined. To illustrate the practical implementation and performance of these technologies, this chapter presents three case studies focused on heavy metal removal in real-world scenarios.
