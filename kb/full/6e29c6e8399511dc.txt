ABSTRACT While previous research often focused on individual electron donors, the comparative performance, potential synergistic or antagonistic effects, and underlying mechanisms of hybrid organic/inorganic systems for simultaneous nitrogen and phosphorus removal remain unclear. This study investigated the efficacy of media, including zero-valent iron (ZVI), pyrite (Py), zero-valent sulfur (ZVS), and orange leaf (OL), over 90 days using laboratory-scale columns (14.4 cm × 120 cm). The hybrid OFS system (comprising OL, ZVI, and ZVS) achieved a peak TP removal efficiency of 97.31%, a performance attributed to ZVS promoting ZVI dissolution, while the coupled oxidation of ZVI and ZVS stabilized pH and optimized FePO4 precipitation.
