This study aimed to evaluate the conservation status of 27 streams within the Pindaré River basin, Maranhão, Brazil. The assessment was conducted using the Habitat Integrity Index (HII), complemented by several physicochemical variables, including conductivity, turbidity, dissolved oxygen concentration, pH, temperature, depth, width, and canopy cover. Based on the HII, streams were categorized as preserved, altered, or degraded. The majority of the sampled streams were classified as altered or degraded, mainly due to riparian vegetation removal, erosion, and sedimentation processes. Preserved streams exhibited higher canopy cover, greater structural complexity, and more stable habitats, whereas degraded streams showed elevated water temperature and turbidity levels.
