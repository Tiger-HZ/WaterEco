Abstract Saline aquaculture effluents contain dissolved and suspended nitrogen, phosphorus and biodegradable organic matter that can have adverse impacts on receiving waters. The effects of three microalgae, Chaetoceros neogracile, Isochrysis galbana and Tetraselmis chuii at different wastewater concentrations (25%, 50%, 75% and 100%) were compared with the standard-medium and uninoculated wastewater controls. The conditions of batch cultures were controlled for 14 days in three biological replicates for each treatment, and a theoretical model was used.
