This dataset contains snowpack and snow strength observations from three study plots in Idaho, five study plots in Colorado, three study plots in Alaska, and four study plots in Wyoming. These data were collected in an effort to model the trafficability and supportability of a seasonal snowpack, and will be assimilated in SnowModel to model strength across time and space. Data collected include stratigraphy, density, permittivity, temperature, specific surface area (SSA), snow depth transects, ram penetrometer profiles, Snow Scope profiles, SnowMicroPen (SMP) profiles.
