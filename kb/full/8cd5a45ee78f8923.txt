Abstract Accurate detection and instance segmentation of rail surface defects are critical for intelligent railway maintenance. Existing methods suffer from feature obscuration under illumination-noise coupling and morphological mismatch for irregular defects. This work presents a lightweight joint detection and segmentation framework for rail surface defects by integrating frequency-domain decoupling, gradient-guided upsampling, and morphological adaptive feature fusion. We design a coordinate-aware frequency-domain enhancement module to suppress noise and enhance defect boundaries. A gradient-guided dynamic upsampling method preserves edge topology for thin cracks.
