A bstract Integrated management strategies are often implemented to achieve comprehensive environmental restoration; however, the potential antagonistic effects among such measures are seldom examined. Fishing bans, aimed at enhancing fish biodiversity, may counteract the effectiveness of nutrient control measures in mitigating eutrophication. This study employs the PCLake model and structural equation modeling (SEM) to evaluate this hypothesis in Lake Hongze, the fourth largest shallow lake in China. The PCLake model was calibrated using observational data from Lake Hongze collected between 2016 and 2020.
