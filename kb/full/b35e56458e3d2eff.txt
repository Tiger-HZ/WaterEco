The importance of water quality monitoring for aquatic ecosystem protection cannot be overemphasized, but the traditional machine learning algorithms used for monitoring data analysis have shortcomings in failing to account for the spatiotemporal interactions of the data. This study seeks to develop a spatiotemporal deep learning framework that employs Graph Attention Networks (GAT), Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT), and SHAP-based interpretability for WQI forecasting and early warning purposes.
