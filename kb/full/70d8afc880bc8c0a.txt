Nonpoint source (NPS) pollution from agricultural, urban, and silvicultural land uses constitutes the leading remaining cause of surface water quality impairment in the United States and a primary driver of aquatic biodiversity loss, eutrophication, and drinking water contamination globally. Best management practices (BMPs) represent the primary technical and regulatory mechanism for NPS pollution control, encompassing a broad array of structural, vegetative, and managerial practices designed to reduce pollutant generation, transport, and delivery at the field, farm, and watershed scales.
