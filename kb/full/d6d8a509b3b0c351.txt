Abstract Synthetic dyes are a major class of compounds used in the textile industry. The effluents released from these industries are carcinogenic and mutagenic in nature and pose potential threat to all life forms. Here, we designed a novel tri-phasic engineered flow through wetland system (TEWS) with three specific microenvironments (tri-phasic aquatic systems) integrated in a defined sequence to effectively treat azo dye-based textile wastewater. Tank 1 with free-floating and emergent aquatic macrophytes create anaerobic/anoxic microenvironments and facilitate reduction of the azo dye molecules.
