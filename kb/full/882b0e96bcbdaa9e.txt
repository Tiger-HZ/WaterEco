Milk remains a critical component of human nutrition, offering essential nutrients including protein, lactose, fat, vitamins, and minerals. This study aimed to evaluate the nutrient composition of pasteurized milk sourced from selected smallholder pastoral dairy farmers across four local government areas (LGAs) in Taraba State, Nigeria. A total of 80 milk samples were aseptically collected and analyzed for fat, solids-non-fat (SNF), protein, lactose, salt, density, freezing point, temperature, and pH using AOAC-standard methods. Results revealed no statistically significant differences (p &gt; 0.05) in the nutrient parameters across the studied locations, suggesting that pasteurization standardizes milk composition regardless of local environmental or management variations.
