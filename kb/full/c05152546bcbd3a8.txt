Abstract Recent landscape transformations resulting from the replacement of natural areas with anthropogenic uses have had an impact on the semi-arid region of Brazil. The objective of the research was to evaluate a land use and land cover classification model in the Caatinga biome, using machine learning techniques integrated with multiple environmental and climatic covariates. The area was segmented into six subunits, based on a combination of hydrographic regions and geomorphological domains.
