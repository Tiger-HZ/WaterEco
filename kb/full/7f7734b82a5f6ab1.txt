Agricultural non-point source pollution (ANPSP) presents a critical threat to water security in the upper reaches of the Yangtze River Economic Belt (UYREB). Traditional export coefficient models (ECMs) often fail to capture the spatially heterogeneous processes of pollutant transport and ecological attenuation in complex mountainous terrains. Here, we constructed an improved ANPSP risk assessment framework by integrating ecosystem regulating functions (water yield, soil retention, and habitat quality) into the ECM.
