This study evaluated the functional, nutritional, and sensory properties of cakes made from unripe plantain–wheat composite flour. Seven different formulations were prepared using varying proportions of unripe plantain and wheat flour: A (80:20), B (60:40), C (50:50), D (40:60), E (20:80), F (100% wheat), and G (100% plantain). Functional property results revealed significant (p &lt; 0.05) differences among samples. Sample B had the highest bulk density (0.87 g/cm³), water absorption (1.80 g/g), and oil absorption (1.80 g/g), while sample G had the highest swelling power (9.74 g/g) and lowest wettability (68 seconds).
