The beef cattle industry is the leading sector of the agricul­ture economy in Kansas. The objectives of this study were to evaluate the water quality of Kansas grazing and feedyard water samples, as well as quantify feedyard water use by month for 10 feedyards in western Kansas. Sixteen grazing sites in western Kansas were selected to collect forage and water samples. Forage samples from each grazing site were collected in August 2024. Forage clippings from each opera­tion, which together constituted a representative sample of the pasture, were collected and sent to a feed analysis labora­tory.
