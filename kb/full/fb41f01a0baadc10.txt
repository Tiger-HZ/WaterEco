Objective To examine the association between postoperative adjuvant venoactive drug (VAD) use and symptom relief and quality of life (QoL) in patients undergoing radiofrequency ablation (RFA) for symptomatic varicose veins of the great saphenous vein (GSV). Methods This retrospective cohort study screened 565 consecutive patients who underwent RFA for symptomatic GSV varicose veins between June 2022 and June 2024. After application of the eligibility criteria, 500 patients were included and classified according to postoperative VAD use into a standard-care group ( n = 250) and a standard-care plus VAD group ( n = 250).
