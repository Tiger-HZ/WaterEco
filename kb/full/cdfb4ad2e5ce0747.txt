This article reviews publications on the development of the cyanobacterium Gloeotrichia echinulata in oligotrophic and mesotrophic lakes, with a special focus on Lake Pleshcheyevo (Yaroslavl region, Russia). The study summarizes the results of phytoplankton investigations conducted over the past 100 years. Since 2017, annual blooms caused by G. echinulata have been observed in the lake. The mass development of this cyanobacterium is driven by a combination of anthropogenic and climatic factors: increased phosphorus loads from adjacent watersheds, rising water temperatures, and reduced water exchange, which promotes stagnation.
