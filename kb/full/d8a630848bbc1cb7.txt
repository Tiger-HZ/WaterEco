Reservoir tributaries are highly vulnerable to eutrophication, leading to recurrent algal blooms. Despite experiencing the same hydrodynamic influence from the Three Gorges Reservoir (TGR) since 2003, the Pengxi and Modao Rivers—both 1st‑order tributaries—showed contrasting bloom frequencies, with 17 and 3 spring events, respectively, from 2003 to 2013. This study integrates the Soil and Water Assessment Tool (SWAT) with partial least squares structural equation modelling (PLS‑SEM) to explain these differences by linking watershed land cover to hydrologic and nutrient‑transport processes. Land use/land cover was the dominant spatial driver in both watersheds.
