Family agriculture faces recurring challenges in water availability, especially during dry periods, which requires strategies adapted to environmental conditions and knowledge built in the territory. This study aims to analyze the mathematical knowledge and collaborative practices mobilized by family farmers, associations and public authorities in the use of small dams in Águia Branca, Espírito Santo, considering their perceived effects on water management, agricultural production and the valorization of Popular Education. This is a qualitative, exploratory and descriptive research, developed with eight family farmers from four rural communities, through semi-structured interviews and documentary analysis.
