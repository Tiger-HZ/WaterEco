Advancements in high-power laser technology have opened new possibilities for electron acceleration, enabling the development of novel irradiation platforms for radiobiology studies. Ultrahigh dose rate (UHDR) radiation delivery is emerging as a promising approach for cancer therapy. The FLASH effect, observed under UHDR irradiation, has demonstrated the ability to preserve antitumor efficacy while reducing damage to healthy tissues. High-intensity petawatt laser systems (such as the one available at ELI-NP) offer a potential solution, capable of generating very high-energy electrons (VHEEs) at extreme dose rates greater than to 1012 Gy/s in ultra-short pulses ( 1 ps).
