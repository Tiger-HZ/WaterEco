Based on the field survey data collected in winter (January, 2018) and spring (April, 2017), the characteristic variability of chromophoric dissolved organic matter (CDOM) in different seasons in Zhanjiang Bay was analyzed. The results demonstrated that CDOM absorption coefficient at 280 nm ( a g (280)) and the spectral slope from 275 to 295 nm (S 275-295 ) representing the molecular weight of CDOM could both maintain good correlations with salinity in winter and spring, indicating that CDOM was more likely to exist as a conserved substance during its migration in Zhanjiang Bay. The characteristics of CDOM and the weak correlation between Chlorophyll a (Chl a) and CDOM revealed that the influence of algal activity on CDOM was limited.
