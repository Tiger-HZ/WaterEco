ABSTRACT Graphical abstract outlines the aims of the study, the approach, findings and opportunities. Contaminants of emerging concern (CEC), such as pharmaceuticals, hormones and industrial chemicals, pose a significant challenge for the water industry due to their presence in matrices including wastewater and drinking water. This is compounded by a lack of knowledge about their occurrence, environmental fate and/or toxicity, making it difficult to assess and mitigate risks. In this systematic review we investigated approaches used to prioritise CEC in matrices relevant to the water industry for research and management.
