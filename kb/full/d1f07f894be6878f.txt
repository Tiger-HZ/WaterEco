Abstract Objectives Motorcycle couriers work in traffic-intensive and physically demanding environments, but evidence on recent musculoskeletal complaints in this occupational group remains limited. This study estimated the 7-day prevalence of neck, shoulder, and low back complaints among motorcycle couriers and examined associations with self-reported working-condition indicators. Methods A cross-sectional online survey was conducted among 286 actively working motorcycle couriers in Konya, Turkey. Six perceived working-condition indicators—traffic, air pollution, noise, road irregularity/vibration, heat, and cold exposure—were assessed using 0–10 numeric rating scales.
