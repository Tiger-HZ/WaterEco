In high water-cut reservoirs, water flooding efficiency declines due to high-permeability channels that aggravate heterogeneity and cause water channeling. Gas flooding technology can improve recovery but is limited by operating conditions and costs. Water-alternating-gas (WAG) injection combines the advantages of both methods, but the complex reservoir connectivity and the strong inter-well interference hinder accurate displacement path control, which will reduce the oil production efficiency and increase costs. In this paper, a reinforcement learning-based dynamic optimization strategy is proposed to solve the injection optimization problem for WAG injection with inter-well connectivity.
