The problem of diffuse intrinsic and specific nitrogen pollution of surface and groundwater is relevant for many countries worldwide, including Ukraine. There are various methods for assessing the potential of diffuse nitrate pollution of surface and groundwater, each of which has its own advantages and disadvantages. The LOS-index methodology, developed within the international project EU.WATER, is free from the shortcomings of traditional assessment methods. The aim of the article is to assess the potential of diffuse nitrate pollution of surface and groundwater of the Odesa region of Ukraine under present-day climatic and economic conditions on the basis of the LOS-index methodology and geographic information (GIS) technologies.
