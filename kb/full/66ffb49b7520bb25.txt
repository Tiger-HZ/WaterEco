Abstract The Mars 2020 mission has explored depositional environments recorded in a fan‐shaped deposit on the western side of Jezero crater. Kodiak butte is an isolated outcrop of sedimentary rock ∼1 km south of the western fan scarp and may be an erosional remnant of said fan. Sedimentary strata of Kodiak butte were first interpreted to represent Gilbert delta foresets and a lake depth of at least 10 m. Subsequent closer‐range images of the northern face of Kodiak have resolved additional details of stratal geometries, permitting alternative interpretations of depositional environments.
