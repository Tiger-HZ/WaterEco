Abstract As survival among individuals with congenital heart disease (CHD) improves, effective transition from pediatric to adult care has become increasingly important. We evaluated the impact of a structured transition education program on transition-related knowledge among adolescents with CHD. This single-center quality improvement study enrolled adolescents aged 12–18 years with CHD between February 2025 and April 2026. A baseline survey was administered to cardiology providers at our institution to characterize their current practices.
