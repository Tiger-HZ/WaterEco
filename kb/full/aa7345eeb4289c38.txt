Quantitative hydrochemical source apportionment underpins precise mine water inrush prevention and inter-aquifer pollution control. Ninety-two groundwater samples from four aquifers in the Anhui Banji Mining Area were tested for six major ions (Ca&lt;sup&gt;2+&lt;/sup&gt;, Mg&lt;sup&gt;2+&lt;/sup&gt;, Na&lt;sup&gt;+&lt;/sup&gt;+K&lt;sup&gt;+&lt;/sup&gt;, Cl&lt;sup&gt;−&lt;/sup&gt;, SO&lt;sub&gt;4&lt;/sub&gt;&lt;sup&gt;2−&lt;/sup&gt;, HCO&lt;sub&gt;3&lt;/sub&gt;&lt;sup&gt;−&lt;/sup&gt;). Piper diagrams and Pearson correlation analysis identified dominant HCO&lt;sub&gt;3&lt;/sub&gt;-Ca·Mg hydrochemical facies, with some Quaternary pore water evolving into the Cl·SO&lt;sub&gt;4&lt;/sub&gt;-Na type due to evaporite leaching.
