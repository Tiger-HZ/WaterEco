Urban flooding has become an increasingly critical challenge worldwide, driven by urbanisation-induced changes to natural drainage systems and intensifying climate-driven precipitation extremes. Previous research on water-sensitive urban design has largely focused on stormwater runoff in simplified urban layouts, while giving less attention to the complexity of real urban form and its effect on flood behaviour. This study addresses this gap by systematically evaluating how diverse and complex urban configurations influence flood flow characteristics. Eight representative urban layouts, including four procedurally generated forms and four realistic forms, were developed within a two square kilometre area (1 × 2 km) using UrbanXTool, OpenStreetMap data, and ArcGIS.
