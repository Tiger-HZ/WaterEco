Background The quality of flue-cured tobacco depends on nutrient acquisition, carbon–nitrogen allocation, and quality-related secondary metabolism, particularly phenylpropanoid and flavonoid pathways. However, whether fertilizer placement and exogenous soil metabolite formulations can jointly improve leaf nutritional physiology and cured-leaf quality remains unclear. Methods A field experiment was conducted with the flue-cured tobacco cultivar Yunyan 87 ( Nicotiana tabacum L.). Six treatments combined two fertilizer placement methods, strip application and well-cellar application, with two soil metabolite formulations at the same total fertilizer application rate.
