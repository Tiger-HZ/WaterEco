Seasonal hydrological variability strongly shapes eutrophication risk in small coastal catchments, but the transition from low-flow source accumulation to rainfall-driven transport remains poorly quantified. We analysed monthly eutrophication index (EI) patterns for 30 coastal rivers on the Shandong Peninsula, China, from 2020 to 2024 using an integrated interpretable machine-learning and PLS-SEM framework. The EI, derived from TN, TP, DO, and CODMn, was evaluated against terrain, hydroclimate, hydrology, human activity, point-source pollution, and fertilizer-application variables. The EI peaked in the wet season, declined during normal-flow months, and was lowest in the dry season, indicating a clear seasonal pattern across hydrological periods.
