ABSTRACT Traditional comprehensive trophic level index (TLI) methods for lake eutrophication assessment often neglect redundancy among evaluation factors and face challenges with spatial interpolation accuracy using ArcGIS. To address these issues, in this study, we propose an optimized eutrophication evaluation framework by integrating a novel “redundancy correction” weighting method and a refined GIS‐based spatial interpolation optimization strategy. A year‐long water quality monitoring campaign was conducted in 2022 to analyze the spatiotemporal distributions of permanganate index (COD Mn ), total nitrogen (TN), total phosphorus (TP), and chlorophyll a (Chl‐a) concentrations, as well as water transparency (SD).
