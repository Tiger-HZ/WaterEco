Abstract Algal meal is an emerging sustainable ingredient in animal nutrition, particularly valued for its high content of docosahexaenoic acid (DHA), an essential omega-3 fatty acid. It offers a renewable alternative to fish oil, helping address concerns related to environmental impact, supply variability, and oxidative stability. DHA plays important roles in neural function, immune response, and overall health in companion animals. However, there is limited information in the literature on the effects of supplementing algal DHA meal in extruded diets for adult dogs.
