Immersive technologies, specifically augmented reality, virtual reality and Metaverse-based learning platforms have evolved higher education and have transformed the way higher education systems are taught and implemented. In healthcare education, these technologies could enhance students' comprehension of complicated knowledge by using realistic simulation strategies and three-dimensional visualization. This study was conducted to evaluate the technology readiness, students’ prior experience and learning expectations regarding the deployment of the EON XR Metaverse platform in a healthcare service quality course.
