Abstract Objective To compare five mainstream large language models (LLMs) in answering common questions about small cell lung cancer (SCLC) across patient-education suitability, overall information quality, and readability. Methods This cross-sectional benchmarking study analyzed 100 responses generated by DeepSeek, Doubao, GPT-5, Tongyi Qianwen, and Wenxin Yiyan to 20 standardized SCLC questions spanning five domains. Patient-education suitability and overall information quality were evaluated with the Chinese version of the Patient Education Materials Assessment Tool for Printable Materials (C-PEMAT-P) and the Global Quality Score (GQS).
