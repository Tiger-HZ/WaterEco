Beam quality is one of the core indicators of high-power fiber lasers. This paper proposes and validates a beam quality optimization method based on introducing a periodic refractive index modulation structure into the fiber cladding using femto second laser pulses. By appropriately designing the modulation region length, duty cycle, and refractive index modulation depth, differential mode attenuation is achieved in few-mode fibers, characterized by low loss for the fundamental mode and high attenuation for higher-order modes. In the experiment, devices with three different refractive index modulation strengths were fabricated in a 20/400 μm large-mode-area fiber using a line-by-line scanning method. The beam quality and insertion loss were tested in a 1080 nm oscillator seed source system
