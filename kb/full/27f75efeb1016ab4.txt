Field experiments were carried out during the 2024 and 2025 cropping seasons at the Teaching and Research Farms of College of Agriculture Science and Technology Lafia Nasarawa State, Nigeria to determine the effect of Nodumax and SSP fertilizers on the growth, yield and grain quality of soybean. Routine analysis was carried out on the field in both cropping seasons using standard analytical procedures. The field was manually cleared and demarcated into plots measuring 3 x 3 m. Four ridges of 3 m long and a spacing of 0.75 m were constructed per plot with a 1m inter plot boundary.
