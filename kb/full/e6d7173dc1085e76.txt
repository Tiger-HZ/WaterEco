The area from Tonggu Cape to Qinglan Bay off eastern Hainan Island, China, is a bedrock headland coast with a river-mouth lagoon and hosts typical habitats such as coral reefs. Qinglan Bay is located on the path of sediment transportation along the north continent of the South China Sea, the crucial area of clastic sediment diffusion in the continent and Hainan Island; therefore, investigating the sedimentary dynamic environment in the nearshore area off Qinglan Bay is particularly important. Grain size analysis was performed on 321 seafloor sediment samples collected from the waters off Qinglan Bay.
