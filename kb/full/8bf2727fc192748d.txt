Abstract Recent advancements in earth observation and computational technologies have enabled the prudent use of remote sensing (RS) for monitoring and managing water resources. RS is a spatially explicit and effective approach for field-level to large-scale monitoring through data-driven insights on key agricultural water management (AWM) aspects of water consumption, productivity, agricultural drought and irrigated area extent mapping. However, the progress made in the South African context is not well documented. Hence, this review aimed to assess how RS is utilized for AWM in South Africa.
