This study investigates the geochemical characteristics and spatial distribution of heavy metals in soils within the Toro mining district, Bauchi State, Nigeria, to evaluate the environmental impact of prolonged artisanal tin mining. A total of ten (10) composite soil samples were collected from depths of 10–15 cm across active, abandoned and control sites. Samples were analysed using Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy (AAS) at the Nigerian Geological Survey Agency (NGSA) Laboratory, Kaduna, with quality control protocols observed throughout the analytical process. The concentrations of Pb, Cd, As, Cr, Cu, Zn, Fe, and Mn were determined and compared with World Health Organization (WHO) soil quality standards.
