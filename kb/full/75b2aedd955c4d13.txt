Background Southern Africa’s transboundary river basins face unprecedented pressures from climate change, population growth, and competing water demands, necessitating integrated governance approaches that simultaneously address ecological sustainability, climate resilience, and conflict prevention. Transboundary water governance, climate adaptation, and peacebuilding are typically addressed separately in policy and practice, creating implementation gaps that undermine regional water security. Methods A systematic literature review was conducted covering 200 peer-reviewed publications (2012–2024) drawn from Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar, and grey literature.
