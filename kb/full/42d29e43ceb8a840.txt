Marine cyanobacteria represent a promising but underexplored bioresource for rare earth element (REE) management. This study investigated the effects of lanthanum (La, 10 mg L−1) on four cyanobacterial strains isolated from Black Sea periphyton: Desertifilum tharense AqMaPh-IBSS-CYA-1, Toxifilum mysidocida AqMaPh-IBSS-CYA-10, Salileptolyngbya sp. IBSS-CYA-8, and Cyanobium sp. AqMaPh-IBSS-CYA-15. La induced a delayed hormetic response in Cyanobium sp. Under suboptimal light and temperature conditions, La supplementation supported 100% survival of all strains over 35 days, whereas controls perished.
