Calcified cyanobacteria and associated microfossils were systematically investigated in 10 shallow-marine carbonate sections – reefal and non-reefal – from Cambrian Series 2 of the Yangtze Platform, South China. A total of 15 species in 10 genera of calcified cyanobacteria and 5 species in 3 genera of Microproblematica were identified, including 2 new species ( Streptubularia robustus sp. nov. and Xianella mollis sp. nov.). Girvanella , Subtifloria , Razumovskia , Acuasiphonoria , Xianella , and Streptubularia are attributed to Oscillatoriales (cyanobacteria); Kordephyton , Bija , Hedstroemia , and Botomaella are attributed to Nostocales (cyanobacteria); Renalcis , Izhella , and Epiphyton are attributed to Microproblematica.
