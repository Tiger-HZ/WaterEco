Abstract – Every year, millions of people get sick from water that isn't clean. Making sure our drinking water is safe isn't just nice; it's a huge task, especially with new threats popping up all the time. Imagine tiny, harmful bugs, chemical spills, or even just weird tastes and smells in your tap water. These problems make it tough to deliver truly clean water to homes and businesses. Thankfully, smart solutions exist. Oxidation and ozonation are two big players in advanced water treatment. They both help make water pure, but they work in different ways. Understanding these methods shows us how we fight invisible dangers to keep our water safe to drink.
