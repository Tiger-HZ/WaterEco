ABSTRACT As typical periglacial landforms, thermokarst lakes (TLs) are undergoing rapid evolution under ongoing climate warming and increasingly influence hydrological redistribution across the “Asian Water Tower.” However, systematic quantitative assessments of their long‐term dynamics and scale‐dependent driving mechanisms remain limited, particularly given the pronounced regional heterogeneity of permafrost environments. Using multi‐temporal Landsat and Sentinel‐2 imagery within the Google Earth Engine (GEE) platform, this study investigated the spatiotemporal dynamics and driving factors of TLs in the permafrost region of the northwest Tibetan Plateau (TP) from 1990 to 2025.
