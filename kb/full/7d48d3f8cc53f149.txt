Introduction Harmful algal blooms (HABs) pose significant threats to aquatic ecosystems, fisheries, and public health worldwide. Materials and methods This study investigated bloom dynamics at two stations (El-Fanar and El-Mex) within the highly eutrophic El-Mex Bay, Alexandria, Egypt, by integrating in situ physicochemical and phytoplankton data with Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery processed through ArcGIS 10.5. Three spectral indices the Red Tide Index (RTI), Maximum Chlorophyll Index (MCI), and Normalized Difference Chlorophyll Index (NDCI) were computed and evaluated using a literature-referenced threshold method.
