Objectives: The present study was intended to compare the water quality of two rivers, Tlawng and Tuirial as per their dissolved oxygen, biological oxygen demand, chloride and water temperature and to find out the variables which best discriminated the two river systems and seasons among the evaluated parameters by discriminant analysis. Method: Water samples were collected on a monthly basis from the selected monitoring sites from January 2023 to December 2023. Discriminant analysis was done with two different analytical dimensions: temporal (seasonal) and spatial (river system) discriminant analysis, which were performed under four active water quality parameters: dissolved oxygen, biological oxygen demand, chloride and water temperature.
