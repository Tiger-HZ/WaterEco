Eutrophication is a crucial factor for the degradation of freshwater ecosystems. It has been noticed that in India several lakes have been already facing this type of adverse effect of eutrophication. Naini Lake is a fresh natural water body located in the Kumaon region of the Nainital district of Uttarakhand, and it is one of the most tourist destinations of India. With period it has been noticed that there has been a rise of eutrophication level in this lake. In this research systematic monitoring has been done to monitor the trophic status of the lake. Trophic State Index (TSI) was used to identify eutrophication. A combination of different environmental parameters of the lake and Sentinel-2 Multispectral Instrument (MSI) was used to model TSI.
