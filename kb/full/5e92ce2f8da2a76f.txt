Abstract Complex cardiac arrhythmias exhibit dynamic and spatially heterogeneous activation patterns that are difficult to characterize using individual mapping modalities. Experimental platforms that enable synchronized acquisition of complementary electrophysiological measurements across cardiac preparations could facilitate mechanistic studies and quantitative evaluation of cardiac mapping methods. Here, we present an open-source platform for synchronized multimodal mapping of ex vivo perfused hearts, integrating optical mapping, contact-electrical mapping, torso-tank recordings, pacing and ar-rhythmia induction, physiological monitoring, three-dimensional morphology reconstruction, and centralized experimental control.
