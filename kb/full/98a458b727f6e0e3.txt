Eutrophication is a major threat to the urban wetland Deepor Beel, situated in northeast India, adjacent to the city of Guwahati in the Brahmaputra River valley, northeast India. The influx of water into it from the city having a poor quality, and spatial shrinkage due to urbanization have plagued the wetland, a Ramsar site and a resting place for migratory birds and home to a number of endangered floral and faunal species. The wetland's major source of inflow, the Basistha channel, drains the nearby urban catchments, and this study models the combined urban catchment-inlet channel-wetland system using a coupled 1D-2D hydrodynamic and water quality model using the modelling software, MIKE.
