BACKGROUND Atrial fibrillation (AF) is a prevalent arrhythmia associated with increased stroke risk, morbidity, and healthcare burden. Oral anticoagulation (OAC) is essential for stroke prevention, but adherence is often suboptimal. Smartphone-based mobile health (mHealth) applications offer potential to enhance adherence, quality of life (QoL), and reduce healthcare utilization. AIM To systematically review randomized controlled trials (RCTs) evaluating the impact of smartphone-based mHealth interventions on OAC adherence, health-related QoL, and healthcare utilization in adults with AF.
