Abstract Silicon heterojunction (HJT) solar cells exhibit outstanding passivation performance and low temperature coefficient, nevertheless traditional doped a-Si:H contacts suffer severe parasitic absorption, poor thermal stability and high cost, limiting efficiency promotion.
