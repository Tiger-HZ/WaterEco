We analyzed the changes in protist biodiversity and community structure in the coastal lagoon of the Mar Menor after the phytoplankton bloom that produced the collapse of the ecosystem in 2016. The analyses were based on a time series of 18S ribosomal RNA gene metabarcoding data covering from Aug-2016 to Jun-2018. Relative abundances of amplicon sequence variants (ASVs) assigned at least to order level were categorized into four tropic modes (autotrophic, dinoflagellates, heterotrophic, and Syndiniales) and used for network analyses based on co-occurrence matrices. This post-bloom period was characterized by large fluctuation in phytoplankton biomass, including phases with chlorophyll (Chl) a concentrations lower than 1.0 mg L-1 and peaks above 12.7 mg L-1.
