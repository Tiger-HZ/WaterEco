Abstract Although the impacts of nutrient pollution on coral reefs are well known, surprisingly, no statistical relationships have ever been established between water quality parameters, coral biodiversity and coral cover. Hong Kong provides a unique opportunity to assess this relationship. Here, coastal waters have been monitored monthly since 1986, at 76 stations, providing a highly spatially resolved water quality dataset including 68 903 data points. Moreover, a robust coral species richness ( S ) dataset is available from more than 100 surveyed locations, composed of 3453 individual colonies' observations, as well as a coral cover ( CC ) dataset including 85 sites.
