INTRODUCTION
 
Large

are
 
hybrid

infrastructures.
 
They
 
buffer
 
seasonal
 
discharge,
 
generate
 
electricity,
 
supply
 
irrigation
 
networks
 
and
 
reduce
 
flood

but
 
they
 
also
 
interrupt
 
sediment
 
transport,
 
alter
 
residence
 
times,
 
reorganize
 
thermal
 
and
 
oxygen
 
regimes,
 
and
 
retain
 
or
 
transform
 
nutrients
 
and
 
contaminants.
 
These

are
 
especially
 
important
 
in
 
semi-arid
 
basins
 
where
 
flow
 
regulation
 
and
 
consumptive
 
use
 
interact
 
with
 
climate
 
variability.
 
The
 
Kura–Aras

is
 
a
 
prominent
 
example
 
because
 
upstream
 
pressures
 
are
 
transmitted
 
across
 
borders
 
and
 
accumulate
 
toward
 
lowland
 
Azerbaijan
 
(Mamedov
 
et

2005;
 
IPCC,
 
2023).
 
Mingachevir

impounded
 
on
 
the
 
Kura

in
 
the
 
early

largest

in

South
 
Caucasus
 
and

component
 
of
 
Azerbaijan’s
 
water–energy–food
 
system.
 
Its

include
 
hydropower
 
production,
 
regulation
 
of
 
the
 
Middle
 
and
 
Lower
 
Kura,
 
irrigation
 
supply
 
through
 
the
 
Upper
 
Karabakh
 
and
 
Upper
 
Shirvan
 
canals,
 
fisheries
 
and
 
recreation.
 
This
 
multifunctionality
 
raises
 
the
 
cost
 
of
 
ecological
 
deterioration:
 
changes
 
in
 
water
 
quality
 
affect
 
not
 
only
 
aquatic
 
biota
 
but
 
also
 
downstream
 
agriculture,
 
potable-water
 
treatment
 
and
 
infrastructure
 
operation.
 
Earlier
 
studies
 
described
 
the
 
reservoir’s
 
microbiological
 
regime,
 
nutrient
 
dynamics,
 
organic
 
pollution
 
and
 
major-ion
 
composition,
 
but

is
 
fragmented
 
and

presented
 
in
 
tables
 
without
 
integrated
 
interpretation
 
(Salmanov
 
&
 
Ansarova,
 
2016a,

Some

series

inconsistent
 
decimal
 
notation
 
or
 
summary
 
rows

the
 
reported
 
station

At

recent

along
 
the
 
Kura
 
has
 
renewed

to
 
major-ion
 
trends
 
and
 
faecal-indicator

demonstrating
 
the

of
 
mineralization
 
and
 
microbial
 
safety
 
in
 
2025
 
(Abduyev

al.,
 
2025;
 
Aliyev
 
&
 
Abduyev,
 
2025).
 
A

and

contemporary

This

(1)
 
what

and

the
 
legacy

and

(2)

the
 
Middle–Lower

(3)

The

label
 
the
 
present-day
 
reservoir
 
condition
 
from
 
old
 
measurements.
 
Instead,
 
the
 
study
 
reconstructs
 
an
 
auditable
 
baseline,
 
identifies
 
robust
 
patterns

and
 
proposes

for
 
current
