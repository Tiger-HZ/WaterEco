In the warm summer months, filamentous diazotrophic cyanobacterial blooms frequently occur in the Baltic Sea. Their abundance is predominantly regulated by phosphate concentrations in surface water. This study aimed to investigate intracellular phosphorus transformation in filamentous cyanobacteria. Polyphosphate (polyP), phospholipids, and adenosine triphosphate (ATP) concentrations were analyzed in cyanobacteria from surface waters. Onboard and laboratory experiments were conducted to assess the response of these phosphorus-containing structures to phosphate enrichment. The results indicated that polyP was the dominant phosphorus reservoir, driving variations in cyanobacterial phosphorus (P total ) in both surface waters and under experimental conditions.
