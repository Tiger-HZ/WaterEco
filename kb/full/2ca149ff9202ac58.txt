In this work, we study an extended (3+1)-dimensional nonlinear shallow water wave equation arising in fluid dynamics and systematically derive a variety of solitary wave solutions. A rich class of exact solutions is achieved using similarity variable reduction via Lie symmetry analysis. The optimal system of one-dimensional subalgebras from one class is constructed. This is followed by the Kumar-Malik method that includes bright, dark, periodic and singular solutions. Graphical simulations in 2-dimensional, 3-dimensional surface and contour plots show the dynamical features and structural diversity of the solutions.
