The article addresses the current problems of nutrition among elderly people in the Dnipropetrovsk region under martial law conditions. The importance of rational and balanced nutrition as a key factor in maintaining health, preventing non-communicable diseases, and ensuring quality of life for older age groups is substantiated. Modern scientific data on the specific nutritional needs of the elderly and the impact of socio-economic factors on the formation of their diet are analyzed. The aim of the study was to investigate the actual nutrition and nutritional status of individuals aged 60-75 years.
