This article examines adverse changes in the ecological status of a small reservoir and its surface runoff. The hydrochemical composition of the Peschanka River water within the city of Voronezh was monitored for the autumn-winter period of 2024-2025. Based on the results, the current ecological status of the studied section of the Peschanka River was characterized and a hydrochemical index of water pollution was calculated. Water composition parameters exceeding maximum permissible concentrations were identified. The causes of the deterioration in water quality were identified. The objective of this study was to determine the hydrochemical composition of the Peschanka River's surface waters and analyze seasonal changes in the river's hydrological regime.
