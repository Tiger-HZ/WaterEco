ABSTRACT Background Implementation of Age‐Friendly Health Systems (AFHS)—an initiative promoting healthcare safety and quality for older adults—is particularly important in inpatient settings, where older adults are at increased risk of hospital‐associated complications and disability. The Veterans Health Administration (VHA) is the largest integrated AFHS in the country, but AFHS inpatient implementation within VHA is heterogeneous. To support broader implementation, we selected high‐performing VHA Medical Centers (VAMCs) to examine factors contributing to their AFHS implementation success and the remaining challenges.
