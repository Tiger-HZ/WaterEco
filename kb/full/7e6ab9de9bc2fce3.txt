Professional pedicure services in the United States are expanding alongside broader wellness and personal care markets, yet a persistent methodological gap exists: the technical, foot-skin-care, and experiential dimensions of professional pedicure service are routinely delivered as disconnected stages rather than as an integrated professional system. This study examines the MILANOVA SPA Pedicure, a practitioner-developed protocol created by Liudmyla Nikohosian , a licensed nail technician , salon owner, and educator based in Florida.
