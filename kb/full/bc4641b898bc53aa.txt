Access to safe and sustainable drinking water is essential for achieving sustainable development goal (SDG 6). This study assessed the water quality of selected boreholes and hand-dug wells in Donga town of Taraba State, Nigeria. The study examined physiochemical and biological parameters, calculated WQI value and deduced spatial temporal map of water quality variation. Water samples were collected from 24 points and analyzed using DR 6000 spectrophotometer machine and the result was presented in tables and compared with NSDWQ. Sampled points coordinates were acquired using GPS and was used to generate water quality variation map using inverse distance weighting (IDW) interpolation technique in QGIS 3.44.
