Background Modic changes (MCs) are classified into non- sclerotic and sclerotic types based on endplate morphology. Our previous work showed that non- sclerotic MCs increase cage subsidence risk after posterior lumbar interbody fusion (PLIF), while sclerotic MCs reduce bony fusion rates. Endplate bone quality (EBQ), an magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)- derived surrogate for regional bone density, may predict these distinct complications. This study evaluated EBQ’s predictive value for cage subsidence in the non- sclerotic group and for bony fusion failure in the sclerotic group.
