Abstract Large-scale particle image/tracking velocimetry using drones is an evolving method for measuring surface flow in natural environments, providing access to areas challenging for human operators. However, current applications face two main limitations: the challenge of controlling drone positioning over large rivers without ground control points in the field of view (FOV), and insufficient understanding of measurement sensitivity to flight parameters such as hover height and stability. This study proposes an integrated measurement system featuring dual cameras and three lasers.
