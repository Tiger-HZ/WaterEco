Beyşehir Lake, located within the provinces of Konya and Isparta, is Turkey’s third-largest lake and the country’s largest freshwater resource. Increasing industrialization, agricultural activities, and untreated wastewater discharges within the basin have raised concerns regarding heavy metal and metalloid contamination, particularly due to the growing number of firearms manufacturing facilities in the region. This study aimed to evaluate the seasonal distribution and potential environmental risks of selected pollutants in both water and sediment matrices of Beyşehir Lake.
