Eutrophication can alter methane (CH 4 ) cycling in lakes, yet its long-term effect on sediment microbial communities remains unclear. We analyzed a 400 year-old sediment record from the historically eutrophied Lake Joux, Switzerland, combining porewater and solid-phase geochemistry with 16S rRNA gene amplicon analyses to elucidate the effects of nutrient and carbon loading on methanogenic and methanotrophic sediment communities. Lithological and chemical stratification defined three intervals (deep eutrophic, middle carbonate, upper eutrophic) correlated with changes in organic matter sources.
