Abstract Background Oral health is essential to child health, yet the impact of cow's milk allergy (CMA) is poorly defined. This study aimed to compare oral health outcomes between children with CMA and healthy controls and to identify dietary, clinical, and behavioral factors associated with these outcomes. Methods Patients with CMA and healthy controls were examined for dental caries, gingival health, dietary habits, and oral health risk factors. A three‐day food diary was used to calculate intake and cariogenic food exposure, and laboratory parameters were documented. Results Sixty‐six patients with CMA aged 2–12 years [56.1% male, median age 39.3 months, 39.4% with multiple food allergies] and 44 healthy controls [61.4% male, median age 42.5 months] were included.
