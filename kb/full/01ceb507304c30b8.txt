Environmental contamination by microplastics is closely associated with the widespread use of plastics. However, reliable assessment of this problem remains challenging because of the lack of fully harmonized definitions and considerable variability in analytical procedures. This review critically compares the procedures used for the collection, preparation, and identification of microplastics in water samples. The review is based on literature identified through searches in the Scopus and Web of Science databases using keywords related to microplastics, aquatic environments, sample preparation, separation procedures, and analytical identification methods.
