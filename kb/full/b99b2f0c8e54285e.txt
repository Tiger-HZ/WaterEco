ABSTRACT Nutritional benefits and physical appearance are of great importance in determining the acceptance of confectionary products from composite flour by the consumers. Composite flours with inclusions of Celosia seed flour coded as KEM1 (5%), KEM2 (10%), KEM3 (15%), KEM4 (20%), and KEM5 (100% Celosia flour); and KEM6 (100% wheat flour) which served as control were evaluated for nutrient compositions; cakes (KEM1‐C to KEM6‐C) respectively made were investigated for color and sensory quality using standard methods. Proximate composition depicts the lowest moisture content (6.68%) in KEM1, ash was highest (2.09%) in KEM4; crude fat (1.11%–2.06%), fiber (1.15%–2.41%), and protein (11.7%–13.75%) increased with increased percentage inclusion of Celosia flour.
