We present a first-principles study of the vibrational spectra, i.e., vibrational density of states (VDOS) and infrared (IR) and Raman spectra, of amorphous tantala ( a − Ta 2 O 5 ) . Three model structures of up to 336 atoms in size have been generated by means of classical and molecular dynamics and subsequently used for a series of calculations of the vibrational modes and the relevant Raman and IR coupling tensors. For the assessment of size-dependent effects on the calculated vibrational spectra, we employed a machine-learned potential that enabled the calculation of the VDOS with accuracy for models containing up to a few thousand atoms.
