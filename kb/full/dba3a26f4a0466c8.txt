Abstract Background Respiratory motion introduces significant geometric uncertainty in Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy (SBRT) for thoracic and abdominal tumors. Deep Inspiration Breath‐Hold (DIBH) mitigates this, but the surface signal alone may not reflect internal target position, supporting the need for real‐time internal‐anatomy verification during delivery. Purpose To commission a Surface‐Guided Radiation Therapy (SGRT) system and validate an integrated SGRT + Image‐Guided Radiation Therapy (IGRT) + triggered kV imaging (SITI) workflow for DIBH SBRT. Materials &amp; Methods The LAP LUNA 3D SGRT system was commissioned per AAPM TG‐302 using phantom‐based assessment of static and dynamic localization accuracy, reproducibility, and latency.
