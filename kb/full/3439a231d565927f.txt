Globally, there is high competition in the market, leading organisations to set strategies that focus on customer satisfaction. Therefore, customers have remained the centre of focus in organisations, and there is a great need to satisfy them. One of the strategies is service quality, which is crucial for customer satisfaction. The study aims to examine the effect of service quality on customer satisfaction in the service industry. A desk review of the literature was used in the study. The SERVQUAL model was adopted, which is based on five dimensions: reliability, assurance, tangibles, empathy and responsiveness.
