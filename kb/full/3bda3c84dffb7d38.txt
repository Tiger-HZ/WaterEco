Although 28 studies worldwide have documented plastic debris on the deep-sea floor, only one has addressed the eastern Pacific margin. Equally uncommon are long-term beach monitoring programs that integrate debris counts with oceanographic and meteorological records. This study delivers the first combined evidence of abyssal contamination and a six-year coastal time series in the equatorial eastern Pacific, establishing a rare dual perspective on plastic transport and deposition across connected marine environments. In February 2024, the submarine NAUTILE completed 13 dives to depths &amp;gt;3 900 m along Ecuador’s continental margin, surveying debris across the shelf, slope, and abyssal trench.
