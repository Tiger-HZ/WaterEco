As humanity pursues long-duration spaceflight, sustainable life support systems are essential to sustain crew survival. In recent times, Bio-regenerative Life Support Systems (BLSS) have emerged due to its potential of integrating biological, physicochemical, and engineering technologies to ensure the recycle of air, water, nutrients, and waste within closed-loop environments and also supporting the production of sustainable food. This study synthesizes evidence on the BLSS for long-term space exploration. This review provides a comprehensive synthesis of recent evidence regarding the principles, technologies, and current innovations in BLSS, with a focus on microbial-driven nutrient recycling.
