Urban freshwater wetlands are increasingly threatened by sewage discharge, nutrient enrichment, and anthropogenic disturbances that alter aquatic community structure and ecological functioning. The present study investigated seasonal variations in plankton assemblages and their ecological significance in Surajpur Wetland, Uttar Pradesh, India, during December 2024–November 2025. Monthly sampling was conducted at three pollution-gradient sites, and plankton communities were analysed using standard limnological methods. Phytoplankton and zooplankton abundance exhibited marked seasonal fluctuations, with maximum density recorded during summer, particularly at the sewage-influenced Site 1.
