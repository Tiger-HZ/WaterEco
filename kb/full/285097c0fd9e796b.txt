Summary 1. Our goal was to use physiological indicators [photosynthesis–irradiance (P–I) response, nutrient status], population level feedbacks (self‐shading) and ambient environmental conditions (dissolved nutrients, light, temperature) to improve our understanding of the seasonal and spatial population dynamics of Cladophora . 2. Cladophora grew in three distinct phases, rapid growth early in the season (May–July), a mid‐season population collapse (July–August) and autumn re‐growth. Across all sites and dates, mean net maximal photosynthesis [P M (NET)] was 6.9 ± 3.9 mg O 2 g DM −1 h −1 , and α was 0.055 ± 0.025 mg O 2 g DM −1 μ m photons −1 m −2 .
