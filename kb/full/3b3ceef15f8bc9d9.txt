Abstract Objective This paper aims to investigate the effect of a multidisciplinary quality improvement program on newborns with central line associated blood stream infection (CLABSI). Methods In this project, 60 newborns children with Peripherally Inserted Central Catheter (PICC) catheters hospitalized from July 2020 to July 2021 were selected as the control group, and traditional nursing methods were adopted. A total of 60 newborns with PICC catheter from July 2021 to June 2022 were selected as the intervention group, and quality management was carried out in the intervention group through multidisciplinary collaborative nursing mode.
