Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) has been extensively used for the inspection of hydropower components in Nepal. Hydropower component penstock pipes are fabricated by rolling mild steel plate. The edges of the rolled plate are arc welded or submerged arc welded (SAW) along its longitudinal or spiral direction. These pipes are circumferentially welded at hydropower construction site to make a desire length of penstock from headrace to turbine. In Nepal, the quality of such welded penstock pipe is inspected using four different NDT methods such as radiography, ultrasound, magnetic and dye penetrant.
