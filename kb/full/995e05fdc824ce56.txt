Abstract The structural integrity of such pipeline infrastructure is of prime importance for oil, gas, and water industries, but designing an optimal Leak Detection System (LDS) remains a complex challenge due to trade-offs between sensitivity, computational costs, and hardware reliability. In this paper, a critical review and synthesis of recent developments in LDS methodologies are presented, considering the shift from deterministic models of fluid dynamics using mass balance equations to sophisticated data-driven models using Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) and Machine Learning classifiers.
