Abstract Traditional field tracer tests often utilize non‐reactive, non‐natural halides, such as iodide or bromide, added to injection water to characterize advection and dispersion via analyses of their concentration versus time profiles. However, non‐reactive, natural solutes and signals, such as chloride, conductance, and temperature, can be used, if their contrast between the injection water and groundwater is sufficient. Furthermore, the cost to utilize natural solutes and signals can be a fraction of traditional field tracers, for example, conductance is readily measured with a down‐well sensor.
