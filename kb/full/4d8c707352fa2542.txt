This dataset contains the supplementary data supporting the manuscript: "National wastewater surveillance of norovirus and rotavirus in Slovenia: temporal dynamics and epidemiological associations" The dataset was generated during a pilot wastewater surveillance study investigating the presence and temporal dynamics of rotavirus and norovirus in municipal wastewater. Wastewater samples were collected from 9 wastewater treatment plants between September 2023 and September 2024. Samples were analysed using molecular methods targeting norovirus GI, norovirus GII, and rotavirus. The dataset includes: WWTP metadata (wwtp_metadata.json) Wastewater dataset used for statistical analysis and visualisation (wastewater_dataset.xlsx) Clinical cases dataset used for statistical analysis and visualisation (clinical_cases_dataset.xlsx) Outbreak dataset used for statistical analysis and visualisation (outbreaks_dataset.xlsx) The associated analysis scripts are available at:GitHub: National-wastewater-surveillance-of-norovirus-and-rotavirus-in-Slovenia
