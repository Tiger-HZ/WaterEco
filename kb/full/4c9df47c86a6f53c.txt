ABSTRACT Micropollution is an ever‐increasing concern to human health and wildlife caused by population growth, industrialization, and intensive agriculture. An example of such micropollutants are natural and synthetic estrogen hormones. They are found everywhere in the aquatic environment at very low but dangerous concentrations. In this article, we demonstrate a supramolecular design principle for adsorption of such low‐concentrated estrogens onto tailored superparamagnetic iron oxide nanoparticles (SPIONs) enabling efficient magnetic water cleaning.
