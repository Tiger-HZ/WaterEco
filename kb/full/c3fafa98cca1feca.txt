Diagnostic imaging plays an important role in modern health care, but its benefits come with risks to patients and health care workers due to the presence of ionizing radiation, so ensuring radiation safety and design compliance is a technical and public health priority. This study assessed the structural, preventive, and operational compliance of four X-ray diagnostic facilities in the Merowe area in accordance with international standards established by the IAEA SSG-46, ICRP 103, and NCRP 147. The criteria were used to evaluate X-ray facilities across five main areas: room design, adequacy of protection, radiation leakage, and availability of safety equipment.
