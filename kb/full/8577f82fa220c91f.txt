We measured postsmolt growth patterns from scales of Atlantic salmon postsmolts captured in the Labrador Sea and adult returns from stocks at the southern end of the range of salmon in North America. Because Labrador Sea postsmolts are believed to represent the juvenile nursery for the entire stock complex, the growth variation for southern stocks may yield insights on the time scale of stock mixing during the postsmolt year. Circuli spacing patterns were extracted from the scales of 1525 salmon for 3 smolt years. For 2 of the 3 years, growth trajectories for fish from the southern stocks intersected the trajectories for Labrador Sea postsmolts after 4-5 circuli pairs.
