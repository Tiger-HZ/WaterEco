Use of biochar as a raw material in compost mixture has demonstrated numerous benefits during the composting process and in the end product. Changes in quality parameters in compost produced by co-composting raw materials with poultry litter (PL) and PL biochar (PLB) were studied. PLB was prepared by co-pyrolyzing PL with (5% w/w) and without (0% w/w) Eppawala Rock Phosphate (ERP). Thus, four types of composts were produced: compost without biochar or PL (C), co-compost with 10% (w/w) PL (CCPL), co-compost with 10% (w/w) PLB (CCPLB), and co-compost with 10% (w/w) PLB co-pyrolyzed with ERP (CCPLB-ERP).
