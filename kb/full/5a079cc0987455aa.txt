Background: Focal cartilage lesions (FCLs) of the knee can cause pain and dysfunction. Focal resurfacing with mini-prostheses offers an alternative to biological treatments, yet current clinical options remain limited. This preliminary study investigated a novel mini-prosthesis in a porcine model. Methods: A Ti6Al4V mini-prosthesis with a carbon-based nano-multilayer film (NMF)-coated articular surface and a 3D-printed porous stem was developed (NMF@3D-Ti). Two comparators were included in the study: an NMF-coated Ti articular surface with a hydroxyapatite (HA)-coated stem (NMF@HA-Ti) and a cobalt-chromium (CoCr) prosthesis with a polished articular surface and an HA-coated stem (HA-CoCr).
