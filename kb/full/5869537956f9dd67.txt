The North Sinai Offshore Basin (NSOB), located along the southern margin of the Eastern Mediterranean Basin (EMB), represents a strategically important yet relatively underexplored hydrocarbon province situated between the prolific Nile Delta and Levant basins. This study reconstructs the tectono-stratigraphic evolution of the NSOB through the integration of regional two-dimensional seismic reflection data, well information, seismic stratigraphy, structural interpretation, and seismic attribute analysis to evaluate the controls of tectonic inheritance on basin evolution, sediment routing, and hydrocarbon prospectivity.
