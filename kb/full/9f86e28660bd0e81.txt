In response to issues such as the high degree of subjectivity, static assessment models and insufficient integration of multisource data in the safety assessment of drinking water sources in Guangdong Province, this paper introduces Bayesian networks to conduct research on dynamic safety assessment and management optimisation. Taking into account local hydrological conditions, water quality and the current state of pollution, an evaluation system comprising 13 core indicators has been constructed across four key dimensions: water quality safety, hydrological safety, pollution input and management safeguards. Through correlation analysis, a factor-safety-level Bayesian network model was established to achieve the integration of multi-source heterogeneous data and dynamic risk simulation. Ta
