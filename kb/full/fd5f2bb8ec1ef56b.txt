Groundwater carried the green revolution. It is now being spent faster than it is replaced across most of the world's intensively irrigated plains. This review synthesises 37 peer-reviewed studies on the measurement, drivers, consequences and governance of agricultural groundwater depletion, with north-west India treated as the principal case. Satellite gravimetry made the problem visible: Rodell and colleagues reported a loss of about 109 cubic kilometres from the aquifers of north-west India over roughly six years and Tiwari and colleagues put the northern Indian rate near 54 cubic kilometres a year .
