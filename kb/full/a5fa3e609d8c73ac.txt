Abstract Biocrust induction is increasingly explored to stabilize mine tailings, but the microbial interactions driving biocrust assembly, especially those between cyanobacteria and fungi, remain largely uncharacterized. In this study, five cyanobacterial strains ( Nostoc sp., Trichormus sp., Tolypothrix sp. , and two Chroococcidiopsis sp. ) and five fungal strains ( Penicillium oxalicum , Purpureocillium lilacinum , Aspergillus clavatus , Penicillium brasilianum , Talaromyces wortmannii ) were tested individually and in all cyanobacteria–fungi co-inoculations on quartz sand tailings.
