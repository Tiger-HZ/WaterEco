Abrupt environmental transformations create two simultaneous problems: rapid change in the physical system and disruption of the data required to understand and manage it. This contribution examines this challenge through two contrasting cases. The first is the catastrophic breach of the Nova Kakhovka Dam in Ukraine on 6 June 2023, which drained the reservoir, interrupted irrigation, flooded downstream areas and mobilised pollution. A rapid assessment after the breach shows what could be established from available information, which concerns required further analysis, and how data quality affected interpretation and decision-making.
