The Curonian Lagoon is a freshwater lagoon where zooplankton has developed for a long period under the conditions of intense eutrophication and water blooms. The dynamics of primary production of phytoplankton, chlorophyll, structure, abundance, biomass and ration of zooplankton were examined for two five-year periods (2014–2018 and 2019–2023). Over a long period up to 2014–2018, regular water “hyperbloom” was observed with intensive development of cyanobacteria, while the primary production and abundance (chlorophyll a) of phytoplankton corresponded to the hypertrophic level. In recent years (2019–2023), “hyperbloom” has steadily ceased and primary production has decreased to the eutrophic level (water de-eutrophication).
