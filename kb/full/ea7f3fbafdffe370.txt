Abstract Background Health disparities remain a persistent challenge in medicine, exacerbated by inequitable practices within healthcare systems and among professionals. Quality improvement (QI) methods are well-suited for discovering and addressing disparities, and many medical school curricula incorporate QI. However, most of these curricula do not highlight the applicability of QI to health disparities as a primary learning objective. This study explores medical students’ learning about disparities through a project-based QI curriculum and facilitators and inhibitors of learning through this approach.
