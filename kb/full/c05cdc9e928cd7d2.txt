Background: Ocular-surface abnormalities are common in patients with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis and may predispose to dupilumab-associated ocular surface disease (DAOSD). We investigated early ocular-surface changes during dupilumab treatment and whether baseline-guided hyaluronic acid (HA) eye-drop use was associated with more favorable ocular outcomes. Methods: In this prospective single-center cohort study, patients with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis underwent ophthalmologic assessment before dupilumab initiation and after 4 months. HA 0.15% eye drops were prescribed to patients with at least one basal or reflex Schirmer value ≤15 mm and ocular symptoms.
