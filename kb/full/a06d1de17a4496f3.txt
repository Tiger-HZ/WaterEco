Along with the development of intelligent algorithms in radio frequency systems, the design of frequency source is gradually becoming more accurate, adaptable, and intelligent. In order to meet the needs of a wide band radar receiver for a fixed 3.5 GHz IF, a high performance frequency source design method is proposed, which combines the Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) and the Direct Digital Synthesis (DDS) architecture. In this paper, a Backpropagation (BP) Neural Network and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) are used to model the temperature drift and adaptive compensation. Phase noise and power fluctuation are efficiently supplied by implementing a closed loop reconstruction and feedback adjustment mechanism in an Field-Programmable Gate Array (FPGA) control platform.
