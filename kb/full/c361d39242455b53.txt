Abstract Maize ( Zea mays L.) is a major cereal crop in Ethiopia, yet its productivity remains constrained by soil fertility decline and inefficient fertilizer use under rain-fed conditions. This study evaluated the response of hybrid maize variety BH-546 to different nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) fertilizer rates, with emphasis on grain yield, biomass production, nutrient use efficiency, and economic profitability in the central rift valley of Ethiopia. Field experiments were conducted from 2022 to 2024 at Negelle Arsi research station and on farmers’ fields in a randomized complete block design with three replications.
