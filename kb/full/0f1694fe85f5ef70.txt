Increasing agricultural production while lowering greenhouse-gas (GHG) intensity is a defining challenge for climate-smart agrifood systems. This critical review evaluates nitrogen-centered nutrient innovations through a boundary-explicit, two-axis framework: evidence confidence for a stated productivity-GHG decoupling outcome and implementation readiness in a specified context. The synthesis covers enhanced-efficiency fertilizers (EEFs), biological nitrogen fixation (BNF), biological nitrification inhibition (BNI), site-specific nutrient management (SSNM), 4R stewardship, precision placement, digital decision support, circular nitrogen recovery, and integrated nutrient management (INM).
