Infrared small and dim target detection is highly sensitive to background clutter because target responses are usually weak, sparse, and easily confused with non-target thermal structures. To improve detection robustness under complex infrared scenes while maintaining computational efficiency, this paper presents SASE-DETR, an improved end-to-end detector built on RT-DETR. The proposed framework enhances feature representation at three levels. First, a C2f-SASCG module is introduced into the backbone, where spatially adaptive sampling and channel gating are combined to strengthen local structural responses of small targets. Second, a Hierarchical Attention Fusion Block is designed to guide cross-scale feature interaction and reduce the propagation of background-dominated information. Third, a Distribution-aware Hierarchical Self-Attention encoder is incorporated to model feature response distributions during global semantic representation. Experiments on HIT-UAV and SIRST show that SASE-DETR improves mAP@0.5 by 3.7% and 2.5% compared with RT-DETR-r18, respectively. Meanwhile, the proposed model contains 16.7M parameters and achieves an inference speed of approximately 84 FPS. Additional evaluation on IRSTD-1K further demonstrates its stable generalization under different infrared data distributions. These results indicate that SASE-DETR provides a favorable trade-off between detection accuracy, model complexity, and real-time inference for infrared small target detection.
