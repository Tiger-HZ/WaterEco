Municipal sewage sludge contains carbon and mineral constituents that can be recovered as water-treatment materials. This study compared four silicon-rich carbons prepared under different carbon dioxide activation and nitric-acid modification conditions to examine whether material properties ranked arsenic(V) and lead(II) removal consistently. Pre-contact characterisation combined spectroscopy, microscopy, elemental analysis, nitrogen sorption and surface-charge assessment. Separate model-groundwater solutions containing each contaminant were treated in triplicate at pH 6.0, 6.5 and 7.0. Mean removal ranged from 47.8 to 76.2% for As(V) and from 68.8 to 96.3% for Pb(II).
