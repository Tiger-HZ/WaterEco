Water infiltration is the process by which water enters the soil surface and moves downward through the soil profile. The inability of water to infiltrate the soil often results in increased surface runoff, which can lead to flooding. Water infiltration is a key component of the hydrological cycle, influencing groundwater recharge, soil moisture availability, crop production, and the flow of surface runoff. To understand water infiltration rates in the three vulnerable LGAs (Ahoada East, Ahoada West, and Ogba/Egbema/Ndoni) in Rivers State, water infiltration was measured using the double-ring infiltration method.
