Soil erosion and land degradation are the main environmental challenges disturbing ecosystem services, agricultural productivity, and community livelihoods in Ethiopia. The Southern Main Ethiopian Rift is one of the regions experiencing high levels of soil erosion and land degradation, driven by active geological processes and anthropogenic activities. However, the contribution of these geological processes to salinization and their subsequent impact on soil erosion and land degradation in the region remains poorly understood and insufficiently studied.
