Abstract This review aims to assess the condition and primary factors influencing reporting quality and bias in systematic reviews of clinical prediction models. We conducted a critical review through three databases (PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library) from inception to January 7, 2023. Screening, data extraction, and assessment were conducted independently by pairs. Employing TRIPOD-SRMA and ROBIS to evaluate the reporting and methodological quality. Data were summarized through descriptive statistics, visual analysis, and multiple linear regression analysis. 1004 reviews were included. Reporting quality was poor.
