Abstract The Mammography Quality Standards Act (MQSA), enacted by Congress in 1992, established nationwide requirements to ensure the safety, consistency, and diagnostic quality of mammography in the United States. Compliance encompasses equipment performance, personnel qualifications, quality assurance and quality control activities, reporting standards, patient notification, and facility certification. Although individual MQSA components are straightforward, the cumulative responsibilities, particularly for the Lead Interpreting Physician (LIP), can be complex. This article provides a comprehensive overview of the MQSA regulatory framework, including the roles of federal and state entities, accreditation bodies, and key personnel essential to maintaining a compliant mammography program.
