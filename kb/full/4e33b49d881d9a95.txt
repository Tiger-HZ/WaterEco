Fractured bottom-water gas reservoirs are prone to water channeling during production, leading to gas well flooding and reduced recovery. This study takes the B9 gas reservoir in the Tarim Basin as the research object and employs numerical simulation methods to conduct collaborative optimization research on early water invasion prevention and CO2 injection for enhanced recovery. Firstly, reasonable gas production rates and water-avoidance heights are optimized to determine the optimal development scheme. Then, the CO2 injection location and rate are systematically optimized to establish the optimal injection scheme.
