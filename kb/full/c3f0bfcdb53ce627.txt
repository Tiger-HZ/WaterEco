This study examines accounting standards convergence and International Financial Reporting Standards adoption with particular emphasis on their effects on financial statement comparability among listed firms. The motivation for the study arises from the increasing globalization of capital markets and the growing demand for transparent, consistent and comparable financial information to support investment and regulatory decisions. The study adopts an ex post facto research design and relies on secondary data obtained from the audited annual reports and financial statements of selected listed firms over the period 2019 to 2024.
