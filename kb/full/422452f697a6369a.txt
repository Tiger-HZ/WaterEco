ABSTRACT This study proposes a Multi-Variable Integrated MCDM–GDM framework to evaluate and rank CMIP6 GCMs for precipitation (Pr), atmospheric maximum and minimum temperatures (Tasmax and Tasmin). The framework is demonstrated for the Upper Mahanadi River Basin, India, using 0.25° gridded IMD observations. In the proposed framework twenty-three (Pr), twenty-five (Tasmax), and twenty-four (Tasmin) bias-corrected CMIP6 models were assessed against several statistical performance indicators, such as mean error, bias, variability, and extremes, with AHP weights prioritising precipitation. Six complementary MCDM techniques were applied grid-wise and aggregated through a Group Decision-Making consensus to identify the best-performing CMIP6 models.
