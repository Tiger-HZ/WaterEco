The increasing demand for sustainable engineering materials has accelerated the development of high-performance biocomposites reinforced with renewable resources. In the present investigation, hybrid epoxy biocomposites were developed using silane-treated Regenerated Bamboo-Viscose (RBV) fibers and biomass-derived biosilica nanoparticles extracted from Manihot esculenta (cassava) tuber skins. Both the regenerated fibers and biosilica nanoparticles were surface-modified using 3-aminopropyltrimethoxysilane (3-APTMS) to enhance interfacial compatibility with the epoxy matrix. Hybrid composites containing 40 vol.% RBV fiber and varying biosilica contents (0–5 vol.%) were fabricated through the hand lay-up technique followed by post-curing.
