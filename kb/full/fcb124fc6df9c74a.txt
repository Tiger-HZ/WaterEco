Abstract Water supply wells often require large open-hole intervals across aquifers to deliver high yields with high-capacity pumps, which traditionally necessitates heavy multi-string casing. This abstract presents a new casing configuration that streamlines the well design by using two successive open-hole sections in place of conventional cemented casing strings. The result is a technically innovative and more sustainable well architecture that meets all performance and integrity requirements with fewer materials and operations. The new design places a 13 3/8″ casing above the first aquifer, then drills a continuous 12 1/4″ hole through both aquifers down to the well's total depth. A 9 5/8″ pre-perforated liner is installed across the upper aquifer, and two 9-5/8″ swell packers are used
