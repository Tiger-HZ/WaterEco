In recent times, water stress is a major factor affecting the growth and quality of lettuce, which makes early and reliable stress identification is significant for efficient irrigation and sustainable crop management. The existing deep learning-based approaches often rely on a single sensing modality and may contain redundant high-dimensional features, which limit the robustness and efficiency of water stress identification. To tackle these limitations, this research proposes a Modified Inception V4 with Adaptive Cuckoo–COOT optimization for multimodal lettuce water stress identification. The proposed framework integrates thermal infrared images, Red Green and Blue images, pseudo-colored Red Green and Blue images, and soil moisture measurements to jointly characterize thermal, morphological, localized stress, and root-zone moisture information. A Modified Inception V4 is employed to extract discriminative deep features from the image modalities, while statistical features are derived from the soil moisture measurements. The extracted representations are then integrated through feature-level multimodal fusion to construct a unified feature vector. To address feature redundancy and reduce the dimensionality of the fused representation, an Adaptive Cuckoo–COOT algorithm is designed to select the most informative features using COOT-based movement, Cuckoo Search, and adaptive Lévy flight strategies. The Multi-Layer Perceptron is deployed to distinguish healthy and water-stressed lettuce plants from the selected features. For the detected water-stressed samples, a Water Stress Severity Index is further computed from the normalized multimodal responses to provide quantitative stress assessment. The Modified Inception V4 with Adaptive Cuckoo–COOT Optimization was validated on the multimodal lettuce dataset and achieved an accuracy of 98.83%, a precision of 98.74%, and an error rate of 1.17%. Such outcomes demonstrate that the proposed framework effectively integrates heterogeneous sensing information, reduces redundant feature representation, and provides accurate and reliable automated lettuce water stress identification. It demonstrates its effective solution in intelligent irrigation management and precision agriculture applications.
