Salivary pH plays an important role in maintaining oral health, particularly in regulating the oral environment and preventing dental caries. Young coconut water contains various minerals and bioactive compounds that may influence oral conditions, including salivary pH. However, evidence regarding the effect of rinsing with young coconut water on salivary pH among elementary school students remains limited. This study aimed to determine the effect of rinsing with young coconut water on changes in salivary pH among sixth-grade students at SD Negeri Kajhu, Baitussalam District, Aceh Besar Regency.
