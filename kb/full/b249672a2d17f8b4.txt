Knowledge and practice of nutrition and climate change education among adolescents in karu LGA Nasarawa state, Nigeria towards the consumption of fruits and vegetables was studied by administering a well-structured questionnaire. A cross-sectional survey of 442 adolescents aged 10-19 were selected through multistage random sampling. Results showed that mean respondents with good nutritional knowledge of fruits and vegetables consumption after intervention was 66.42% while mean respondents with poor knowledge prior to the intervention was 23.85%. In terms of nutritional practice, outcome showed mean score of 6.22% representing respondents with poor nutritional practice towards fruits and vegetables consumption but increased with a mean score of 28.75% after the intervention.
