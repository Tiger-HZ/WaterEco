Coastal oceanographic buoys provide high-frequency observations, but their scientific use requires channel-specific quality assessment and explicit treatment of representativeness. This study evaluates a coastal buoy deployed off Cabo de Gata, southeastern Spain, during two non-contiguous observation windows in 2025 and 2026. Buoy records were standardized, screened with quality-control tests, aggregated daily without gap filling, and compared with Copernicus Marine and ERA5 products as an external-consistency assessment. Gridded products were interpolated to the mean buoy position using inverse-distance weighting, and Copernicus Marine temperature was vertically interpolated to the 5 m and 10 m buoy-sensor depths.
