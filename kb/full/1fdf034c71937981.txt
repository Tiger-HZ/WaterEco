Optimizing alum dosage remains a major operational challenge in surface-water treatment because coagulant demand is governed by nonlinear physicochemical interactions, episodic source-water disturbances, and non-Gaussian variability. This study developed a reliability-validated and explainable machine-learning framework for predicting alum dosage using operational data from the Tamburawa Water Treatment Plant, Kano, Nigeria. The framework integrated distributional analysis, dependency assessment, stationarity diagnostics, principal component analysis (PCA), ensemble modelling, and SHAP interpretation.
