Using fishes as indicators of estuarine degradation is informative in long-term monitoring programs. Beach seine hauls were used in four estuaries that differed in their trophic and tidal status. The study found that inner, middle, and outer estuarine nearshore fish communities differed significantly at from all estuaries, for all sampling times. To reduce the effects of within-estuary variability, between-estuary differences were examined separately for inner, middle, and outer estuarine areas. These analyses revealed differences in north-south and trophic status in communities between estuaries.
