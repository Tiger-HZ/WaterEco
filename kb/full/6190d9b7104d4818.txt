Benthic photoautotrophic organisms significantly contribute to the productivity of shallow tropical coastal ecosystems. However, measurements of photosynthetic light use and dissipation in benthic organisms are complicated by taxonomic diversity, spatial heterogeneity, natural variability in local nutrient, irradiance, and temperature regimes, as well as destructive sampling protocols. To help overcome these problems, we developed a SCUBA‐based fast repetition rate (FRR) fluorometer for measurements of variable chlorophyll fluorescence in corals, sea grasses, macroalgae, and algal turfs. Photosynthetic light use and electron transport can be readily calculated from variable fluorescence kinetics.
