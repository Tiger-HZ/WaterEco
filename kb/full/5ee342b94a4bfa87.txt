It was previously observed that partial regrowth can be induced to begin in adult Drosophila limb by modulating nutrients. In this work, to further investigate the observation, we characterized the early cellular and tissue-level processes that are promoted by nutrient treatment after injury. We find that response to nutrient treatment can be readily identified within hours, by alteration in wound healing. Subsequently, at day-scale, dramatic reversal of large-scale tissue loss occurs in treated flies. Finally, at week-scale, tissue remodeling begins and a dome-shaped protrusion forms at the injury site.
