Introduction. Water sports include a wide range of recreational and competitive activities, such as swimming, surfing, windsurfing, kitesurfing, wingfoiling and sailing. Although these sports share the aquatic environment, they differ substantially in biomechanics, equipment use, speed and environmental exposure. As a result, they are associated with diverse injury patterns, ranging from overuse conditions to acute traumatic injuries. Aim of the study. The aim of this structured narrative review was to summarize the available literature on injuries occurring in water sports, with particular emphasis on epidemiology, anatomical injury patterns, mechanisms of injury and prevention strategies across swimming, surfing, windsurfing, kitesurfing, wingfoiling and sailing.
