Log parsing transforms unstructured logs into structured templates for downstream analysis. Syntax-based parsers are fast but lose accuracy on logs that deviate from predefined rules. Recently, large language models (LLMs)-based log parsers have shown superior parsing accuracy (PA) but face three issues: (1) manual labeling for fine-tuning or in-context learning, (2) high cost from large volumes and limited context size of LLMs, and (3) privacy risks with commercial models. We present LibreLog, an unsupervised approach using open source LLMs to enhance privacy and reduce cost while achieving state-of-the-art accuracy.
