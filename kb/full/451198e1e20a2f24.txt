Abstract Background Medical aesthetics science popularization videos have progressively emerged as a crucial channel of information for patients. Therefore, the significance of their scientific rigor and standardization has become increasingly pronounced. Nevertheless, there is a dearth of comprehensive quality evaluation for such videos in China. Objectives This study evaluates the current state and quality of such videos on China’s three major social media platforms. Methods The top 200 videos for six key words on three major social media platforms were collected. Baseline characteristics and quality evaluation (PEMAT - A/V score and DISCERN questionnaire) of the videos was gathered.
