 Nutrient Fertilization 
 
 Ocean-based carbon dioxide removal via nutrient fertilization is the
addition of nutrients such as iron, phosphorus, nitrogen or silica to
nutrient-poor regions of the surface ocean in order to: 
 
 increase photosynthesis by marine phytoplankton, and thus enhance
uptake of carbon dioxide (CO 2 ) from surface waters,
and 
 enhance the transfer of the newly formed organic carbon to the
deep sea, where it can be sequestered for a century or longer.
