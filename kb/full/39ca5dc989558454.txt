Abstract In the present study, orange peel biochar has been utilized as the adsorbent for the removal of phenol from contaminated water. The biochar was prepared by thermal activation process at three different temperature 300, 500 and 700°C and are defined as B300, B500, and B700 respectively. The synthesized biochar has been characterized using scanning electron microscopy (SEM), X‐ray diffraction (XRD), Fourier transformation infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), RAMAN spectroscopy, X‐ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), and UV–Vis spectroscopy. SEM analysis revealed a highly irregular and porous structure for B700 as compared with others.
