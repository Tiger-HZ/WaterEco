Purpose Educational goal attainment in the primary schools of Gamo and Gofa Zones, South Ethiopia, has been constrained by persistent internal inefficiency, resulting in substantial resource wastage because of delayed student progression. Despite ongoing efforts to address this challenge, the performance was below expected standards. Thus, the purpose of this study is to improve education quality through application of balanced leadership dimensions in the target primary schools. Design/methodology/approach This study used a quantitative research approach and multiple regression analysis to identify the relative importance of leadership dimensions in predicting internal efficiency.
