ABSTRACT This study investigates how tidal and seasonal fluctuations impact the Kapatakkha River's irrigation suitability, addressing the oversight of tidal‐scale dynamics in traditional water quality assessments. The problem centres on a suitability paradox in southwestern Bangladesh. The objectives were to profile spatiotemporal hydro‐chemical dynamics and evaluate overall suitability using an integrated Water Quality Index. Methods involved analysing major ions (Na + , K + , Ca 2+ , Mg 2+ , Cl − , SO₄ 2− , HCO₃ − and PO₄ 3− ) and field parameters (pH, EC, TDS and DO) from eight stations during pre‐ and postmonsoon cycles, utilising principal component analysis (PCA), hierarchical cluster analysis (HCA) and spatial interpolation.
