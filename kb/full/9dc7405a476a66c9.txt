Ecological restoration in arid regions can enhance ecosystem functions but may coincide with groundwater stress. Using nine headwater basins of the Tarim River Basin, we analyzed a GRACE/GLDAS-derived groundwater storage anomaly (GWSA) proxy for 2003–2025 and four ecosystem services and the integrated index (ESI) for 2000–2025; joint analyses covered 2003–2025. Correlation analysis, joint-trend zoning, and CatBoost–SHAP characterized spatial mismatches and nonlinear associations. The GWSA proxy declined by −0.31 mm month−1, with negative anomalies intensifying after 2020 and a northwest-decrease/southeast-increase pattern.
