ABSTRACT Efficient Li + /Mg 2+ separation is essential for lithium recovery from salt‐lake brines, yet remains highly challenging because of the similar physicochemical properties of these ions and the large excess of Mg 2+ in practical feed streams. Polyamide (PA) membranes offer a promising solution, representing the most widely used platform. However, their intrinsically negative surface of conventional PA membranes limits selectivity under Mg 2 + ‐rich conditions. Although introducing positive charges can enhance selectivity through Donnan exclusion, current approaches rarely achieve a homogeneous distribution.
