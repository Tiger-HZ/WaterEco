Rural communities in Burkina Faso depend almost exclusively on groundwater due to the scarcity and poor quality of surface waters, often affected by diffuse pollution. This resource remains highly vulnerable to the combined effects of climate variability, socioeconomic development, and increasing anthropogenic pressures. Groundwater exploitation in basement terrains is complicated by strong geological heterogeneity, with a drilling failure rate of about 20% based on a dataset of 368 boreholes. The national access rate, targeted at 80% in 2025, declined to 71.9% in 2015 and only slightly improved to 76.4% in 2020.
