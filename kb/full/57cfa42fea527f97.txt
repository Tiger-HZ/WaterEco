Abstract Plant stress due to nutrient deficiencies in hydroponic cultivation is difficult for farmers to recognize. The hydroponic nutrient solution uses a standard formula whose nutritional composition is often unknown. If the nutrient solution is not properly maintained, it can lead to nutrient deficiencies in the plants. Early detection of nutrient stress symptoms in plants can enable immediate nutrient treatments. This research aims to develop a nutrient stress detection model for early detection in leaf vegetable crops using a deep learning algorithm.
