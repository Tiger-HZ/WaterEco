A field experiment was conducted at Crop Research Center, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel University of Agriculture &amp; Technology, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, with a view to compare the production potential under different nutrient management. The treatments comprised of Control (T1), Control (T2), Recommended NPK (150: 80:60), (T3), 75 % Recommended NPK + FYM @ 5t/ha (T4), 75 % Recommended NPK +Bio stimulant-G @ 25 kg /ha (T5), 75 % Recommended NPK + Bio stimulants - L @ 625 ml/ha foliar spray each at 50 &amp; 70 DAS (T6), 75 % Recommended NPK + FYM @ 5t /ha + Bio stimulants -G @25 kg/ha (T7) 50 % Recommended NPK + FYM @ 10 t/ha + NPK - Consortia (Seed treatment) + Bio stimulant - G @ 25 kg/ha and (T8) 50 % Recommended NPK + FYM @ 10 t/ha + NPK-Consortia (Seed treatment) + Bio stimulants - L @ 625
