Top soil (0-20cm) samples were collected from six sampling points under different land use type (Cultivated and uncultivated wetland). The total concentrations of Ca, Fe, Cu, K, Mg, Na, Za and C were determined using AAS Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer in order to assess and compare contamination levels of the sampling points. The results showed that the average concentration of these heavy metals were generally higher in cultivated wetland than those in uncultivated wetland.
