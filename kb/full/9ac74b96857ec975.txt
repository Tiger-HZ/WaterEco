Introduction. Hypercholesterolemia is typically associated with obesity and metabolic syndrome, yet elevated total and LDL cholesterol are also frequently observed in underweight individuals, including patients with anorexia nervosa (AN). This paradox can cause diagnostic uncertainty when lipid results are interpreted based on concepts derived from obesity-related dyslipidemia. Aim. To summarize current evidence on mechanisms underlying hypercholesterolemia in underweight individuals, with emphasis on AN, and to outline its clinical significance and management. Materials and Methods. A narrative review of PubMed and Google Scholar literature published up to July 2026, prioritizing systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and observational studies on lipid abnormalities in underweight populations
