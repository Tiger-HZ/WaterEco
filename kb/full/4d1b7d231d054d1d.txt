Abstract Upcycling food-processing by-products into protein-rich ingredients could improve the sustainability and nutritional diversity of plant-based foods. This study evaluated coconut protein concentrate (CPC), recovered from a virgin coconut oil processing by-product, as an ingredient in coconut–chickpea plant-based milk alternatives. CPC contained 82.6% protein and was blended with chickpea protein (CHP) at CPC ratios of 100:0, 0:100, 50:50 and 34:66. Formulations underwent simulated gastrointestinal digestion using the INFOGEST 2.0 protocol. Protein hydrolysis was assessed by SDS-PAGE, and bioaccessible amino acids and minerals by LC-MS and ICP-MS, respectively.
