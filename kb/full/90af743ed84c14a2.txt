This study evaluated whether microbial fertilizer combined with a 50% reduction in chemical fertilizers can maintain or improve growth performance of curly lettuce (Lactuca sativa L. var. crispa) under greenhouse conditions. The study was arranged in a completely randomized design with seven treatments and four replications: control without fertilizer application (C), optimum chemical fertilization (OF), three combined treatments consisting of 50% reduced chemical fertilizer plus microbial fertilizer at 5 mL L⁻¹ (RCF_MF5), 10 mL L⁻¹ (RCF_MF10), and 20 mL L⁻¹ (RCF_MF20), and two sole microbial fertilizer treatments at 10 mL L⁻¹ (MF10) and 20 mL L⁻¹ (MF20). Irrigation was applied at approximately four-day intervals by restoring the depleted soil moisture to field capacity based on pot weight
