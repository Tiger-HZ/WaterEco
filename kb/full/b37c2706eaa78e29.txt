Abstract This study evaluates the spatial and historical patterns of primary productivity proxies and trace elements in core and surface sediments from the southern Marmara Sea following the 2020 mucilage event, with emphasis on the Erdek and Bandırma Gulfs. Stratigraphic records reveal a sustained rise in eutrophication indicators and metal enrichment over the last ~ 200 years, coinciding with accelerated industrialization and urban expansion in the region. Surface sediments from the southern Erdek Gulf display pronounced accumulation of nitrogen, phosphorus, biogenic silica, organic carbon, and trace metals (As, Cd, Cr, Ni, Zn, Hg), primarily driven by terrestrial inputs associated with the Gönen River catchment.
