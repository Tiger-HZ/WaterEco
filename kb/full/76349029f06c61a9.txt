The provision of safe, high-quality drinking water is becoming increasingly important due to the growing anthropogenic pressure on water resources, the deterioration of environmental conditions, and stricter requirements for the sanitary and hygienic characteristics of water. Drinking water quality is one of the key factors determining public health, ecosystem stability, and regional environmental safety. Therefore, a comprehensive investigation of natural and drinking water is of considerable scientific and practical significance. The aim of this study was to conduct a comprehensive assessment of groundwater quality based on physicochemical and radiological parameters using modern statistical methods.
