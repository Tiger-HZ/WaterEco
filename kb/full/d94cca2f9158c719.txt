Microplastics serve as vectors for heavy metals in aquatic environments, posing potential ecological and human health risks. This study assessed the concentrations of cadmium (Cd), copper (Cu), chromium (Cr), iron (Fe), manganese (Mn), nickel (Ni), lead (Pb), and mercury (Hg) adsorbed onto microplastics in surface water (MCPWs) from the Otuoke River, South-South Nigeria, during dry and wet seasons. Samples were digested using the US EPA 3050B method and analyzed via Flame Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometry (FAAS). The ecological and human health risks were evaluated through hazard quotient (HQ) and hazard index (HI) assessments.
