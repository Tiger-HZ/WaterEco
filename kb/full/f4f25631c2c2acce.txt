Abstract Large-scale 3D subsurface models constitute, among others, the foundation for groundwater flow simulations and management, geothermal potential assessment, carbon capture and storage (CCS) initiatives, as well as site selection strategies for nuclear waste repositories. As part of a larger project dealing with numerical predictions of subglacial meltwater erosion during future glaciations, we present a flexible workflow that integrates surface-based structural modelling with voxel-based hydrostratigraphic grid modelling to spatially model the complex fill of the Northwest German Basin. The modelling workflow is applicable to other sedimentary basins with complex fills and heterogeneous data coverage. The workflow was applied to construct regional 3D subsurface grid models to 2,000
