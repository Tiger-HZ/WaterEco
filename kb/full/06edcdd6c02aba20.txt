The Andakill Protected Habitat Area is a natural reserve adjacent to the estuary of the Borgarfjörður Fjord. The Andakilsá River is home to numerous marshes and wetlands. A sediment flushing of a local hydropower reservoir resulted in restoration works using nature-based solutions to restore the riverbanks, which were previously jeopardized by hydropeaking. Bank erosion has been reduced by using locally available materials and implementing sustainable bank-protection measures, thereby improving biodiversity. Various educational initiatives have been carried out in this area, such as a collaboration between Reykjavík University and the Agricultural University of Iceland, which also involved cooperation with other international universities under Unigreen Alliance cooperation. These include courses, workshops, polar winter schools and summer schools aimed at addressing water management as a resource from a holistic and interdisciplinary perspective. This collaboration has yielded numerous results in terms of understanding the local area and involving stakeholders in knowledge-building and research processes, with significant interest also shown by young international students. This chapter aims to publish landscape analysis studies and educational experiences conducted at the university level that include relationships with the hydroelectric power plant, geothermal infrastructure, vegetation, riverbank restoration projects, and cultural aspects such as fishing, from a holistic perspective that views the landscape as a blend of human-caused and natural phenomena, to improve the planning and management of the natural area. Particular emphasis is placed on the importance of involving the population and landowners who preserve specific knowledge and expertise about the area. Examples that could benefit the research worldwide.
