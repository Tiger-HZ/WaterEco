The paper tackles the topic of water in its various physical forms and the symbolic aspects triggered by the metaphoric mappings of two important rivers – the western Thames, and the eastern Tigris – in the interpretation of Elif Shafak’s latest novel. There Are Rivers in the Sky by Elif Shafak is an ambitious poetic novel that weaves together three stories across different time periods and locations, all connected by the symbolic power of water. Starting in Mesopotamian times by the Tigris, shifting through Victorian London by the Thames, moving back to Mesopotamian territories, but in the time of ISIS Middle Eastern Wars and turning back to present time London, the novel's central theme revolves around water as both a life-giving and destructive force.
