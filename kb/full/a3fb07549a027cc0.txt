ABSTRACT Questions How have species richness, functional composition and community structure of aquatic macrophytes in standing waters changed since the mid‐20th century? Do these changes differ between oligotrophic and meso‐eutrophic waters, and to what extent do protected areas conserve macrophyte diversity? Location Northwest Germany. Methods We analysed temporal changes in 76 macrophyte species across 119 waters. Sites were originally surveyed in the mid‐20th century with resurveys in the early 21st century; 55 sites also had late 20th century records. Changes in species richness, growth forms, ecological indicator values and individual species occurrences were assessed using permutational paired t ‐tests and generalised linear mixed models.
