The untapped genetic diversity of extremophilic microalgae provides distinctive physiological and biochemical traits with potential to support Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), and SDG 15 (Life on Land). This review examines the diversity, stress-adaptation mechanisms, and biotechnological potential of extremophilic microalgae, with emphasis on their roles in resource recovery, carbon utilization, and bioproduct generation.
