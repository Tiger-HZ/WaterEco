Anthropogenic pollution contributes to increased nutrient concentrations in aquatic environments. These nutrients are responsible for eutrophication, which promotes the growth of certain floating macrophytes. This study aims to characterize the physico-chemical properties of water in the Akeu river and evaluate the growth of Lemna minor in a mesocosm under eutrophic conditions. Physico-chemical variables from three zones of the river (upstream, middle, and downstream) were collected from January to March 2025. The results obtained allowed the river to be classified as mesotrophic to hypereutrophic. This trophic level led to testing the ability of Lemna minor to grow in a nutrient-enriched environment.
