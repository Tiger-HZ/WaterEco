ABSTRACT Due to the significant influence of multiple‐source input and frequently changing sedimentary environments, the mechanisms of organic matter enrichment and preservation in marine‐continental transitional source rocks have long lacked in‐depth discussion. In this study, the lower Pinghu Formation (Eocene) coal‐measure source rocks in the Bao‐Wu area, Xihu Sag, East China Sea Basin, are selected as the research target to investigate the critical coupling between multiple sources and sedimentary environmental factors that control organic matter enrichment. The marine‐continental transitional shales in this area exhibit complex lithologies and diverse organic matter (OM) sources.
