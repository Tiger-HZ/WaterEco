Abstract Water scarcity and irrigation water quality pose critical challenges in wheat cultivation. This study evaluates the water requirements, yield, and water productivity of three wheat varieties: NIA Amber, NIA Sarang, and NIA Zarkhaiz, under surface water (EC 0.5 – 1 dS / m ) and marginal groundwater (EC 2 – 2.5 dS / m ) irrigation, using minilysimeters at the Drainage and Reclamation Institute of Pakistan (DRIP) during the 2023–2024 wheat season. Under surface water, NIA Amber had the highest crop water requirement (CWR) at 450 mm, followed by NIA Zarkhaiz (440 mm) and NIA Sarang (424 mm).
