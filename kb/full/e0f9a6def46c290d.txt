Artificial intelligence (AI) has demonstrated significant potential in advancing food processing through applications such as food quality prediction, classification, sensory evaluation, and reducing post-harvest losses. This review systematically examines key AI technologies, including artificial neural networks (ANN), machine learning (ML), natural language processing, big data analytics, and knowledge-based expert systems, and their integration with external sensors like electronic noses, electronic tongues, near-infrared spectroscopy, and computer vision systems. The findings highlight that ANN and ML models achieve high accuracy in modeling complex food processes, predicting sensory attributes, and supporting quality control. Moreover, combining AI algorithms with sensor technologies enables real-time, non-destructive, and precise food quality monitoring. The review also identifies the critical role of fuzzy logic and expert systems in decision-making and process optimization within the food industry. Overall, AI-driven innovations are poised to enhance operational efficiency, ensure food safety, and support sustainable production, marking a transformative step toward smart food processing.
