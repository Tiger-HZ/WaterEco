Diospyros lotus Linn. is a multipurpose tree species widely used as a rootstock for Japanese persimmon, but its propagation is constrained by poor and erratic seed germination. The present study assessed variability among three seed sources—Kishtwar (T₁), Bhaderwah (T₂), and Poonch (T₃)—in fruit characteristics, seed traits, and germination performance under controlled laboratory conditions. The experiment was conducted using a completely randomised design with three replications. The evaluated parameters were number of seeds per fruit, average fruit size, 100-seed weight, germination percentage, fresh fruit weight, and dry fruit weight.
