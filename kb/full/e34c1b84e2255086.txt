&lt;p&gt;The national agricultural product quality and safety pilot (APQS) is not only related to food safety but may also affect agricultural green transformation by changing agricultural production practices. Using panel data from Chinese prefecture-level cities over the period 2010–2021, this study applies a multi-period difference-in-differences approach to assess the effects of this policy on cultivated land green utilization efficiency (LGUE) and further examines its effects on outcomes related to the resource, market, and technology dimensions. The results indicate that the pilot policy significantly improves LGUE, and this finding remains robust across staggered DID estimation and other robustness checks.
