The aim of this article is to explore how Liquid Death converted an ordinary product into a rebellious consumer identity. Bottled water brands usually adopt traditional brand design methods, such as blue packaging, transparent cans, natural landscape images, and the concept of water purification. However, Liquid Death is different from this trend and successfully created a unique brand image: black aluminum cans, gothic fonts, skull patterns, dark humor, and the slogan concept of "Murder Your Thirst". Therefore, Liquid Death did not shape the traditional brand image of bottled water, but innovated an unconventional image of a rebel, whose style is closer to a heavy metal concert rather than an ordinary and healthy water brand.
