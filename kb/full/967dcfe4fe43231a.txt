Weed competition can markedly constrain organic beetroot production, where reliance on synthetic herbicides is restricted. A two-season field experiment (2022–2023 and 2023–2024) was conducted at Professor Jayashankar Telangana Agricultural University, Hyderabad, to compare nine weed-management treatments for beetroot (Beta vulgaris L.). The treatments included groundnut-shell and rice-husk mulches, polyethene mulch with intra-row hand weeding, rice-straw mulch with intra-row hand weeding, hoeing, green-leaf vegetable intercropping with hand weeding, tamarind-leaf aqueous extract with hand weeding, an unweeded control and a non-organic pendimethalin reference treatment.
