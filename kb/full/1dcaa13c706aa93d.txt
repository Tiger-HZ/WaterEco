Abstract This study aimed to evaluate diets with different inclusion levels of cactus pear in BRS 716 sorghum biomass silage for feeding lactating crossbred cows (approximately 80 days in milk, with an average initial body weight of 471.5 ± 52.8 kg). Ten lactating cows were assigned to two simultaneous 5 × 5 Latin squares over a 90-day period (five periods of 18 days). Five diets with increasing levels of cactus pear (0%, 15%, 30%, 45%, and 60% on a fresh matter ((FM) basis) or (0%, 6%,13.4%, 19.9%, and 35.1% on a dry matter basis) in sorghum biomass silage were evaluated. The diets had a roughage-to-concentrate ratio of approximately 75:25, being isonitrogenous and supplied as TMR. Increasing cactus pear in the silage did not affect dry matter intake but decreased NDF intake by 12.03 g/da
