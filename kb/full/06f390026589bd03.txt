ABSTRACT Graphical overview of Na2S precipitation of real IC substrate wastewater. A optimized condition of pH 6.50, contact time 7.5 min, and Na2S:Cu ratio 1.71 achieved 99.29 plus or minus 0.22% Cu removal and produced a copper sulfide precipitate. Real integrated circuit substrate wastewater is a chemically complex treatment matrix in which elevated copper coexists with chloride, organic matter, and other dissolved constituents that can alter sulfide demand and precipitation behaviour. This study evaluated copper removal from combined real wastewater by sodium sulfide precipitation and optimized solution pH, contact time, and sodium sulfide-to-copper molar ratio using response surface methodology with a central composite design.
