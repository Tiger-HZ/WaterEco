Large simulation models of eutrophication processes are commonly used to aid scientific understanding and to guide management decisions. Confidence in models for these purposes depends on uncertainty in model equations (structural uncertainty) and on effects of input uncertainties (model parameters, initial conditions, and forcing functions) on model outputs. Our objective herein is to illustrate two strategies, a generalized likelihood uncertainty estimation (GLUE) approach combined with a simple Monte Carlo sampling scheme and a Bayesian methodological framework along with Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) simulations, for elucidating the propagation of uncertainty in the high‐dimensional parameter spaces of mechanistic eutrophication models.
