Abstract Although the presence of nanoplastics in aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems has received increasing attention, little is known about its potential effect on ecosystem processes and functions. Here, we evaluated if differentially charged polystyrene (PS) nanoplastics (PS‐NH 2 and PS‐SO 3 H) exhibit distinct influences on microbial community structure, nitrogen removal processes (denitrification and anammox), emissions of greenhouse gases (CO 2 , CH 4 , and N 2 O), and ecosystem multifunctionality in soils with and without earthworms through a 42‐day microcosm experiment.
