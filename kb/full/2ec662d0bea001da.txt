Precision agriculture emerged as a key strategy for rational management of water and nutrients amid growing food demand driven by global population expansion. Therefore, timely monitoring of field crops is crucial in maintaining adequate nutrient and water levels in the crop fields. Vegetation indices are increasingly being studied for their use in plant nutrient management and in guiding fertilizer recommendations. However, these indices were not adequately evaluated for nutrient management of crops grown with different water and nitrogen application levels.
