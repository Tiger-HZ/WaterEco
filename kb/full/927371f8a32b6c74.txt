Purpose The purpose of this study is to examine how “others” – such as shopkeepers, domestic workers, drivers and security guards – contribute to the everyday support networks of older Anglo-Indian adults in urban neighbourhoods of Calcutta. It aims to highlight the forms of informal assistance that extend beyond family, friends, neighbours and formal caregivers, which are usually emphasised in gerontological research. This study also seeks to understand how digital technologies like WhatsApp and digital payment systems mediate these support relationships. By focusing on digitally enabled neighbourhood interactions, this research broadens existing perspectives on ageing and care in urban environments.
