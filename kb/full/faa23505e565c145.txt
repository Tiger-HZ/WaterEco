High human activity in the coastal area of Merak Besar Island, Banten Province, such as tourism, port, and industrial activities, potentially degrades water quality and affects benthic organisms that serve as biological indicators of coastal ecosystem health. This study aimed to describe the physicochemical characteristics of the water, total organic matter (TOM), sediment texture, and macrozoobenthos community structure, and to determine the ecological condition of the waters using a macrozoobenthos bioindicator approach based on the AMBI (AZTI's Marine Biotic Index) method.
