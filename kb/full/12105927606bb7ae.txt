INTRODUCTION. The strength of adhesive luting of zirconia restorations largely depends on the surface state. Primers based on the functional monomer 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate (MDP-10) provide chemical bonding of the adhesive to the oxide surface; however, during try-in the framework contacts mixed saliva, whose organic components may adsorb onto the surface. The effect of prior MDP-10 application on such contamination and its subsequent removability is insufficiently studied. PURPOSE. To study the influence of the functional monomer MDP-10 on the elemental composition of the zirconia surface and on the resistance of the MDP-10-primed surface to salivary mucin contamination using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS).
