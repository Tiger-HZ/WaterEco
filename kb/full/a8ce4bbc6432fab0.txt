Abstract This study characterizes reach-scale pollution gradients and two seasonal water-quality snapshots from an urban river heavily impacted by wastewater in northern Vietnam. At 12 stations, the physicochemical, organic, nutrient, microbial, and metal attributes of water samples were analyzed during one dry-season (DS) and one rainy-season (RS) campaign. Due to the lack of within-season replicates, the comparison of DS-RS results is descriptive. An organic-nutrient-microbial gradient was observed across R2-R7. At the reach scale, median dissolved oxygen (DO), biochemical oxygen demand (BOD5), ammonium (NH4+), and phosphate (PO43−) were 1.76, 42.7, 23.0, and 5.77 mg/L in DS and 2.65, 19.5, 10.0, and 3.82 mg/L in RS.
