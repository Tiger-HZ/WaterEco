This chapter explores how a sense of belonging can be nurtured in the Early Years Foundation Stage, where children&s;s earliest experiences of school play a crucial role in shaping their confidence, relationships, and engagement with learning. Using the metaphor of feeling like ‘a fish in water’, the chapter examines how young children flourish when learning environments are carefully designed to support comfort, curiosity, and connection. Drawing on examples from early years practice, the chapter considers how play-based learning, responsive relationships with adults, and thoughtfully organised environments can help children feel secure and valued within the setting. It highlights how early years practitioners can recognise and respond to the diverse needs, interests, and identities that children bring with them when they begin their educational journeys. The chapter argues that belonging is a foundational element of effective early years education. By creating environments where children feel safe, recognised, and able to explore freely, practitioners can support the development of confidence, independence, and a positive disposition towards learning from the very beginning of school life.
