Abstract Water Safety Planning (WSP) and Water Quality Monitoring (WQM) implementation at the national level are two crucial steps to enhance the quality of drinking water. The objectives of this study were to contrast Ethiopia's WQM practices and methods with those of other East African countries and the WHO's recommendations. Thus, the microbiological data were generated by referring to relevant databases, including Scopus, Google Scholar, and PubMed. Two independent reviewers examined the manuscripts. The analysis included English-language publications on WQM subjects from 1990 to 2021. For the comparison, eight representative Eastern African countries were chosen.
