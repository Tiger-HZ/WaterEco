Quality control of concrete works is essential for ensuring durability and safety in large infrastructure projects, especially hydropower projects in Nepal. Conventional quality control practices are mostly based on compliance with code limits and often do not adequately address process variability or early signs of quality deviation. This creates the need for a data-driven and process-oriented approach to quality control.
