Nanotechnology is an interdisciplinary field which is evolving at a rapid pace and is widely applied in areas such as medicine, cosmetology, agriculture, pharmaceutical industry, environmental science, and biotechnology.Biological fabrication of nanoparticles by means of microbes has attracted considerable interest due to its environmentally friendly, economical, and sustainable nature.Actinomycetes belong to microbes that have been considered as excellent choice for the biological synthesis of nanoparticles due to their remarkable metabolic diversity and bioactivity.The current research work is focused on the biosynthesis of silver nanoparticles by Actinomycetes using samples isolated from different environments of Gujarat.Total 33 isolates of Actinomycetes were isolated and preserved from forest soil, field soil, lake soil, marine soil, stream sediments and marine sediments.Biochemical and physiological characterization of these isolates was carried out.Some selected promising isolates were then screened for the ability to synthesize extracellular silver nanoparticles by employing silver nitrate solution.The successful formation of nanoparticles was evident through the characteristic color change from pale yellow to brownish coloration.Initial characterization of the samples was performed using UV-Visible spectroscopy, and the results showed the presence of nanoparticles with characteristic surface plasmon resonance peaks in the range of silver nanoparticles.Optimization experiments for pH, temperature and AgNO₃ concentrations were carried out to improve nanoparticle synthesis process.Results obtained showed some differences in the efficiency of the nanoparticle formation among different isolates.Stable nanoparticle suspensions were successfully produced by selected strains of Actinomycetes.Further characterization experiments using SEM, TEM and FTIR analysis were planned for the detailed characterization of nanoparticle formation process.The current study has shown the potential of environmental Actinomycetes as efficient biocatalysts for nanoparticles synthesis and their future applications in antimicrobial formulation and cosmetic dermatophytic cream formulations.
