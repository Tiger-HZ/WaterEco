Background Patients with gastric cancer often have reduced quality of life (QoL). This study aimed to examine whether nutritional behaviors and nutritional status serially mediate the association between health beliefs and QoL among patients with gastric cancer. Methods This cross-sectional study included 274 patients with gastric cancer. Health beliefs, QoL, nutritional behaviors, and nutritional status were measured using validated instruments. Pearson correlation analysis was performed to examine bivariate relationships. Serial mediation analysis was conducted using structural equation modeling with bootstrap resampling (5,000 samples).
