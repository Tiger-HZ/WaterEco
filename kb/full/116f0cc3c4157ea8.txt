Bioassays are rarely used in ecotoxicological studies of high-altitude lakes located above 3000 m a.s.l., as ultra-low mineralization of water poses a major obstacle to toxicity assessment in biological objects. For the first time, we conducted a 96 h bioassay of surface water from supraglacial, proglacial and moraine-dammed lakes of two distinct mountain ranges of the Northern Tien Shan (average elevations of 3000–3600 m a.s.l.) using Danio rerio embryos. The study demonstrates the feasibility of using the D. rerio embryo toxicity test to survey the initially low-mineralized surface water of high-altitude lakes.
