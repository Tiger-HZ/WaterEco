Spring-planted sugarcane is vulnerable to early weed competition because of its slow initial growth and wide row spacing, while short-duration intercrops may improve system productivity and returns. A three-year field experiment was conducted at the Main Sugarcane Research Station, Junagadh Agricultural University, Kodinar, Gujarat, India, from 2013–14 to 2015–16 to assess the influence of summer intercrops and weed-control practices on the growth, yield, quality, weed dynamics and economics of spring-planted sugarcane.
