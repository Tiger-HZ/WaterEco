Water supports ecosystems that provide habitats for various plant and animal species. Without regular monitoring, contamination may go unnoticed. Water sensors for physicochemical parameters are commercially available, but contaminant sensors still encounter obstacles. This paper explores the feasibility of an optical sensor platform based on fluorescence targeting water quality. This was designed to prevent photobleaching and other sources of fluorescence signal loss and has self-referencing capabilities. This paper details the optical setup and its optimization, including a 450 nm laser source with a measured variation of 0.55% and a fluorophore emitting at 511 nm.
