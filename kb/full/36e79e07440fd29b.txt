The paper examines how these jurisdictions define inventorship, allocate responsibility, and regulate the interaction between human and machine creativity within the current intellectual property protection system. Whether Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems can qualify as inventors under national and international patent law has become as one of the most contested issues in contemporary intellectual property doctrine. The findings indicate that WIPO advocates for a neutral “AI-assisted inventorship” model, emphasising global harmonisation, whereas the EPO follows a human-centred approach that allows only natural persons to be considered inventors.
