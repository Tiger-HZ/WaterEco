ABSTRACT Maintaining soil health and crop productivity in the semi‐arid Vertisols of central India poses a significant challenge under intensive agricultural practices. This 6‐year field study assessed the combined impact of different tillage methods, maize‐based crop rotations, and nitrogen management on soil's physical, chemical, and biological characteristics, soil quality indices (SQI), and crop yields. The experiment used a split‐split‐plot design, featuring no‐till (NT) and conventional tillage (CT) as the main treatments, maize‐wheat and maize–chickpea rotations as sub‐treatments, and four nitrogen levels (0%, 50%, 100%, and 150% of the recommended dose) as sub‐sub treatments.
