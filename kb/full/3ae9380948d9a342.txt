Microplastic contamination has emerged as a significant threat to freshwater ecosystems globally, yet data from African water bodies remain critically limited. This study assessed the occurrence of microplastics and their effects on water quality and oxidative stress responses in Oreochromis niloticus from Jakara Reservoir, Kano State, Nigeria. Physicochemical parameters including temperature, dissolved oxygen (DO), biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), total dissolved solids (TDS), electrical conductivity, turbidity, pH, phosphate-phosphorus, and nitrate-nitrites were analyzed across four sampling sites (A-D).
