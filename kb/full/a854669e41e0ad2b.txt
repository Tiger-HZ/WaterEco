Under the current conditions of global turbulence and unprecedented sanctions, the need to adapt the sustainable development concept to the Russian economic entities in the public sector is growing. The article presents the results of systematizing and analyzing threats to the sustainable development of these entities and identifies cross-cutting links between different threat categories to support a comprehensive approach to neutralizing them. The methodological base draws on general scientific methods: analysis, synthesis, systemic and logical approaches. The theoretical base is a review of Russian and international sources (scientific articles, strategic documents of international organizations and the Russian Federation). The empirical base includes statistics and analytical reviews of the Federal State Statistics Service and Bank of Russia for 2020-2025. Results are as follows: an original classification of threats for the public sector is proposed, covering geopolitical, economic, technological, social, and environmental threats. Direct and reverse links between these threat categories are identified, illustrating a “domino effect” and the formation of a polycrisis, where the realization of one threat triggers a chain reaction across other spheres of sustainable development. The article concludes that countering modern threats calls for a shift from a sector-by-sector to an integrated approach, grounded in ESG principles in public sector management. The authors propose a methodology for neutralizing the identified threats to sustainable development, which can be used in drafting and updating sustainable development strategies and ESG policies of public sector economic entities.
