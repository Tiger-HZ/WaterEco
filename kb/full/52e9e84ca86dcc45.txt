Long-term lake storage monitoring remains challenging in data-scarce high-elevation regions because continuous water-level records and bathymetric data are often unavailable. Annual Landsat-derived areas of Xiao Qaidam Lake from 1996 to 2025 were combined with DEM-based height–area–volume (H–A–V) relationships to reconstruct DEM-referenced relative water-level anomalies and DEM scenario-based relative volume anomalies. Sentinel-2, three DEMs, ICESat-2 ATL13, uncertainty propagation, and buffer tests were used for evaluation. All three DEM scenarios showed long-term increases but different magnitudes.
