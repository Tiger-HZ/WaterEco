Efficient utilization of artificial insemination improves livestock genetic profile in the shortest possible time. For this technique to succeed, semen preservation without damaging spermatozoa's fertilizing ability is necessary. However, liquid storage of semen at 17 °C can reduce cellular integrity due to perceived oxidative activities through cellular metabolic process during storage.
