The North China Plain is a major wheat-producing region in China where appropriate irrigation and nitrogen management are essential for coordinating grain yield, grain nutritional quality, and resource-use efficiency. We hypothesized that moderate water and nitrogen inputs would achieve a better balance among these objectives than excessive or insufficient inputs. The objective of this study was to quantify the effects of irrigation and nitrogen application on wheat growth, grain yield, selected grain nutritional quality indicators, and water–nitrogen-use efficiency, and to identify a management regime that balances these objectives using the entropy-weighted TOPSIS method.
