Abstract In the last decades, forest in the Netherlands have been exposed to high levels nitrogen (N) deposition, which allows to assess the fate and impacts of N and evaluate the concept of N saturation in this large scale “experiment” and related soil acidification. This was done by repeating an inventory of the stocks of nitrogen, in combination with carbon (C), with the base cations calcium (Ca), magnesium (Mg) and potassium (K) as well as aluminum (Al) and of the pH of the organic layer and topsoil (0–30 cm) of 127 forested stands on sandy soils across the Netherlands in 1990 and in 2023.
