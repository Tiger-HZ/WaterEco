Abstract Reservoirs are widely recognized for altering river water quality through physical, chemical, and biological processes. However, their integrated effects on multiple water quality indicators remain poorly quantified, particularly in tropical semiarid basins affected by prolonged droughts. This study evaluated the influence of damming on 15 water quality parameters using long-term monitoring data from eight reservoirs in the Brazilian semiarid region, encompassing a severe multiyear drought during which reservoir storage declined to up to about 1% of capacity. Reservoir effects were quantified using outlet-to-inlet (O/I) parameter ratios.
