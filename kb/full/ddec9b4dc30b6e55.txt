Markonahalli Reservoir in Tumkur District, Karnataka, is used mainly for irrigation and fishing. This study assessed fish diversity and selected physicochemical water-quality parameters from February 2024 to January 2025 to examine reservoir conditions in relation to the recorded fish community. Fish were collected with the assistance of local fishermen using nets of different sizes and types, while water samples were collected monthly from four sampling sites. Water temperature, pH, turbidity, electrical conductivity, nitrate, sulphate, dissolved oxygen, and biological oxygen demand were analysed using standard methods.
