The aim of this study was to assess the prognostic value of tumor mutational burden (TMB) as a biomarker for metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC) in patients receiving immunotherapy-containing regimens. This retrospective cohort study analyzed 221 patients with mCRC. From January 1, 2020 to December 31, 2021, patients diagnosed with unresectable mCRC at the time of initial diagnosis were screened and stratified into 2 groups according to baseline TMB status: a TMB-H group (high tumor mutational burden, n = 113) and a TMB-L group (low tumor mutational burden, n = 108). All patients received anti-PD-1/PD-L1 immunotherapy combined with standard chemotherapy.
