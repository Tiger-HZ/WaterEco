The Murray River is Australia’s longest and most important river. It is the dominant river supporting the Murray-Darling-Basin (MDB), known as Australia’s ‘food bowl’. However, the Murray River has long experienced environmental destruction from low flows, due largely to excessive irrigation. Since the 1990s, the Murray River has further deteriorated under accelerated climate change, generating over 30 years of mostly dry or drought conditions, interspersed with floods. Here, we analyze annual Murray River level time series at two locations, Doctors Point (Upper Murray) and Waikerie (Lower Murray), to assess the significance of climate change impacts on Murray River levels.
