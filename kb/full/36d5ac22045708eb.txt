Worldwide, and also in the Netherlands, eutrophication is happening in surface waters. This means that there are too many nutrients in the water, especially nitrogen and phosphorus. As a consequence, these surface waters emit the greenhouse gases carbon dioxide (CO2), methane (CH4) and nitrous oxide (N2O). Major sources of nutrients are agricultural runoff and the discharge of treated wastewater, called effluent. These sources contribute to eutrophication and greenhouse gas emissions from our surface waters. By further purifying water, in this case effluent, and filtering out the last traces of nutrients, the negative effects on surface waters can be limited.
