Phytophthora infestans (Mont.) de Bary causes one of the most devastating diseases of tomato (Solanum lycopersicum L.) and can cause severe losses in yield and economy worldwide (late blight). Rapid growth and several infection cycles occur in a single growing season under favorable environmental conditions (cool temperatures and high humidity). Traditional control practices are mainly depending on the synthetic fungicides, because the pathogen has aggressive and polycyclic nature which leads to the high need for control measures. Contact fungicides like mancozeb and chlorothalonil as well as systemic fungicides like phenylamides, carboxylic acid amides and newer chemistries like oxathiapiprolin have proved to be very effective in reducing disease severity and protection yield.
