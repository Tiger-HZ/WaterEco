This study aimed to classify the attributes of semi-permanent makeup training quality using the Kano model and analyze the effects of each quality factor on educational satisfaction. For this purpose, a questionnaire survey was conducted with professionals across the country who had completed semi-permanent makeup training at least once, and a total of 369 responses were used for the final analysis. The collected data were analyzed through frequency analysis and descriptive statistical analysis, focusing on the Kano model’s two-dimensional quality classification and Timko’s customer satisfaction coefficients. The results are as follows: Among the participants, many were between 35 and 45 years old, married, and college graduates (or currently enrolled). A large proportion also had 1 to less than 5 years of work experience and an average monthly income between KRW 2 and 4 million. Regarding training status, training at a private salon, taking four or more training sessions, and small practical classes with 3 to 5 participants were the most common. In the Kano model analysis, among the 20 items of training quality, 8 were classified as must-be quality attributes, 8 as one-dimensional quality attributes, and 4 as attractive quality attributes. For the Better index, “the instructor’s voluntary help” and “the instructor’s confident attitude and communication” showed high values, while for the Worse index, “the instructor’s sufficient knowledge” and “keeping class time as scheduled” were high. The above results show that the physical learning environment, the instructor’s expertise, and interaction with learners are important factors in improving educational satisfaction in semi-permanent makeup training. Therefore, it is necessary to standardize the curriculum, improve practice-based learning environments, strengthen instructors’ competencies, and expand personalized communication with learners. It is expected that the study results will serve as basic data for improving the quality of semi-permanent makeup training and for developing educational systems.
