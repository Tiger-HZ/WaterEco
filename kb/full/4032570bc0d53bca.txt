The olive oil sector faces increasing pressure to enhance environmental performance while simultaneously improving industrial efficiency through adoption of digital technologies. Although Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) is widely used to quantify environmental impacts in olive oil production, conventional studies typically rely on static inventories and lack the capability to evaluate alternative operational strategies prior to real implementation. This study presents a Digital Twin-driven Life Cycle Assessment of an industrial olive oil mill, integrating a highly parameterized virtual model within a cyber–physical system to support scenario-based environmental evaluation. A hybrid gate-to-gate LCA was performed, with 1 kg of olive oil as the functional unit. Primary inventory data were generated from the Digital Twin, which enables the simulation of multiple operational configurations without interfering with real production processes. The integrated assessment revealed a total global warming potential of 1.30 kg CO₂-eq/kg of olive oil, with only 0.05 kg CO₂-eq/kg attributable to industrial operations and an overall energy demand of 0.055 kWh/L. The Digital Twin established a validated baseline and fine-resolution monitoring framework, laying the foundation for future consistent comparisons of alternative operational scenarios. The integration of Digital Twin technology with LCA transforms environmental evaluation from a static assessment instrument into a predictive and prescriptive decision-support framework. This approach provides a framework to support sustainability-oriented decision-making within agro-industrial systems by enabling virtual experimentation, minimizing implementation risks, and reinforcing environmental decisions-making.
