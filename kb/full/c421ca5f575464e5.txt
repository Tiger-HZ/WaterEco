This study shows that microplastics create a distinct zone in the surrounding soil, known as the soil-plastisphere, where soil properties differ from those of the bulk soil. As the microplastics age, their surfaces become less water repellent, while the surrounding soil becomes more water-repellent and the forces between soil particles and microplastics weaken. These changes can affect how water and pollutants move through soil, with potential consequences for soil health and environmental quality.
