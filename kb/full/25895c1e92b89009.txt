This study investigated the functional properties of flour, proximate and sensory properties of pastry product (chi-chin) produced from wheat and tiger nut flour blends. Analysis was done using standard methods. Chin-chin was prepared from blends of wheat and tiger nut flour and substituted at (100:0, 90:10, 80:20, 70:30, 60:40 and 50:50) respectively. Data obtained was subjected to analysis of variance (ANOVA) and was separated using Duncan's multiple range test. Results of functional properties of wheat and tiger nut flour blends showed that bulk density ranged from 0.70-0.80gml, water absorption 0.79--1.17g/g, oil absorption 1.55-2.40g/g and least gelation concentration (LGC)2.00-6.00%.
