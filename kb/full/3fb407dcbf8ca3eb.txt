Abstract Purpose The aim of this study was to evaluate the influence of environmental factors, particularly the geological substrate, soil properties, and atmospheric pollution, on the elemental composition of ash produced from wood biomass combustion. The study also assessed the implications of these factors for the use of wood biomass as a high-quality energy resource and for the safe utilization or disposal of the resulting ash. Materials and methods The study was conducted in six forest areas in Poland, differing in soil types (natural and technogenic), geological substrates (sands, ultrabasic rocks, post-industrial waste), and historical dust deposition rates.
