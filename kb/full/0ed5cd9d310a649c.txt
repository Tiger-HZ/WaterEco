Abstract The influences of leaf characteristics on stream invertebrate communities were investigated to improve the basis for selecting plants for riparian management to enhance stream invertebrate abundance and biodiversity. Invertebrate colonisation of leaves of five native and five exotic riparian plants were compared over 4 months in field experiments in a New Zealand pasture stream and adjacent channels, and laboratory experiments were carried out on the leaf preferences and effects on growth of the larvae of Olinga feredayi .
