Abstract La 2 O 3 and CeO 2 , as main rare earth oxides, with unique physical and chemical properties have been widely used in catalyst and grinding industry. In this study, the effects of La 2 O 3 and CeO 2 on the anaerobic process were investigated. The biological methane production tests showed that 0–0.05 g/L La 2 O 3 and 0–0.05 g/L CeO 2 enhanced anaerobic methanogenesis process. The result showed maximum specific methanogenic rates of La 2 O 3 and CeO 2 were 56.26 mL/(h·gVSS) and 49.43 mL/(h·gVSS) and, compared with the control, increased 4% and 3%, respectively. La 2 O 3 significantly reduced the accumulation of volatile fatty acids (VFAs), whereas CeO 2 had no similar effect.
