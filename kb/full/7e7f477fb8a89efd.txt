Abstract Piezo-photocatalysis offers a promising technology for H2O2 production, yet its practicability is limited by insufficient charge separation, inert reaction sites, and inadequate polarization fields. Herein, we present a size-minimized nitrogen-rich carbon nitride, engineered with cyano vacancies and intercalated K+/Na+ ions. In this system, cyano vacancies function as strong electron-withdrawing sites to accelerate charge separation, while K+/Na+ ions act as efficient electron traps that suppress charge recombination. Moreover, the size-minimized nanosheet morphology further intensifies the piezoelectric polarization.
