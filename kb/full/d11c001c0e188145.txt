Using a novel laboratory system, we exposed early benthic juvenile American lobsters (Homarus americanus), raised from ovigerous females coming from three Eastern Canadian locations, to 15 temperatures (5-28°C) and followed their development for approximately four months. We calibrated thermal performance curves (TPCs) for survival and growth rate, and the relationships were well described by Weibull functions with an exponential increase from 5 to ~16 °C, a levelling-off before the thermal optimum of ~21°C and a rapid decline above the optimum; no individual survived to the end of the experiment above 26 °C.
