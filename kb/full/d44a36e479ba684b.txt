Reservoirs are increasingly recognized as active components of the global carbon cycle, acting as both sinks and sources of organic carbon while providing essential drinking-water services. Yet, the spatiotemporal behavior of organic carbon fractions and their coupled controls by hydrological extremes, algal blooms, and reservoir operations remain poorly resolved in large, regulated water-supply reservoirs. Here, we quantified the distribution, drivers, and transport balance of organic carbon in Danjiangkou Reservoir (China) through monthly monitoring from March to December 2024 across the main reservoir, downstream reach, and major tributaries.
