Biofortification of maize genotypes with micronutrients such as beta - carotene is a promising solution to the eradication of Vitamin A deficiency (VAD) in developing countries like Nigeria. A critical factor to consider for retention of beta - carotene in food products is the method/technique of processing such food crops. Processing maize for food exposes the grains to environmental factors such as air, light and temperature which may reduce beta - carotene content. The research question asked in this work says, is there significant difference in beta-carotene content of the product of different maize samples processed by different methods? The aim of this study is to determine beta - carotene concentration in raw samples of the selected maize genotypes and its retention after processing
