Foliated metamorphic rock exists widely in the surrounding rock of tunnel structures, underground utility caverns and mountain civil structures, and its laminated foliation structure induces obvious anisotropic time-dependent creep deformation, which seriously threatens the long-term service safety of underground building support systems. The strength and time-dependent deformation of foliated phyllite vary sharply with bedding dip angles, and long-term creep easily triggers large convergence, secondary-lining cracking and structural intrusion failure of tunnel structures.
