Phytoplankton are sensitive indicators of environmental change in high-altitude aquatic ecosystems, but their community responses across river–lake confluence zones remain insufficiently understood. We investigated phytoplankton community characteristics and environmental associations in the Siling Co river–lake continuum. This endorheic system lies above 4500 m, where river inflows, lake-water mixing, and evaporative concentration generate marked hydrochemical gradients. Genus-level cell density and estimated biomass were assessed with 22 environmental variables from 14 sampling events at 10 lake and river sites.
