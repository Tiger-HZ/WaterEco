Lushan West Reservoir is a typical deep-water reservoir in the southern mountainous and hilly region. As an artificial freshwater reservoir system with a storage capacity of 7.92 billion cubic meters, the relationship between its internal environmental factors and the structure of the biological community is of great significance for maintaining ecological balance, ensuring water quality security, and promoting sustainable development of water ecology. This study selected 20 sampling points in the Jinkou and Sima areas of the Lushan West Reservoir and conducted monthly dynamic monitoring from May 2022 to April 2023 to analyze the spatiotemporal variation characteristics of the phytoplankton community structure and its relationship with environmental factors.
