Najafgarh drain is the largest drain in Delhi, which enters Delhi at the Dhansa barrage and joins the river Yamuna at the Wazirabad barrage, contributing about 60% of the city’s wastewater to the Yamuna River. In present study, the water quality and heavy metal analysis was done from Dhansa barrage to Wazirabad barrage was carried out during 2022-2023. Water quality assessment through WAWQI and HPI, based on CPCB and BIS standards, showed extremely poor conditions of water quality, with WAWQI 917.73 at Dhansa (S1) to 2010.47 at Wazirabad (S10) classifying all sites as unsuitable for drinking.
