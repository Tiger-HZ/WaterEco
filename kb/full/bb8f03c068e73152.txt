ABSTRACT AZ31 magnesium alloy has been widely used in the aerospace and automobile industry due to its weight‐to‐strength ratio and good corrosion resistance properties. However, due to its low melting point and high reactivity, machining this alloy is difficult. Wire Electrical Discharge Machining (WEDM) is a noncontacting machining process that can effectively remove material by electrical discharges. This research work has attempted to investigate the material removal rate of the AZ31 magnesium alloy with various combinations of pulse on‐time ( T on ), pulse off‐time ( T off ), and servo voltage (SV).
