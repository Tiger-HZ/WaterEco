The different hosts available throughout the world is a major source of occurrence of insect pest population. The distributions of furgivorous pest have variety of host plant. The forecasting and prediction are the core of the integrated management of these harmful fruit eating pests. The melon fruit fly Bactrocera cucurbitae (Coq.) is considered as a serious pest of cucurbit vegetables throughout the world. The management of the pest needs a proper monitoring before application of any management tool.
