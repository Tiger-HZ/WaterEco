In the above-mentioned publication, calculated fluxes 
of nutrients (N-NH 4
+, N-NO x
−, DIN and P-PO 4
3−) 
were tenfold lower than they should be. This is a 
careless error made during the conversion of fluxes 
measured per cm2 of column to fluxes per m2 of sedi-
ment surface (a multiplication per 1000 was applied 
while it would have been a multiplication per 10,000). 
This calculation error led to several errors in the 
paper. Although it does not modify the comparisons 
of fluxes among tested sediments and oxygen treat-
ments, the flux values presented in the text and dis-
played on two figures are erroneous.
