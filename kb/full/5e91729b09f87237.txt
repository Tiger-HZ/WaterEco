The accurate disaggregation of water consumption events remains a challenge, particularly in distinguishing devices with similar flow rates and identifying devices operating simultaneously. This study proposes and experimentally evaluates a physics-based method for identifying overlapping consumption events using synchronized flow rate and pressure measurements. For this purpose, a controlled laboratory setup was developed, in which six devices were characterized under different pressure conditions, allowing their flow rate–pressure relationships to be established. The analysis of isolated events revealed significant overlap among the devices when flow rate and event duration were considered, while the inclusion of pressure provided greater distinction between their operating regions.
