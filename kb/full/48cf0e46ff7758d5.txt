Abstract Urbanisation alters resource availability and exposes wildlife to novel environmental stressors, potentially driving phenotypic divergence. Carotenoid‐based plumage colouration is a condition‐dependent trait linked to dietary carotenoid acquisition and physiological allocation trade‐offs. Although urban populations are often reported to exhibit paler carotenoid colouration than rural populations, these patterns have rarely been tested along continuous fine‐scale urbanisation gradients at a continental scale. We investigated how reflectance parameters of the yellow breast plumage of adult Great Tit ( Parus major ) covary with territory‐scale local imperviousness, used here as a broad descriptor of urban habitat structure, across six European cities.
