Abstract The coupled interactions between particulate microplastics and dissolved plastic-derived additives were investigated using magnetite (Fe₃O₄). The investigated materials included tire wear particles (TWP), poly(vinyl chloride) (PVC) pipe particles, and reference polymers. Magnetite rapidly induced heteroaggregation and enabled efficient magnetic separation of the investigated MPs. Simultaneously, dissolved plastic-associated organic compounds were scavenged from model solutions, with removal depending on their physicochemical properties. SEM, FTIR, and magnetic analyses confirmed attachment of Fe₃O₄ particles to microplastic surfaces, the formation of Fe₃O₄–MP aggregates, and the absence of detectable chemical modification of the investigated polymers.
