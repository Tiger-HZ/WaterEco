Abstract The growing demand for sustainable crop management has intensified interest in protein hydrolysates (PHs) as bioactive biostimulants. While the efficacy of foliar PH applications has been documented in tomato at concentrations of 2.5 mL/L and above, or through combined foliar-drench programs, evidence for significant agronomic responses at low foliar rates- particularly in legumes- remains limited. The present study evaluated the effects of two novel plant- and microbial-derived PH formulations, AM2025 and MP2025, applied twice as foliar sprays at 0.05% and 0.1% (v/v) (equivalent to 0.5 and 1.0 mL/L) on vegetative growth, photosynthesis and water-use efficiency in Phaseolus vulgaris L. and Solanum lycopersicum L. under controlled greenhouse conditions.
