Abstract BACKGROUND Maintaining the internal quality and functional properties of shell eggs during storage remains a critical challenge for the egg industry. Non‐thermal technologies such as ultrasound (US) and electrolyzed water (EW) have attracted attention due to their potential to enhance microbial safety and quality preservation. This study investigated the individual and combined effects of US and EW treatments on the internal quality, functional characteristics, and storage stability of shell eggs over a 5‐week period. RESULTS The results demonstrated that all treatments significantly improved quality retention compared to the control ( P &lt; 0.05).
