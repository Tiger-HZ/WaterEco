Abattoirs play a vital role in meat supply chains but often generate poorly managed organic waste, which poses risks to water quality and public health. This study examines waste production, management practices, and their impacts on surface and groundwater at Zango Abattoir, Tudun Wada, Kaduna South LGA, Nigeria. Field observations, key informant interviews, and laboratory analyses of water samples from the borehole, well, effluent discharge point, and downstream River Kaduna were conducted in July 2025. Twenty-six physicochemical and microbiological parameters were analysed following the World Health Organisation [WHO] (2022) and NESREA (2009; 2011) standards.
