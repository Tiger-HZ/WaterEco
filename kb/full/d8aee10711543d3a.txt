Climate change threatens water availability in semi-arid Andean watersheds, where cities such as Huánuco (Peru) rely primarily on surface water for urban supply. This study evaluates future changes in water availability in the Higueras River Basin by integrating projections from 22 CMIP5 Global Climate Models (GCMs), Bias Correction, the GR2M hydrological model, population growth projections, and the Water Stress Index (WSI).
