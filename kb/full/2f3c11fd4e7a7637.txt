Abstract This topic has been discussed broadly in the industry, however only a few examples are available or published. This paper discusses the practices carried out during the drilling operations of two deep water wells in the Gulf of America (GOA) in the Salt Exit interval. Hole stability problems can be difficult to deal with while drilling utilizing conventional methods often resulting in sidetrack operations. The solution often comes from a combination of techniques, technologies and or practices planned prior to the project execution. Managed Pressure Drilling (MPD) enables rapid adjustment of bottomhole pressure, making it an effective tool for mitigating hole stability challenges.
