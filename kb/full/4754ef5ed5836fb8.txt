Fodder maize (Zea mays L.) is a high-biomass, nutrient-responsive forage whose economic value depends on both harvested mass and nutritive quality. Fertiliser recommendations based on regional averages can therefore be inefficient when fields differ markedly in native nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium supply, organic matter, pH, texture, previous management and residual fertility. This critical narrative review evaluates soil test crop response (STCR) and related targeted-yield strategies as a basis for improving fodder maize nutrient management across soil fertility gradients. Literature published principally from January 2010 to 26 June 2026 was considered, with older foundational methodological sources retained where necessary.
