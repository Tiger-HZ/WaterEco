Waterborne parasites represent an important public health problem due to their ability to survive in aquatic environments and contaminate drinking water sources. The aim of this study was to determine the occurrence and distribution of waterborne pa rasites in drinking and spring water sources in Kırıkkale Province, Türkiye. A total of 120 water samples were collected month ly from 10 different sampling stations between April 2024 and March 2025. Five-liter water samples were concentrated using sedimentation followed by centrifugation. Microscopic examinations were performed using native Lugol, Kinyoun acid-fast staining, and trichrome staining techniques to detect parasitic forms.
