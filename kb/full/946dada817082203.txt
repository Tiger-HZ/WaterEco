Poor aqueous solubility remains the single most common cause of variable and incomplete oral absorption among new chemical entities, with a substantial proportion of marketed and pipeline molecules falling under Biopharmaceutics Classification System (BCS) classes II and IV. Self-nanoemulsifying drug delivery systems (SNEDDS) isotropic, anhydrous blends of oil, surfactant and co-surfactant that spontaneously generate droplets below 200 nm on mild agitation in gastrointestinal fluid have emerged as a robust formulation platform to overcome this limitation.
