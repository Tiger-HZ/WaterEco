To evaluate an appropriate supplementation level, cassava starch-processing residue (CR) was added to liquid diets for growing-finishing pigs at 0, 3, or 6% of the basal-feed weight. A total of 480 pigs (initial body weight, 34.6 ± 0.46 kg) were fed the experimental diets for 12 weeks. Feed intake increased linearly during weeks 1–3, 4–6, and 7–9 (p &amp;lt; 0.05) and tended to increase over the entire feeding period (p = 0.057). Average daily gain decreased linearly only during weeks 7–9 (p = 0.013), whereas overall average daily gain and final body weight were unaffected. In contrast, the gain-to-feed ratio decreased linearly during weeks 1–3, 4–6, 7–9, and overall (p &amp;lt; 0.05).
