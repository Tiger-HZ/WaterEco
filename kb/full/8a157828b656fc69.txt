Introduction: Machine learning (ML) techniques have been increasingly applied to model Heavy metals (HMs) adsorption from aqueous environments; however, variations in datasets, evaluation strategies, and reporting practices have limited the development of generalizable conclusions regarding model performance. This systematic review comparatively evaluated ML applications in heavy metal adsorption modeling and identified factors influencing predictive performance across heterogeneous adsorption systems. Methods: Peer-reviewed studies published between January 2010 and December 2024 were identified through searches of Scopus, Google Scholar, ScienceDirect, and PubMed.
