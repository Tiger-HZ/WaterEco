Introduction Mixing sand into the surface soil, combined with nutrient management, may improve the hydraulic properties of saline–alkali soil and promote crop growth. However, the effects of sand mixing and nutrient management on soil water and nutrient transport and foxtail millet growth remain unclear. This study investigated the effects of sand-mixing ratio and nutrient supply on soil infiltration, water and nutrient distribution, and the agronomic performance of foxtail millet in moderately saline–alkali soil in northern Shanxi Province.
