This study aimed to determine fish diversity and distribution as well as water quality conditions in Burung Lake, Petuk Katimpun Village, Palangka Raya City. The study was conducted from September to November 2025 using purposive sampling at five stations representing different conditions of the lake. Fish samples were collected using fishing gears commonly used by local fishermen, while water quality parameters measured included temperature, transparency, depth, Total Suspended Solids (TSS), Electrical Conductivity (EC), dissolved oxygen (DO), pH, phosphate, nitrate, and ammonia. Fish diversity was analyzed using the Shannon-Wiener diversity index, relative abundance, occurrence frequency, and Simpson dominance index.
