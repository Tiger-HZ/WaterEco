ABSTRACT The purpose of this review is to clarify how emerging hybrid, physics-informed, and network-aware machine learning (ML) approaches are reshaping sediment-transport modeling. The central research question is: How can ML methods, when aligned with data regimes, physical constraints, and spatial structure, improve sediment-transport prediction across diverse river environments? To address this, the review reorganizes existing studies into six environmentally grounded clusters and conducts a cross-study comparison to identify model performance patterns, limitations, and future directions.
