Abstract The production of green hydrogen via proton exchange membrane water electrolysis (PEMWE) is significantly constrained by mass transport limitations at the anode, where oxygen bubble evolution obstructs catalyst sites and compromises performance at high current densities. Conventional models fail to capture the nanoscale interplay between bubble dynamics and local electrochemical reactions, thereby obscuring critical design principles for nanostructural optimization. To address this, we developed a nanoscale multiphysics framework using the Lattice Boltzmann Method to directly couple bubble dynamics with electrochemistry.
