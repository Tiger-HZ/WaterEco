Poor quality sleep has been a perennial problem especially for internally displaced persons who are traumatized by critical incidents in their communities. Many factors are intricate in determining the quality of sleep experienced by displaced persons. Therefore, this study investigated emotion regulation and sleep quality among internally displaced persons in Benue North-west. The study adopted cross-sectional survey design where the participants were 384 internally displaced persons comprising of 155 (40.4%) males and 229 (59.6%) females. Their ages ranged from 20-66years with a mean age of 48.11years (SD=11.050). The study adopted Multi-Stage Sampling technique to draw the participants for the study.
