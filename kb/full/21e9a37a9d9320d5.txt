The process of tufa deposition created the Plitvice Lakes, a unique freshwater cascade system of 16 lakes separated by tufa barriers. This complex karst hydrosystem reacts very sensitively to even small changes, and eutrophication can directly and indirectly affect tufa formation. With the purpose of determining the influence of nitrogen (N) and phosphorus (P) on periphyton’s chlorophyll a concentration, tufa deposition, and macrozoobenthos, we used nutrient-diffusing substrates. The in situ experiment combined the effects of seasons, stronger/weaker tufa deposition, and the presence/absence of macrophytes.
