ABSTRACT Accurate forecasting of operational expenditure (OPEX) in wastewater treatment plants is critical for financial planning and utility management, yet it remains challenging due to complex, nonlinear interactions between plant capacity, influent characteristics, and fluctuating market prices. This research develops an interpretable predictive framework for OPEX estimation by systematically evaluating multiple advanced machine learning algorithms on a dataset of 2847 operational records (1139 empirical and 1708 synthetic).
