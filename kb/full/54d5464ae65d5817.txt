Borewell water serves as a primary groundwater source widely utilized by communities to fulfill daily domestic requirements. However, the physical quality of this groundwater is increasingly vulnerable to degradation due to domestic waste infiltration, rapid urbanization, and local environmental geological conditions. This study aims to evaluate and describe the quality characteristics of borewell water based on Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) and turbidity parameters in South Sempaja Village. This quantitative research employed an observational study design with a cross-sectional approach.
