Abstract Liquid nitrogen assisted abrasive suspension jet (LN₂-ASJ) is a hybrid machining method that performs pre-cooling prior to cutting, which has high cutting efficiency for hard and brittle metals. Hard and brittle metals often have both ductile and brittle fracture areas on the cut surface, and the surface quality is poor during LN₂-ASJ one-time forming processing. This paper took YG20 tungsten steel as the research object, conducted cutting experiments, analyzed the surface morphology characteristics and surface roughness distribution characteristics of the cut surface, and proposed the "inverted reverse secondary cutting" scheme to optimize the surface quality.
