This work addresses the challenges of low ion-transport efficiency and poor focusing performance encountered in miniature mass spectrometers operating at the microscale. A combined theoretical and simulation study is presented on the ion-optical design of the instrument’s ion source. Using a three-dimensional model, we systematically investigate the effects of electron-source geometry and ion-transport system parameters on electron emission and ion-transport characteristics. Simulation results demonstrate that the optimized electron-source geometry improves electron transmission efficiency to over 45 %.
