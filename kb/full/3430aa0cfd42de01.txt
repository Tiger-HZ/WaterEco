Pawpaw is a commonly consumed agro-based product in Nigeria but due to its highly perishable nature, there is little or no organized cultivation of this fruit in Nigeria attributed to reduced expertise on value addition. This study, therefore focused on preliminary conversion of Bayelsagrown pawpaw into a shelf-stable, higher-value product (jam) aimed at reducing postharvest losses, increase farmer incomes, strengthen local value chains and bridge the gap on value addition technical expertise. Pawpaw at three different stages of maturity were used in this formulation and based on consumer appreciation, one of the three was further subjected to quality evaluation following standard procedure.
