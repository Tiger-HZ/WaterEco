This study assessed the physico-chemical parameters of water in Kafin Gana Dam, Jigawa State, Nigeria, to evaluate its quality and suitability for aquatic life and domestic use. Four sampling sites were selected based on anthropogenic activities, and samples were collected over a period of six months following standard procedures. Parameters measured included temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen (DO), total dissolved solids (TDS), electrical conductivity (EC), turbidity, biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), and others. Results showed that temperature ranged from 29.0ºC at site D to 30.00ºC at site B. pH ranged from 6.71mg/L at site B to 7.1mg/L at site D.
