Rare earth elements (REEs) are traditionally regarded as emerging environmental contaminants. Due to their increasing anthropogenic release and interactions with essential mineral nutrients, they are attracting growing interest for their potential applications in sustainable agriculture. The REEs are a unique group of 17 chemically similar elements, including scandium (Sc), yttrium (Y), and lanthanides. Most existing reviews treat REEs as a single group, with limited emphasis on concentration-dependent uptake mechanisms or species-related responses. However, based on atomic weight, REEs are classified into light and heavy rare earth elements (LREEs/HREEs).
