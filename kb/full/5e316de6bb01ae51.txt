Water quality monitoring has become a critical research area due to increasing human-induced pressures, climate variability, and the need for sustainable water resource management. Over the past decade, rapid technological advancements and methodological diversification have significantly reshaped this field. However, existing syntheses have either examined broader river-pollution research or focused on specific technological and pollutant-related domains, leaving the recent interdisciplinary structure of water quality monitoring and assessment insufficiently integrated.
