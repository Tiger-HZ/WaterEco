As part of the UN Sustainable Development Goals 2020 emphasising the need for clean water and sanitation, it is imperative that all wastewater from a refinery plant should be adequately treated to safeguard our environment from toxic pollutants. Globally, the volume of petroleum refinery wastewater (PRWW) usually generated is approximately 1.6 times the crude oil volume processed in a refinery. This paper critically reviews the existing treatment methodologies and their fundamental challenges. In addition, it highlights the future prospects of zero liquid discharge (ZLD) reverse osmosis systems powered by renewable energy.
