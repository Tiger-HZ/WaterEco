The Yogyakarta and Central Java regions of Indonesia have diverse geological conditions. This geological diversity has encouraged researchers to conduct hydrogeological research, particularly on the parameters of temperature (T) and electrical conductivity (EC) of groundwater. This research was carried out using hydrogeological mapping methods and T/EC measurement experiments both in the studio and in the laboratory. This study aims to formulate the relationship between T and EC of groundwater under various geological conditions. Geological variations, particularly lithology and geothermal processes, can influence the thermal dynamics and electrical properties of groundwater.
