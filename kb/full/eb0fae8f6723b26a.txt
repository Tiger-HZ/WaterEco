Background: Hormonal fluctuations during the perimenopausal phase due to decreased ovarian function are influenced by various factors, including nutrient intake and sleep quality, which are thought to play a role in hormonal regulation. Objective: This study aimed to evaluate the relationships between macronutrient and micronutrient intake, sleep quality, and serum FSH levels in Indonesian perimenopausal women. Methods: This study used an analytical observational design with a cross-sectional approach involving 120 perimenopausal women in Gondanglegi, Malang, from September to November 2025. The sample was selected by cluster random sampling.
