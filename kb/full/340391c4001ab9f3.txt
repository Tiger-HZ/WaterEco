Liquid organic fertilizers (LOFs) produced from animal manures are inexpensive locallyavailable nutrient sources for smallholder systems, but their composition and short-term plant effects vary with feedstock and processing. This study compared nutrient composition and early growth responses of bean (Phaseolus spp.) seedlings to LOFs prepared from rabbit and chicken droppings. LOFs were produced by 1:5 (w/v) aqueous extraction, settled and decanted. Chemical analysis quantified K, P, Mg and total N (mg·L⁻¹). Seedlings were grown in pots (soil: sand: compost, 2:1:1), and treatments (rabbit LOF, chicken LOF, and water control) were applied as 50 mL pot⁻¹ drench at sowing and weekly.
