The full-scale war in Ukraine has had a considerable impact on environmental security, not only in Ukraine but in the whole world in particular. The matter concerns damage to the ecosystems of the Azov and Black Seas, damage to soils and, therefore, a decrease in soil fertility, and pollution of rivers, groundwater, and air. There were many cases of the destruction of natural parks, reserves, and unique, so-called red-book animals. At the same time, there are many unique natural objects in the territory of Ukraine. Full-scale wars as well as armed conflicts have an impact not only on immigration, economic, and security policies, but on environmental policy in particular. This situation demands appropriate measures, one of which could be the development of an international legal framework for environmental crimes and compensation for environmental damage.
