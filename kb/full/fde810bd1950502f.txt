Background: Fatigability is prevalent among older adults and affects quality of life. It has become a public health concern in the current era of rapid population aging. China has the world&amp;rsquo;s largest older population. However, no study has addressed this issue in China. Objective: This study examined the associations of physical and mental fatigability with physical and mental measures of health-related quality of life (HRQoL), respectively, among community-dwelling older adults in regional China. Methods: We collected cross-sectional data from residents aged 60 years or older in Nanjing municipality, China, in 2023.
