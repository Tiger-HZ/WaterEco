Background: Breast engorgement is a frequent postpartum condition that can greatly impair breastfeeding success and maternal comfort. Timely and evidence-based therapies are critical to effectively relieving these symptoms and promoting optimum breastfeeding. Objectives: To assess the efficacy of reverse pressure softening (RPS) and lukewarm water compression (LWC) in reducing breast engorgement and promoting breastfeeding in postnatal mothers. Methods: A randomized experiment including 60 postnatal mothers was carried out at a tertiary care facility in Tamil Nadu, India. Participants were randomly assigned to one of two groups: LWC ( n = 30) or RPS ( n = 30).
