Coastal lagoons and semi-enclosed marine ecosystems are increasingly threatened by eutrophication resulting from anthropogenic nutrient enrichment and climate-driven environmental change. Gorgan Bay, a shallow semi-enclosed embayment in the southeastern Caspian Sea, has experienced considerable ecological degradation during recent decades; however, the relationships between phytoplankton community structure, nutrient dynamics, and trophic status remain poorly understood. This study investigated spatial and seasonal variations in phytoplankton assemblages and trophic conditions along a gradient extending from Gorgan Bay to adjacent coastal waters of the southeastern Caspian Sea.
