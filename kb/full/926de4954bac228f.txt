To address the simultaneous linear trend and complex nonlinear fluctuations in groundwater level time series, a combined groundwater level prediction model is proposed, integrating the Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA) model, a Bidirectional Gated Recurrent Unit (BiGRU) network, and an attention mechanism. First, the ARIMA model is used to model the linear trend in the groundwater level series, yielding initial predictions. Then, a residual sequence is constructed between the actual values and the predicted values from the ARIMA model, and the Bidirectional Gated Recurrent Unit learns the bidirectional temporal dependencies in the residual sequence.
