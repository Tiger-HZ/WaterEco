Tanzania has made a noticeable progress in improving the quality and relevance of education through the implementation of Tanzania’s 2014 (2023 edition) Educational and Training Policy. The 2023 Educational Policy introduced significant reforms aimed at enhancing access and equity to education, competence-based learning, inclusive education, digital integration, and alignment of education with labor market demands. Despite these progressive intentions, the extent to which policy implementation predicts improvements in quality education outcomes remains underexplored.
