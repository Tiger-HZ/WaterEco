In the German Medical Informatics Initiative (MII), Medical Data Integration Centers (MeDICs) make routine care data from German university hospitals available to researchers. The MII common data model is the Core Data Set (CDS) built on HL7 FHIR. Extensive effort is put into providing FHIR profiles with corresponding Implementation Guides (IGs). While the technical specification of IGs is centrally governed, implementation is distributed among all MeDICs. Each MeDICs is to build compatible processes within the diverse landscape of Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems in Germany. Therefore, the characteristics of FHIR resources between MeDICs might differ.
