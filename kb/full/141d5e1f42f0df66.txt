This study aimed to investigate the mediating effect of health information seeking behavior on the relationship between health literacy and quality of life among the Neo-silver generation. For this purpose, an online survey was conducted from March 26 to April 6, 2026, targeting 310 adults aged 50-64 in Korea who actively practice self-management. The results found the following: First, health literacy had a statistically significant positive effect on health information seeking behavior. Second, health literacy had a positive influence on quality of life. Third, both health information production and consumption activities partially mediated the relationship between health literacy and quality of life. It is anticipated that the study results would contribute to providing theoretical and practical foundations for supporting the autonomous health management of the Neo-silver generation in a super-aging society.
