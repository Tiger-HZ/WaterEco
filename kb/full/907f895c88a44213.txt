This study examined how well aquatic plants removed pollutants from the Kaduna Brewery industrial effluent. Brewery wastewater had poor quality and was unsuitable for agricultural irrigation. Through efficient nutrient removal, constructed wetlands have become a viable, affordable, and sustainable way to improve water quality. At the Nigeria Defence Academy (NDA) and Kaduna Polytechnic (KADPOLY), in Kaduna State in northern Nigeria, the research was carried out using the American Public Health Association standard for the evaluation of water and wastewater for laboratory analysis. Comparing water hyacinth and water lettuce as wetland plants for wastewater treatment was the primary emphasis of the study.
