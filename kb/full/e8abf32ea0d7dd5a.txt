Water governance is a strategic component for water sustainability in rural communities with limited availability of the resource. The objective of the study was to determine the relationship between water governance and the sustainable use of water resources. Therefore, it is a quantitative, non-experimental, cross-sectional and correlational research, with 60 participants selected through non-probabilistic convenience sampling. Structured questionnaires were applied and Spearman's Rho coefficient was used for inferential analysis. A high level of water governance predominated (51.7%), while sustainable use was mainly concentrated at the medium level (56.7%).
