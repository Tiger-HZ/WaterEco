Wetland degradation induced by climate change and human disturbance poses severe threats to migratory waterbirds, particularly the critically endangered Siberian crane (Leucogeranus leucogeranus). The Erlongtao River Basin, a key stopover wetland complex on the Songnen Plain, exhibits prominent mismatches between general waterbird habitat quality and species-specific habitat suitability, together with widespread conservation gaps. In this study, line-transect and point-sampling field surveys were conducted during spring and autumn migration (2020–2024).
