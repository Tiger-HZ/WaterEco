Aims: To determine whether the spatial distribution of soil textural classes across the Yadgir and Gurmitkal blocks can predict groundwater recharge conditions observed at borewell sites, using an infiltration-capacity-based suitability ranking. Study Design: Spatial soil-texture mapping combined with a five-tier recharge suitability ranking, cross-validated against field-observed borewell hydrogeological parameters. Place and Duration of Study: Yadgir and Gurmitkal blocks, Yadgir District, Karnataka, India, covering 1,711.6 sq. km and 45 target villages; geohydrological data collected under the Kalike programme, with rainfall records spanning 1995–2024.
