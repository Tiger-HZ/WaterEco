Abstract A multidisciplinary forensic study was conducted to evaluate the geological and diagenetic factors controlling reservoir heterogeneity and completion impacts within the Matulla Formation, a Cretaceous mixed siliciclastic–carbonate section. This study provides a comprehensive insight into the textural and diagenetic evolution of this succession, offering a robust methodological framework for enhanced reservoir characterisation. Despite comparable porosity responses across the studied intervals, production performance varies significantly. To resolve these inconsistencies, an Enhanced Reservoir Quality workflow was employed, integrating wireline and LWD cluster analysis, time series log analysis, chemostratigraphy, and applied mineralogy.
