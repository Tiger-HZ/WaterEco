This study aims to determine and analyze the effect of Service Quality and Price on Purchasing Decisions at Cafe Lapinea Lembang, West Bandung Regency, both partially and simultaneously. The research is motivated by issues of inconsistent service, inefficient operational systems, facility quality, price fairness, and intensifying competition among cafes in the Lembang area. This study employs a quantitative, explanatory research approach. The sample consisted of 100 respondents determined using the Slovin formula with accidental sampling, collected through a Likert-scale questionnaire, observation, and interviews.
