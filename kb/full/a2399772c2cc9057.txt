Long‐term trends in annual evaporation and watershed Bowen ratio were studied on 51 eastern United States watersheds which had experienced an estimated 0–58% urban/residential development between 1920 and 1990. Annual watershed evaporation was estimated as the difference between annual streamflow and average watershed precipitation, corrected for watershed elevation effects and gage catch error. Annual watershed Bowen ratios were estimated using spatially and temporally averaged net radiation values and annual evaporation.
