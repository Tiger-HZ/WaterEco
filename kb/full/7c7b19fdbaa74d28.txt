In response to the paradigm shift in urban wastewater treatment that has been driven by emerging regulatory requirements for the removal of chemical contaminants of emerging concern (CECs), it has become essential to evaluate the environmental and economic implications of wastewater treatment upgrades using Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and comparative cost analysis. The present study evaluates, using full-scale operational data, strategies that can be used in activated sludge systems and downstream ozonation that target CECs and conventional pollutants.
