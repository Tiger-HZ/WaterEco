Studies on the nutrient composition and fungal flora of Pleurotus ostreatus and Pleurotus tuberregium grown in Port Harcourt were carried out in the Department of Plant Science and Biotechnology Rivers State University. Fresh and Dry P. ostreatus and P. tuber-regium were grown and harvested from Dilomat farms and Services Rivers State university Port Harcourt. The samples were collected and analyzed in Food Science &amp; Biotechnology Laboratory. Mycological studies were carried out in the Department of Plant Science and Biotechnology Laboratory using standard methods. The result for nutrient composition revealed moisture, ash, fiber, lipid, carbohydrate and protein.
