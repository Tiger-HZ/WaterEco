Southern Appalachian streams host a rich diversity of fishes, but the Southern Appalachian Assessment concluded that 70% of stream locations showed significant fish community degradation, partly due to acid deposition. About 40% of total Southern Appalachian trout stream length occurs in Virginia. Our research in Shenandoah National Park, Virginia, has documented both chronic and episodic acidification in streams and brook trout (Salvelinus fontinalis) mortality during acid episodes. Here, we analyze a representative sample of 60 Virginia brook trout streams on noncarbonate bedrock with minimal human disturbance in their watersheds.
