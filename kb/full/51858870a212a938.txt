ABSTRACT Developing single‐atom nanozymes with stereoconfigurations is a new paradigm for biomimetic construction of next‐generation natural enzyme alternatives and for achieving high enzyme‐like selectivity and activity. This work developed a high‐temperature pyrolysis tandem chemical vapor deposition technique to produce Cu SAzymes (Cu‐B/N‐C) with axial B coordination as laccase mimics, with Cu‐B axial N 3 ‐C as the core structure. Axial ligands modulate the d‐band center of Cu SAzymes to identify the equilibrium point between the energy barriers of reactant adsorption and product desorption.
