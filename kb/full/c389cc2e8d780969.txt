Abstract Tropical cyclone (TC) activity is expected to change under future climate mitigation pathways, yet its response to carbon dioxide removal (CDR) remains poorly understood. Here, we investigate the hysteretic response of genesis potential index (GPI) over the western North Pacific (WNP) using an Earth system model under four idealized CDR scenarios with different peak CO2 concentrations. Although the WNP-mean GPI exhibits no significant hysteresis between the ramp-down and ramp-up periods across all scenarios, the underlying dynamical mechanisms fundamentally differ depending on the forcing magnitude.
