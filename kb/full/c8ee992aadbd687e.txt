Abstract This study examined the effects of surface-modified silica nanoparticles on the cellular structure and sound absorption of polyurethane foams. Unmodified silica nanoparticles may show poor dispersion and weak compatibility with the polymer matrix. Therefore, the silica nanoparticles were modified with GPTMS and APTES silane coupling agents. A total of 26 foam samples were prepared. These samples included pure polyurethane foam, foams with unmodified silica nanoparticles, and foams with surface-modified silica nanoparticles. FT-IR analysis confirmed successful surface modification. The observed acoustic trends were consistent with changes in nanoparticlematrix interaction and foam structure after surface modification.
