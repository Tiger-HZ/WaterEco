The integration of digital health technologies is increasingly recognized as a critical factor in improving healthcare delivery, particularly in low- and middle-income countries. In Uganda, hospitals are progressively adopting technologies such as electronic medical records, telemedicine, and digital diagnostic systems; however, variability in technological adaptability remains a key challenge affecting service quality. This systematic review examines the role of technological adaptability as a driver of health service quality, with contextual insights relevant to hospitals in Uganda, particularly private Hospitals.
