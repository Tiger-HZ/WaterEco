Arsenic (As) is a ubiquitous and highly toxic metalloid with well-established carcinogenicity. Its accumulation and secondary release from lake sediments pose potential risks to lake ecosystem integrity and human health. Meanwhile, the ongoing intensification of lake eutrophication at the global scale has altered the sources, composition, and environmental behavior of internally derived dissolved organic matter (DOM). These changes have profoundly influenced As mobilization and transformation at the sediment-water interface (SWI).
