Abstract Aquatic macroinvertebrates were sampled annually at 66 sites of the National Rivers Water Quality Network (NRWQN) from 1989 to 1996. At each site, temporal trends in number of taxa, total abundance, Macroinvertebrate Community Index (MCI), the quantitative version of MCI (QMCI), number and percent of Ephemeroptera, Plecoptera, and Trichoptera, Shannon's Diversity Index (H'), Jaccard's similarity index, and Bray‐Curtis similarity index were analysed using non‐parametric Spearman rank correlations. Sixty‐nine significant ( P &lt; 0.10) trends were found out of a total of 594 analyses. There were 27 positive trends (i.e., showing increases in invertebrate community measures through time) and 42 negative.
