ABSTRACT Solar‐driven photoelectrochemical (PEC) water splitting represents a promising pathway toward carbon neutrality, yet the practical conversion efficiency remains severely constrained by the substantial charge recombination losses. Herein, CuSCN as hole transport layer (HTL) coupled with a grain‐boundary‐enriched NiFe cocatalyst (NiFe‐GB) was integrated with BiVO 4 photoanodes to improve hole extraction/transport and interfacial oxygen evolution reaction (OER) kinetics. The optimized NiFe‐GB/CuSCN/BVO photoanode delivered a photocurrent density of 6.54 mA cm −2 at 1.23 V versus RHE.
