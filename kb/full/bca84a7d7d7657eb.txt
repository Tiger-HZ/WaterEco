Abstract Biological nitrogen (N) fixation is crucial for sustaining the N fertility in rice paddy soils. The N-fixing activity of paddy soils is higher than that of upland soils and increases during the flooded period as soil reduction progresses. Previously, we identified that N fixation in paddy soils is actively driven by iron (Fe) -reducing bacteria belonging to Deltaproteobacteria , which use Fe oxides as electron acceptors for respiration. Applying oxidized Fe powder to paddy soil enhanced the energy metabolism of these microorganisms, thereby increasing their N fixation activity. However, the Fe-oxide minerals responsible for this enhancement remain unknown.
