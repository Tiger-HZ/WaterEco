California’s San Joaquin Valley is home to some of the nation’s most productive farmland. Yet, this agricultural productivity has been achieved through unsustainable groundwater use to support irrigation. The Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (SGMA) seeks to address the negative consequences of groundwater overdraft and requires cutbacks in pumping, most notably in 21 critically overdrafted basins, 11 of which are in the San Joaquin Valley. On the pathway towards groundwater sustainability, concern about disproportionate impacts of pumping cutbacks on smaller and less-resourced farms has prompted a closer look at water resources and vulnerability across farm size.
