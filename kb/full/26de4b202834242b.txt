Surface-water extent provides a spatial measure of reservoir condition and supports water-resource monitoring where field observations are limited. This study assessed the spatio-temporal dynamics of surface water in Kopa Dam, Niger State, Nigeria, from January 2022 to January 2026 using Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery and the Modified Normalized Difference Water Index (MNDWI). One image was selected for January of each year to maintain seasonal consistency. MNDWI was calculated using the green band (B03) and shortwave infrared band (B11), with B11 resampled to 10 m. Water and non-water reference points were used to determine image-specific thresholds of −0.01, −0.01, −0.18, −0.01, and −0.25 for 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026, respectively.
