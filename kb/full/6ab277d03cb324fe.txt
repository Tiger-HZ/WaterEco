ABSTRACT Recovering phosphorus (P) from sewage sludge is increasingly important, but treatment configuration and influent characteristics shape the quality of the recovered product, including its P solubility and metal loading. We examined how treatment processes and sewer type govern the composition and temporal variability of sludge-derived fertilizers at three full-scale facilities differing in biological treatment, anaerobic digestion, and coagulant dosing. Fertilizers were sampled approximately weekly for seven months and analyzed for total, inorganic, citrate-soluble, and water-soluble P and 21 metals.
