Human activities are altering nutrient inputs into coastal waters, impacting the C:P and N:P ratios of dissolved matter. These changes may modify present nutrient limitations, especially P limitation, in microbial communities. The response of microbial communities from the Ría de Vigo (NW Iberian Peninsula) to both the type (inorganic versus inorganic and organic mixture) and the dissolved inorganic N (DIN) to dissolved inorganic P (DIP) ratio (above and below Redfield) of nutrient inputs was assessed in 8 microcosm experiments. Concomitantly, the response of phytoplankton to nutrient treatments in the presence or absence of bacterial activity was explored.
