This study focuses on the analysis of Lamé’s parameters in clastic rocks of different reservoir quality in the Akau field of the Central Swamp, Niger Delta. Two channel-reservoir models filled with sands of different geological ages and located at different depths were considered to investigate reservoir heterogeneities. The channel sands are of different reservoir quality, differing in their shale content and porosity. Three Gassmann fluid-substitution cases (brine, oil, and gas) were analysed. In each case, compressional- and shear-wave impedances and Lamé parameters were calculated. Interpretation of well-log data was based on crossplots of elastic parameters, allowing differentiation of reservoir sands from shale intervals and other lithofacies.
