This study evaluated the effects of different pretreatment methods on the phytochemical composition and amino acid profile of “Nyama Asabo” (Diplazium esculentum) to determine the most effective processing method for preserving its nutritional quality in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. Fresh leaves of D. esculentum (300 g each) were subjected to oven drying at 60°C, steam blanching at 70°C for 2 minutes, hot water blanching at 70°C for 2 minutes and hot water blanching with 2.5% sodium bicarbonate at 70°C for 2 minutes, while fresh leaves served as the control. The pretreated and fresh samples were analyzed for phytochemicals, including flavonoids, oxalates, saponins, alkaloids, trypsin inhibitors, phytates, tannins and cyanide, as well as the amino acid profile of the fresh leaves.
