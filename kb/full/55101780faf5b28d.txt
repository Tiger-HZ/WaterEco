Introduction Medical students are exposed to chronic stress, unhealthy lifestyle habits, and emerging cardiometabolic risks, which are usually related to their nutritional status and quality of life (QoL). This study aimed to assess the relationship between overweight or obesity, elevated blood pressure, and QoL among medical students in Poland, and to identify its determinants over a 2-year longitudinal period. Materials and methods A total of 887 first-year medical students from the Medical University of Silesia in Katowice were enrolled in the prospective POLLEK study, with 705 students followed up in their second year.
