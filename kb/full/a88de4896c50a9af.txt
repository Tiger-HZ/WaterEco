The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. After publi-
cation, a number of image integrity concerns were found:
• In Fig. 3, the image of Fragilaria sp. is cropped from a 
website called “Culture Collection of algae and proto-
zoa” [1] rather than an original image by the authors 
as described.
• Fig.  9 Synechocystis (sp.pcc6803) internal seawater 
treatment and Fig.  10 Fragilaria (sp. CCAP1029) 
appear to overlap
• Within Fig. 11, location 1 Synechocystis (sp.pcc6803) 
appears to overlap with both location 1 Fragi -
laria (sp. CCAP1029) and location 2 Fragilaria (sp. 
CCAP1029)
• Four panels overlap within Figs.  11 and 12, control 
panels for locations 1 and 2
• The control EDX spectra appear to overlap in Figs. 13 
and 14
Due to the extensiveness of the concerns raised, the 
Editor has lost confidence in the data and conclusions of 
this article.
Khaled N. M. Elsayed has stated on behalf of all authors 
that all authors disagree with this retraction.
