Abstract Background Groundwater represents a vital resource for drinking water supply in arid and semi-arid regions, particularly in northeastern Algeria where surface water resources are limited. The assessment of groundwater quality is essential for sustainable management and public health protection. Objective This study aims to evaluate the physicochemical quality of groundwater from five boreholes (J37, OL1, O45, H47, I40) in F'kirina municipality and its surrounding areas (Aïn Beida), Oum El Bouaghi province, using the Groundwater Quality Index (GWQI) and multivariate statistical techniques including Principal Component Analysis (PCA).
