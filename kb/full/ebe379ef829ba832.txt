The hydrological problem to correctly partition rainfall at various scales may require scale‐dependent models. This study introduces a new method to identify scale‐ dependent state space models in a hydrological context. In addition, it investigates relations between model structure, resolution, and uncertainty for the case of overland flow from a hillslope. The main strengths of the method are the ability to evaluate a very large set of candidate models, general applicability, the possibility of interpreting identified models in a physical sense, and computational simplicity. Weaknesses are the possibility complex models that result, computation time required, and the relatively poor fit of models compared to methods with more strict assumptions on errors.
