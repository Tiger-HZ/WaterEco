Quantitative reconstructions of past bottom-water oxygen (BWO) are essential for evaluating changes in ocean ventilation, carbon storage, and marine biogeochemical cycling. Existing calibrations of the benthic foraminiferal carbon-isotope offset (Δδ 13 C) commonly map each observation to a single BWO estimate and attach uncertainty separately. Here we present BayDelC, a Bayesian proxy system model that represents the Δδ 13 C–BWO relationship probabilistically and propagates calibration-parameter uncertainty, measurement uncertainty, and residual variability through forward and inverse calculations, while accommodating BWO input uncertainty and optional priors.
