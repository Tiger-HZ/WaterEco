Introduction. The use of anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS) in strength sports has well-documented somatic effects; however, the withdrawal phase remains marginalized by sports medicine. Discontinuation of exogenous androgens triggers profound suppression of the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis. This collapse drastically reduces athletes’ quality of life, manifesting through severe depressive states and exacerbated body image disorders. Objective. This review synthesizes current scientific knowledge on the psychosomatic consequences of the AAS withdrawal syndrome (the “steroid crash”).
