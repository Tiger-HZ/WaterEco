Abstract Groundwater-dependent wetlands (GDWs) sustain biodiversity, regulate freshwater systems and store carbon, yet their vulnerability to changing groundwater and land use is poorly understood. Using a high-resolution global groundwater model, five global climate models (GCMs) and land use scenarios, we simulate global GDW extent from 1979 to 2050 under three Shared Socioeconomic Pathway-Representative Concentration Pathway (SSP-RCP) scenarios. We estimate a global extent of 3.43 million km 2 (3.39-3.47 across GCMs) for the 2005–2014 baseline. Ensemble-mean GDW extent is projected to decline by mid-century under all scenarios, including the most sustainable pathway, largest under SSP3-RCP7.0 (-173 [-337.1 to -11.7] thousand km 2 ).
