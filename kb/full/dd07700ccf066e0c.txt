Water is very essential and widely used by humans, animals and for the success of agricultural products. The inhabitants resort to use of borehole, sachet and bottle water for drinking and domestic use. There is paucity of information on bacterial contamination and phytochemical properties of drinking water sources in major slums of Lagos city. Such contaminants may be the cause of frequent febrile illnesses experienced amongst the inhabitants of these slums. The aim of this study is to determine the bacteriological quality and physicochemical properties of drinking water sources in slums of Lagos State, Nigeria.
