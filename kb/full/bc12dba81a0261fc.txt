Introduction Nutrient enrichment in coral reef systems is increasingly recognised as a major driver of ecological change, yet integrated assessments across groundwater, coastal, and reef environments remain limited in small island developing states (SIDS). Methods We assessed dissolved inorganic nutrients (total oxidised nitrogen, ammonium and phosphate), N:P ratios, and isotope-based tracers (δ 15 N, C:N) across groundwater, coastal-surface, and reef domains of Laamu Atoll, Maldives, between 2022 and 2024 to characterise spatial nutrient patterns and establish a baseline for environmental monitoring.
