Crop water content is a critical indicator of crop growth status, and its efficient and accurate monitoring is essential for agricultural water resource management. Conventional methods for monitoring winter wheat water content, however, rely mainly on destructive sampling and are labor-intensive and time-consuming. To address these limitations, this study explored the potential of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) remote sensing for the rapid and accurate assessment of winter wheat water content. High-resolution canopy remote sensing images were acquired using UAVs equipped with multispectral (MS), RGB, and thermal infrared (TIR) cameras during the flowering and filling stages under six irrigation treatments.
