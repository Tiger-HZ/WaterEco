Abstract To evaluate the capability of the newly developed SWOT satellite for lake water level retrieval and basin-scale hydrological monitoring, this study selected 513 lakes larger than 1 km 2 in the Yangtze River Basin. SWOT L2 HR Raster data from November 2023 to May 2025 were used to extract lake water levels, and their accuracy was assessed using DAHITI, ICESat-2, CryoSat-2, and in situ hydrological station observations. During the study period from November 2023 to May 2025, SWOT obtained at least one valid water level observation for 501 of the 513 lakes, corresponding to a lake coverage rate of 97.66%, and more than 80% of the covered lakes accumulated more than 10 valid observations.
