Objective Chronic venous insufficiency (CVI) and varicose veins have been associated with restless legs syndrome (RLS), impaired sleep quality, and reduced quality of life. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of endovenous radiofrequency ablation (RFA) on RLS severity, daytime sleepiness, and sleep quality in patients with symptomatic venous insufficiency. Methodology This pre–post intervention study included 160 consecutive patients with venous insufficiency who underwent endovenous RFA between January 2022 and January 2023.
