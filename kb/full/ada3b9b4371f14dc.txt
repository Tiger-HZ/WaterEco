A pot experiment was conducted using a split-plot design with four replications to determine the optimal Nitrogen (N) and Zinc (Zn) levels for maximizing nutrient uptake, recovery efficiency, agronomic use efficiency, milling quality, and grain Zn concentration in rice. The main plots comprised four N rates based on Department of Agriculture (DOA) guidelines (102 kg N ha⁻¹, 27 kg P₂O₅ ha⁻¹, and 117 kg K₂O ha⁻¹): 0% (N0), 75% (76.5 kg N ha⁻¹, N75), 100% (102 kg N ha⁻¹, N100), and 125% (127.5 kg N ha⁻¹, N125). The subplots included three Zn treatments: 0 kg Zn ha⁻¹ (Zn0), 20 kg Zn ha⁻¹ applied basally to the soil (Zn20), and 40 kg Zn ha⁻¹ (20 kg Zn ha⁻¹ basal + 20 kg Zn ha⁻¹ split into two foliar sprays at the heading and grain-filling stages, Zn40).
