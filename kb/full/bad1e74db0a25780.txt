Water Treatment Plant (WTP) sludge consists of solid residues generated during the water treatment process. These residues are composed of sediments and organic and inorganic materials—such as sand, clay, organic matter, and heavy metals—removed from the water during treatment. As a potential use for this material, this study aimed to evaluate the incorporation of WTP sludge into the manufacture of soil-cement bricks as a raw material for production. The raw materials used were natural soil, WTP sludge, and Portland cement. Eight mix designs were evaluated, featuring cement-to-soil ratios of 1:7 and 1:10, with sludge incorporated at levels of 0% (reference mix), 6%, 12%, and 20% by mass as a partial replacement for soil.
