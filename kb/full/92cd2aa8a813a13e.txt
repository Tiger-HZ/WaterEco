With the increasing frequency of water pollution incidents due to urbanization and industrialization, this paper proposes an Improved Wolf Pack Algorithm (IWPA) for coordinating multiple Unmanned Surface Vessels (USVs) to achieve rapid river pollution traceability. The IWPA incorporates a dynamic step adjustment mechanism and a dynamic population adjustment strategy to overcome the limitations of the fixed step factor and random repositioning in the standard Wolf Pack Algorithm (WPA). These enhancements prevent the algorithm from converging on local optima and improve the overall traceability efficiency of the multi-USV formation.
