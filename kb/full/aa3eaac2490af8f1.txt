Abstract 
Background: cluster bean is important legume vegetable mainly grown under irrigated condition during 
summer and rainy season. The crop also survives even at moderate salinity and alkalinity conditions. This 
investigation is intended to assess the integrated nutrient m anagement of organic, inorganic, and bio - 
fertilizer for higher productivity of cluster bean under condition in subtropical semi arid zone of up. 
Methods: The experiment was carried out during two consecutive years (2021, and 2022) of Kharif season 
on cluster bean at Horticultural Research Farm RBS College Bichpuri Agra with the combination of twelve 
treatments. The experiment was laid out in randomized block design with three replications using the variety 
Pusa Novbahar. 
Result: The pooled results of two y ears revealed that the highest growth, and quality parameters were 
observed in treatment T10 (Vermicompost (10 t ha-1+ Rhizobium 25 g kg-1 seed + PSB 5 kg ha-1) with 
maximum Germination count (89.05%), Fiber content of pod (3.25 gm/100 g and T12 (100% RDF through 
organic fertilizers 50% RDF through vermicompost+50% RDF through FYM + Bio fertilizers Rhizobium 
25 g kg-1 seed + PSB 5 kg ha-1) maximum plant height at 90 DAS(143.01 cm), Fresh weight of plant (255.15 
gm), spread of plant at 90 DAS (40.78 cm), Number of pods per cluster (8.01), length of pod (10.37 cm), 
Diameter of pod (0.98 CM), Dry weight of pods (21.35 gm)/10 0 g) and protein content of pod (3.67 g/10 0 
g) of cluster bean which was found significantly superior over other treatments. 
 
Keywords: Cluster bean, integrated nutrient management, RDF, FYM, vermicompost, rhizobium , PSB, 
growth, quality 
 
Introduction 
Cluster bean [Cyamopsis tetragonoloba L. Taub] popularly referred as “Guar” may be a vegetable 
crop of Indian origin belonging to the family lenguminoceae. Cluster bean is a self-pollinated crop 
with a chromosome number of 2n=14 [1]. 
It is an important legume crop mainly grown under irrigated con dition during summer and rainy 
season. The crop also survives even at moderate salinity and alkalinity conditions. There is no 
other legume crop so hardy and drought tolerant as guar, which is particularly suited for the soil 
and climate of western Uttar P radesh. There is a big demand for Indian gum products, food 
additives, and food thickener. Guar seed endosperm gum is used in numerous industries, including 
textiles, confectioneries, medicines, cosmetics, mining, drilling and water treatment [2]. 
Nutrition value- Its tender pods are rich source of energy (16 Kcal), moisture (81 g), protein (3.2 
g), fat (1.4 g), carbohydrate (10.8 g), vitamin A (65.3 IU), vitamin C (49 mg),calcium (57 mg) and 
iron (4.5 mg) for every 100 g of edible portion [3], Being approximately 75% dietary fiber, it allows 
the fiber to be added to a food with a minimal effect o n taste and texture. Cluster bean pods are 
rich in soluble fibre and lower blood cholesterol levels. The use of guar gum has increased 
tremendously, as it is a natural absorbent. In the plains, it is sown twice, from summer season and 
rainy season. It is o ne of the most popular beans grown in India. India accounts for about 80% 
production of guar gum in the world, remaining 20% is produced by countries like Pakistan, USA, 
Australia, South Africa, Sudan and Argentina [4]. India produced 16.80 lakh tonnes of cluster bean 
vegetable pods on an area of 23.30 lakh hectares, with a productivity of 428 kg per hectare, in the 
year 2017-18 [61]. It is grown on a higher scale in Rajasthan (18.18 lakh ha), Gujarat (2.27 lakh ha),

The aim of integrated nutritional management is to integrate the 
use of natural and artificial soil nutrients to increase crop 
productivity and maintain soil fertility for future generations. In 
long-term crop studies, balanced use of inorganic fertilizers 
cannot maintain higher yields over the years due to lack of 
secondary and micr onutrients and deterioration of soil physical 
properties. Increased use of fertilizer will undoubtedly increase 
the production of raw materials significantly, but in the 
sustainable fertility it will have a negative impact on soil health. 
In case of INM ap proach, careful application of all nutrients can 
sustainably improve soil fertility and economic yields [7]. This 
current study shows the actual impact of the integrated use of 
inorganic fertilizers combined with organic fertilizers and bio -
fertilizers on the growth, and quality of cluster ban and 
sustainable soil fertility. 
 
Materials and Methods 
The study was conducted under irrigation during the year 2021 to 
2022 cropping season in on rainy season at R.B.S. College 
horticulture research farm, Bichpuri, Agra. The location of the 
experiment remained unaltered in both the years. The research 
farm is situated abou t 1 km to the near Bichpuri railway station 
Uttar Pradesh India. Bichpuri Agra is located latitude of 27.2 o 
North and longitude of 77.9o east with an altitude of 163.4 meters 
above means sea level. Agra has a semi arid and sub tropical 
climate with hot and dry summers and severely cold. The average 
annual maximum and minimum temperature is 36 °C and 4 °C 
respectively. The soil type in the area is light textured sandy loam. 
The experiment was laid out as a factorial randomized block 
design (FRBD) arrangement treatment twelve combination, three 
replication and thirty six total treatments. Trail others detail is 
following-Variety- Pusa Novbahar, Spacing - (row ×pant) 45×30 
cm, Net plot size-2.25 m×1.5 m=3.375 mete2. 
 
Data collection and analysis: Data on growth and development, 
yield components and quality variables were collected and 
subjected to analysis of variance (ANOVA) using the General 
Linear Model of the SAS statistical package (SAS, 2007). All 
significant pairs of treatment means were compared using the 
critical difference (CD) at 5% level of significance, coefficient of 
variation (C.V.) and standard error of mean (SEM).

Results and Discussion 
• Growth parameters 
The inorganic and organic fertilizers or manures along with bio -
fertilizers alone or in combination were found to have significant 
effect on growth characters as compared to RDF treatment. It is 
evident from Table 1 that among all the twelve treatments, T12 
(100% RDF through organic fertilizers 50% RDF through 
vermicompost+50% RDF through FYM + Bio fertilizers

through organic fertilizers (50% RDF through 
vermicompost+50% RDF through FYM). The reason might be 
due to higher availability of nutrients as a result of integration of 
organic and inorganic sources with bio fertilizers.

Conclusion 
It can be concluded from the study that the variety, Pusa Novbahar 
as well as application of inorganic fertilizers with organic sources 
of FYM /Vermicompost with bio fertilizers when combined 
resulted in better growth and quality of cluster bean in sandy 
loamy soil of semi arid zone of Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, 
India. It is noted that the application of T12 (100% RDF through 
organic fertilizers 50% RDF through vermicompost+50% RDF 
through FYM + Bio fertilizers Rhizobium 25 g kg-1 seed + PSB 5 
kg ha-1) resulted in best growth and quality parameters e.g. plant 
height, Fresh weight of plant, spread of plant, Number of pods per
