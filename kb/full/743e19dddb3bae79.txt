Abstract Background Since the approval of immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICI) and targeted therapies (TT), the number of long-term survivors of metastatic melanoma (LTS) has increased. However, data on their complaints and needs remain limited. Objective To examine the prevalence and spectrum of treatment-related adverse events (TRAEs), psycho-oncological distress, and socioeconomic outcomes in long-term survivors (LTS), to evaluate their association with elevated distress levels, and to determine whether these findings support the implementation of dedicated survivorship programmes.
