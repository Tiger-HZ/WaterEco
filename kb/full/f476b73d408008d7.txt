Abstract Efficient photocatalytic water splitting to produce hydrogen using low-cost semiconductor thin films in photoelectrochemical (PEC) cells is a promising approach for harnessing solar energy. In this study, possibility to develop p-type cuprous oxide thin films with a flower-like surface morphology on ITO substrates was examined in order to improve the photocatalytic activity of Cu2O photocathodes in a PEC. For this, electrodeposition technique was employed with a novel aqueous peroxide bath containing 0.1 M CuSO4, 0.3 M H2O2, 0.05 M NaNO3 and NaOH solutions. Resulted films were characterised using grazing incidence X-ray diffraction (GIXD) spectra, scanning electron microscopy (SEM), X-ray diffraction spectra (XRD), and chopped light currentvoltage measurements in a PEC.
