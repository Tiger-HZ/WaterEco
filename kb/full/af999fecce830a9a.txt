Mining-generated tailings ponds, waste-rock dumps and spoil grounds are commonly characterized by poor fertility, heavy-metal contamination, acid drainage and limited natural revegetation. Here, we present a multilayer composite ecological restoration mat that integrates hydraulic isolation, slope reinforcement, and vegetation restoration within a single system. The mat consists of a geosynthetic clay liner (GCL) that functions as the barrier and water-retention layer, together with a nutrient substrate layer, a plant seed layer and an anti-erosion layer.
