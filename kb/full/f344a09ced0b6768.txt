Abstract Perovskite light-emitting diodes (PeLEDs) are inherently suitable for stretchable devices owing to their mechanical flexibility and outstanding optoelectronic properties, yet their practical application remains hindered by the lack of encapsulation materials that simultaneously provide high stretchability and effective protection against moisture and oxygen. Herein, we report a stretchable densified microscale liquid metal–polydimethylsiloxane (μLM–PDMS) encapsulation layer through an integrated ultrasonic dispersion and centrifugal densification (IUCDC) strategy. Through ethanol-assisted ultrasonication, monodisperse μLM particles are generated.
