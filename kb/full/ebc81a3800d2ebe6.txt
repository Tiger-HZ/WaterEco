ABSTRACT The image shows different sources of HMs from the ecosystem, and its subsequent treatment using CWs incorporating different substrates, microbial communities and macrophytes. The treated water can then be sustainably utilized for agricultural applications, recycling, and reuse. Heavy metals (HMs) are persistent in nature and cause serious threats to the environment and human health. This generates an urgent need for an efficient and long-term solution. In contrast to the conventional treatment system, constructed wetlands (CWs) are considered a green and cost-effective system to remediate HMs-contaminated wastewater.
