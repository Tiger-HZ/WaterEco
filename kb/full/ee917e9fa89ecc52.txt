Environmental conditions are an important but often neglected determinant of teaching and learning in tertiary institutions. Nigerian campuses commonly experience noise from generators, traffic and social activities; emissions from fossil-fuel-powered electricity generation and vehicles; inadequate solid-waste collection; blocked drainage; and unsafe water supplies. This narrative review synthesizes evidence on how these exposures affect educational processes, health and institutional performance in Nigerian universities, polytechnics and colleges of education. The literature base was updated in July 2026 using peer-reviewed studies and reports from relevant national and international organisations.
