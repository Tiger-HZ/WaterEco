Abstract Gas outbursts in coal mine goafs can entrain coal dust from the surface of spontaneously combusting coal, significantly increasing the risk of methane/coal dust explosions that often result in catastrophic accidents. However, few studies have investigated the explosion characteristics of methane/combustible dust mixtures ignited by high-temperature heat source. To address this, a self‑developed gas explosion setup was employed to examine the effects of coal dust on methane/air explosions under high‑temperature ignition conditions. Specifically, experiments were conducted to clarify the relationship between coal dust concentration and ignition temperature.
