Hypertension is one of the most prevalent cardiovascular disorders worldwide and requires long-term pharmacotherapy. Olmesartan Medoxomil is an effective antihypertensive drug; however, its poor aqueous solubility may limit its dissolution and bioavailability. The present study aimed to formulate and evaluate Fast Dissolving Oral Films (FDOFs) of Olmesartan Medoxomil to improve patient compliance and enhance drug release. β-Cyclodextrin was utilized to improve the solubility of the drug through inclusion complex formation. Fast dissolving oral films were prepared by the solvent casting method using HPMC E5 as the film-forming polymer, PEG-400 as plasticizer, and sodium starch glycolate as superdisintegrant. The prepared films were evaluated for appearance, thickness, weight variation, folding endurance, surface pH, drug content uniformity, disintegration time, moisture content, and in-vitro drug release. All formulations showed satisfactory physicochemical properties and rapid disintegration. The optimized formulation exhibited excellent mechanical properties, uniform drug content, minimum disintegration time, and maximum drug release. The study concluded that fast dissolving oral films of Olmesartan Medoxomil can serve as a promising alternative to conventional dosage forms by providing rapid drug release, improved patient compliance, and enhanced therapeutic efficacy in the treatment of hypertension.
