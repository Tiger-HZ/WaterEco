This study presents the development of a multi-sensor technology for water quality monitoring intended for an aquaponics system integrated with cloud-based artificial intelligence assistance. The system utilizes an Arduino UNO, an RS-485 soil sensor, and a TDS sensor to collect data, which is transmitted to ThingSpeak for visualization and analyzed using OpenAI’s ChatGPT for feedback and optimization. A user-friendly dashboard interface enhances system usability. To evaluate the system’s performance, statistical analysis was conducted, including t-tests to assess sensor accuracy, multiple linear regression to identify significant factors influencing water quality, and the Water Quality Index (WQI).
