Abstract Micron silicon nitride (Si3N4) particles are less expensive than nano silicon nitride particles. Therefore, by adjusting the ratio of micron to nano particles, can a Ni-Co composite coating with excellent overall performance be obtained while controlling cost? This study introduced mixed nano and micron Si3N4 particles with different mass ratios. Ni-Co-Si3N4 composite coatings were fabricated via pulse electrodeposition. The influence mechanisms of single-scale and mixed-scale particles on the microstructure, corrosion resistance, mechanical properties, and wear resistance of the coatings were investigated.
