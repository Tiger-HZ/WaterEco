ABSTRACT The aim of this study is to evaluate the effectiveness of a raw organic composite, made from flower waste, in promoting plant growth and enhancing soil fertility. The flower‐based amendment was prepared by thermally treating (55°C) dried flower wastes from Rosa indica , Chrysanthemum morifolium , and Jasminum auriculatum. Physicochemical parameters were also measured. The presence of bioactive compounds in the flower‐based amendment was confirmed through gas chromatography–mass spectrometry (GC–MS). Its efficacy in promoting seed germination and plant growth was tested using Arachis hypogaea L., with assessments of seedling emergence, shoot length, total foliage, chlorophyll content, and groundnut yield in pot assays.
