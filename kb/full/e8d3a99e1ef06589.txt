Addressing the urgent need for air pollution and carbon emission reduction and the uncertainty in local policy implementation under fiscal decentralization, this paper quantifies the intensity of various pollution and carbon reduction policies and examines their effects on carbon–air pollution reduction, as well as their heterogeneity across different dimensions and levels of fiscal decentralization. Findings: (1) Policies overlap significantly, focusing on industry, construction, transport, agriculture, industrial parks, and rural areas.
