ABSTRACT Chemical‐intensive agriculture poses significant threats to human and soil health. One of the primary pathways of soil degradation is the loss of soil organic C through aggregate degradation. However, the long‐term interaction effect of diversified cropping systems and nutrient management (NM) options on soil aggregation, associated C accrual, and their regulatory factors remain insufficiently explored. These aspects are important for sustaining the crop productivity and environmental health. Therefore, a long‐term fixed‐plot experiment (2003–2024) involving organic and conventional NM was undertaken.
