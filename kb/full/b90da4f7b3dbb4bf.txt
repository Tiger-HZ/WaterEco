Abstract Biodiesel production from used cooking oil (WCO) is a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels, but its high free fatty acid (FFA) content (&amp;gt;5 wt %) makes direct basic transesterification difficult. This study presents an integrated two-stage strategy that optimizes the acid esterification of oleic acid (the dominant FFA in WCO) using response surface methodology (RSM, Box–Behnken) and then evaluates downstream transesterification, while reporting green chemistry metrics alongside conversion. The RSM model identified an optimum at a methanol-to-oleic acid ratio of 8.44:1, 2 wt % H2SO4, and 363 min.
