No-reference image quality assessment (NR-IQA) quantifies image distortion. It plays an important role in computer vision. Distorted images vary greatly in content. Many existing methods tend to fuse content information with quality prediction. However, they often overlook human visual perception. To address this issue, we propose an attention-guided content-aware NR-IQA method. It combines meta-learning with image content understanding. The approach uses refined deep semantic features for quality evaluation. First, we train a meta-model on a baseline network. This improves sensitivity to diverse distortions.
