This study aimed to provide basic data for intervention programs that can improve the quality of life of modern adults in the beauty industry by examining the effects of beauty and health Management behaviors on quality of life, with self-esteem serving as a mediating factor. For this purpose, questionnaires were distributed both online and offline to adult men and women across the country, and a total of 390 responses were collected and used for statistical analysis. Beauty and health Management behaviors were found to be factors that influence both self-esteem and quality of life. In addition, self-esteem showed a partial mediating effect in the relationship between beauty and health Management behaviors and quality of life. These findings indicate that beauty and health Management behaviors affect quality of life both directly and indirectly. The results have academic significance and may serve as basic data for developing intervention programs aimed at improving the quality of life of customers in the beauty industry.
