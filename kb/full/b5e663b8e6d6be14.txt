Lembayung is a purple-fleshed sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas L.) variety with potential for fresh consumption and food processing. However, its production on BRIS (Beach Ridges Interspersed with Swales) soils is constrained by low soil fertility, poor nutrient-retention capacity, and rapid nutrient leaching. Appropriate fertiliser management is therefore required to improve crop performance and storage root quality under these marginal soil conditions. This study evaluated the effects of different fertiliser packages on the growth, yield, tuber quality, and sensory attributes of the purple-fleshed sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas L.) variety Lembayung cultivated under nutrient-deficient BRIS (Beach Ridges Interspersed with Swales) soil conditions.
