General description This repository documents and provides spatial modelling datasets prepared for the Namoi River Basin case study in New South Wales, Australia, within the framework of the WaterTheft project, Grant Agreement No. 101124884. The deposited files were prepared for hydrological and hydro-economic modelling and include: - a processed Digital Elevation Model;- soil-property layers adapted to a common 250 m × 250 m modelling grid;- a model-oriented land-use allocation layer generated using the HILAM methodology;- lookup tables, provenance records and processing documentation; and- metadata describing the Bureau of Meteorology stations and climate variables used to prepare the model forcing. The deposited spatial products were derived from third-party datasets identified below and from original processing undertaken by the dataset creators. Copyright and all other pre-existing rights in the source datasets remain with their respective data providers and rights holders. Digital Elevation Model 2.1 Original data source The Digital Elevation Model used for the Namoi River Basin was derived from: Gallant, J., Wilson, N., Dowling, T., Read, A. and Inskeep, C. (2011).SRTM-derived 1 Second Digital Elevation Models Version 1.0.Geoscience Australia, Canberra.Geoscience Australia Persistent Identifier: 72759. The original product includes three elevation surfaces: - Digital Elevation Model — DEM;- Smoothed Digital Elevation Model — DEM-S; and- Hydrologically Enforced Digital Elevation Model — DEM-H. The layer used in this repository was: DEM. The original product has an approximate spatial resolution of one arc-second, approximately 30 m, and is referenced to WGS 84, EPSG:4326. The original elevation product was derived by Geoscience Australia from Shuttle Radar Topography Mission data acquired in February 2000. 2.2 Processing applied The original elevation data were processed to produce a terrain layer for the Namoi River Basin modelling domain. The processing included, as applicable: - extraction of the Namoi River Basin study area delineated at Gunnedah;- mosaicking of source tiles;- clipping to the study-area boundary;- reprojection to CRS EPSG:3577 - GDA94 / Austral
