Health cadres play a strategic role as a bridge between communities and primary health care facilities, particularly in promotive and preventive activities. Limited cadre involvement may contribute to suboptimal health service quality, including low Posyandu coverage, delayed detection of health problems, and limited community participation. This study aimed to analyze the relationship between health cadre involvement and public health service quality based on patient perceptions in the working area of Ambacang Padang Health Center. A quantitative cross-sectional design was employed involving patients who received health services at the health center.
