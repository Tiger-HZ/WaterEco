Abstract Surface coal mining in central Appalachia (USA) often results in salinization of headwater streams, with documented loss of several macroinvertebrate taxa and changes in community metrics across streams that vary in specific conductance (SC). However, the extent to which this salinization alters within-stream patterns in water chemistry and macroinvertebrate communities in these headwater streams is largely unknown. Here, we aimed to examine macroinvertebrate community responses to physical and chemical gradients within six Appalachian headwater streams (four salinized and two reference).
