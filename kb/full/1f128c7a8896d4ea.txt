The electrochemical CO(2) reduction reaction offers a pathway to producing fuels and high-value chemicals from waste CO2 using renewable electricity. Among known catalysts, only copper in aqueous electrolytes enables the formation of C2+ products with high activity. The process of the C-C coupling is believed to be limited by the dimerization of two adsorbed *CO to form a *OC-CO* intermediate. Experimental strategies to improve selectivity toward C2+ products have recently focused on increasing temperature and pressure in the system, but the origins of these effects remain poorly understood.
