Surface water assessment is key to ecosystem protection, irrigation applicability, and appropriate management, yet traditional water quality index (WQI) techniques miss nonlinear interactions, spatial variation, and temporal effects. Hence, a hierarchical stress-sensitive neuro graph learning framework (HSS-NGF) is proposed for early, interpretative, and use-specific water quality evaluation. Initially, scalar indices average various physicochemical variables and conceal sub-lethal effects of contaminants, allowing localized degradation to persist, resulting in a latent discovery of progressive degradation.
