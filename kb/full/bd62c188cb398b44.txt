Abstract The development of the unconventional gas and CO 2 sequestration is moving to deep formations. Because of the small flow pathways in the matrix, the Knudsen number might be high even though the gas is dense. In fact, due to the relatively high pressure at in situ conditions, gas flow in microfractures usually manifests a strong slip and nonideal gas effects. Therefore, understanding the coupling mechanism of these two on gas flow in rough‐walled microfractures is required to accurately model subsurface flow behavior. In this study, pressure‐driven gas flow in rough‐walled microfracture is analyzed in depth.
