The objective of this document is to provide a comprehensive background on water reuse of wastewater effluent and how it can enable Army installations to have secure water resources. Wastewater effluent provides a renewable source of water that can be treated to meet specific applications and offset potable water usage. There are many aspects to implementing reuse, including navigating regulations, understanding the different fit-for-purpose applications, and knowledge of the different technologies required for advanced water treatment.
