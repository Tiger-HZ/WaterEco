This study investigated the potential of cattle abdominal waste as a viable raw material for producing high-quality organic fertilizer. The experiment focused on proximate analysis of the waste to determine its nutrient content and suitability for composting or anaerobic digestion. Parameters such as pH, total solids, organic matter, moisture content, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and ash content were examined before and after digestion. Experimental data were statistically analyzed using Microsoft Excel and SPSS. Mean values, standard deviations, and percentage differences were computed to assess nutrient variations between predigested and digested samples.
