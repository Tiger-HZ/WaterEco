Objectives: To build an explainable model that predicts paddy yields based on the fertilizer application, removing the influence of the climatic, irrigation and spatial factors. Method: The study suggests a new model called Fertilizer-Focused Stacked Ensemble Model (FFSEM). Context residualisation eliminates contextual effects. Mutual information, fertilizer-context synergy, redundancy suppression and stability penalization generate fertilizer sensitivity scores. Softmax weighting forms a Context-Integrated Feature (CIF) space. The Gradient Boosting meta-learning technique is used for stacking regressors like Light GBM, XGBoost and Multilayer Perceptron (MLP).
