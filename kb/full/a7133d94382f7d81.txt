The production of extruded snacks or ready-to-eat (RTE) foods with desirable quality characteristics, over the years, from underutilized/lesser known crops using extrusion processing technology has been of great concern to food processors. This resulted in the need for appropriate selection of extrusion process variables regimes that could guarantee the production of acceptable extruded snacks with minimal levels of anti-nutrients This study evaluated the effect of barrel temperature, screw speed and feed moisture, as extrusion process parameters, on the phytates and tannins contents of extruded snacks produced from aerial yam and soybean flour blends, using Response surface methodology.
