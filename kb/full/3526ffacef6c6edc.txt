Cyanophyte-harmful algal blooms (CyanoHABs) are a challenging issue in wetland ecosystems around the world. Cyanobacteria produce microcystins (MCs), which are associated with hepatotoxicity in animals. The seasonal blooms of Microcystis are widely reported in both freshwater and brackish ecosystems throughout the world. The present study aims to address a broader understanding of the seasonality of Microcystis in six wetland ecosystems in Eastern India under different management regimes during 2014-2020. Analysis of water physicochemical parameters such as dissolved oxygen, water temperature, pH, total dissolved solids, Sechi Depth, specific conductivity, total alkalinity, and nutrient parameters (nitrate and phosphate) shows significant (p≤0.05) seasonal variations.
