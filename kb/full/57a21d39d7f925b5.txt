Quality 4.0 is conceptualized as an integrative governance approach for smart cities within the present chapter. Quality 4.0 is positioned here as an enabling layer that reframes the set of known quality governance standards in the context of digitalization, data availability, and multi-stakeholder governance. It critically reviews the application of traditional standards and approaches—ISO management system approaches, Six Sigma methodologies, and Total Quality Management approaches—as its architectural, analytical, and cultural constituents that can be enhanced by Internet of Things (IoT)–based digital enablement of artificial intelligence (AI) for the purpose of converting static quality compliance approaches into dynamic learning-driven operation architectures. It also reviews the principal challenges of smart cities that require holistic data availability with institutional fragmentation, long time scales of operation, regulatory restrictiveness, and social differentiation. Quality 4.0 is outlined as providing the aforementioned necessary enabling infrastructure for smart cities without obstructing the need for politics in smart decision-making. It also presents the complete Smart City Quality 4.0 Framework with its constituents of multidimensional quality of cities, architectural governance through processes, and domain architectures in modularity with feedback loops in connectivity with social resilience through risk management. It concludes by affirming that Quality 4.0 offers the long-awaited enabling infrastructure in smart city policy for energizing strategic governance of smart cities with real-time processes in its learning organizational capacities in decision-making.
