Abstract Background Freshwater Lake eutrophication is one of the most widespread problems in the world. Freshwater lakes are threatened especially by anthropogenic activities. Nutrients from different sources enter lakes via untreated human wastes disposal and agricultural wastes containing usable forms of phosphorous and nitrogen which are critical for the growth of phytoplankton and other algae. This study undertook an integrated assessment of trophic status, nutrient enrichment, chlorophyll-a, and comprehensive pollution index. This study aimed to determine the water quality status and trophic state of Lugo Lake in northeastern Ethiopia. Methods A cross-sectional study was conducted to determine the status of nutrient enrichment, algal biomass and assess associated anthropogenic factors
