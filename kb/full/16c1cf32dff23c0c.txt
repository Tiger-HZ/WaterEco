Irrigation-water quality and soil physical properties influence water movement through agricultural soils, but their interactions under field irrigation conditions remain poorly characterized in Eastern Africa. This study evaluated irrigation-water quality, soil physicochemical properties, and infiltration behaviour in the Kagitumba and Nasho Government-Funded Irrigation schemes in Eastern Rwanda. A comparative observational design was applied at 20 sampling locations per scheme. Soil samples were collected at 0–30 and 30–60 cm depths, and 20 irrigation-water samples were collected from each scheme.
