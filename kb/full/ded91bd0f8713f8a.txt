The purpose of the study is to provide a scientific substantiation and experimental evaluation of the effectiveness of Sub–Pro probiotic application to increase productivity, improve feed conversion and biological usefulness of meat from Cobb 500 cross broiler chickens in industrial cultivation. The experiment was conducted on 100 birds of one day-old broiler chickens, divided into control and experimental groups (50 heads each). The birds of the experimental group received a probiotic solution at a concentration of 10 g per 1 ton of water for 42 days. The microbial background of the chyme of the cecum was assessed by the method of limiting dilutions at inoculation on selective media.
