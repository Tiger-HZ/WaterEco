Recent urbanization and overexploitation have significantly degraded groundwater quality in the shallow unconfined aquifer in Barjisiyah. The study area is located within the discharge area and is exposed to natural salinization from the underlying layer and anthropogenic sources with limited rainfall recharge. Six groundwater samples have been collected from the unconfined aquifer across urbanized zones. Total Dissolved Solids reach an extremely high level of 22,200 mg/L, with a wide range of major ion concentrations. Hydrochemical facies are dominated by sodium-chloride and calcium-chloride, confirming advanced mineralization.
