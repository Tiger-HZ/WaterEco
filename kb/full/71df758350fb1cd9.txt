Wetlands provide vital ecosystem services, yet realising their potential for sustainable business and community benefit through Nature-based Solutions (NbS) requires understanding the broader conditions that enable or hinder restoration efforts. This article presents a combined methodology for prioritising wetland NbS-related business opportunities, developed within the Horizon 2020 project Restore4Life. Drawing on site-specific catalogues of business-related ecosystem services across five countries, opportunities were identified in two clusters: direct uses (water storage, irrigation, water-based recreation, fish farming and angling, biomass harvesting and grazing, carbon sequestration, and environmental monitoring), and indirect uses (tourism, product processing, and sales).
