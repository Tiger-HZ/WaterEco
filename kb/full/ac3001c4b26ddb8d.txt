What Makes Check Valves Essential in Process Systems? A check valve is an automatic non-return valve that allows flow in one direction and prevents reverse flow through pressure differential alone, with no external actuation or power supply required. When forward flow is present, the disc moves away from the seat and flow passes through. The moment flow reverses, the closure element returns to the seat automatically, protecting pumps, compressors, and downstream equipment from back pressure damage and water hammer.
