Abstract Reductions in insect biodiversity are widely documented and result from numerous pressures that operate from local to global scales. Identifying and synthesizing information on these drivers has become a central focus of research over the past decade, yet existing reviews often emphasize a limited set of well-established threats. Here, we provide a policy-oriented review of the drivers of insect decline, structured explicitly around the Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services (IPBES) assessment framework and accompanied by statements of confidence to facilitate direct uptake into biodiversity assessments.
