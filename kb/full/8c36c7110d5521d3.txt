In the shadowed craters of the Moon&s;s south pole, where sunlight has not touched for billions of years, a new mission prepares for launch to capture every fragment of data. Since light levels are very low in this region, scientists cannot obtain complete information from images. This may lead to incorrect mission planning from Earth; this will cause scientists to get insufficient data. Low Light images often exhibit characteristics such as low brightness, low contrast, colour distortion as well as blurry image. Due to these low-light conditions, the human eye cannot extract useful information directly from the images. This paper reviews multiple low-light image enhancement algorithms that have received considerable attention over the past decade. Low-light image enhancement methods are mainly categorized into histogram equalization, frequency domain approaches, data-index-based methods, and Zero-DCE solutions. In addition, we summarize findings from previous review papers and present subsequent research directions as recommended.
