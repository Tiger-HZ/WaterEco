Groundwater serves as a vital resource for domestic, agricultural, and industrial needs in Nigeria. However, increasing concerns have emerged regarding its depletion, especially in rural areas where reliance on groundwater is high due to limited surface water availability. This study investigates community perceptions on the causes, effects, and coping strategies related to groundwater depletion in a selected region of northern Nigeria. A survey was conducted involving 120 respondents using structured questionnaires. Descriptive statistical analysis was employed to interpret the data. Findings revealed that 79.2% of respondents were aware of groundwater depletion.
