ABSTRACT The cyanosphere is composed of non-cyanobacterial microorganisms living within the exopolysaccharide sheath of cyanobacteria, interacting with the cyanobacterial hosts and their surrounding environment. Understanding the interactions between cyanobacteria and their cyanospheres can help predict the success of terrestrial cyanobacteria in providing ecosystem services in nutrient-poor environments. However, knowledge of the microbial diversity and functions within the cyanosphere remains limited. Here, we used metagenomic sequencing to reconstruct 415 metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) from cyanosphere-associated microbes linked to 56 terrestrial cyanobacteria cultures, representing 12 cyanobacterial orders.
