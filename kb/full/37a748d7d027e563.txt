Understanding microbial survival mechanisms in aquatic environments is essential for public health. This study investigates the potential role of free-living amoebae (FLA) as environmental shelters for Helicobacter pylori in Istanbul’s potable and wastewater treatment plants. Sampling was conducted during wet (February–March 2024) and dry (May–June 2024) seasons from influent and effluent points across various facilities. Standardized protocols for water collection, filtration, DNA extraction, and sequencing were employed. Metagenomic analysis revealed the presence of Dictyostelium discoideum as the only detected FLA and identified Helicobacter DNA fragments, with an average of 31.8% assigned to H. pylori within this genus.
