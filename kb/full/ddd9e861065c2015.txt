A unifying approach for the derivation of watershed‐scale conservation equations governing hydrologic responses has recently been introduced. The approach is based on space‐time averaging of the corresponding point‐scale conservation equations over an averaging region called the representative elementary watershed (REW). The conservation equations are supplemented by constitutive relationships needed for the closure of various mass and momentum exchange terms. In this paper, we present a summary of the resulting equations for a somewhat simpler problem and show how these equations can be employed to model the long‐term water balance of a single, hypothetical REW.
