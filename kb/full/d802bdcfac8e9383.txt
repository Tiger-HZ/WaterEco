This paper presents a theoretical and numerical investigation of microbubble vibration near curved solid boundaries. Most existing theoretical studies focus on flat boundaries, although curved surfaces are widely encountered in practical cavitation processes. The novelty of this work is the development of a curvature-dependent model based on the fluid velocity potential and the method of images. A small-amplitude vibration equation is derived that includes the effects of boundary curvature and bubble-boundary distance, while the flat-boundary case is recovered as a limiting result. The model also enables a direct comparison between convex and concave boundaries.
