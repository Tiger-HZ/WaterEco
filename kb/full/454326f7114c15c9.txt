Abstract Climate change is amplifying water extremes that jeopardize agricultural production and food security in vulnerable regions such as river deltas. Cambodia, located in the Lower Mekong Basin, is among the world's most climate-vulnerable countries. However, the availability of actionable, context-specific evidence from existing empirical and modelling studies to inform national adaptation planning remains uncertain. This study presents a PRISMA-guided systematic review (2000–2024) to synthesize current knowledge on climate-induced water stress in Cambodian agriculture and identify key research gaps limiting evidence-based policy and practice.
