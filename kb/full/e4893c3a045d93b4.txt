Introduction Accurate dietary intake assessment remains challenging, particularly for portion-size estimation, and context-specific photographic aids remain limited in Argentina. This study aimed to develop an open-access digital atlas of foods and recipes consumed in Argentina, integrating portion-size photographs and nutrient-composition data, and to describe its performance for food and recipe retrieval and visual portion-size matching under controlled direct-observation conditions. Methods The Smart24Food atlas was developed as a digital resource integrating food photographs, standardized recipes, and nutrient-composition information.
