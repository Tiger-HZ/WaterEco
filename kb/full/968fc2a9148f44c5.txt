Introduction. Climate change has become an increasingly pressing global challenge, with water resources representing one of the sectors most vulnerable to its adverse impacts.Problem Statement. Ukraine is among the least freshwater-secure countries in Europe and is experien cing a significant decline in both the quantity and quality of its freshwater resources. This trend is largely attributable to climate change, the adverse impacts of economic activity, and deficiencies inwater resource management. Consequently, improving the legal regulation of water relations has become a priority.Purpose.
