Abstract A two-year (2023–2024) field trial in Arsi Zone evaluated the effects of full lime and partial lime combined with vermicompost (VC) under rotational cropping on bread wheat and malt barley productivity and soil chemical properties. Lime-based treatments significantly increased plant height, grain yield, and biomass, while most yield components were unaffected. Full aglime + recommended NP (RNP) produced the highest yields, whereas 50% lime + VC + RNP also significantly improved yields over RNP alone, providing a viable lower-input option for resource-limited farmers. Treatment effects were generally consistent across years, with wheat showing additional yield improvement in the second year, suggesting residual lime and rotation benefits.
