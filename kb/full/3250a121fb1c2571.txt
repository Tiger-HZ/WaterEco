Abstract Conventional open-field vegetable production is increasingly constrained by climate volatility and natural resource endowments, creating supply instabilities and shortages that threaten global food resilience and human health. Here, we integrate solar photovoltaics and atmospheric water harvesting with plant factories, creating a technically mature off-grid agricultural system using plant factories (OASIS). By decoupling production from local water and grid infrastructure, OASIS significantly expands the area suitable for cultivation. Globally, OASIS can meet the recommended upper vegetable intake (600 g capita–1 day–1) for at least 98.3% of the population, leveraging mostly non-arable land.
