Potentially toxic elements (PTEs) are one of the most concerning pollutant types in the surface environment. In this study, a total of 115 farmland soils from the 0–20 cm layer were collected from the Ibinur Lake Basin (ILB) in northwest China to investigate the concentration characteristics, pollution status, potential ecological risks, and possible sources of seven PTEs (As, Cd, Cu, Ni, Pb, Hg, and Zn). The pollution load index (PLI) method was applied to quantify soil pollution status, while the potential ecological risk index (RI) was adopted to evaluate PTE-induced ecological risks. Multivariate statistical approaches were further implemented to trace the potential sources of these PTEs.
