The accurate and quantitative retrieval of atmospheric water content plays an important supporting role in extreme weather monitoring, aviation meteorological support, cloud precipitation formation mechanism research, atmospheric radiation effect assessment, weather modification and climate change analysis. In this paper, the retrieval model algorithm of cloud water content parameters is studied based on multiple data quality control processing. The results show that the algorithm has good rationality and accuracy, and can lay the foundation for obtaining more abundant 3D fine structure and generation and dissipation mechanism of clouds and weak precipitation.
