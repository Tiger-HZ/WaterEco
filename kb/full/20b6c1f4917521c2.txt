Abstract The genus Merismopedia (Merismopediaceae, Chroococcales), characterized by rectangular colonies with cells arranged in regular perpendicular rows and columns, represents a challenging study system due to its limited cultivability. The genus recently underwent taxonomic revision using cultivation‐independent techniques and a polyphasic approach. Following the designation of an epitype, its type species, M. tranquilla was placed within a phylogenetic framework. Polyphyletic “ Merismopedia ” was split, and its species clustered within at least three distinct families. The case represents a clear example of convergent evolution, a phenomenon not uncommon among Cyanobacteria.
