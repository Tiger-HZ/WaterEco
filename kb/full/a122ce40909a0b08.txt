ABSTRACT The cooperative effects between different metal centers play a crucial role in photoelectrocatalytic applications. Therefore, the design and synthesis of heterometallic coordination polymers (CPs) have become a significant research direction. Herein, we report the solvothermal synthesis and characterization of three novel isomorphous heterometallic CPs, namely {[M 1 M 2 (H 2 O) 6 (DODDA)]·4H 2 O} n ( SNUT‐42‐MnZn , M 1 M 2 = MnZn; SNUT‐42‐CoZn , M 1 M 2 = CoZn; SNUT‐42‐CoMn , M 1 M 2 = CoMn), based on the tetracarboxylic ligand H 4 (DODDA) (1‐(3,5‐dicarboxyphenyl)‐4‐oxo‐1,4‐dihydropyridazine‐3,5‐dicarboxylic acid).
