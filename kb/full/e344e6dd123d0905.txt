ABSTRACT The extensive use of antimicrobials has contributed to the emergence of multidrug-resistant (MDR) microorganisms in municipal wastewater. The prevalence, resistance patterns, and virulence factors of Enterococcus species were studied in 25 untreated municipal wastewater samples collected from Ardabil, Iran (2017–2018). We identified 87 Enterococcus isolates using biochemical and PCR assays, of which 59.8% were E. faecium, 33.3% E. faecalis, and 6.9% other species. Susceptibility testing showed considerable resistance to rifampicin (61%) and tetracycline (57.5%), while all isolates were sensitive to teicoplanin.
