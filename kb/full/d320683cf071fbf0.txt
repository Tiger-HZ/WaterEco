The rapid expansion of industrial and agricultural activities has increased pressure on natural water resources, making continuous monitoring of groundwater quality critically important. The present study was conducted to assess the suitability of groundwater for drinking purposes in the HSIIDC (Haryana State Industrial and Infrastructure Development Corporation) industrial regions of Sonipat district. Groundwater samples were collected from ten industrial locations within the HSIIDC industrial estates of Sonipat City, Barhi, Kundli, Murthal and Rai along NH-44 (National Highway-44) corridor in Sonipat district.
