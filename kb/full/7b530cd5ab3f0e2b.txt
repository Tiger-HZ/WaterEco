The purpose of the study is to develop a comprehensive program for the development of coordination abilities in primary school children with mild intellectual disability through adaptive swimming with elements of water-based motor activities. Research methods and organization. Methods of analysis of scientific and methodological literature and a sociological survey were used. The developed comprehensive program was tested at the swimming pool of the Municipal Budgetary Institution of Additional Education "Sports School Temp" in the city of Artem, Primorsky Krai.
