ABSTRACT Increasing atmospheric nitrogen (N) deposition can stimulate tree growth, raising phosphorus (P) demand relative to supply and thereby intensifying relative P limitation. Yet how relative P limitation and above‐ and belowground resource‐use strategies change during plantation development, and whether responses to chronic N enrichment vary with stand age, remain unclear. We integrated a synthesis of 926 observations from plantation forests across China with two co‐located field experiments: a seedling P‐addition bioassay and a 15‐year N‐addition experiment across a larch plantation chronosequence.
