A centrifuge method was adapted for evaluating solute transport during steady state flow. Solute breakthrough curves were obtained on three sediment types at average water contents ranging from 14 to 84% moisture saturation. Interruptions in flow for sampling, a potential limitation of the method, had no apparent effect on breakthrough curves for the tracers and conditions evaluated. Compaction was observed for a silt loam sediment but was not observed for fine and coarse sand sediments. Regions of immobile water developed with decreasing moisture saturation; these results extend the range of previous reports.
