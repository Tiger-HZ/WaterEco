ABSTRACT Hydro‐climatic variability in tropical river basins is shaped by changes in rainfall extremes and coupled land‐surface responses. This study assesses rainfall extremes and land‐atmosphere interactions across the Volta River Basin, West Africa, using daily CHIRPS precipitation and MODIS‐derived Normalised Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), evapotranspiration (ET), land surface temperature (LST), and Crop Water Stress Index (CWSI) for 2000–2024. ETCCDI‐style indices included consecutive dry days (CDD), consecutive wet days (CWD), Rx1day, Rx5day, and R95p. Trend inference used all 25 annual observations.
