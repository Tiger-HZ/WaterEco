Abstract Background Emergency departments are high-risk, high-workload settings where safety incidents and near misses may occur. However, incident reporting systems do not consistently translate reported events into timely learning and actionable system change. We evaluated a quality improvement initiative that integrated frontline peer review and recurring joint incident meetings into a centrally managed hospital incident reporting system. Methods We conducted a single-center retrospective before-and-after evaluation in an urban tertiary-care emergency department.
