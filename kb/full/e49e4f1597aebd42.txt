ABSTRACT This paper explores the significant advantages of using adaptive Type‐II progressive hybrid censoring in step‐stress accelerated life tests. By implementing this method, researchers can significantly reduce testing time and costs while improving the precision of statistical analysis. The study focuses on ‐level step‐stress accelerated life testing, incorporating adaptive Type‐II progressive hybrid censoring and assuming that test unit lifetimes follow the Chen distribution. In this model, the scale parameter of the Chen failure time distribution is treated as a log‐linear function of the stress level.
