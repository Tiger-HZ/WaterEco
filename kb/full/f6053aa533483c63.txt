Abstract Saltwater extraction is a key strategy of the Salt Interception Schemes (SIS) in Australia's Murray River Basin, aimed at intercepting saline groundwater loads to the river and adjacent floodplains. Despite their widespread application, a general analytical framework is lacking to evaluate how pumping configuration and regional boundary conditions control riparian freshwater lenses, or to predict the maximum saltwater extraction rate that avoids capturing river‐derived fresh groundwater. Here we derive sharp‐interface analytical solutions that quantify pumping‐induced expansion of riparian freshwater lenses and the maximum saltwater extraction rate.
