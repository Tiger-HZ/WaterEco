Abstract Unlike closure mechanisms for laser or EDM machining, pulsed water jet manufacturing does not currently have any. The reason is timing; nothing tracks the chamber resonance quickly enough. Although FFT analysis requires 30–50 ms, control of a HAPOS (hydrodynamic acoustic pulse oscillatory system) operation up to 450 MPa has to be kept below 10 ms of inference budget. The answer is our 12-layer physics-guided CNN (PG-CNN) which reads the resonance frequency f rez directly from a model’s raw pressure signals (100 kHz, Hann-windowed, N = 4096 samples). Training runs in two stages.
