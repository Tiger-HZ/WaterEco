Background Food–drug interactions (FDIs) and drug–nutrient interactions (DNIs) can significantly affect therapeutic outcomes and patient safety. Clinical nutrition specialists play a key role in identifying and managing these interactions; however, their knowledge, attitudes, and practices (KAP) regarding FDIs and DNIs remain insufficiently explored in Saudi Arabia. Methods A cross-sectional study was conducted among 353 licensed clinical nutrition specialists across Saudi Arabia. Data were collected using a validated self-administered questionnaire. Three separate multivariable linear regression models were developed to examine factors associated with knowledge, attitudes, and practices.
