Background: Adequate fixation is an essential pre-analytical step in pleural effusion cytology because it preserves cellular morphology and supports optimal Papanicolaou staining. Although 96% ethanol is commonly used as a wet fixative, 10% formal alcohol may provide an alternative when conventional alcohol fixation is less practical.
