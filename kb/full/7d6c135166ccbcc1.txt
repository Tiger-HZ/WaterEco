Abstract Cyanobacteria play an increasingly important role in wastewater bioremediation and carbon recovery, owing to their ability to assimilate nutrients while converting CO2 into biomass and value-added products. However, despite the exceptionally high quantum efficiency of the photosystems, the overall conversion of absorbed solar energy into biomass remains constrained by multiple photosynthetic and downstream energy-conversion processes.
