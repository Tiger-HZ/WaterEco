ABSTRACT -Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a growing global health threat that extends beyond clinical settings and increasingly involves environmental reservoirs. Wastewater represents a critical yet often overlooked component of AMR surveillance and control, particularly in low-income countries where sanitation infrastructure and environmental monitoring remain limited. This review adopted a narrative approach because of heterogeneity in study designs, environmental matrices, detection methodologies, and policy-oriented and institutional evidence not amenable to quantitative meta-analysis.
