Aims: To evaluate the influence of Integrated Nutrient Management (INM), combining inorganic fertilisers, farmyard manure (FYM), and biofertilisers (Azotobacter and phosphate-solubilising bacteria [PSB]), on the growth, yield attributes, bulb yield, and economic performance of garlic (Allium sativum L.) cv. Yamuna Safed, and to identify the most agronomically and economically viable nutrient combination for the Central Plateau and Hills Region of Satna, Madhya Pradesh. Study Design: The field experiment was laid out in a randomised block design (RBD) with ten treatments (T0–T9), each replicated three times.
