Abstract Benthic diatoms are widely used as bioindicators in freshwater assessment, and diatom-based biomonitoring tools have been applied in China for several decades. However, existing knowledge in China remains fragmented across Chinese and English literature, geographical regions, ecosystem types, and methodological traditions. Here, we conducted a review of 62 studies that used benthic diatoms for freshwater bioassessment in China and summarized their geographical distribution, ecosystem coverage, publication trends, and bioassessment methods, and identified the future research needs in diatom monitoring.
