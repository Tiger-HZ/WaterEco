Background. Fixture congestion is common in soccer and reduces the time available for recovery between matches. Recovery is multidimensional, and physical, neuromuscular, biochemical, and perceptual markers may normalize at different rates. Aim. This narrative review aimed to synthesize evidence on the effects of fixture congestion on post-match recovery kinetics in soccer players. Material and methods. A predefined full-text literature set relevant to fixture congestion, fatigue, post-match recovery, workload, performance, and injury risk was synthesized qualitatively. Original studies formed the primary evidence base, with review articles used for context. Results. Recovery was heterogeneous.
