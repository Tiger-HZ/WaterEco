Micronutrient deficiencies, particularly those of zinc (Zn) and iron (Fe), remain important nutritional concerns and contribute to hidden hunger. Greengram (Vigna radiata L. Wilczek) is a nutrient-rich pulse crop, and improving its Zn and Fe concentrations through agronomic biofortification may enhance its nutritional quality. Foliar and soil applications of Zn and Fe therefore offer practical approaches for increasing micronutrient accumulation in greengram grain.
