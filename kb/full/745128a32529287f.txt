Zebrafish are widely used to study learning and memory. Here, we describe zAcademy, a programmable conditioning arena released in full as open hardware and open software. zAcademy combines a 3D-printed dual-chamber arena, bottom-mounted LED matrices that generate visual patterns under software control, graphite rod electrodes gated by an Arduino-controlled relay, an overhead infrared camera, and a position-based analysis workflow. In one 180-min session, adult zebrafish were classically conditioned to associate a checkerboard light pattern beneath the arena (conditioned stimulus, CS) with a mild shock (unconditioned stimulus, US).
