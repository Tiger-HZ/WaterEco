This paper proposes a multi-task deep learning framework for multi-source engineering time-series prediction, integrating dual-channel temporal modeling and graph structure learning. To model heterogeneous monitoring signals, structural dependencies, and multiple prediction objectives, the framework constructs primary sensor and auxiliary sensor channels to extract dynamic patterns from different observation sources. A Graph Attention Network (GAT) is introduced to capture spatial or functional dependencies among monitored process variables. Then, a cross-channel fusion mechanism integrates temporal and structural representations, while multi-task learning jointly predicts abnormal-state probability, process health index value, and sensor-source deviation.
