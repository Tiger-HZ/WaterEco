This study focused on the development of gluten-free and casein-free diets for the management of nutritional challenges of children with autism in Rivers State. Seven specific objectives guided the study. The study adopted the Research and Development (R and D) and qualitative designs. A gluten-free diet excludes foods that contain gluten, a protein found in wheat, barley, rye, and their derivatives. A casein-free diet, on the other hand, excludes foods containing casein, a protein found in milk and dairy products. Gluten and casein-free foodscrops include rice and rice-based products, such as rice flour and rice cakes, are naturally gluten-free.
