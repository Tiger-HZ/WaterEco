The aim of this paper is to propose a framework addressing Big Data quality aspects in the context of Web data. The identified research gap, based on a review of existing literature, is that Big Data quality is typically evaluated using broad and general dimensions, such as data consistency, without providing detailed methodologies for measurement or specifying metrics suitable for continuous quality monitoring. This paper focuses on challenges identified in case studies involving the use of Web data, particularly in the context of large-scale web scraping, to derive information about enterprise characteristics.
