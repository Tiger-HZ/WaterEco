Abstract Dhaka, home to more than 22.4 million residents, delivers a wide spectrum of ecosystem services through its green spaces and water bodies, yet these systems are being converted to built-up land at an accelerating rate. This study maps, monitors, and values those services using primary and secondary data. Primary data came from a field-based observation protocol, transect assessment, a questionnaire survey of visitors and adjacent residents, key informant interviews, and geospatial recording. Secondary data came from published land cover, remote sensing, water quality, and valuation studies. Surface water fell from 11.82 percent of the city area in 1990 to 5.16 percent in 2021.
