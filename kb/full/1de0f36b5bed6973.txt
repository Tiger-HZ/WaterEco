An experiment was conducted at the Teaching and Research Farm of Akwa Ibom State University, Obio Akpa Campus, Oruk Anam Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, to evaluate the growth, yield, and nutritional quality of orange-fleshed sweet potato (OFSP) varieties (UMUSPO 1 and UMUSPO 3). The study was arranged as a split-plot experiment fitted into a randomised complete block design (RCBD) with three replications. The main plot consisted of the two OFSP varieties, while the subplot comprised five nutrient management treatments: no amendment (CA), 400 kg ha⁻¹ NPK fertiliser (CB), 5 t ha⁻¹ poultry manure + 200 kg ha⁻¹ organomineral fertiliser (RA), 10 t ha⁻¹ poultry manure (RB), and 5 t ha⁻¹ biochar (RC).
