Sugar factories rank among the largest contributors to agro-industrial pollution in freshwater river systems in India and their effluents also come under extremely high biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), total dissolved solids (TDS), suspended solids and heavy metals. all these physicochemical parameters severely degrade the water body and its aquatic biodiversity. In this research, case study of the Chambal River Basin of Rajasthan is selected for study purpose as despite its ecological significance as a National Sanctuary, it is facing the challenge of industrial discharge, especially from sugar factories.
