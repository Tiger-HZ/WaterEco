The increasingly rapid and competitive development of the Indonesian local fashion industry encourages the Erigo brand to win the competition by building a strong brand image and maintaining product quality. This study aims to determine the influence of brand image and product quality on consumer purchasing decisions for Erigo T-shirt products in the DKI Jakarta area. The study uses a quantitative approach with a survey method. The sample consists of 100 respondents selected using a purposive sampling technique with the criteria of having purchased an Erigo T-shirt, aged 14 – 29 years, and domiciled in DKI Jakarta.
