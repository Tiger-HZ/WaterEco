Abstract Background To construct a standard operating procedure (SOP) for bedside nursing handover (BNH) in the emergency observation unit, and to provide theoretical references for clinical nursing handover among emergency nurses. Methods This project integrated literature review and Delphi expert consultation to develop the BNH SOP. A before-after quasi-experimental self-control design was adopted. Nurses working in the emergency observation unit of a Grade A tertiary hospital from March to September 2023 were enrolled as research subjects.
