Acid precipitation has been shown to reduce phosphorus (P) outputs from terrestrial sources. We present field evidence that the in‐lake P cycle may also be disrupted in atmospherically acidified watershed‐lake ecosystems. Plešné Lake in the Bohemian Forest is an acidified forest lake with epilimnetic pH of 4.5–4.9, total P concentration of ∼10 μg L −1 , and pronounced anoxia above the bottom during winter and summer stratification periods.
