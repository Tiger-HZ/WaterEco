Abstract Excessive dissolved nitrogen and phosphorus drive aquatic eutrophication, yet conventional treatments such as biological nitrification–denitrification and chemical precipitation are costly, generate secondary waste, and are not well suited to simultaneous multi-nutrient removal in a single step. Despite growing interest in chitosan-based adsorbents, the competitive adsorption behavior of ammonium nitrogen (NH4+–N), nitrate nitrogen (NO3––N), and phosphate (PO4–P) coexisting in a single matrix has not been systematically evaluated under optimized conditions. This study synthesized nano-chitosan crosslinked with tripolyphosphate (NCS) via ionotropic gelation and evaluated its simultaneous removal of these nutrients from synthetic wet-market wastewater.
