ABSTRACT Organ transplantation is among the most complex domains of modern medicine, requiring the integration of advanced surgical techniques, individualized immunosuppressive therapy, structured professional training, and comprehensive quality assessment systems. As transplantation programs expand globally, ensuring consistency in clinical outcomes and patient safety has become increasingly dependent on the governance structures that regulate these domains. While Western systems emphasize decentralized, profession‐led models of training and quality assurance, Chinese Mainland has developed a centrally coordinated framework integrating regulatory oversight, standardized education, and data‐driven monitoring.
