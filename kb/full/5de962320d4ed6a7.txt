Eight flume experiments were conducted to study the development of bed surface texture and structure in the presence of partial bed material transport. The experiments have two phases, a no‐feed degradational phase followed by a feeding phase. A surface structure of irregular, reticulate stone nets and clusters was developed before sediment feeding commenced. Bed load transport equaled or slightly exceeded the fed supply, except at the highest feed rate. The bed structure was maintained, but bed surface texture fined with increasing sediment load. The two phenomena may coexist because the largest grains on the bed moved only very sporadically.
