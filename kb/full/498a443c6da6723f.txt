Background/Objectives: Early rehabilitation in critically ill patients has been associated with improved physical outcomes. However, evidence regarding the association between occupational therapy and quality of life (QOL) remains limited. This study examined the association between acute-phase occupational therapy and QOL. Methods: Data were obtained from the multicenter prospective EMPICS study. Patients who remained in the intensive care unit (ICU) for at least 48 h were included. QOL was assessed using the EuroQol 5-Dimensions 5-Levels (EQ-5D-5L) at hospital discharge and 90 days after discharge.
