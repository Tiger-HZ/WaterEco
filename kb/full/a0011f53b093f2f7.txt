Abstract The aim of this study was to determine the potential of using biochar derived from Chlorella sp . algal biomass as an effective and environmentally friendly sorbent for the preliminary removal of contaminants present in acidic spent washing baths. The research included characterising the obtained biochars using SEM/EDS, an evaluation of sorption efficiency using solid - phase extraction, and an analysis of sorption kinetics in solutions. The SEM/EDS analysis revealed an increase in the porosity of the materials with rising process temperature, and the highest C + O+N content was observed for biochar produced at 600°C (92.45%).
