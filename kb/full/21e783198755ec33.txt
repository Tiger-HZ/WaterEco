Abstract Coastal sediments are globally significant sources and sinks of greenhouse gases (GHGs), yet their contributions to climate feedbacks of warming and ocean acidification remain uncertain, in part due to limited understanding of short‐term variability. Here, we use a fully factorial laboratory experiment to disentangle how diel light–dark and tidal inundation and exposure interact with warming and elevated p CO 2 to regulate benthic fluxes of CO 2 , CH 4 , and N 2 O in estuarine sediments, alongside concurrent changes in benthic oxygen exchange.
