This study assesses how Scrutinize committee quality, Scrutinizeor Self-Reliance, and Internal Audit weaknesses relate to each other in the Nigerian business environment. Findings indicate a connection, suggesting that a well-qualified and independent Scrutinize committee is linked to better Internal Audits.
