Abstract Reports of declining sperm concentration, count, and motility remain contested amid methodological heterogeneity, geographic bias, and reference-limit changes, and data from the Middle East and North Africa are scarce. We conducted a retrospective single-center cohort study of 3,078 men undergoing first diagnostic semen analysis at the National Fertility Teaching Center, Eastern Libya, between 2016 and 2025/26, with stable personnel, equipment, and protocols. Multivariable regression adjusted for age and semen volume assessed temporal trends, using restricted cubic splines for nonlinearity, robust sensitivity analyses, and Benjamini-Hochberg correction; samples were classified under both WHO 2010 and WHO 2021 reference limits.
