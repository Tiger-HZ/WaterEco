General Background: Pharmaceutical companies have legal obligations to ensure the quality, safety, and proper distribution of medicines delivered to consumers. Specific Background: Drug quality assurance involves production and distribution standards, including Good Manufacturing Practices and Good Distribution Practices, while consumers require legal protection when medicines are defective or do not meet applicable standards. Knowledge Gap: Previous studies discussed pharmaceutical production, regulatory supervision, and drug distribution, but this study specifically examines the legal liability of pharmaceutical companies for drug quality defects throughout the distribution process.
