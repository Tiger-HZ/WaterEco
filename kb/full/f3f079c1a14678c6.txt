Abstract With increasing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, seaweeds have been extensively studied for their potential to mitigate GHG emissions in the rumen. Undaria pinnatifida (UP) is a brown alga widely consumed in Asia, whereas its sporophyll (UPS) is mostly wasted. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of blanching process and supplementation level of UPS on nutrient digestibility, fermentation characteristics, methane emissions, and microbial diversity in the rumen in vitro. UPS was washed at 15 °C (Non-blanched, NBLU) or 70 °C (Blanched, BLU) for 3 minutes. NBLU and BLU was supplemented at 0, 0.25%, 0.5%, and 1% of the basal diet, which was formulated for Hanwoo steers in growing period.
