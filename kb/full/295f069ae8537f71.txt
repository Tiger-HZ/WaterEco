This study decodes the BDS-3 PPP-B2b binary data broadcast by BDS satellites using a self-developed software decoder, RT-B2b (Real-Time PPP-B2b). The orbit, clock corrections, and differential code biases (DCB)—collectively referred to as State Space Representation (SSR) corrections—are extracted from the decoded messages. Subsequently, precise satellite orbits and clock offsets are recovered in real time by integrating the SSR corrections with the broadcast ephemeris. The characteristics and accuracy of the SSR corrections are first evaluated. The performance of real-time PPP is then assessed through both static and kinematic experimental schemes, with particular emphasis on the kinematic positioning of a marine buoy.
