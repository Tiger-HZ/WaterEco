Abstract Tracing jadeite origin is crucial for market regulation and provenance research. Guatemalan jadeite production has increased steadily, and its blue water variety visually resembles that of Myanmar, posing identification challenges. In this study, blue water jadeite samples from both sources were analyzed via Fourier transform infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy, laser Raman spectroscopy, X-ray powder diffraction (XRD), X-ray fluorescence (XRF), and micro-FTIR, thereby systematically comparing their gemological properties, mineral and chemical compositions, microstructures, and structural water contents.
