Abstract Integrated nutrient management (INM) with the application of chemical fertilizers, farmyard manure (FYM), micronutrients and rhizosphere microbial consortia as a seed treatment is a sustainable approach for enhancing crop productivity and nutritional quality. A field experiment was conducted in a randomized complete block design with eight treatments and three replications during the monsoon and winter seasons of 2024-25 at the ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research, Regional Research Station, Dharwad, India, to determine the effects of INM practices on grain and straw yields, nutrient concentration and uptake in a maize‒chickpea sequential cropping system in the Vertisols of southern India.
