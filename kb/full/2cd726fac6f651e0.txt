Acid mine drainage (AMD), acid rock drainage (ARD), and related acidic or metalliferous mine waters remain among the most persistent liabilities of sulfide-bearing mining because oxidation, hydrologic transport, secondary-mineral reactions, and legacy infrastructure can sustain contaminant release for decades after extraction ceases. This critical review synthesizes international case studies published from 2020 through September 2026, emphasizing the causal links among mineralogy, climate, hydrology, mine-waste architecture, hydrochemistry, treatment design, residuals management, and long-term field performance.
