Trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) is an extremely persistent and highly mobile substance that is accumulating in the environment as a result of continued emissions from diverse sources. Yet time trend data for TFA in groundwater remain scarce, despite growing concern about TFA as a persistent groundwater contaminant. We analyzed temporal trends in official Austrian monitoring data from 2018–2019 and 2024, comprising 98 baseline sites and 107 sites in 2024, including 16 sites sampled in both periods. TFA increased at all paired sites; mean concentrations rose from 1.36 to 2.86 µg/L (+110%) and median concentrations from 1.01 to 2.05 µg/L (+103%).
