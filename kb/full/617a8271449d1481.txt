Crop diversification is a key component of sustainable agriculture, as it improves system resilience, enhances resource-use efficiency and minimises the risk of crop failure. A three-year field experiment was conducted at the Agricultural Research Station, Kattuthottam, Thanjavur, India, under randomised block design (RBD) to evaluate the effects of diversified rice-based cropping systems on productivity, profitability and soil fertility. About 10 different cropping systems were evaluated in the study, incorporating a range of crops such as legumes, vegetables, oilseeds, millets, green manures and fodder crops.
