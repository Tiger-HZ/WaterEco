Purpose: to examine the effect of audit fee, audit tenure, and audit rotation on audit quality in infrastructure companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange during the 2020–2023 period. Method: this study uses a quantitative approach and secondary data from the annual reports of 15 infrastructure companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange during 2020–2023, resulting in 60 firm-year observations selected through purposive sampling. Data were analyzed using binary logistic regression in SPSS. Findings: audit fee, audit tenure, and audit rotation do not significantly affect audit quality, either individually or simultaneously.
