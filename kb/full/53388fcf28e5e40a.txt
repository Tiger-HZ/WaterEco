High-resolution images of food tend to exhibit noise, uneven lighting, and varied textures that may impact the accuracy of food quality analysis performed automatically. The traditional bilateral filtering algorithm yields good results but suffers from high computational cost and thus is not recommended for use in real-time processing tasks. This paper suggests a framework for CUDA implementation of bilateral filtering to achieve high-quality enhancement and low computational delay. This work proposes to perform bilateral filtering on the GPU by means of parallel processing in which each CUDA thread would operate independently over image pixels.
