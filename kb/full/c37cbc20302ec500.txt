Background The healthy ocular surface microbiome consists of Actinobacteria , Proteobacteria , and Firmicutes phyla, with Corynebacterium , Propionibacterium , and Staphylococcus as dominant genera. This microbial environment is essential for maintaining immune homeostasis and barrier integrity. Objective To synthesize existing evidence on how periocular skincare and cosmetic products affect the ocular surface microbiome, tear film stability, and downstream ocular surface disease. Methods A systematic literature search was conducted across PubMed, Cochrane Library, Embase, Web of Science, and Scopus using Boolean MeSH term strategies.
