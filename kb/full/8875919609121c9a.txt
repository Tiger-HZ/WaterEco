Successfully addressing and resolving water challenges, in a global context where the vectors of population growth, urbanisation, climate change and globalisation are at work, is a key element to foster sustainable development and achieve an improvement in the quality of life of societies and people. It is essential that water is on the political agenda and that many actors play their role properly, with the appropriate governance. The Sustainable Development Goals are not always synergistic among them, and SDG 6 on water interacts with a number of other SDGs.
