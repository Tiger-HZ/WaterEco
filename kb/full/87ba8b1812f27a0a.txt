Aiming to explore the phytoremediation effect and potential adaptability of wetland plant combination under the dual background of salinization and eutrophication, three kinds of wetland plants including Iris pseudacorus, Nymphaea tetragon and Ceratophyllum demersum were selected, cultivated in different eutrophicated water, consisting of the control group (T1, 0 g· L-1), low-salt group (T2, 2 g· L-1) and high-salt group (T3, 4 g· L-1) for 30 d, in order to measure the changes of water remediation effect, growth morphology and physiological characteristics.
