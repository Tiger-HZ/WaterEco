Moisture monitoring in historic masonry requires non-destructive solutions that can be adapted to the characteristics and value of the asset, the spatial extent of the phenomenon, and the need for repeated measurements over time. This work presents an integrated reflectometric methodology that combines accurate Frequency-Domain Reflectometry (FDR) characterization with the development and validation of a custom-developed, compact, portable, low-cost, and potentially scalable Time-Domain Reflectometry (TDR) prototype. Resonant patch antennas operating at different frequencies were used with a Vector Network Analyzer (VNA) to characterize Lecce stone, Carparo, and plaster applied to Carparo during controlled drying.
