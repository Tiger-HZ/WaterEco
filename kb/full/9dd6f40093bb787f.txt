Tailings ponds associated with the beneficiation of low-grade sulphide ores are a recognized source of acid mine drainage (AMD) and heavy metal contamination in receiving watercourses. This study assesses the physicochemical quality of process water stored in the Valea Șesei tailings pond, which receives flotation tailings from the Roșia Poieni porphyry copper deposit (Apuseni Mountains, Romania), and evaluates the impact of its discharge on the Arieș River. Ten water samples were analysed for pH, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, conductivity, total dissolved solids, salinity, hardness, and dissolved copper and iron (ICP-OES).
