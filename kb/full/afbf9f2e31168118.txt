Against the backdrop of observed climate changes, there is growing interest in the extreme course of processes and phenomena in the natural environment. This trend is also being explored in relation to various components of the hydrosphere, in which one fundamental characteristic is water temperature. The aim of this study was to assess the temporal and spatial variability of heat waves occurring in lakes. The research material included daily water temperatures from 1964 to 2023 for nine temperate-zone lakes with diverse morphometric parameters. The study found that the duration, cumulative intensity, and maximum intensity of heat waves increased over the last six decades.
