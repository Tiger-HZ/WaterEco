Climate change and global warming have significantly impacted the water cycle and water resources in the Himalayan region. This study focused on the effects of climate change and human intervention on water resources in the Ladakh region over the past decades. Changes in snowfall and glacial patterns, rapid runoff, and erratic rainfall due to changes in the snow season and an increase in temperature have resulted in water scarcity in this area. Primary and secondary data on groundwater status, temperature changes, precipitation, glacial retreat, and increased water demand were collected and analyzed.
