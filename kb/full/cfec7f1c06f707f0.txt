The fixed-dose combination of candesartan cilexetil/hydrochlorothiazide (16/12.5 mg) remains a first-line option in managing essential hypertension, owing to its complementary antihypertensive mechanisms. This study evaluated the physicochemical quality and drug content of three commercially available brands of this combination marketed in Tripoli, Libya. Three brands, coded A (Atacand Plus®, Sweden; innovator/reference), B (Atacand Plus, Turkey), and C (Hytacand, Algeria), were evaluated for weight variation, friability, hardness, thickness/diameter uniformity, and disintegration time in accordance with USP procedures.
