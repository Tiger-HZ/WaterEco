Abstract Background Artificial intelligence (AI) systems for gastrointestinal (GI) endoscopy have advanced rapidly in recent years, driven by deep learning architectures and availability of large image and video datasets. These developments have enabled automated polyp detection, lesion characterization, and disease activity scoring. Contemporary studies increasingly report models trained on hundreds of thousands or even millions of endoscopic images and video frames. However, dataset scale alone does not guarantee robustness or clinical generalizability. Methods In this narrative review, we examine the characteristics of datasets used to develop AI systems in GI endoscopy and discuss how dataset structure influences model performance and generalizability.
