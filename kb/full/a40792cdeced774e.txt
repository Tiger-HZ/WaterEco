A field experiment was carried out during kharif season of 2022–23 and 2023–24 at the Agronomy Main Research Farm, CoA, OUAT, Bhubaneswar. The experiment was laid out in randomised block design (RBD) with 12 treatments and replicated thrice. The rice variety “Kalinga Dhan 1201” was transplanted in kharif season with 12 treatments. The pooled analysis results revealed that application of T9 resulted in maximum plant height (118.4 cm), tillers m-2 (372), dry matter accumulation (1207 g m-2) at harvest. The maximum leaf area index (4.91), chlorophyll index readings (53.8) and green seeker readings (0.82) were recorded at 75 days of transplanting.
