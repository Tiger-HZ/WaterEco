Abstract Introduction Self-warming chemical blankets are widely deployed in military, prehospital, and hospital settings as a power-independent warming solution. Despite widespread use, no harmonized safety standard governing surface temperature limits for this device class is known to exist, and independent bench-testing data are sparse. This study aimed to characterize the thermal performance and safety profiles of commercially available self-warming chemical blankets and evaluate their suitability for use in military, prehospital, and perioperative contexts. Materials and Methods Four self-warming chemical blanket devices were subjected to standardized laboratory bench testing following the IEC 60601-2-35:2021 framework as an analogical reference standard.
