Mesquite (Prosopis juliflora) invasion has become a major ecological and socio-economic challenge in arid and semi-arid ecosystems due to its rapid spread through interconnected land, water, and animal-mediated dispersal pathways. Unlike previous studies that primarily considered isolated invasion mechanisms or single control interventions, this study develops an integrated mathematical model combining ecological transmission dynamics, sensitivity analysis, optimal control, and cost-effectiveness evaluation. Model parameterization was based on field observations conducted in Kahe Ward and published ecological studies.
