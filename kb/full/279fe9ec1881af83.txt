Fodder availability remains inadequate to meet the increasing demand for quality livestock feed in India. Fodder jowar (Sorghum bicolor (L.) Moench) is an important forage crop because of its high biomass production, drought tolerance and adaptability to diverse agro-climatic conditions. Optimising varietal selection and nitrogen management is therefore essential for improving fodder productivity, nutrient uptake and post-harvest soil nutrient status under rainfed conditions.
