Rainfall and fluctuations in reservoir water levels are critical external factors influencing the stability of landslides along reservoir banks. This study investigates the Yetan Primary School landslide, which serves as a representative example of a metamorphic rock landslide influenced by reservoir conditions in Shiyan City. We quantitatively investigate the evolutionary characteristics of slope seepage and stress responses by integrating GNSS field monitoring data (2021–2025) with FLAC3D numerical simulations. The analysis encompasses five distinct working conditions, including groundwater level rises of 1 m, 3 m, and 5 m, as well as periodic fluctuations in reservoir water levels ranging from 236 m to 242 m.
