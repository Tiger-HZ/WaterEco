Abstract Laboratory experiments examined the impact of model vegetation on wave‐driven resuspension. Model canopies were constructed from cylinders with three diameters ( d = 0.32, 0.64, and 1.26 cm) and 12 densities (cylinders/m 2 ) up to a solid volume fraction ( ϕ ) of 10%. The sediment bed consisted of spherical grains with d 50 = 85 μm. For each experiment, the wave velocity was gradually adjusted by increasing the amplitude of 2‐s waves in a stepwise fashion. A Nortek Vectrino sampled the velocity at z = 1.3 cm above the bed. The critical wave orbital velocity for resuspension was inferred from records of suspended sediment concentration (measured with optical backscatter) as a function of wave velocity.
