Abstract While the global geological availability of critical minerals is likely sufficient to support energy transition efforts, geographically imbalanced energy transition mineral production and consumption raise concerns around supply chain disruption and import dependence. While regions like Europe and the United States are actively studying such vulnerabilities, they remain relatively underexplored for emerging economies. This study uses a new, customizable mineral demand model for a wide set of commodities to estimate future energy transition mineral demand in the power generation and transport sectors for India, Bangladesh, and countries in ASEAN. Results indicate that ASEAN and South Asia may become significant consumers by 2050 on par with developed economies like the United State
