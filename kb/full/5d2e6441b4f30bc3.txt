This study produced and determined the nutritional composition of snacks made from local foodstuffs (pumpkin, sweet potatoes, dates, tiger nuts, native bananas) and the implications for sustainable health amongst the elderly in Imo North, Imo State. There were five specific objectives and hypotheses. The study adopted the phases of research and development (R and D) cycle proposed by Gall, Gall and Borg. The raw materials which were used in this study (pumpkin, sweet potatoes, dates, tiger nuts and native bananas). The Names of the snacks are Potakin Snacks (made from pumpkin flour, sweet potato flour and dates flour: aka PTS); Spotta Chips (made from sweet potatoes flour and tiger nuts flour: aka SPC) and Native Chips (made from only unripe native bananas flour: aka NBC).
