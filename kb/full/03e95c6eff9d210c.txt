Relevance. Digital measurement systems in combat sports enable quantitative assessment of contact parameters, force and the spatial structure of impact. The microstructure of the glove–target contact patch remains insufficiently characterized in the «strike force» discipline. Aim. To evaluate a sensor-equipped boxing glove for recording the spatial distribution of contact load during maximal-intensity hook punches and to characterize between-athlete variability. Methods. This was an observational, instrumental, cross-sectional study. Peak punch force was recorded with the CROCUS system (3500 Hz), whereas contact-load distribution was recorded with a wearable 8×8 piezoresistive matrix integrated into a 12 oz GREEN HILL glove.
