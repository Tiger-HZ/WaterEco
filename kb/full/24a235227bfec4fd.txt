Abstract Transportation is California’s largest source of greenhouse gas emissions, and vehicle electrification alone does not reduce vehicle-miles traveled (VMT). This paper asks whether Class 3 e-bikes—pedal-assisted to 28 mph—could replace some of the intermediate-distance commutes that conventional bicycles rarely serve. Using 2024 LOCUS mobility data for Los Angeles County, we define an addressable commute band (ACB) as weekday peak-period Motorized travel of 5–15 miles one way. This market contains 11.98 million passenger-miles per weekday, while conventional bicycles account for only about 0.3% of passenger-miles under the same filters.
