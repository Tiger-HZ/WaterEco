This article discusses the development of software for the analysis, assessment and forecasting of water bodies eutrophication using the Python programming language. Eutrophication is a process characterized by excessive accumulation of nutrients in water bodies, which leads to deterioration in water quality and negative consequences for the ecosystem. As part of the work, software for a personal computer was developed that uses machine learning and statistical analysis methods to process data on the chemical composition of water: the content of minerals, hydrogen pH and temperature, which affects the eutrophication of water bodies.
