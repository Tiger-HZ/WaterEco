Flooded rice paddies emit large quantities of greenhouse gases, and biochar amendment serves as a promising strategy to conserve water, sequester carbon, mitigate emissions and stabilize crop yields. Nevertheless, the DNDC (DeNitrification-DeComposition) model has no specific biochar module and fails to precisely quantify coupled water–carbon–nitrogen cycles in biochar-amended paddies. Using two-season field observations from the Jianghan Plain, this study constructs the Biochar-DNDC model by introducing biochar pH and substrate adsorption parameters into the original organic fertilizer module and coupling a double-tank exponential decay model.
