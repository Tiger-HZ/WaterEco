Abstract Anion exchange membranes (AEMs) for water electrolysis may suffer from oxidative degradation under high-potential operating conditions due to the attack of reactive oxygen species (ROS). Addressing the oxidative durability challenge for high-performancewater electrolysis remains imperative. Here, we report the preparation of AEMs with intrinsic antioxidant capability by directly copolymerization of nitroxide radicals into poly(aryl piperidinium) copolymer backbones. A series of TEMPO-containing AEMs (TEMPO-PAP-x%) are synthesized by incorporation of 4-oxo-2,2,6,6-tetramethylpiperidine-1-oxyl (4-oxo-TEMPO).
