The demand to enhance irrigation system performance through modernization and more effective water management has grown due to climate change and growing water constraint. It is necessary to use objective and standardized performance assessment techniques to evaluate the effectiveness of irrigation modernization. Using globally accepted benchmarking indicators, this study evaluated the effect of irrigation modernization on water delivery performance in the Uluborlu Irrigation Scheme, Türkiye. The project was first put into service in 1984 as a conventional open-channel, gravity-fed irrigation system. Since 2012, it has been updated and run as a pressurized, piped irrigation network.
