ABSTRACT Headwaters often contribute disproportionately to network‐scale benthic biodiversity as differences in community composition between separate headwater streams (β‐diversity) can be high although local (α) diversity may be low. Most research on stream β‐diversity has adopted a spatial perspective, whereas temporal β‐diversity remains largely uncharacterised, but may be highest in headwaters, then decreasing with distance downstream. We studied the spatial α and β‐diversity of benthic invertebrate communities in five subarctic stream catchments in a single sampling year, with four mainstem and four headwater sites each and temporal β‐diversity over five years in one stream catchment.
