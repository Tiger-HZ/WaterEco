A field experiment was conducted during 2023-2024 at the Orchard, Department of Horticulture, Faculty of Agriculture, Annamalai University, Annamalai Nagar, Chidambaram, Tamil Nadu, to evaluate the synergistic effects of plant growth regulators and micronutrients on the growth, flowering, yield and fruit quality of watermelon (Citrullus lanatus) cv. Arka Shyama. The experiment comprised fourteen treatments laid out in a Randomised Block Design with three replications. The treatments included the recommended dose of fertilisers with farmyard manure, either alone or in combination with foliar application of Ethrel, Brassinosteroid and a micronutrient mixture at different concentrations.
