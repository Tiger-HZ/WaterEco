We present XAS3DLive , an integrated platform for X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) analysis that unifies machine-learning-driven XAS simulation, 3D structure visualization and editing, and experimental XAS fitting into a single interactive environment. The platform enables 3D structure visualization and manipulation, spectral analysis, and structure fitting to be performed by a web browser. Specifically, the machine-learning-based model enables rapid XAS simulation of spectral changes in response to structural variations; the 3D structure visualization and editing supports intuitive, atomic-level manipulation and precise adjustments; the global optimization algorithm helps to achieve automatically a detailed 3D structure of a material in an experimental XAS fitting.
