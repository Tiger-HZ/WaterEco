Uneven distributions of water resources upstream and downstream in the Yellow River Basin, along with outdated measures for water resource management and protection, have led to severe water resource conflicts. The prerequisite for using a graph model for conflict resolution (GMCR) to solve water resource conflicts in the Yellow River Basin is obtaining a true and reliable preference ranking for each decision-maker (DM), which can be used to carry out conflict analysis. However, the traditional preference acquisition method of GMCR only considers decision situations where DMs have simple preferences.
