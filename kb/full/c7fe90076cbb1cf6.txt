Carbon escape, or the release of carbon dioxide (CO₂) and other greenhouse gases (GHGs) into the atmosphere, contributes significantly to climate change, with localized impacts in regions like Kogi State, Nigeria. This review examines the sources of carbon emissions in Kogi State, their environmental and socio-economic effects, and potential mitigation strategies. Key sources include agricultural practices, deforestation, industrial activities, and transportation. The effects are evident in altered rainfall patterns, increased temperatures, and impacts on agriculture, such as cassava processing.
