Bread is one of the staple foods most widely consumed around the world, including in Nigeria. The aim of the study was to assess bacteriological quality and detect residual potassium bromate of some bread sold in Fagge Local Government area Kano State. Bacteriological quality assessment was conducted using standard microbiological techniques. Spectrophotometric method was employed for determination of residual bromate in the bread samples. Bacteriological quality assessment of bread showed that, the bread consumed in Kano state are within acceptable microbial loads of 0 - 103 cfu/g and 105 cfu/g according to international commission for microbiological specification food (ICMSF, 2016) and food and Agricultural organization (FAO, 2019) standard limit respectively.
