One of the key issues in sustainable grassland management is ensuring adequate nutrient replenishment in an environmentally friendly manner. The aim of this study was to investigate the effects of different doses of organic manure and wood ash on the productivity and botanical composition of a saline grassland. During the experiments, we measured soil pH, electrical conductivity, moisture content, CO₂ emissions, as well as the green biomass and species composition of the plant community. Based on the experiments, manure had a positive effect primarily on increasing biomass yield, while wood ash had a positive effect on reducing soil acidity.
