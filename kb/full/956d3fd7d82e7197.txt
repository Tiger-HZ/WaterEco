The prediction of mechanical properties at the molecular scale relies on matrix-based approaches, such as the diagonalization of large Hessian matrices in elastic network models (ENMs) or molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. These methods exhibit O(N3) computational complexity and map molecular connectivity to macroscopic stiffness through numerical operations that do not explicitly resolve load-transmission pathways. Here, we present 2 ElastoGraph, an open-source Python framework that shifts the analytical focus from numerical prediction to the topological mapping of elastic paths—the specific routes through which mechanical force is transmitted between terminal vertices.
