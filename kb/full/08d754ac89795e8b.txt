In this work, a spectrophotometric method is proposed for Bisphenol F (BPF) tracking in environmental water system, which is based on targeted chemical derivatization. The practical aspects of the synthesis, diazotization and controlled coupling of sulfanilic acid to BPF in alkaline media to produce the highly stable dark-red azo chromophore were successfully completed. In the lab, optimal reaction conditions were determined and the reaction conditions were maintained at 50 °C for 50 minutes to obtain a peak absorbance λmax at 431 nm. This binding ratio was confirmed by the stoichiometric testing. A high sensitivity (LOD: 0.0115 µg/mL) and an excellent linear working range (1.0-12.0 µg/mL with an R2 = 0.997) were found.
