Performance of different Lettuce (Lactuca sativa L.) 
cultivars in Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) system of 
hydroponics 
 
Vaibhav Vishnu Jagtap, Ravindra D Pawar, Shreyas M Honrao, Sima A 
Sarvade, Mayuri R Kharade, RA Karande, US Shinde and DS Podar 
 
DOI: https://www.doi.org/10.33545/26174693.2026.v10.i9Sj.9909 
 
Abstract 
Aims: To evaluate quality performance of different lettuce cultivars under NFT hydroponic conditions 
Study design: Completely Randomized Design 
Place and Duration of Study: Hydroponics unit , RCSM Govt. College of Agriculture, Kolhapur, 
Maharashtra between December, 2025 and January, 2026. 
Methodology: The treatments comprised of T1: Butterhead Green, T 2: Batavia Green, T 3: Catalogna 
Green, T4: Lolo Bionda, T5: Lolo Rossa, T6: Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red, T 7: Romain Little Gem, T 8: 
Romain Paris Island Cos, T 9: Green Coral and T 10: Phule Padma. Lettuce was transplanted on 10 th 
December, 2025 and Sensory and quality parameters were recorded after harvesting. 
Results: The results revealed that lettuce cultivars significantly influenced the sensory and quality 
attributes under NFT hydroponic conditions. Romain Paris Island Cos recorded the highest overall 
acceptability (7.90) and crispness (8.71), while Green Coral recorded the highest firmness (8.43). For 
visual appearance and colour, Lolo Rossa (7.86) and Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red (7.71) were superior 
compared to the other cultivars. Among biochemical parameters, Catalogna Green recorded the highest 
chlorophyll content (1.29 mg g⁻¹) and total potassium content (2.26%). Similarly, Red Salad Bowl 
Oakleaf Red recorded the m aximum moisture content (95.68%), whereas Romain Little Gem recorded 
the minimum potassium (1.84%) and moisture (93.66%) content. 
Conclusion: Catalogna Green proved to be the most effective cultivar for major biochemical and 
quality attributes, recording t he highest chlorophyll and total potassium contents, while Red Salad 
Bowl Oakleaf Red recorded the highest moisture content. Romain Paris Island Cos was superior for 
overall acceptability and crispness, whereas Green Coral recorded the highest firmness. Lolo Rossa and 
Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red were superior for visual appearance and colour. Overall, cultivar 
selection effectively influenced the sensory and biochemical quality of lettuce under NFT hydroponic 
conditions. 
 
Keywords: Lettuce, sensory, biochemical, NFT hydroponics 
 
1. Introduction 
Lettuce (Lactuca sativa L.), a leafy vegetable crop, is widely cultivated because of its high 
market demand, short growth cycle and suitability for year-round production under protected 
conditions. It is considered an important vegetable crop due to its nutritional value and 
consumption as a fresh leafy vegetable (Kim et al. 2016) [7]. Lettuce responds exceptionally 
well to hydroponic cultivation because of its shallow root system and short crop duration, 
making it suitable for intensive production systems. Hydroponic cultivation als o provides 
opportunities for higher productivity, improved produce quality and efficient utilization of 
water and fertilizers (Martinez 2016; Khan 2018) [6, 9]. 
Hydroponics is a soilless production system in which essential macro and micronutrients are 
supplied directly to the root zone through nutrient solutions. The importance of hydroponics 
has increased due to rapid urbanization, climate change, shrinking cultivable land and 
increasing pressure on freshwater resources. It enables efficient water use, higher 
productivity per unit area, reduced dependence on soil quality and year -round production 
under protected environments (Delaide et al. 2016; Pantoja Neto et al. 2016) [2, 11]. Among 
different hydroponic systems, the Nutrient Film Technique (NFT) is widely adopted fo r 
lettuce cultivation, in which a thin film of nutrient solution continuously flows through

Hydroponic lettuce p roduction offers several advantages 
over conventional cultivation, including reduced incidence 
of soil -borne diseases, improved yield, enhanced visual 
quality, longer shelf life and reduced use of water and 
fertilizers (Khan 2018; Sambo et al. 2019) [6, 15]. Sever al 
studies have demonstrated superior growth rate, early yield, 
total yield and quality characteristics of lettuce cultivated 
under NFT systems compared with conventional soil 
cultivation and other soilless systems (El -Shinawy and 
Gawish 2006; Kim et al. 2016) [7, 14]. However, productivity 
and quality under NFT systems are strongly influenced by 
nutrient solution composition and management practices. 
Despite the growing popularity of NFT systems, 
identification of cultivars specifically adapted to hydroponic 
environments remains a major constraint. Cultivar 
performance under open -field conditions cannot be directly 
extrapolated to hydroponic systems because environmental 
conditions and nutrient dynamics differ substantially 
between production methods. Informatio n regarding the 
comparative performance of lettuce cultivars under NFT -
based hydroponic systems remains limited in India. 
Therefore, systematic evaluation of available lettuce 
cultivars is required to identify those best suited for 
hydroponic production un der protected cultivation 
conditions. The present investigation was undertaken to 
study the quality performance of different lettuce cultivars 
under NFT-based hydroponic conditions. 
 
2. Material and Methods 
The present investigation was conducted during the w inter 
season from December, 2025 to January , 2026 at the 
Hydroponics Unit, Instructional -cum-Research Farm of the 
Horticulture Section, Rajarshee Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj 
Government College of Agriculture, Kolhapur, Maharashtra 
(16°68′ N latitude and 74°2 6′ E longitude) situated at an 
altitude of 546 m above mean sea level. The experimental 
site falls under the Sub-montane zone of Maharashtra, with 
the temperature inside the hydroponics unit maintained 
between 26 and 28 °C and relative humidity between 60 and 
70 per cent using a sensor -based automatic climate control 
system. The experiment comprised ten lettuce cultivars with 
three replications laid out in a Completely Randomized 
Design (CRD). The cultivars included Butterhead Green, 
Batavia Green, Catalogna Green, Lolo Bionda, Lolo Rossa, 
Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red, Romain Little Gem, Romain 
Paris Island Cos, Green Coral and Phule Padma. Healthy 
seedlings were raised in pro -trays using cocopeat and peat 
moss in 1:1 ratio and shifted into the NFT system on 10 th 
December 2025 at 30 days after sowing, maintaining a 
spacing of 15 cm × 20 cm. The NFT channels were supplied 
with a continuous film of nutrient solution to provide water 
and essential nutrients directly to the root zone. Growth, 
yield, sensory and bioc hemical parameters were recorded at 
appropriate stages, and the experimental data were 
statistically analyzed following the procedure described by 
Panse and Sukhatme (1985) [10] under uniform protected 
environmental and management conditions.

3. Results and Discussion 
3.1 Sensory parameters 
Sensory evaluation of lettuce cultivars grown under NFT 
hydroponic system was conducted with the help of a seven -
member panel employing the nine -point h edonic scale 
developed by Amerine et al. (1965) [1]. The samples were 
evaluated for important quality attributes viz., firmness, 
crispness, colour and overall acceptability. The average 
scores for organoleptic evaluation assigned by the panelists 
were calculated which indicated that different cultivars had 
a marked influence on the sensory attributes like firmness, 
crispness, colour and overall acceptability of the leaves. 
 
3.1.1 Firmness 
The data pertaining to firmness at harvest of different lettuce 
cultivars is presented in the table 2. With respect to 
firmness, Green Coral obtained the maximum mean score of 
8.43, while the sensory evaluation indicated that the 
remaining cultivars recorded comparatively lower scores. In 
support of this Pooja Dhikale (2022) and Dhumal (2024) [3, 
14]. 
 
3.1.2 Crispness 
The effect of different cultivars on crispiness at harvest in 
detail is given in table 2. Regarding crispness, the highest 
mean score (8.71) was no ted in Romain Paris Island Cos, 
whereas the remaining cultivars recorded comparatively 
lower scores. In support of this Pooja Dhikale (2022) and 
Dhumal (2024) [3, 14]. 
 
3.1.3 Colour 
The influence of different cultivars on colour of lettuce at 
harvest is present ed in table 2. For colour, Lolo Rossa 
recorded the highest mean score of 7.86, followed by Red 
Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red (7.71), indicating better visual 
appeal. Among the cultivars, Lolo Rossa and Red Salad 
Bowl Oakleaf Red achieved higher scores for visual 
appearance and colour, reflecting better consumer 
preference. On the other hand, the remaining cultivars 
recorded comparatively lower scores. These findings 
indicate that cultivar selection significantly influenced the 
visual quality and colour characteris tics of lettuce leaves 
under NFT hydroponic conditions. In support of this Pooja 
Dhikale (2022) and Dhumal (2024) [3, 14]. 
 
3.1.4 Overall Acceptability 
The influence of different cultivars on overall acceptability 
of lettuce at harvest is presented in Table 2. For overall 
acceptability, Romain Paris Island Cos recorded the highest 
mean score of 7.90, followed by Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf 
Red (6.71) and Green Coral (6.67), indicating better

Biochemical parameters 
Biochemical constituents are important quality attributes in 
lettuce tissues involved in various physiological processes 
that support normal plant growth and development. The 
results of the present investigation showed that different 
lettuce cul tivars significantly influenced the biochemical 
composition, particularly chlorophyll, potassium and 
moisture content under NFT hydroponic conditions. 
 
Total Potassium Content (%) 
The data pertaining to total potassium content among 
different lettuce cult ivars is presented in Table 3. The 
potassium content among the cultivars varied between 1.84 
and 2.26 per cent . Among the cultivars, Catalogna Green 
registered the maximum potassium content of 2.26 per cent 
and remained statistically comparable with Green Coral 
(2.20%), Romain Paris Island Cos (2.18%), Phule Padma 
(2.12%) and Red Salad Bowl Oakleaf Red (2.09%). The 
minimum potassium content of 1.84 per cent was noticed in 
Romain Little Gem and was statistically comparable with 
Lolo Rossa (1.87%), Batavia Gr een (1.96%) and Lolo 
Bionda (1.98%). The higher potassium content in Catalogna 
Green may be attributed to differences in genetic 
constitution, nutrient absorption, translocation and 
utilization efficiency under the NFT hydroponic system. In 
support of this , Pineda et al. (2018); Pooja Dhikale (2022); 
Grzegorzewska et al. (2023); Pastor -Arbulú et al. (2025) 
and Dhumal (2025) [3, 5, 12, 13, 14] reported similar variations in 
potassium content in lettuce. 
 
Total Chlorophyll Content 
The data pertaining to total chlorophyll content among 
different lettuce cult ivars is presented in Table 3. The 
chlorophyll content among the cultivars varied between 0.61 
and 1.29 mg g⁻¹. Among the cultivars, Catalogna Green 
registered the maximum total chlorophyll content of 1.29 
mg g⁻¹, whereas Green Coral recorded the minimum 
chlorophyll content of 0.61 mg g⁻¹. The higher chlorophyll 
content in Catalogna Green might be attributed to 
differences in genetic constitution, pigment synthesis 
efficiency and adaptability to the environmental conditions 
maintained under the NFT hydroponic system. Moreover, 
nutrient availability and root zone conditions might have 
also influenced chlorophyll biosynthesis and accumulation 
in the leaves. In support of this, Mampholo et al. (2016); 
Sapkota et al. (2019); Santos et al. (2021); Thomas et al. 
(2021); Pooja Dhikale (2022); Grzegorzewska et al. (2023); 
Singh et al. (2023) and Dhumal (2025) [3, 8, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19] 
reported similar variations in chlorophyll content in lettuce. 
 
Moisture Content 
The data pertaining to moisture content among different 
lettuce cult ivars is presented in Table 3. The moisture 
content among the cultivars varied from 93.66 to 95.68 per 
cent. However, the effect of different cultivars on moisture 
content was found to be non -significant. Red Salad Bowl 
Oakleaf Red re corded the maximum moisture content 
(95.68%), followed by Lolo Bionda (95.65%) and 
Butterhead Green (95.59%), whereas Romain Little Gem 
recorded the minimum moisture content (93.66%). The 
variation in moisture content may be attributed to 
differences in cu ltivar characteristics and water 
accumulation in leaf tissues under the NFT hydroponic 
system.

Conclusion 
Different lettuce cultivars significantly influenced the 
sensory and biochemical quality attributes under NFT 
hydroponic conditions, with Romain Paris Island Cos (7.91) 
producing the best overall performance in terms of overall 
acceptability and crispness, while Green Coral recorded the 
highest firmness. Additionally, Lolo Rossa and Red Salad 
Bowl Oakleaf Red were superior for visual appearance and 
colour, whereas Catalogna Green recorded the highest 
chlorophyll and total potassium contents. Red Salad Bowl 
Oakleaf Red recorded the highest moisture content.
