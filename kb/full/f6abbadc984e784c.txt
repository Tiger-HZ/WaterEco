Abstract The study assessed Heavy Metal Concentrations and the current pollution status of Nworie River in Imo State, Nigeria. Water sample were collected from the river and analysed for heavy metals using Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer. The level of heavy metal pollution was assessed using single pollution indices; Contamination factor (C f ) and Ecological risk factor (Er). The findings from this work was used to checkmate the previous works done on this river on heavy metal pollution level. Cadmium (0.014mg/l), Mercury (0.001mg/l) and Chromium (0.03mg/l) were all found to be below WHO/FMEnv standard for good water. Lead (0.05mg/l) and iron (0.71mg/l) were found to be above the standard value and this attributed to the littered presence of dredging material found along the river ban
