Abstract The River Wave Concept is a theoretical interpretation of river ecosystem processes that links flow to multiple river ecosystem concepts and contextualizes how a river functions with or without anthropogenic impacts. Here we propose a framework that can map the River Wave Concept to ecosystem concepts and wave metrics using widely available flow data from gauging station networks. The framework can produce a three-dimensional River Wave visualization, a time series of ecosystem process changes over time and wave metrics that can be related to organism patterns across space.
