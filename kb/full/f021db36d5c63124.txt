This study evaluated the nutrient composition of raw fresh milk and overnight raw milk from smallholder pastoral dairy farmers in selected local government areas of Taraba State, Nigeria. Eighty milk samples were randomly collected from lactating cows in good condition across four locations: Ardo Kola, Jalingo, Bali, and Gassol. Key parameters analyzed included protein, fat, solids non-fat (SNF), lactose, salt content, density, temperature, and freezing point using standard analytical methods. Results revealed significant variations (P &lt; 0.05) in nutrient composition of fresh milk across locations.
