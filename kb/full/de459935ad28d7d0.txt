This dataset contains measurements of seagrass tissue carbon (%C), nitrogen (%N), phosphorus (%P), carbon and nitrogen stable isotope composition (d13C, d15N), and aboveground biomass collected from nine seagrass meadows across Northern Ireland during 2024. Sites include intertidal and subtidal meadows dominated by <em>Zostera marina</em> and <em>Zostera noltii</em>. Samples were collected to assess nutrient exposure, eutrophication status, and ecological condition of seagrass meadows and were used in the study "Revised nitrogen benchmarks identify a critical transition zone in seagrass meadow degradation".
