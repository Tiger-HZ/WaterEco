Abstract While exposure to PPAR active compounds can lead to various adverse health effects, studies on the environmental contaminants and their transformation products with PPAR activity remain limited. This study employed PPARγ protein affinity selection-mass spectrometry (AS-MS) to comprehensively identify and prioritize PPARγ-active contaminants in the Yangtze and Yellow Rivers, key drinking water sources in China.
