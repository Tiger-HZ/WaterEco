The present investigation titled “Integrated Nutrient Management (INM) on Morphological Attributes of Black gram Vigna mungo L. (Hepper)” in the Malwa Region (M.P.). The present experiment was conducted at Crop Research Cafeteria, under Mandsaur University, Mandsaur (Madhya Pradesh). Mandsaur (Madhya Pradesh) which is situated at latitude 240C 4’36.61’’N, longitude 7504’9.46’’ E and at an altitude of 442.16 meters above the mean sea level. A randomized block design with 8 treatments and 3 replications was used, consisting of 24 plots measuring 3 × 3 m, with 30 × 30 cm row and plant spacing.
