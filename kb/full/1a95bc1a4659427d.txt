Water pollution is defined as any significant alteration in the physical, chemical, or biological quality of water caused directly or indirectly by waste discharge and various anthropogenic activities. Such alterations may deteriorate water quality, making it unsuitable for potable water, domestic use, irrigation, and the sustenance of aquatic life. Water is not pure in a chemical term. In a relatively pristine region, rainwater primarily contains dissolved CO₂, O₂ and N₂. It may also carry suspended dust or other particles acquired during its passage through the atmosphere. The study aims to assess seasonal changes in the physico-chemical characteristics of the Yamuna River at Firozabad and Etawah, Uttar Pradesh, India, during the before and after monsoon periods of 2024.
