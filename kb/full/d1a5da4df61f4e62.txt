This study addresses the underrepresentation of empirical research on female entrepreneurship in informal urban settlements in the Western Cape, South Africa. The objective of this research is to explore the entrepreneurial challenges and opportunities encountered by women managing businesses in the Masiphumelele informal settlement in Cape Town and to establish a context-specific multi-pillar empowerment framework that enhances the viability of their businesses. The research adopted a qualitative, interpretivist research design that entailed carrying out semi-structured interviews with 17 female entrepreneurs recruited through purposive and convenience sampling.
