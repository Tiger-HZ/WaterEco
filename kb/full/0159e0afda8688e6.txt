Abstract The Brantas River is an important freshwater resource supporting domestic, agricultural, industrial, and ecological functions in East Java, Indonesia. Increasing urbanization, industrial expansion, agricultural intensification, and population growth have contributed to substantial environmental pressures on river water quality, creating challenges for sustainable watershed management and Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6) implementation. This study evaluated seasonal water quality variation and developed GIS-based pollution mapping to identify the spatial distribution of pollution intensity along the Brantas River.
