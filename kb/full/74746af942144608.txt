Abstract Bisphenol A is a chemical widely used in industry and production of various products and has been often detected in surface waters worldwide. There have been few environmental surveys on BPA in surface waters in Iran. This study was conducted to determine BPA concentration in water resources and wastewater treatment plants of eight cities of Khuzestan province during wet and dry seasons and to estimation of potential health risks in terms of non-carcinogenic risks by hazard quotient (HQ) and hazard index (HI). BPA in the samples was extracted by solid-phase extraction method and determined using high performance liquid chromatography.
