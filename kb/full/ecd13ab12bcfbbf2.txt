Environmental federalism asks how regulatory authority should be allocated across government levels. We exploit the staggered release of provincial plans for China's sub-provincial environmental vertical management reform and use listed-firm data for 2012 to 2023 in a staggered difference-in-differences design. The estimand is the intention-to-treat effect of plan announcements and the expectations they induced. Following plan release, firms' comprehensive water pollution-equivalent index declined significantly. The baseline coefficient is -0.165, or approximately 0.037 sample standard deviations.
