Indigenous foods in tourism have become a sustainable force in rejuvenating intangible cultural heritage in tourism across the globe. Observed decline in the consumption of some indigenous dishes due to modernization and preference for fast foods is a concern that justifies this research. Neglected indigenous dishes of Osogbo and Ikogosi ecotourism destination host communities were identified by direct Interview of 50 respondents who are mostly female indigenes culturally connected to culinary focuses via snowball sampling.
