The coastal lake Veli-Akkulam is crucial to Thiruvananthapuram's urban watershed, yet it is under threat from pollution caused by urbanization and industrialization. Conserving freshwater resources is a key part of Sustainable Development Goals, and the only way to protect the lake is by consistently monitoring its water quality. This study aims at evaluating the pollution level of the lake throughout the seasons (pre-monsoon, monsoon, and post-monsoon) of 2022–2023 using the Carlson Trophic State and Water Quality indices.
