Small watercourses of coastal cities with a recreational specialization in the Kaliningrad Region are experiencing significant anthropogenic stress from municipal wastewater and diffuse pollution. However, the seasonal dynamics and spatial distribution of chemical pollution in these resorts have not been sufficiently studied to date. The city of Svetlogorsk has the status of a resort of federal importance, and the quality of its water bodies is an important indicator of the ecological well-being of the area. During 2025–2026, hydrochemical monitoring of two watercourses was carried out at three sampling sites: the Otradnenka stream, the Svetlogorka river upstream and downstream the Tikhoye lake.
