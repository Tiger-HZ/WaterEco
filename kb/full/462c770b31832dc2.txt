Introduction: This paper explores the impact of the transition from voluntary to mandatory Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) legislation in the EU on the Durable Water Repellent (DWR) global supply chain. The query is presented in the context of green procurement. DWR material is essential in outfitting public-facing personnel, thus impacting government purchasing policies for uniforms—a sector valued at about $30 billion a year. Under EPR mandates, apparel commerce is to penalize unrecycled waste and deadstock. DWRs are non-recyclable. Public procurement managers have to reconcile this reality when choosing preferred suppliers. The EPR legislation’s directives are to prioritize firms that follow best-available practices and demonstrate genuine innovation in recyclable DWR technology.
