Urban growth and the expansion of the automotive sector have increased the generation of wastewater from vehicle washing and lubrication activities, which contains potentially polluting compounds. The objective of this study was to evaluate the physicochemical quality of the effluents generated by these establishments in the Abraham Calazacón parish, Santo Domingo canton, and to verify their compliance with environmental regulations. The research, which was quantitative and descriptive in nature, was based on direct observation, surveys, and wastewater sampling. BOD₅, COD, oils and greases, temperature, and total suspended solids were analyzed, and the results were compared with the limits set by Ecuadorian regulations.
