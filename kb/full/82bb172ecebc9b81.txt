A Correction on
Umbilical cord blood: a promising source for allogeneic CAR-T cells
By Liu D-D, Hong W-C, Qiu K-Y, Li X-Y, Liu Y, Zhu L-W, Lai W-X, Chen H, Yang H-Q, Xu L-H and
Fang J-P (2022). Front. Oncol. 12:944248. doi: 10.3389/fonc.2022.944248
There was a mistake in Figure 4B as published the panel corresponding to the CB
CAR-T group at day 21 was inadvertently misused. The corrected Figure 4 appears below.
In the Statistical analysis section, “Kruskal– Wallis Friedman tests” has been corrected to
“Kruskal– Wallis test, ”“ one-way analysis of variance ” has been corrected to “one-way
ANOVA,” and “signiﬁﬁcant” has been corrected to “signiﬁcant.”
The correction has been made to the section Materials and methods, Statistical analysis:
“SPSS 24.0 and GraphPad Prism version 8.0 (GraphPad Software, San Diego, CA, USA)
were used to perform one-way analysis of variance, t-tests (with Welch ’s correction for
unequal variances), and Kruskal – Wallis test, depending on the data distribution (normal
distribution or not). p-values <0.05 were treated as statistically signi ﬁcant (*p < 0.05, ** p <
0.01, *** p < 0.001).”
In the Results section, Antitumor efﬁcacy of cord blood CAR-T, peripheral blood CAR-T,
and peripheral blood (patient) CAR-T in vivo , Paragraph 2, line 4 to 6. the numbers of
surviving mice in the CB CAR-T and PB CAR-T groups were reversed and have
been corrected.
“At the end of the experiment, there were three surviving mice in the CB CAR-T group
and four in the PB CAR-T group ( Figure 4B ).” has been corrected to ”At the end of the
experiment, there were four surviving mice in the CB CAR-T group and three in the PB
CAR-T group ( Figure 4B ).”
The original version of this article has been updated.
