Coastal groundwater resources are highly vulnerable to the combined risks of heavy metal contamination and saltwater intrusion where artisanal small-scale gold mining is active. This study used a GIS-based analysis to identify susceptible coastal aquifers in the municipality of Jose Panganiban, Philippines, by examining the reduction in groundwater potential coincident with the seasonal oscillation of precipitation, the main driver of recharge. The study showed that the general reduction in groundwater potential approaches 25% during the period with the lowest precipitation across the whole study site.
