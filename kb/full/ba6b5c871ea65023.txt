In this study, the 2021 emission measurement results of a cement production facility located in Düzce Province were tested against emission concentrations calculated using the facility’s production capacity and process-based emission factor values for the same year, as defined by the Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change. The analysis focused on Particulate Matter (PM) and Nitrogen Oxides (NOₓ) parameters, evaluating both theoretical (calculation-based) and practical (measurement-based) concentration values. The primary objective was to compare the agreement between these two approaches.
