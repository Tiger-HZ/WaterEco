Introduction Phthalate esters (PAEs) are ubiquitous environmental contaminants and endocrine-disrupting chemicals that have raised growing public health concerns. Methods The aim of this study was to investigate the occurrence, concentration profiles, and human health risks of 18 PAEs in tap water (n = 64) and community direct-drinking water (n = 27) samples collected from Bengbu, China. Results Most PAE congeners were widely detected, with the total concentrations of Σ 18 PAEs ranging from 189.3 to 53,385 ng/L in tap water and 101.6−15,238 ng/L in direct-drinking water, respectively. Dimethyl phthalate, DAP, DBP, DIBP, and DPhP were the most abundant congeners in both water types.
