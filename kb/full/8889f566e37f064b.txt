A variety of hydraulic and ecological processes in rivers depend in part on the grain size distribution of the riverbed. For this reason it is important to accurately sample the bed to determine the grain size distribution. To describe the accuracy of riverbed sediment samples, techniques based on the multinomial distribution are presented. These techniques allow confidence intervals to be calculated for grid, areal, and volume samples. Additionally, sample sizes can be estimated to achieve a desired accuracy for the three sample types. A transformation is necessary when considering areal and volume samples. This is because neither of these two sample types is well conditioned for statistical analysis.
