To address asynchronous acquisition, heterogeneous data dimensions, and the difficulty of directly applying multi-source measurements to fusion modeling in online water quality monitoring, this paper presents a multimodal water quality detection system for data fusion. The system uses a 940 nm light-emitting diode (LED) near-infrared scattering channel for turbidity and particulate-matter characterization, and integrates 200–1000 nm broadband spectral detection, pH detection, and electrical conductivity detection. An embedded node performs data buffering and preliminary processing, while PC-based software manages calibration, spectral preprocessing, timestamp recording, and nearest-neighbor temporal matching.
