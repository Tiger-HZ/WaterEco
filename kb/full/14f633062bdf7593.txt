Background: Reliable representation of river inflow variability is crucial for sustainable reservoir management, especially in data-scarce regions with highly variable hydrological regimes. Limited records and intermittent inflows restrict effective water resources planning and risk-informed decision-making. This study develops a stochastic inflow simulation framework for Menjer Lake, Indonesia, to better characterize inflow uncertainty and support reservoir operations. Methods: A dataset of 1,003 observed inflow time steps was statistically analyzed. Candidate probability distributions were evaluated using maximum likelihood estimation, the Akaike Information Criterion, and the Kolmogorov–Smirnov test. Temporal dependence was incorporated using a first-order autoregressive model combined
