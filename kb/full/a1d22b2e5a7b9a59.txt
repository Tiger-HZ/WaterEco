l-Rhamnose (6-deoxy-l-mannose) is a rare deoxyhexose recognized for its significant value in the food, pharmaceutical and nutraceutical industries due to its unique physiological effects. While primarily utilized as a reagent in organic synthesis, l-Rha and l-Rha-enriched fractions are conventionally sourced from terrestrial plants (fruits, buds, leaves, barks, and stems) and bacteria (including Klebsiella sp., Pseudomonas aeruginosa, and Mycobacterium spp.). Yet, l-Rha co-extraction often leads to contamination with other monosaccharides or aglycone compounds prior to hydrolysis, which is typically carried out using chemical and/or enzymatic methods.
