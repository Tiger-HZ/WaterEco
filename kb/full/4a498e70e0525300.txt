Basmati rice is valued for its distinctive aroma and quality, but its performance under different nutrient management practices needs to be evaluated under Bangladesh conditions. The present study was undertaken to evaluate the varietal performance of basmati rice varieties in T. aman season, to evaluate the yield performance of two basmati rice varieties under an integrated nutrient management system in T. aman season and to assess the combined effect of variety and integrated nutrient management on the yield performance of basmati rice varieties in T. aman rice.
