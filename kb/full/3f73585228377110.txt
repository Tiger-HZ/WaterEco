Heavy metals are elements that are naturally present in the Earth’s crust in different concentrations. Anthropogenic activities have disrupted the natural cycle and balance of certain heavy metals causing heavy metal contamination. This research was conducted to study the efficiency of different bioadsorbents viz. amaltas leaves, bar leaves, coconut husk, cow dung, jamun leaves, olive stone, maize cob, neem leaves and date stone for the removal of heavy metals from aqueous solutions as well as industrial effluents prior to optimization of conditions and various parameters. The bio adsorbents were pretreated and chemically activated. The samples were then analyzed using the atomic absorption spectrophotometer version 210VGP to check the efficiency and adsorption.
