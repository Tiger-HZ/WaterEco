Aim of study: The objective is to determine the effect of CNC surface machining parameters on the surface roughness (Ra and Rz) of lake paint applied to MDF surfaces. Material and method: The test specimens were machined on a CNC machine using three different spindle speeds (10.000, 15.000, and 20.000 rpm), four different feed rates (1.500, 2.000, 3.000, and 5.000 mm/min), three different depth of cut (3, 5, and 8 mm), and two different tool diameters (5 and 8 mm) were machined on a CNC machine. A polyurethane primer and two cross-coats of lake paint topcoat were applied to the machined surfaces.
