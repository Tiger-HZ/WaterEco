Urban lakes are increasingly threatened by eutrophication resulting from rapid urbanization and anthropogenic nutrient enrichment.This study evaluated the trophic status of eight urban lakes (seven artificial and one natural) in Yamoussoukro, Côte d'Ivoire, through physicochemical characterization, chlorophyll-a biomass assessment, and phytoplankton community analysis conducted from December 2015 to October 2017.Physicochemical parameters including temperature, pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, transparency, ammonium, nitrite, phosphate, and total phosphorus were analyzed using standard methods.The organic pollution index (OPI) values ranged from 1.66 to 2.33, indicating heavy to very heavy organic pollution in all lakes.Elevated nutrient concentrations were recorded, particularly ammonium (1.00-2.61mg/L), nitrite (113.75-188.5 µg/L), phosphate (255-720.5µg/L), and total phosphorus (210-710 µg/L).According to the OECD trophic classification criteria, all lakes were categorized as hyper-eutrophic due to high Trophic status …
