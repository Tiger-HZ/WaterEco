Bangladesh, a riverine country, relies heavily on its intricate network of rivers and water bodies. With over 700 rivers flowing through its landscape, water resources are integral to the nation’s agriculture, transportation, and overall way of life. Effective management of these resources is vital to mitigating the risks of floods, droughts, and other hydrological hazards. This study specifically investigates the capacity of various Machine Learning (ML) models to forecast monthly discharge of the Karatoya-Atrai River 1 month ahead, using only rainfall and temperature, together with antecedent (lagged) discharge.
