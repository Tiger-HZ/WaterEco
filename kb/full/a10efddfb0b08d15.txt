The quality of health services for children under five is an important determinant of the success of maternal and child health programs, particularly in growth monitoring, immunization, and malnutrition prevention. Posyandu cadres play a strategic role as a link between the community and health workers in providing basic health services. However, variations in cadres involvement may affect the coverage and quality of Posyandu services. This study aimed to analyze the relationship between cadre roles and the quality of health services for children under five at Posyandu in the working area of Puskesmas Pakuan Baru, Jambi City.
