ABSTRACT Determining environmental water requirements and ecohydrological thresholds is essential for sustaining wetland functionality in semi‐arid regions subject to expanding water withdrawals and climatic variability. This study presents an integrated system‐dynamics framework to identify thresholds and water requirements in the Ramsar‐listed Alagol, Ulmagol and Ajigol (AUA) wetland complex in northeastern Iran. The framework couples daily water balance, wetland morphometry, vegetation dynamics, anthropogenic withdrawals and waterbird habitat responses within a STELLA modelling environment.
