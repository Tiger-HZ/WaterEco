Harmful algal blooms (HABs) are increasingly frequent due to anthropogenic nutrient enrichment, posing significant ecological and economic threats. It includes hypoxia, toxin production, and a decline in tourism due to odour pollution. This study investigates the HAB event in Karimunjawa National Park (KNP) on September 19, 2023, attributed to nutrient loading from inadequate wastewater treatment in the shrimp pond. The bloom coincided with the shrimp harvest season, indicating a strong correlation between pond effluents and coastal eutrophication. To identify the dominant algal species and primary environmental drivers of bloom formation, nutrients and water quality parameters were analysed using field and satellite observations from three locations in KNP.
