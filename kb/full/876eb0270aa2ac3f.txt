Khon Kaen Province in Northeastern Thailand is repeatedly exposed to both floods and droughts, undermining agricultural livelihoods. This study examines perceived climate risks and adaptive governance through a participatory action research (PAR) approach at four reservoir communities of the Khon Kaen Irrigation Project—Huai Sai, Huai Toei, Huai Yang, and Laloeng Wai. Using a mixed-methods design, the study combined semi-structured interviews with 12 irrigation officials, focus group discussions with Joint Management Committees, community workshops with 240 residents, and a household survey (N = 400).
