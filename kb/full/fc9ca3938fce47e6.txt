Satellite-derived reservoir area is useful where continuous in situ records are unavailable, but reported extent can depend on source product, compositing, threshold, and post-processing. This study compared five water-mapping rules for Yamula Dam Lake, Türkiye, using 16 spring and late-summer periods from 2017 to 2024. The rules comprised fixed-threshold and Otsu-thresholded Modified Normalized Difference Water Index composites from Sentinel-2 Level-2A reflectance and Dynamic World median water probabilities thresholded at 0.50, 0.60, and 0.70. Outputs were evaluated over a common final valid-pixel domain and subjected to the same reservoir-component filter, although the source products retained distinct observation support.
