Abstract The South Truckee Meadows Water Reclamation Facility (STMWRF) in Washoe County, Nevada, commissioned a biosolids facility with jet aerated aerobic digestion. The jet aerators were not performing as designed, so they were tested on‐site in the new tanks in both clean and process water according to ASCE standards. The aerators failed by substantial margins. To make up for the aeration shortfall, Washoe County elected to replace the jet aerators with coarse bubble aerators and to add the additional blower capacity necessary to meet the oxygen requirements in this configuration. After partial replacement, with one basin containing coarse bubble diffusers and the other still containing jets, the efficiency of both systems was tested in process water.
