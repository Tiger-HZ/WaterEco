Objective: To evaluate the effects of Withania somnifera (ashwagandha) root extract supplementation on sleep quality, body composition, and menopausal symptoms in postmenopausal women. Methods: This randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled 66 postmenopausal women aged 45-65 years at a single center in Amritsar, India. Participants were randomly assigned to receive either ashwagandha root extract (600 mg/day; n=34) or placebo (n=32) for 12 weeks. Primary outcomes were sleep quality assessed by the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, body composition assessed by dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry, and menopausal symptoms assessed by the Menopause Rating Scale.
