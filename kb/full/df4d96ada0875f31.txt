The present study evaluated the effect of dietary tamarind seed husk supplementation on nutrient intake and apparent nutrient digestibility in lactating crossbred cattle. Twenty-one crossbred lactating cows were selected based on milk yield, body weight, number of completed lactations, and days in lactation and were randomly allotted to three treatment groups, with seven animals in each group. The dietary treatments consisted of a control ration without tamarind seed husk, a ration containing 2.5% tamarind seed husk, and a ration containing 7.5% tamarind seed husk. The animals were fed for 90 days, followed by a five-day metabolism trial during which feed intake, faecal output, and related samples were recorded and analysed.
