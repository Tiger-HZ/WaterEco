Summary We investigated the connection between plant species diversity and climate by using a process‐based, generic plant model. Different ‘species' were simulated by different values for certain growth‐related model parameters. Subsequently, a wide range of values were tested in the framework of a ‘Monte Carlo' simulation for success; that is, the capability of each plant with these parameter combinations to reproduce itself during its lifetime. The range of successful parameter combinations approximated species diversity. This method was applied to a global grid, using daily atmospheric forcing from a climate model simulation.
