We investigated microcystin (MC) production by four cyanobacterial strains (three Microcystis aeruginosa and one Planktothrix agardhii ) in response to different grazer densities (direct exposure: zero, two, four, or eight individuals per 300 mL) and infochemical concentrations (indirect exposure: 0%, 10%, 25%, and 50%) of Daphnia magna and Moina macrocopa . MC production increased after direct exposure to both zooplankton species and was higher with increasing concentration of infochemicals. This MC production was significantly different among the control and three zooplankton treatment levels.
