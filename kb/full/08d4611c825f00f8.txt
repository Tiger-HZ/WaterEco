We examined the seasonal abundance of postlarval (PL) (stage IV) American lobsters, Homarus americanus, from neuston samples from 1989 to 1995 at Johns Bay, Maine, and Seabrook, New Hampshire, located 150 km apart in the western Gulf of Maine. The postlarval season at these sites began between approximately days of the year 180 and 210 and ranged from about 35 to 70 days in duration. Within years, start and end dates were similar for the two sites. Maximum concentrations from single tows exceeded 100 PL·1000 m -2 , but 85% or more of the positive tows had concentrations &lt;15 PL·1000 m -2 and more than half had &lt;5 PL·1000 m -2 .
