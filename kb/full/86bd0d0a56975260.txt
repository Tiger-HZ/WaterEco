Abstract The study aimed to optimize the treatment of oil refinery–contaminated wastewater through modification of the well-established activated sludge process with new nanocomposite (NC) materials to produce high-quality treated effluents for potential reuse. Refinery wastewater samples were collected from one of the major oil refineries, Alexandria, Egypt, where the operation, performance, and efficiency of the current activated sludge (AS) unit were evaluated for 6 consecutive months. Two AS bench scale PVC basins were constructed. Magnetite nanoparticles (Fe 3 O 4 NPs) and magnetite silica (Fe 3 O 4 /silica) nanocomposite (NC) were prepared and characterized.
