ABSTRACT Groundwater quality is crucial for both domestic and agricultural uses, especially in rural areas. This study aimed to assess groundwater quality for drinking and irrigation and analyze potential health risks associated with nitrate content. The Drinking Water Quality Index and the Irrigation Water Quality Index were used to evaluate water suitability. The results showed that only 12% of wells were potable, while 88% were of poor quality. Irrigation water quality was average to high, indicating suitability for agricultural purposes. The study assessed the health risks of nitrates and found that the oral hazard index (HQoral) was less than 1 in 94% of locations, indicating no serious risk to children and infants. However, 6% of wells exceeded 1 in the Skin Hazard Index for children
