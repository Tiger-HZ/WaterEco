We compared fish species compositions among four site types (lake, lake mouth, stream mouth, stream) along the gradient from stream to lake for 12 tributary mouths. Comparison of species richness, rarefaction species diversity, and species density all supported the same pattern: stream-mouth sites contained the highest number of species, followed by stream sites, lake-mouth sites, and lake sites, even though lake and lake-mouth sites yielded more individuals and were larger in area and volume.
