The study assessed the factors affecting the adoption of technological tools among academic staff for quality delivery in tertiary institutions in Rivers State. Three research questions with corresponding hypotheses guided the study. This study adopted the descriptive survey design. The population of the study consisted of 3,154 academic staff from the three Universities in Rivers State. Taro Yamane formular was used to determine a sample size 355 respondents. Multistage sampling technique, stratified random sampling and stage Quota sampling technique were applied. The developed instrument titled: “Assessing the Factors Affecting the Adoption of Technological Tools among Academic Staff for Quality Delivery Questionnaire (AFAATTASSQDQ) was used.
