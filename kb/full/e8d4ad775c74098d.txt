Background: Halophytic shrubs are strategic alternative forage resources for ruminants in arid and saline environments, where conventional feed availability is limited by drought, soil salinity and rangeland degradation. This study evaluated the chemical composition, in vitro fermentation characteristics and estimated nutritive value of three dominant halophytic shrubs from Sidi Okba, Biskra, Algeria. Methods: Edible aerial parts of Atriplex halimus, Arthrophytum scoparium and Tamarix africana were collected during autumn (S1) and spring (S2). Samples were dried, ground and analysed for dry matter, mineral matter, organic matter, crude protein and crude cellulose.
