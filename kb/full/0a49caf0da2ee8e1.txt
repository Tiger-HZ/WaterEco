Water and sediment quality assessments are crucial for understanding environmental contamination and potential health risks. This study evaluates the physicochemical properties, heavy metal concentrations, and associated health risks of water and sediment samples from Ossiama, Odoni, and Agoro in Bayelsa State, Nigeria. Water samples were analyzed for pH, turbidity, total suspended solids (TSS), electrical conductivity (E.C.), biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), and nutrient levels, while sediment samples were assessed for heavy metal contamination using standard analytical techniques.
