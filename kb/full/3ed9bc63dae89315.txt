Fertilizer management and synchronization of flowering for inbred lines play a vital role in commercial maize hybrid seed production. The objective of this study was to optimize nutrient management for flowering induction by evaluating the combined application of chemical fertilizers and specific plant growth regulators (Auxin, Gibberellic Acid, and 2,4-D) in the inbred lines, viz. RML-86, RML-96, and RML-95. The research was conducted in the research field of the National Maize Research Program, Rampur, Chitwan, Nepal, over two consecutive spring seasons, 2020 and 2021. The experiment was laid out in a Randomized Complete Block Design with three replications and nine treatment combinations.
