ABSTRACT Objective This guideline defines recommendations for the provision of quality care for prostate artery embolisation (PAE) treatment for symptomatic benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH). It has been developed using the AGREE‐II framework and aligns with current evidence, sourced from Randomised Controlled Trials, meta‐analyses and expert consensus. Recommendations are graded according to the level of evidence supporting them. Target Audience Men with symptomatic BPH considering their treatment options and referring clinicians including general practitioners, urologists and interventional radiologists.
