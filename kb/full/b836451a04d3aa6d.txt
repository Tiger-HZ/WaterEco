Water quality monitoring is essential for public health and sustainable development, yet laboratory-based analysis remains expensive in resource-limited contexts. Four water quality test strip products available in South Africa were evaluated by comparing test strip measurements of pH, nitrate, nitrite and hardness with reference measurements, with strip readings conducted under indoor (1000 lx) and outdoor (5000 lx) lighting conditions. The results indicate that while pH and nitrate measurements are reasonably accurate, water hardness exhibits the highest error rates (normalised root mean square error up to 43%), highlighting a need for improved assay chemistry.
