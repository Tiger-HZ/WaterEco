Road traffic accidents are a global concern, and dangerous driving behavior is a major cause. Current research faces challenges in indicator selection, temporal modeling, and multimodal data fusion. To address these issues, this study proposes a comprehensive data-feature-modeling solution. Using a multi-source dataset from 40 drivers (naturalistic driving and surveys), we establish a three-dimensional indicator system (driver, vehicle, and environment). CNNs extract deep spatiotemporal features, combined with PCA and silhouette coefficient-based hierarchical clustering to classify driving behaviors into cautious and aggressive types.
