Abstract Temperature is a master variable in aquatic ecosystems. Despite its importance for ecosystem function, our capacity to measure temperature is often limited to focal systems with intensive in situ measurements or larger systems with readily accessible data from disparate data streams. These limitations are consequential for holistically understanding aquatic ecosystem status and change, especially considering that most lakes globally do not have consistent in situ data. To empower researchers with macroscale surface temperature observations, we present the first remotely sensed lake temperature dataset that includes measurements from Landsat 8 made every ~ 16 d for 137,394 lakes of at least 4 ha in surface area throughout the contiguous United States from 2013 through 2025.
