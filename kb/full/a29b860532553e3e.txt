Background The Planetary Boundaries (PB) framework defines a finite safe operating space for humanity. In semi‑arid Jordan—one of the world’s most water‑stressed countries—urban construction is an important driver of pressures on scarce blue water resources, local air quality, and soil function. Objective This study (i) assesses how current construction practices in Jordan’s main urban centers translate into local Earth‑system pressures and (ii) proposes an Earth‑system governance model that links these pressures to enforceable project‑ and sector‑level controls. Methods A multiple case‑study design was applied to 10 active construction projects.
