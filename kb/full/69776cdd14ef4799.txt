Today, global demand for water is outpacing population growth due to rapid urbanization and the rising needs of the agriculture, industry, and energy sectors. Global water stress has held at 18% since 2023, with one in ten people now living under high or critical water stress, and several regions exceeding a 75% stress level. This scarcity is projected to worsen as rising temperatures from climate change disrupt the water cycle. To ensure universal access to safe and affordable drinking water by 2030, we must invest in infrastructure, protect water-related ecosystems, and promote hygiene education.
