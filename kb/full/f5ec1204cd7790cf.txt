Abstract High-resolution spectroscopy of weakly bound molecular complexes requires theoretical predictions with uncertainties below 0.1 cm–1. Achieving this level of accuracy places stringent demands on the underlying full-dimensional potential energy surfaces (PESs). However, constructing such PESs remains challenging because flexible configuration space requires dense sampling and the ab initio energies used for fitting must meet spectroscopic accuracy requirements. Here, we develop a physics-constrained synergetic active- and transfer-learning framework (SynATL) that combines uncertainty-driven active learning, high-level refinement via transfer learning, and an explicit long-range representation to construct spectroscopically accurate full-dimensional PESs.
