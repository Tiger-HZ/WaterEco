Abstract Freshwater lake ecosystems are increasingly affected by altered water level fluctuations (WLF) from hydropower regulation, yet impacts on lake biodiversity remain understudied. We investigated how regulation influences Ephemeroptera, Plecoptera and Trichoptera (EPT) communities in boreal lakes. Three regulated and three nearby control lakes were sampled eight times across 2023–2024. We analyzed differences in abundance and species richness while accounting for temporal and spatial variability. To gain deeper mechanistic insight, we assessed community responses through analysis of composition, beta diversity-partitioning, indicator species, and functional traits.
