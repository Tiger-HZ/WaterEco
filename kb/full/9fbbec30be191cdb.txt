Abstract Background In water quality, genotoxicity is a critical safety endpoint as genotoxicants can damage DNA directly or indirectly. Genotoxic activity has previously been detected in source water from lake Mälaren, throughout the conventional treatment process, and in drinking water at the Görväln drinking water treatment plant, with the highest activity during autumn-winter. Because genotoxicity poses a serious risk to human health, it is essential to investigate whether the underlying genotoxicity arises from direct and/or indirect mechanisms. Aim In this study we combined the micronucleus and oxidative stress bioassays with morphological profiling using Cell Painting with the aim of increasing our understanding of the mechanisms causing the genotoxicity.
