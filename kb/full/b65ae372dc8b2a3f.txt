This study aims to analyze and explain the effects of reputation, service quality, and price perception on repurchase intention for Galeri 24 precious metals, with customer satisfaction as an intervening variable among customers of one Galeri 24 outlet in DKI Jakarta. This study employs a quantitative approach, with a population consisting of customers who have previously purchased precious metals at one Galeri 24 outlet in DKI Jakarta. The sampling technique used was non-probability sampling, with a total sample of 193 respondents. The data used in this study consisted of primary data obtained through questionnaires and secondary data collected from various relevant sources.
