Static groundwater vulnerability assessments assume time-invariant inputs—an assumption hard to defend under climate change, when depth to water and net recharge are precisely the parameters that evolve with the forcing. This study develops a time-varying, multi-model framework for groundwater vulnerability assessment and applies it to the Almyros coastal aquifer (Thessaly, Greece) through the DRASTIC index. Time-varying depth-to-water and net-recharge fields are simulated for a baseline (1991–2018) and two future periods (2031–2060, 2071–2100) using an Integrated Modelling System driven by 19 bias-corrected regional climate model realizations under two emission scenarios (RCP4.5, RCP8.5); the remaining DRASTIC parameters are held time-invariant.
