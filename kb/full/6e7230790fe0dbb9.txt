The rapid expansion of digital connectivity has intensified competition among Internet Service Providers (ISPs), making service quality alone increasingly insufficient to explain customer responses. At the same time, perceived value, digital experience, and environmental credibility have emerged as relevant considerations in service consumption. This study aims to examine the effects of service quality, perceived value, immersion, and green brand trust on post-purchase satisfaction and the effect of post-purchase satisfaction on purchase intention among ICONNET customers. A quantitative, cross-sectional explanatory design was employed using purposive sampling.
