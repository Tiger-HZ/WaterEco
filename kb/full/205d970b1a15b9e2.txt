Abstract Background Sleep quality is an important determinant of overall health and well-being. Lifestyle behaviours and sociodemographic characteristics may be associated with sleep quality. This study aimed to examine factors associated with good sleep quality among non-agricultural working-age adults. Methods A cross-sectional study was conducted in Chiang Klang District, Nan Province, Thailand. Data on sociodemographic characteristics, lifestyle behaviours, dietary intake, and clinical parameters were collected using structured questionnaires, health examinations, and laboratory investigations.
