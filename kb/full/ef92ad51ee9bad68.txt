Mathematical modeling of hydrothermodynamic, chemical, and biological processes has identified the potential causes of Lake Ursul (India) returning to a high trophic status after extensive restoration work. This is due to high nutrient content, which leads to very high phytoplankton primary production and biomass. This causes high oxygen consumption during the bacterial decomposition of organic matter and plankton respiration. In such a situation, short-term stable density stratification, which prevents the aeration of the bottom layers, is sufficient to completely deplete dissolved oxygen, leading to massive fish mortality.
