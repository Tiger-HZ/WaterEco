Introduction Groundwater resources in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India are under increased stress due to rapid urbanization, erratic monsoon patterns and largely unregulated groundwater extraction. Addressing this challenge requires not only continuous monitoring but also accurate forecasting of groundwater fluctuations. Methods This study presents a data driven framework that combines Internet of Things (IoT) enabled sensing with Machine Learning model. Real-time measurements such as groundwater depth, rainfall, temperature, and soil moisture are combined with historical hydro-meteorological data to build a reliable prediction system.
