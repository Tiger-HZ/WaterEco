Vegetable farming plays an important role in food security and farmers’ incomes in Senegal. However, the intensification of production systems is accompanied by significant pesticide use, which can pose risks to human health and the environment. The objective of this study was to characterize the weed flora and assess pesticide use and management practices in vegetable farming systems in Pakh and Mbane, in the Lake Guiers area. A socioeconomic survey was conducted among 69 farm managers 37 in Pakh and 32 in Mbane using a structured questionnaire. Weeds were identified based on botanical criteria, and the pesticides identified were cross-referenced against the list of products approved by the Sahelian Pesticide Committee.
