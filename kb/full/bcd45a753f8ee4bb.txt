Geomorphic setting and seasonal variation shape benthic macroinvertebrate communities, but their effects on community patterns and assembly processes remain unclear. We surveyed 49 sites across seven Sichuan rivers during spring and autumn 2025. Reaches were classified as Mountain, Hilly, or Plain. Principal coordinate analysis (PCoA), random forest models, a neutral community model (NCM), and the modified stochasticity ratio (MST) examined community patterns, predictors, and assembly processes. Diptera had high relative abundances in Mountain and Hilly reaches in spring, whereas the proportion of Ephemeroptera increased across reach types in autumn.
