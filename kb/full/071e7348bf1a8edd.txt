Abstract Aims This study aimed to identify optimal nitrogen management strategies for bioenergy production and phytoremediation of coastal saline soil. Methods A two-year field experiment was conducted in a saline semi-arid area of the Yellow River Delta, China, to evaluate the effects of five nitrogen application rates (0, 45, 90, 135, and 180 kg ha − 1 , designated N0–N4) on sugar accumulation (Brix), nutrient content, biomass of individual nodes and internodes (NAIs) in sweet sorghum, and selected soil properties. Results Brix followed a normal distribution along the stalk, peaking in middle internodes.
