Abstract Enteric methane (CH4) emissions from beef cattle contribute substantially to agricultural greenhouse gas production. Infusing oxygen in the anaerobic environment of the rumen could alter ruminal oxidation-reduction potential and potentially reduce methanogenesis. This study utilized nanobubble technology (Moleaer, Hawthorne, CA) to increase the dissolved oxygen (DO) concentration in drinking water and subsequently evaluated the effects of oxygen-enriched water (OEW) on feed and water intake, gas flux, microbial communities, digestibility, and ruminal fermentation in beef cattle fed a forage-based diet (76.6% wheat straw, 20.9% corn silage, 0.7% distillers grains, and 1.8% mineral supplement).
