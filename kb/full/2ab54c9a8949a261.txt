The total sedimentation rate was measured fortnightly for a year in a large shallow lake (Lough Neagh, Northern Ireland, surface area 383 km 2 , mean depth 8.9 m) at five sites using 10 sediment traps. The total sedimentation rate included both primary and secondary sedimentation. As the annual average total sedimentation rate, 53.2 ± 44.1 g m −2 d −1 , is more than 25 times the primary value, secondary sedimentation due to wind‐induced resuspension is very important. The average of the largest 30% of wave mixed layer (WML) values, a function of wind speed and effective fetch, strongly correlates with the total sedimentation rate ( r 2 values of 0.69 and 0.98 at two westerly sites) at most of the sites.
