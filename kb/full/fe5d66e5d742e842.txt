Tungsten (W) is a highly incompatible element progressively enriched in the continental crust and closely associated with granitic magmatism, yet the processes that concentrate it to ore grades remain debated. Reduced magmatic conditions have long been regarded as essential, but many reduced granites are barren, indicating that low oxygen fugacity (fO2) alone is insufficient.
