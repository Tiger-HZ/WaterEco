Abstract Monitoring antimicrobial resistance (AMR) in aquatic systems is essential for tracking resistance mechanisms and identifying microbial sources. Here, we present a dual-module electrochemical sensing platform that integrates a gold-electrode-based RPA–CRISPR/Cas12a biosensor for detecting antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) with a paper-based electrochemical aptasensor for antibiotic quantification. The system employs thiol-modified CRISPR/Cas12a probes alongside amino-functionalized graphene/thionine/gold nanoparticles (NH2-G/THI/AuNPs) nanocomposites, enabling highly sensitive and specific detection (Limits of detection: 1 copy μL–1 for ARGs and 1 nM for antibiotics).
