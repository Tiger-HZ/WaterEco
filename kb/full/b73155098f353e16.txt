Abstract A simple process-based model was developed to predict total inorganic nitrogen (TIN) concentrations in the largest semiarid river-reservoir system in Brazil, building on a previously developed model for total phosphorus (TP). The model solved the monthly water balance and TIN dynamics along the river, assuming complete mixing within each cross-section, steady-state conditions, piston-flow transport, and first-order decay. Wastewater, reservoir, and river flow rates, together with TIN concentration data, were used for model validation. A consistent decrease in TIN concentrations was observed along the river, with a decay coefficient of 0.3 day⁻¹, identical to that previously reported for TP.
