Unsaturated polyester resin (UPR) composites containing 0.02–1.00 %wt. ZnO nanoparticles (ZnO NPs) were prepared using an MEKP–cobalt octanoate curing system and evaluated for sanitary sewer components. The influence of ZnO NPs content on chemical structure, hardness, tensile and flexural properties, scratch resistance, thermal behaviour, and surface characteristics was investigated using FTIR, XRD, mechanical testing, TGA, DSC, optical surface analysis, and SEM. Based on the initial screening, composites containing 0.06, 0.75, and 1.00 %wt. of ZnO NPs were selected for detailed characterisation and subsequent 28-day exposure to activated sludge.
