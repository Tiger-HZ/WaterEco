Abstract Hybrid decentralized applications (dApps) use cloud-native microservices as off-chain services to observe blockchain events through heterogeneous providers. Such observations may be delayed, duplicated, reordered, mutually inconsistent, or invalidated by chain reorganizations; treating them as authoritative can disrupt dependable processing across downstream cloud-native microservices. This paper presents DORI, a Distributed Observation and Reconciliation Interface that applies resilience-by-design principles to multi-source event synchronization within cloud-native microservice environments.
