Conventional map-accuracy metrics do not indicate where limited quality-control effort should be allocated. This study evaluates uncertainty-based ranking for post-mapping quality control of selected 25 cm RGB land-cover image tiles under spatially disjoint testing. The dataset contained 15,535 tiles grouped by 359 map sheets in three regions of the Republic of Korea; 12,613 tiles were used to train U-Net, DeepLabV3+, SegFormer, and Mask2Former. SegFormer achieved the highest numerical all-valid-pixel mIoU, 0.7066 (95% confidence interval: 0.686–0.724), and a ground-truth-foreground-restricted mIoU of 0.8163.
