Abstract This study evaluated the effects of mixed corn silages, with and without commercially viable cobs, combined with butterfly pea ( Clitoria ternatea L. ) hay on intake, digestibility, water balance, nitrogen balance, and carcass characteristics of lambs. Twenty crossbred intact male lambs (18.0 ± 4.8 kg body weight) were distributed in a randomized complete block design with a 2 × 2 factorial arrangement and five replicates per treatment. Treatments consisted of corn silage with cobs (CSC), CSC plus butterfly pea hay (BH), corn silage without cobs (CSWC), and CSWC plus BH.
