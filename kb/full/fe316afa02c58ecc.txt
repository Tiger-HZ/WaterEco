Abstract This study focuses on the development of robust control strategies for a photovoltaic (PV) system integrated with a grid-connected quazi-Z-source inverter (qZSI). Conventional current control methods, such as proportional–integral (PI) controllers, often fail to maintain satisfactory performance under system uncertainties. In practical grid-connected operation, qZSI systems are subjected to disturbances including DC-link voltage fluctuations, load variations, and fault conditions, which restrict the inverter’s operating range. To address these challenges, robust control techniques are employed to enhance system stability and extend the operational range under uncertain conditions.
