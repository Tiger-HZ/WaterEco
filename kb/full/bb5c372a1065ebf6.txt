The Mekong Delta is experiencing surface-water-quality degradation driven not only by increasing pollution loads but also by altered flow regimes, salinity intrusion, sediment starvation, and shifting hydraulic operation. This paper synthesizes and analyzes monitoring records for 2016–2024, a database of 3,117 samples, supplementary surveys conducted in 2022–2024, and hydrodynamic–salinity–morphological studies to clarify surface-water-quality dynamics in the Mekong Delta through two linked dimensions: the direct impacts of pollution sources and the amplifying role of altered flows. The analytical framework combines descriptive statistics based on WQI, PCA/FA/AHC for identifying major pollution components, and ESAT/MixSIAR for source apportionment.
