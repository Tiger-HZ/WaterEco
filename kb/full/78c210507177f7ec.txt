Abstract Background Pregnancy is associated with extensive physiological and psychosocial changes that can increase maternal psychological vulnerability. Resilience, as a dynamic capacity for positive adaptation in the face of adversity, plays a key role in maintaining mental health during pregnancy; however, the mediating mechanisms underlying this relationship remain less understood. The present study aimed to investigate the mediating role of mindfulness levels in the relationship between quality of life and resilience among pregnant women. Methods This cross-sectional descriptive-correlational study employed structural equation modeling (SEM).
