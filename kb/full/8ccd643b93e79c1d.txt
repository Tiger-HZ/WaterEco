ABSTRACT Background Aesthetic medicine has progressively shifted from volume toward skin quality as a key determinant of perceived age and facial attractiveness. Although non–cross‐linked hyaluronic acid–based injectables are widely used to improve skin hydration and appearance, clinical application remains largely technique‐driven, and structured decision‐making frameworks addressing skin texture and visual skin quality as distinct therapeutic objectives remain limited.
