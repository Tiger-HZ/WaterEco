Abstract In the slipform paving of underground roadway floors, the abrupt dissipation of lateral constraints post-extrusion frequently induces edge collapse, structural deformation, and lateral displacement. To mitigate these challenges, this study proposes a novel composite slipform paving strategy characterized by the localized application of an alkali-free liquid accelerator to the bilateral strips, while the central section is cast with conventional concrete. Field validation was conducted in an underground coal mine utilizing optimized operational parameters: a 9% dosage of FSA-AF alkali-free liquid accelerator, a 14 cm width for the accelerator-modified side zones, an auger rotation speed of 45 r/min, and a paving velocity of 6.0 m/min.
