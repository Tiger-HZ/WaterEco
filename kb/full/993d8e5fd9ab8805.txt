Abstract Although whole water toxicity (WWT) is increasingly recognized as a vital supplement to existing wastewater discharge standards, its practical application is constrained by a lack of high-throughput screening methods and comprehensive baseline data necessary for effective policymaking. To address this gap, we developed a model-driven, high-throughput WWT assay by leveraging Cell Painting technology as a rapid complement to ISO 21115:2019 and conducted a nationwide investigation into influent WWT. Our analysis revealed that four key socioeconomic drivers account for 38.8% of the cumulative absolute effect size on influent WWT, with high toxicity levels in highly economically developed urban areas.
