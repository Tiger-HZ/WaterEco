Assessing the vulnerability of a coastal area to an anthropogenic factor is crucial for developing adaptation or mitigation strategies. As other countries, Cameroon is exposed to ocean acidification (OA), but very little is known about the extent of its vulnerability. This study aim was to assess the vulnerability of the coastal ocean ecosystem of Kribi (Southern Cameroon) to ocean acidification. Thus, simultaneous monitoring of carbonate system parameters, zooplankton communities, and others environmental variables was conducted in the area between September 2021 and August 2022. Three monitoring stations and a seasonal sampling strategy were established to understand organism’s exposition to changes in the carbonate chemistry and OA.
