Essential Ocean Variables (EOVs) in ocean chemistry such as temperature, salinity, chlorophyll, nutrients and dissolved oxygen are critical for ocean monitoring and policy, particularly for assessing eutrophication and ocean acidification. These topics are recognized as priorities in global and regional frameworks, including the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) and the Marine Strategy Framework Directive (MSFD). Despite their importance, implementation remains fragmented across infrastructures like EMODnet, Copernicus, and the World Ocean Database (WOD).
