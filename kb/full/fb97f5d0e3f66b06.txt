Abstract Feed efficiency in dairy cattle is driven by genetic, metabolic, nutritional and physiological factors. From a nutritional perspective, ruminal fermentation and post-ruminal digestion of nutrients significantly influence nutrient utilization efficiency. Direct-fed microbials are commonly used feed additives for lactating dairy cows due to their ability to modulate ruminal fermentation through several mechanisms. Lactic acid bacteria facilitate the growth of ruminal microorganisms tolerant to the presence of lactic acid in the rumen and to stimulate lactic acid–utilizing microorganisms increasing ruminal pH and alleviating ruminal acidosis.
