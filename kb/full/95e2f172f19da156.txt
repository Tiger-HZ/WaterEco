This study used 30 m Landsat imagery from 1995 to 2020 to investigate vegetation dynamics and their driving factors in Yengisar County, an arid oasis region in northwestern China. The Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) was calculated, and the Dimidiate Pixel Model was used to estimate fractional vegetation cover (FVC). Trend analysis and a center-of-gravity migration model were employed to examine the spatiotemporal evolution of vegetation and its responses to climatic and human factors. The results showed that: (1) FVC exhibited clear spatial heterogeneity, with high vegetation coverage mainly distributed along river systems in the southwest and concentric expansion occurring around urban areas in the northeast.
