The present contribution discussed the growing vulnerability of rural regions and natural habitats in advanced economies, where intersecting social, economic, and climatic pressures are reshaping local systems. Demographic shifts, evolving production patterns, and transformations in land-use, may contribute – albeit in distinct ways – to an intrinsic, while continuous, degradation of ecosystems, even when the underlying relationships among drivers are complex, non-linear, or not immediately observable. Confronting such challenges requires a genuinely multidisciplinary and coordinated framework that integrates insights from economics, ecology, spatial planning, and 2 advanced analytical methodologies. Only through a comprehensive understanding of these ongoing transformations, robust strategies for environmental protection, risk prevention, and climate adaptation may be delineated, thereby enhancing the resilience of Mediterranean rural landscapes and the respective local communities.
