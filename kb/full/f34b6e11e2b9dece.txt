The present experiment was carried out to study the water quality, production, economics and greenhouse gas emissions under different dietary protein levels in tilapia (Oreochromis mosssambicus) farming pond. The experiment was carried out using three treatments with varying dietary protein levels viz. T1: 27%; T2: 30% and T3: 33%, each with two replications. Tilapia stocking density (49400 individuals/ha) was similar across the treatments.
