Providing environmentally safe drinking water and treating wastewater relies heavily on efficient desalination methods. Among these, low-pressure reverse osmosis (RO) is highly promising for residential and industrial water conditioning. However, a significant operational challenge remains scaling – the deposition of mineral precipitates on the membrane surface, which reduces membrane lifespan and efficiency. The primary aim of this research is to evaluate the performance of low-pressure RO membranes, specifically the Filmtec TW30-1812-75 model, during the desalination of municipal tap water.
