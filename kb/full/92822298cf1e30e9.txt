Vitamin A (VA) functions as a vital micronutrient supporting growth, immune competence, and stress tolerance in shrimp, though its requirement under industrial high-density rearing conditions has not been established. This study assessed how graded dietary VA levels affect growth, nutrient metabolism, antioxidant capacity, immune function, and hepatopancreas structure in Litopenaeus vannamei reared at 720 shrimp/m3. Five experimental diets were formulated with graded VA supplementation at 0 (A0, control), 3000 (A1), 6000 (A2), 12,000 (A3), and 24,000 IU/kg (A4).
