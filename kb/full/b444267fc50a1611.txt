Purpose: The aim of this study was to examine the relationship between exam anxiety and musculoskeletal disorders, nutritional attitudes, physical activity, and sleep quality in university students during exam periods. Materials and Methods: This study included 204 university students (144 female, 60 males; 20.92±1.81 years) during an examination period. Exam anxiety was assessed using the Revised Test Anxiety (RTA) scale.
