The observation that the relative importance of picophytoplankton is greatest in warm and nutrient‐poor waters was tested here based on a comprehensive review of the data available in the literature from oceanic and coastal estuarine areas. Results show that picophytoplankton dominate (≥50%) the biomass and production in oligotrophic (chlorophyll a [Chl a ] &lt; 0.3 mg m −3 ), nutrient poor (NO 3 + NO 2 &lt; 1 µM), and warm (&gt;26°C) waters, but represent &lt;10% of autotrophic biomass and production in rich (Chl a &gt; 5 mg m −3 ) and cold &lt;3°C) waters.
