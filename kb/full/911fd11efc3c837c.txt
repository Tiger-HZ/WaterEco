Constructed wetlands rely on microscale redox heterogeneity to couple ammonium oxidation with downstream nitrogen loss, yet the causal role of radial oxygen loss (ROL) is often inferred from planting effects, bulk redox potential, functional genes, or outlet performance rather than measured directly. This narrative mechanistic review aims to determine when ROL-generated root oxygen microzones become functionally relevant, how they alter competition and coupling among nitrification, denitrification, anammox, DNRA, and NO/N2O branch points, and whether present evidence supports scaling from individual roots to bed-scale total-nitrogen (TN) performance.
