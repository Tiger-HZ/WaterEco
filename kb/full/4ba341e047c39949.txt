The vertical redistribution of human activities and surface thermal changes poses challenges to Himalayan resilience. Using Nepal’s 77 districts, this study integrates land-use maps for 2000, 2020, and 2023 with annual MODIS land surface temperature (LST) data for 2000–2023. Trend analysis, fixed thermal thresholds, displacement correspondence tests, lag analysis, and GeoDetector were employed to examine land-use restructuring, thermal variability, and their spatial associations. Results show that: (1) Built-up land expanded from 527 to 1817 km2, with its mean elevation increasing from 412 to 617 m.
