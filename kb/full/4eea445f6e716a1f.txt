This study examined sleep quality and its associated factors among clinical nurses in a Grade A tertiary hospital in Jiangsu Province, China. Specifically, it investigated the demographics, lifestyles, health-related issues, psychologies, and administrative factors associated with poor sleep quality and development of nursing-management recommendations based on the findings. A quantitative cross-sectional survey design was used. A total of 150 registered nurses from ten clinical departments participated in the study.
