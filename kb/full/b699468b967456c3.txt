Regional crop coefficients are widely used to estimate agricultural consumptive use, but their ability to represent water use across commercial fields is rarely tested. This study evaluated regional corn crop coefficients in Colorado’s South Platte River Basin against six years (2020–2025) of satellite-derived evapotranspiration from 12,430 field-years processed with the AgroET model. The benchmark is itself AgroET-derived, so the analysis assesses consistency with a field-scale satellite benchmark rather than independently validated accuracy.
