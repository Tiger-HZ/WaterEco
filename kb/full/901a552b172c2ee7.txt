Climate change is causing extreme hydrological events in Central Asia (CA) and influencing the hydrological characteristics of river basins, flood risks, and water resource management. The goal of this systematic review is to synthesize the current knowledge on hydrological extremes and flood-related hazards in CA under changing hydro-climatic conditions, including an understanding of the dominant hydro-climatic processes, regional variability, methodological approaches, quality of the evidence, and implications for climate adaptation in CA. A PRISMA-guided systematic review and structured evidence appraisal framework were used to identify studies for this review from January 2021 to December 2025, published in peer-reviewed journals.
