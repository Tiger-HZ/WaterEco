Abstract Purpose of Review This commentary summarizes terminology and practice standards for assessing the validity of test scores obtained during neuropsychological evaluations. It also highlights conceptual gaps and emerging directions for improving validity assessment. Recent Findings The process of assessing the accuracy and interpretability of psychometric test scores is foundational to neuropsychology. However, terminology used to communicate these findings requires conceptual refinement to align with evolving practice standards and advances in psychometric theory. Such terminology must also reflect that validity problems are not reduced to poor effort or volitional exaggeration.
