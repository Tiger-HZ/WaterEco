User satisfaction is an important indicator of library service success and is influenced by service quality and available facilities. This study aims to analyze the effect of service quality and library facilities on user satisfaction at Taman Ismail Marzuki Library, Central Jakarta. A quantitative approach was employed with 100 respondents selected using accidental sampling. Data were collected through a Likert-scale questionnaire and analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics 26 through validity, reliability, classical assumption, multiple linear regression, t-test, F-test, and coefficient of determination analyses. The findings indicate that service quality has a positive and significant effect on user satisfaction, while library faciities also have a positive and significant effect. Simultaneo
