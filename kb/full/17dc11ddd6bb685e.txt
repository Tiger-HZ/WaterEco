Background and Objective: Postharvest deterioration after ripening severely limits the marketability of 'Kluai Khai' banana (Musa acuminata, AA group). This study evaluated whether an indole-3-acetic acid (IAA)-containing, cell-free solution produced by Micrococcus yunnanensis P2-23 and temperature phasing (TP) could maintain postharvest quality.Methodology: A 4 × 2 factorial completely randomized design with three biological replicates combined with four nominal Salkowski-estimated IAA-equivalent concentrations (0, 250, 500, and 1,000 ppm) with non-TP and TP regimes. After a 3-min dip and storage at 12 ± 2°C for 14 days, fruit were ripened either continuously at 25°C or under TP (25, 22, 20, and 18°C on days 1–4, respectively, followed by 25°C).
