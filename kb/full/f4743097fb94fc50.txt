Purpose The aim of this study is to perform an advanced failure modes and effects analysis on a hybrid solar photovoltaic wiring (HSPW) system to ensure a sustainability-oriented risk framework that identifies and prioritizes failures based on various factors. Design/methodology/approach The proposed study integrates three techniques – Failure Modes and Effects Analysis, Pareto Analysis and the Fuzzy Analytic Hierarchy Process to systematically identify, prioritize and rank critical failure modes using several data sources, including field failure data, literature review and expert evaluations.
