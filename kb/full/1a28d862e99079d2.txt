The graphene/MoS2 composite is a functional heterostructure with broad applications that leverage the synergistic properties of its constituents. Generally, small ambient molecules might penetrate and adsorb into the interlayer of graphene/MoS2 and interact with surface defects, leading to chemical reactions within the confined spaces. Currently, the interfacial interaction and reaction mechanisms remain largely unclear. Herein, we employed density functional theory and ab initio molecular dynamics simulations to investigate the interfacial adsorptions and reactions of O2 and H2O molecules at the Graphene/MoS2 interfaces with various vacancy types of MoS2 surfaces.
