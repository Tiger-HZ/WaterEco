Background: Delayed cord clamping (DCC) is recommended for infants who do not require immediate resuscitation, but reliable implementation requires coordination across delivery-room teams. This quality-improvement project aimed to increase DCC uptake at AUBMC to 80%, promote clamping at ≥60 s and improve staff knowledge. Methods: Monthly aggregate records from January 2024 to February 2025 were analyzed in a single-center, uncontrolled QI evaluation. A multidisciplinary intervention package, reported to have commenced in September 2024, combined education, timing support, protocol/checklist development and electronic documentation initiatives.
