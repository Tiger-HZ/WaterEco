Shopee is the e-commerce platform with the largest number of users in Indonesia, making its application quality an important subject for objective evaluation based on established standards. This study aims to analyze the quality of the Shopee e-commerce application based on the ISO/IEC 25010 standard, focusing on three characteristics: Functional Suitability, Usability, and Performance Efficiency. This research applies a descriptive quantitative approach using three instruments. Functional Suitability was tested through Black Box Testing on eight main features elaborated into 16 test cases. Usability was measured using a System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire with a 1-5 Likert scale distributed to 105 active Shopee users selected through purposive sampling.
