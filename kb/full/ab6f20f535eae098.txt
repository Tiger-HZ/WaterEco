Abstract This study addresses the resource waste and environmental pollution resulting from the conventional disposal of waste Chinese medicine residues (WCMRs) and examines the feasibility of high-value resource recovery using pyrolysis technology. Products obtained through oxygen-constrained pyrolysis of underground (UM) and above-ground (GM) biomass were compared systematically, and a catalyst, CaO, was added to enhance the production of syngas. Several characterization methods and batch adsorption experiments were used to comprehensively assess the performance and composition of the three-phase products.
