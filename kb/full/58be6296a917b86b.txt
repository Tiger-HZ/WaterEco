Silver chalcohalide antiperovskites have emerged as low-toxicity semiconductors with strong near-infrared absorption, although their unusual composition-dependent optical properties, including inverse bandgap bowing, remain poorly understood. Here, Ag3SI1-xBrx solid solutions were synthesized by a solvent-free mechanochemical route and investigated by structural and optical characterization, ultrafast spectroscopy, and first-principles calculations. While all compositions retain an average cubic Pm-3m structure and exhibit a Vegard-like lattice contraction with increasing Br content, strongly nonlinear optical behavior and multiple absorption onsets are observed.
