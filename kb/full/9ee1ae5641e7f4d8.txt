Abstract Soybean meal (SM), an agricultural byproduct, exhibits promising renewable resources for developing biobased adhesives. However, its undesirable hot-pressing efficiency and poor water resistance restricted industrial applications of SM and SM-based materials. Herein, an efficient SM adhesive was fabricated by incorporating blocked polyurethane (BPU) as a reinforcement into the cocrosslinked polyamidoamine-epichlorohydrin (PAE) with melamine-urea-glyoxal (MUG) resin. The addition protocol of epichlorohydrin (ECH) tailored the PAE structure by avoiding ECH hydrolysis, by which a PAE resin (PAEc) with promoted cocrosslinking efficiency was obtained. Adding BPU resin into the cocrosslinked SM adhesive formed denser multicrosslinked networks, which increased the hot-pressing efficiency
