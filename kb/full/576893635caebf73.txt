Background Cataract is one of the main causes of visual impairment and reversible blindness worldwide, and it mainly affects the elderly population. Clinically accurate and sufficiently readable patient education materials play a crucial role in this regard. With the rapid development of large language models (LLMs), patients are increasingly obtaining health information generated by artificial intelligence (AI); however, the reliability of online medical information is often questionable. This study systematically evaluated the readability, quality, and educational suitability of mainstream LLMs when answering questions related to cataract.
