Abstract Unripe plantain is an important staple food in sub-Saharan Africa but is limited by its relatively low protein and dietary fibre contents. This study investigated the potential of millet bran and defatted soybean flour to improve the nutritional, functional and sensory quality of plantain-based dough meals. Four optimized composite flour formulations selected using response surface methodology were processed into dough meals alongside a 100% plantain control and evaluated for proximate composition, mineral profile, pasting properties, colour characteristics, sensory quality and multivariate relationships.
