In this paper, the importance of rain and temperature on the performance of reed beds is examined. Data for 5‐day biochemical oxygen demand (BOD 5 ), total suspended solids (TSS), and ammonia‐nitrogen (NH 3 –N) removal from 16 different reed beds in England were correlated with local weather data for rainfall and temperature. Linear regression and curvilinear regression methods were used to assess if any correlation existed. Data analysis occurred in three stages. First, the equation used for the design of the beds was transformed to a linear equation to compare BOD 5 removal values and monthly average ambient temperature.
