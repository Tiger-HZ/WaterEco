There are basic understanding and data gaps on the suitability of crude palm oil (CPO) from Bayelsa State as a resource in margarine formulation. This study is part of a fundamental step in addressing these gaps, hence, a preliminary investigation into the suitability of Bayelsa crude palm oil for margarine production. The extracted CPO was subjected to refining and emulsification processes to produce the desired oil phase, following standard protocols and quality assurance in margarine production. The effect of combined ratios of oil phase (OP) and aqueous phase (AP) at increasing AP of 10 to 30% was investigated.
