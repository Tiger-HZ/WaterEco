Pressure-driven membrane techniques are increasingly being applied to natural water treatment as an alternative to conventional physico-chemical processes. This makes it essential to compare the effectiveness of these two approaches, particularly under long-term operational conditions. In the preliminary phase of the study, short-term tests were conducted to select the most suitable membranes based on their separation and transport properties: two out of three microfiltration (MF) membranes, two out of three ultrafiltration (UF) membranes, and one out of two nanofiltration (NF) membranes were chosen for subsequent long-term tests.
