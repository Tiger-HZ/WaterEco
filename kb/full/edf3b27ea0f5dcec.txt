Abstract Evaporation from homogeneous and multilayered soil profiles was studied under laboratory conditions using columns packed with different sand types and containers packed with glass beads of different sizes. The impact of positive and negative permeability gradients, and the effect of layer thickness ( H ), has been investigated. The cumulative evaporation from the fine‐to‐coarse ( F‐C ) multilayered profile is higher than the one from the coarse‐to‐fine ( C‐F ) profile. The homogeneous columns evaporate more or less than the multilayered ones depending on the thickness of the layers and the characteristic length of the soil in the top layer.
