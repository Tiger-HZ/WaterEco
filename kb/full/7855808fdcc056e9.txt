The Davita structure is an oil, gas, and condensate producer located in the Davita Field within the Kutai Basin, East Kalimantan, it was discovered in 1974 and commercial production began under PT.X Indonesia in 1991. Situated onshore within the Mahakam Delta system, the field holds Initial Oil in Place (IOIP) of 321 MMSTB and Initial Gas in Place (IGIP) of 1.6 TCF. The Davita structure comprises approximately 4600 reservoirs consisting of stacked sandstone units ranging from 2 to 100 feet in thickness with permeability values of 1-5000 mD and porosity of 7-22%. Production from the Davita structure is derived from the D-series through J-series layers.
