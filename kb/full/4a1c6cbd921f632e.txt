On sloping terrain, the geometry of a structure is irregular, and the load distribution is uneven, which poses significant engineering challenges to construction. When lateral forces, such as earthquakes, act on a structure constructed on sloping ground, the response of the structure can differ significantly from that of a building constructed on a plan surface, increasing the potential for structural instability or failure. The study indicates that dynamic analysis is carried out to investigate the seismic behaviour of structures built on inclined ground and the effect of slope inclination on the structure behaviour is studied and the advantage of shear wall placed at different placement is assessed as a control measure. Several models are developed and analyzed under seismic loadings using software SAP 2000. The results of the analyzed model show that the inclination of the slope and the position of the shear wall affect the seismic performance of the inclined side structure and provide recommendations for improving the stability of the structure.
