Abstract Patch potentials on conductive surfaces introduce significant electrostatic disturbances in high-precision experiments, including space-based gravitational wave detection. In this work, we systematically investigate the effects of various surface treatment processes---including organic cleaning, acid cleaning, ion sputtering, thermal annealing, gold coating, and atmospheric exposure---on the surface potential distribution of the AuPt alloys that were developed for fabricating the test masses used in space-based gravitational wave detection.
