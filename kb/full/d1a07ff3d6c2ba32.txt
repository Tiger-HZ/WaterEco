Service quality is a key determinant of organizational success, particularly in public institutions that provide technical services. This study aims to describe the service quality at the Bandung Center for Textiles from the perspective of service users and to identify the factors that shape their perceptions. Prior studies on service quality have predominantly relied on quantitative satisfaction indices, which have not fully captured users' lived experiences, expectations, and complaints, revealing a gap that this study addresses. A descriptive qualitative approach was employed, involving five purposively selected informants: the head of the institution, a service officer, and three service users.
