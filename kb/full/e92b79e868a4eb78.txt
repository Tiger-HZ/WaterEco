Abstract Background Quality of life may deteriorate in long-term breast cancer survivors after treatment. Evidence on the long-term effectiveness of eHealth interventions remains limited. This study aimed to evaluate the longer-term effect of CUMACA-M on overall quality of life over a 9-month follow-up period among long-term breast cancer survivors, compared with usual care. Methods A parallel-group randomized clinical trial was conducted in Spain from November 2023 to January 2025. A total of 201 disease-free long-term breast cancer survivors (≥5 years post-diagnosis) were randomly assigned to a 9-month eHealth intervention (n = 102) or usual care (n = 99).
