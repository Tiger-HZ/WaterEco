This study aimed to analyze the spatial and temporal variations in water quality status and eutrophication level of Lake Aha, influenced by human activities and seasonal changes. Ten indicators were sampled and analyzed at seven sampling points in Lake Aha in different seasons and vertical layers. The eutrophication level and water quality status were evaluated using the Trophic Level Index (TLI) and Water Quality Index (WQI). Results showed that the average TLI and WQI values during the wet season (July) were 31.06 and 92.14, respectively, compared to 28.64 and 109.14 during the dry season (April).
