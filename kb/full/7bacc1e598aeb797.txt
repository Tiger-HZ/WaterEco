Background. Thymus vulgaris is a medicinal plant rich in bioactive compounds, particularly thymol and carvacrol, with antimicrobial, antiviral, anti-inflammatory, immunomodulatory, antioxidant, and bronchodilatory properties relevant to respiratory diseases. Aim. To summarize the current evidence on the mechanisms of action and therapeutic potential of Thymus vulgaris in respiratory diseases. Material and methods. A narrative review of review articles, preclinical studies, and clinical trials published between 2000 and 2024 was performed using the PubMed and Google Scholar databases. Results.
