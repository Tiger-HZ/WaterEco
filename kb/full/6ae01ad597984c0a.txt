Medium-chain-length polyhydroxyalkanoates (mcl-PHAs) are biodegradable microbial polyesters with attractive elastomeric properties and considerable potential as sustainable alternatives to fossil-derived polymers. Their industrial production requires not only efficient biomass and biopolymer productivity but also consistent material quality. In this study, Pseudomonas putida KT2442 was cultivated under different nutrient conditions to evaluate the effects of carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and magnesium supplementation on biomass growth, poly(3-hydroxyoctanoate) (PHO) accumulation and its properties.
