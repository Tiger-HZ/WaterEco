ABSTRACT Despite growing interest in γ‐PGA as a soil amendment for salt‐affected soils, its integrated effects on root‐zone water and salt conditions, nitrogen retention and crop productivity remain insufficiently understood. A soil box experiment was conducted to evaluate the effects of four γ‐PGA application rates, designated as CK (0), T1 (0.05%), T2 (0.10%) and T3 (0.15%), on grain yield and resource use efficiency. γ‐PGA significantly increased soil water availability while reducing salt accumulation within the root zone throughout the wheat‐growing season.
