Abstract Where cropland is no longer expanding, few measures jointly reduce fertilizer N, reactive N losses, and irrigation withdrawal. A legume in the summer slot of an existing rotation is one of them. For China, importing about 85% of its soybean, it is also a route to domestic supply. But when unfertilized soybean replaces maize, a season of fertilizer and irrigation is withdrawn with it. In field trials and existing model assessments the two effects are inseparable by design. Addition and substitution act on different land uses, so their outcomes cannot be inferred from one another. Here we simulate soil carbon, nitrogen, food, and water from 2020 to 2100 with gridded LPJ-GUESS over 5035 cells of eastern China's winter wheat belt.
