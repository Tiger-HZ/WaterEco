Vietnam approved a scheme to publish a national suite of public sector accounting standards in 2019 and has since announced sixteen of them, while a new accounting regime for administrative and public service units took effect in the 2025 fiscal year. Whether those units are ready to apply the standards has been asserted repeatedly and assessed rarely, and the assessments that exist rest on perception surveys of small samples. This paper takes a different route. It builds a readiness assessment entirely from the public documentary record, coding seven dimensions against a published rubric that any reader can re-apply, and reports four findings that perception data would not have surfaced.
