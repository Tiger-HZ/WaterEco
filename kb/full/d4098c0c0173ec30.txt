Cyanotoxins, Microcystin LR (MC-LR) and Cylindrospermopsin (CYN) are hepato and nephro toxins and considered as one of the hypotheses for Chronic Kidney Disease of Unknown Etiology (CKDu) in Sri Lanka. Recent studies have revealed that significant number of dug wells in the North Central and Uva provinces where CKDu is prevalent are contaminated with cyanotoxins MC-LR and CYN.
