Clean water is an essential public need, and its provision at the local level is carried out by the Regional Water Supply Company (PDAM). In Kemili Village, Bebesen Subdistrict, Central Aceh Regency, customers of PDAM Tirta Tawar frequently experience service problems, including prolonged water outages, poor water quality, pipe blockages caused by sediment, and slow responses to customer complaints. Of the 268 registered customers in the village, only 90 remain active, while 178 are inactive, despite the overall growth of PDAM customers in Central Aceh Regency from 8,476 in 2020 to 9,035 in 2024.
