Low temperature is one of the critical limiting factors for anammox. To address this, our study investigated whether biochar could enhance the nitrogen metabolism of anammox consortia at 15 °C by serving as an exogenous electron shuttle. Compared with the control, biochar addition effectively improved the total nitrogen removal (520 vs. 450 g-N/(m3·d), p &lt; 0.05), with notable enhancement in nitrate elimination. Specifically, biochar increased the abundance of denitrifying bacteria and anammox bacteria (predominantly Candidatus Kuenenia).
