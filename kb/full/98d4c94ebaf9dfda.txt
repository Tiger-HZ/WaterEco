A pollution status assessment of Amadi-Ama Creek, Port Harcourt, was carried out to determine its water and sediment physico-chemical properties due to the vulnerability of Niger Delta freshwater ecosystems to pollution. A quantitative descriptive research design was used to sample areas of likely impact of contamination along the axis of the creek at low tide during the rainy season. In situ measurements of water temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen (DO), and pH were taken. The grab sampling technique was used to collect water samples, whereas sediment samples were collected with an Ekman Grab.
