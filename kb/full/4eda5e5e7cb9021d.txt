The microbiological hygiene quality of natural cocoa mass is an important consideration for public health safety, economic valorisation, and the sustainability of the cocoa sector in Côte d’Ivoire. During the production of natural cocoa mass, particularly during cocoa bean fermentation, the cocoa mass is exposed to a succession of microorganisms, some of which may indicate faecal contamination or inadequate hygiene practices. Objective: This study aimed to assess the level of hygiene control and the risk of faecal-origin contamination of natural cocoa masses produced in Côte d’Ivoire from March to May 2025, using total and thermotolerant coliforms as microbiological indicators.
