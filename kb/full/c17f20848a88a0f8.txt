Water resource management in Nigeria is a complex issue shaped by its diverse geography, climate variability, and rapid population growth. Despite substantial water resources, the country faces significant challenges in ensuring reliable access to clean and safe water. This paper examines the current status of water resources in Nigeria, highlighting issues such as uneven distribution, infrastructure deficiencies, pollution, and the impacts of climate change. It explores the technological innovations and opportunities that can enhance sustainable water resource management, including smart water management systems, advanced water treatment technologies, efficient irrigation techniques, and integrated water resource management (IWRM).
