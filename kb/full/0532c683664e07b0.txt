Purpose The transition to a net-zero economy is vital for global sustainability, but it could cause social problems if it is not managed carefully. This paper contributes to this pressing concern by examining how climate policy stringency affects just transition and whether governance quality shapes this relationship. Design/methodology/approach A panel data set of 46 countries covering the 2011–2022 period was used for the analysis. Panel-corrected standard errors (PCSE) estimation, which accounts for cross-sectional dependence, heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation, was employed to test the hypotheses.
