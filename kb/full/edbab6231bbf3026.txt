Intelligent inspection technology has become a key support to ensure the safe and stable operation of the power grid. In view of the shortcomings of traditional inspection methods in terms of efficiency, accuracy and adaptability, this paper proposes a multi-source fusion architecture for intelligent inspection for digital power grids. By integrating multi-source heterogeneous information such as Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) remote sensing data, sensor timing information, GIS geographic data and equipment operation and maintenance text, combined with dynamic threshold adjustment, spatiotemporal correlation analysis and cascade attention mechanism, a complete processing process covering data collection, feature extraction, multi-source fusion and decision output is constructed.
