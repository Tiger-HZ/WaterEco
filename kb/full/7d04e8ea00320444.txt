Abstract This study integrates satellite and surface NO₂ measurements through the ensemble square root filter (EnSRF) and the GEOS‑Chem model to estimate anthropogenic NOₓ emissions for China, the contiguous U.S., and Europe over 2019–2020, and further examines how the COVID‑19 pandemic altered emission patterns and sectoral contributions. Our results show that in 2020, anthropogenic NOₓ emissions were 19.20 ± 5.87 Tg in China, 8.96 ± 2.90 Tg in the contiguous U.S., and 10.56 ± 2.95 Tg in Europe. Compared with 2019, emissions decreased by about 5.38% in China, 8.94% in the contiguous U.S., and 15.38% in Europe.
