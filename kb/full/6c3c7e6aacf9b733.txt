Correction: NPK nano-fertilizers 
enhance growth, oil quality, and 
yield regularity in Picual olive trees
Ibrahim Hmmam , Hassan Mahmoud Korkar, Islam Ahmed, Asmaa Gamal Abd El-hamied & 
Ayman Shaban
Correction to: Scientific Reports https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17267-9, published online 12 September 
2025
The original version of this Article contained an error in the Materials and Methods section, where the equation 
was mathematically incorrect because the denominator was incorrectly given as Final fruit set percentage.
“Fruit drop (%) = (Initial fruit set percentage − Final fruit set percentage/Final fruit set percentage) × 100. ”
now reads:
“Fruit drop (%) = [(Initial fruit set percentage − Final fruit set percentage) / Initial fruit set percentage] × 100. ”
The original Article has been corrected.
