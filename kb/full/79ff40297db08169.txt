Road transport is indispensable to urban functioning. As people-centered development and community-oriented planning have gained prominence, pedestrian environments and road network quality have received increasing attention. Existing reviews, however, generally examine either city-scale networks or street-level environments in isolation, leaving a gap in cross-scale synthesis centered on walking needs. Using Web of Science (WOS) as the data source, this review screened 31,650 records and retained 508 publications; CiteSpace 6.4 was used to map the knowledge structure and major research themes.
