The Huaihe River Basin (HRB) lies in the climatic transition zone between northern and southern China. Its precipitation shows obvious spatiotemporal heterogeneity, and frequent meteorological droughts seriously threaten regional food and water security. Clarifying the spatiotemporal variations, non-linear abrupt changes and multi-scale driving mechanisms of meteorological droughts in the basin is of great significance for regional drought risk prevention and control, as well as the optimized allocation of water resources. In this study, the one-month time scale Standardized Precipitation Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI-1) was adopted as the primary indicator for quantifying meteorological drought.
