In the modern digital era, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems have evolved from routine administrative tools into vital strategic assets. However, the direct technological implementation of ERP does not automatically guarantee superior organizational performance unless mediated by the optimal quality of information it produces.This study investigates the complex mechanisms through which ERP Integration influences Managerial Decision-Making Quality, explicitly examining Information Quality as a critical mediating variable. Utilizing a quantitative research design, data were gathered through a cross-sectional survey administered to 150 professionals actively utilizing ERP systems across diverse organizational roles (managers, supervisors, business analysts, and administrative staff).
