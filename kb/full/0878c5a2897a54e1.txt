This study aimed to analyze the effect of seed soaking methods on the growth and quality of bird’s eye chili peppers (Capsicum frutescens L.). The study was conducted in Br. Titigalar, Bangli Village, Baturiti Subdistrict, Tabanan Regency, Bali, from September 2025 to February 2026. The study used a randomized block design with one treatment factor and three replications. The treatments consisted of Bp, which involved soaking the seeds for 12 hours in warm water with an initial temperature of 50 °C; Bg, which involved soaking the seeds in warm water with an initial temperature of 50 °C supplemented with 1 mL L⁻¹ of Golden Grow; and Bc, which involved soaking the seeds in tap water supplemented with 1 mL L⁻¹ of Golden Grow.
