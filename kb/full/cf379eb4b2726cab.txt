The Vishera River (a tributary of the Kama, Volga River basin) is a foothill river characterized by sandy substrates and high current velocities in its lower reaches. Psammorheophilic benthic communities in this river section remain poorly studied, and all available data on macrozoobenthos describe it only for the summer and autumn months. The aim of the study was to identify the species composition of macroinvertebrate fauna and the structure of psammorheophilic benthic communities using various samplers (hydrobiological and entomological methods), with a focus on winter and spring aspects. Based on the processing of macrozoobenthos samples, 59 taxa (56 species) were identified. The application of various additional methods expanded the list to 154 taxa (151 species).
