Abstract Wastewater-based epidemiology (WBE) yields city-level drug loads; national estimates require extrapolation to unmonitored populations and, for amphetamine, separation of the illicit signal from pharmaceutical amphetamine and from amphetamine excreted after methamphetamine use. We combined all German SCORE/EUDA data (16 catchments, 2013–2025, 95 site-weeks of daily amphetamine and methamphetamine loads) in a Bayesian mass-balance model with a hierarchical extrapolation to a frame of treatment-plant size classes by federal state built from the Destatis wastewater statistics.
