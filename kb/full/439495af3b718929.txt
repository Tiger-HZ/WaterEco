Abstract The use of reclaimed water in agriculture is a potential solution to regional water shortages. A controlled lysimeter study investigated the impact of irrigation with reclaimed water on the soil's water and nutrient balance, and the potential impact on drainage water quality for groundwater recharge. Planted and unplanted lysimeters with defined soil profiles were irrigated with reclaimed or tap water for 12 months (August 2024 to August 2025). The reclaimed water was obtained from a membrane bioreactor. Water balance (precipitation, irrigation and potential evapotranspiration) and drainage water formation were recorded and sampled weekly.
