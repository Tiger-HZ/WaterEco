The relocation and closure of electroplating industries have generated numerous contaminated brownfields, many of which are located in areas with potential for residential and commercial redevelopment. However, information regarding the distribution characteristics, migration, and health risks of heavy metals at decommissioned electroplating sites remain limited. In this study, 160 soil samples were collected from a decommissioned electroplating factory in Dezhou, Shandong Province, China, to characterize the spatial distribution and migration behavior of Ni, Zn, and Cr(VI), identify contamination hotspots and potential pollution sources, and evaluate associated carcinogenic and non-carcinogenic risks under the planned future land-use scenario.
