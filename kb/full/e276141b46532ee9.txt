The development of sustainable nanomaterials is essential for efficient wastewater treatment. This study evaluates silver nanoparticles (AgNPs), synthesized through an ecofriendly green route using Algerian propolis extract, as reusable nanoadsorbents for the rapid removal of Basic Blue 41 (BB41) from aqueous solutions. AgNPs were characterized before and after adsorption using UV–Vis, XRD, FTIR, SEM/EDS, and XPS. Adsorption performance was tested alongside thermodynamic, regeneration, and density functional theory (DFT) analyses. Characterization confirmed the face-centered cubic structure of metallic silver and dye uptake via propolis-derived surface functional groups.
