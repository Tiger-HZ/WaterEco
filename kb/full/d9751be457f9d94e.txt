Abstract Purpose: To characterize 12-month patient-reported quality-of-life (QoL) trajectories in NMIBC patients undergoing repeat transurethral resection (re-TURBT), and to describe patterns of early burden and subsequent recovery. Methods: Retrospective multicenter cohort (n = 90 NMIBC patients, 5 centres, 5 time points over 12 months). SF-36, HADS, and EORTC QLQ-NMIBC24 scored per official manuals. BH-adjusted Wilcoxon comparisons (per-subscale, family of four); Cohen’s d_z with bootstrap 95% CI (5,000 iterations); GEE sensitivity. A conservative no-change imputation scenario served as missing-data sensitivity.
