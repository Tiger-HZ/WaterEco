Background: Chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease are rising in South Asia, with poor diet a major contributor. Most dietary apps are designed for Western foods and struggle with culturally diverse dishes such as Pakistani cuisine. Objective: This study aimed to build a machine learning-based system that can recognise Pakistani dishes, estimate portion sizes, and calculate their nutritional value. Methods: We developed a multi-stage automated dietary assessment pipeline for Pakistani cuisine using a multi-source dataset covering 83 dish classes across 10 categories. Following data cleaning, class balancing, and targeted augmentation, deep learning architectures were evaluated for food classification and segmentation.
