Abstract. The understanding of the interactions between the lower and upper compartments of the Critical Zone (CZ) is of major importance to the maintenance of life across continental landscapes. The extent to which deep root uptake supplies nutrients from depth and redistributes them to the entire ecosystem remains unknown. Identifying the processes at stake and quantifying their effects on nutrients cycles is of paramount importance for assessing ecosystem resilience and designing management practices enhancing biologically mediated CZ processes in agrosystems.
