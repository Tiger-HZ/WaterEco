The Ogan River, part of the Musi River Basin, plays a vital role in the urban environment and the lives of the people of Palembang. It receives pollution from residential areas, domestic waste, tie-dye production, rubber processing, river transport, and coal mining. This study aimed to analyze water quality, identify dominant pollutant parameters, evaluate spatial patterns of pollution, and determine potential pollution sources and management priorities for the Ogan River. The study was conducted at eight locations based on land use characteristics and activities along the river. The parameters analyzed included physical characteristics, chemical characteristics, heavy metals, and inorganic compounds.
