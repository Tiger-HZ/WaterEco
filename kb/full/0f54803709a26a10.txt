Background: Hospitals are resource-intensive institutions that generate considerable environmental impacts and incur high operational costs. Cleaner Production (CP), originally developed for industrial sectors, offers a preventive strategy for improving resource efficiency while minimizing waste and environmental risks. Objective: To present a leadership- and management-oriented framework for implementing Cleaner Production in hospitals, with particular relevance to the Sri Lankan health-care system. Methods: This paper synthesizes national policies, World Health Organization guidance, published literature, and international best practices to develop a practical framework for Cleaner Production implementation in hospital settings. Results: The proposed framework emphasizes leadership commitment, establishment of hospital Cleaner Production committees, staff engagement, operational audits, resource optimization, infrastructure improvements, and continuous monitoring using performance indicators. The framework supports phased implementation beginning with low-cost interventions and progressing to long-term sustainability investments. Conclusion: Cleaner Production is an effective management strategy that improves operational efficiency, reduces costs, enhances environmental performance, and strengthens institutional resilience in Sri Lankan hospitals. Successful implementation depends on committed leadership, structured governance, staff participation, and continuous performance monitoring.
