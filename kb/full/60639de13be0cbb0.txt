Printed product surface defect inspection faces critical challenges in real industrial production, including insufficient detection accuracy, poor robustness, and scarcity of labeled defective samples. This paper proposes an improved Siamese network for high-resolution printed image defect detection to address the above limitations. Original 1300 × 460-pixel printed images are segmented into 64 × 64 patches to construct a training and testing dataset. We optimize the feature measurement strategy of contrastive loss and integrate a position matching module and multi-threshold evaluation strategy to balance detection precision and stability.
