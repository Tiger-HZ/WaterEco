Forest and land fires in Southern Riau, Indonesia, vary seasonally with environmental and landscape conditions. This study evaluated whether separate seasonal models reveal susceptibility patterns not captured by an all-season model and whether preceding environmental change adds predictive information. All-season, dry-season, and rainy-season models were developed from multi-source satellite and geospatial data for 2010–2024 using Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM under condition-only and condition-change configurations. Models used a 1:5 fire-to-non-fire ratio, a stratified 70:30 train–test split, and PR-AUC as the primary metric.
