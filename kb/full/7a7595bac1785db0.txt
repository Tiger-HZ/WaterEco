This study investigated the water quality of the Otuoke River in Bayelsa State, Nigeria, during high and low tides. Like many coastal communities, Otuoke has relied on this river for its water needs. However, the community has not known the river’s quality status, especially during different tidal regimes. This research addressed the pressing question of which season is more viable for communal use. The study employed both quantitative and qualitative methods. It included water quality analysis using standard laboratory methods. Water samples were collected from five locations along the river during both low and high tides.
