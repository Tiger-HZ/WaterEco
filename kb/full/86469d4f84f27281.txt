The valorization of agri-food industry waste is an important approach to obtaining value-added materials for agricultural applications while supporting the principles of a circular economy. Such waste may contain valuable nutrients and biologically active compounds, including plant hormones. This study aimed to evaluate the phytohormone content of post-production residues from the agri-food industry and to assess the biostimulatory potential of the material with the highest total phytohormone concentration that was also available in sufficient quantities as a post-production residue in oat (Avena sativa L.) cultivation.
