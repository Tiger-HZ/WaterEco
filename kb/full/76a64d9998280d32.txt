Abstract We used statistical models to predict the distributions of 15 native diadromous fish species across New Zealand's river and stream network, and demonstrate their potential use for guiding the restoration of freshwater ecosystems. Models were fitted to an extensive collection of field samples describing the distributions of individual fish species, coupled with a set of environmental predictors chosen primarily for their functional relevance to diadromous fish species. Models were fitted to observations of species occurrence using boosted regression trees (BRT), an advanced regression technique that combines excellent predictive performance with good description of relationships between species occurrence and individual environmental predictors.
