Advances in renewable and sustainable energy technologies

The transition to renewable and sustainable energy sources is 
a global imperative in the fight against environmental degra-
dation and climate change. The depleting fossil-fuel reserves 
and rising energy demand pose challenges to energy secu-
rity and contribute to increasing concerns over energy costs. 
Additionally, emissions from conventional energy resources 
contribute significantly to environmental deterioration and 
raise grave health concerns. Greenhouse gases generated 
by these energy resources aggravate global warming, while 
 NOx and particulate matter are linked to respiratory ail-
ments. To address these pressing issues, research efforts are 
being directed toward the development of cleaner energy 
technologies, renewable feedstocks, advanced catalysts and 
electrocatalysts, sustainable energy-storage systems, waste 
valorization strategies, and environmentally responsible pro-
duction processes.
The second edition of International Conference on Novel 
Materials and Technologies for Energy and Environment 
Applications (NMTE2A-2024) was organized by Birla Insti-
tute of Technology and Science, Pilani Hyderabad Campus 
from 17 to 18 February 2024. The conference provided an 
interdisciplinary forum encompassing diverse aspects of 
emerging materials, energy technologies, and environmental 
applications. It was our privilege to welcome more than 250 
participants during the conference. The conference featured 
leading scientists from academia and industry who discussed 
recent developments, emerging challenges, and future direc-
tions in energy and environmental research. More than 200 
submissions were received for NMTE2A-2024, alongside 5 
keynote speeches and 20 invited talks. Following the confer-
ence, selected contributions were invited for consideration 
in this Special Issue, subject to the journal’s peer-review and 
editorial processes. Forty-five articles were submitted to this 
issue, of which nine were accepted for publication following 
rigorous peer review.
The Special Issue entitled “Advances in Renewable and 
Sustainable Energy Technologies” brings together nine 
research articles addressing interconnected aspects of sus-
tainable energy, environmental protection, resource recov -
ery, and sustainable materials. Collectively, the contribu-
tions reflect the interdisciplinary nature of research at the 
intersection of energy, environmental sustainability, resource 
efficiency, and sustainable materials. The articles cover five 
broad and interconnected themes:
• Hydrogen and Catalytic Energy Conversion: Methane 
cracking for hydrogen production and catalytic conver -
sion of biogas through dry reforming.
• Electrocatalysis and Sustainable Energy Materials: 
Development of photo/electrocatalytic materials for 
hydrogen evolution, oxygen reduction, and urea oxida-
tion.
• Life-Cycle Assessment and Environmental Sustainabil-
ity: Life-cycle assessment of energy-storage technologies 
and food-production systems to evaluate environmental 
impacts and sustainability.
