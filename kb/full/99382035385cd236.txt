Groundwater quality is a critical factor constraining the sustainable and healthy development of the economy, particularly in regions with scarce water resources. Given the combined influences of natural processes and human activities, understanding the hydrochemical evolution and pollution status of groundwater in the Yinchuan Plain is essential for effective groundwater resource management in this area. This study collected 94 phreatic groundwater samples and integrated traditional hydrochemistry methods, the entropy-weighted water quality index (EWQI), and the human health risk assessment (HHRA) model to analyze the hydrochemical characteristics, water quality, and sulfate- and nitrate-related health risks in the Yinchuan Plain.
