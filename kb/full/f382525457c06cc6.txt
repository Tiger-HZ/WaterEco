Abstract Groundwater vulnerability assessment is a key tool for sustainable water resource management, especially in urban and agricultural aquifers under stress. In this study, the Fuzzy DRASTIC+ model was developed for the first time to examine the vulnerability of the Karaj aquifer by combining eleven criteria. These criteria include the main DRASTIC parameters (water table depth, net recharge, aquifer environment, soil texture, slope, unsaturated zone influence, and hydraulic conductivity) and four supplementary criteria (chloride, electrical conductivity, total dissolved solids, and land use).
