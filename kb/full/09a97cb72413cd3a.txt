Hydroclimatic extremes are projected to intensify across the Iberian Peninsula (IP) under future climate change, challenging water resources, agriculture, and ecosystems. This study evaluates future conditions using mean precipitation plus three precipitation-based indices: Consecutive Dry Days (CDD), number of dry spells (CDDn), and R95pTOT (Very Wet Days). Projections used a high-resolution CHELSA-ISIMIP3b CMIP6 ensemble under three Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSP1–2.6, SSP3–7.0, and SSP5–8.5) and three future periods (2041–2060, 2061–2080, and 2081–2100). A hydroclimatic stress framework based on historical CDD and the fraction of the R95pTOT quintiles was applied at grid-cell and watershed scales.
