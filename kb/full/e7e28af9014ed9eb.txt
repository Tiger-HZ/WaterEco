Background. Saliva analysis is considered a useful non-invasive tool for monitoring physiological reactions to exercise stress in athletes. Aim. This review was intended to assess the exercise-induced alterations in salivary composition and functions and their significance for athletic performance and oral health. Material and methods. This paper searched the literature in electronic databases, including PubMed, using keywords: “Salivary Changes”, "Salivary Biomarkers”, "Exercise", “Athletes”. Articles included randomized controlled trials, meta-analyses, and systematic reviews since 1995 supplemented by randomized crossover trials, pilot studies, and prospective cohort studies. Results.
