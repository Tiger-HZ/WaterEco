To estimate triazine herbicide concentrations and sources in the St. Lawrence River, water samples were collected at its two major inlets (from the Great Lakes, Cornwall station, and from the Ottawa River, Carillon station) and at the outlet (Quebec City station) of the fluvial section. Sampling was carried out over an 18-month period between 1995 and 1996. Triazines were detected only in the dissolved phase at concentrations ranging from 2 to 91, from &lt;0.4 to 15, and from &lt;0.4 to 13 ng·L -1 for atrazine, cyanazine, and simazine, respectively. Dilution models show that, despite the presence of sporadically high concentrations of herbicides in St. Lawrence tributaries during periods of their application, loading from the tributaries is minor.
