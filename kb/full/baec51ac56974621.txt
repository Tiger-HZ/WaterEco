ABSTRACT This study proposes a dynamic Bayesian networks (DBN)‐guided adaptive reliability experimental design framework for dynamic reliability learning and uncertainty reduction in complex engineering systems under operational and budgetary constraints. The methodology integrates DBN, Bayesian experimental design, entropy‐based uncertainty quantification, expected information gain, and utility‐based decision theory into a unified probabilistic framework capable of sequentially selecting the most informative experiments according to their expected contribution to uncertainty reduction while considering implementation costs. The proposed approach transforms reliability analysis from a passive estimation process into an active learning methodology capable of guiding adaptive experimentation
