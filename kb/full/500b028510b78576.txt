IOb-SoC-Linux is an open-source framework for configuring and generating Linux-capable System-on-Chip (SoC) designs targeting RISC-V processors. Built on the Py2HWSW hardware/software co-design framework, it provides a specialized SoC template that contrasts with bare-metal-focused alternatives, offering a reproducible environment for the design, integration, and testing of Intellectual Property (IP) cores under a full Linux software stack.
