The secondary production of rocky shore macroinvertebrate assemblages impacted through sewage discharge was assessed, taking into account the trends of production among dominant species and feeding guilds. The present study was conducted on the Peniche peninsula (central-western Portuguese coast, temperate region), in three areas: one area near a sewage discharge and two undisturbed reference areas. Within each area, three intertidal zones were monitored - littoral fringe, eulittoral and sublittoral fringe - by taking seasonal samples during one year. The empirical model of Cusson and Bourget (2005) was used to evaluate secondary production.
