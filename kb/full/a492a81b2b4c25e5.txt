The Yangtze, Asia’s longest river, supports ecosystems and people, but rising stress from development is degrading water quality and altering algae. Using 2016–2021 water quality data from 83 sites and 2021 phytoplankton (55 sites) and periphyton (44 sites) surveys, this study analyzes the spatiotemporal variations in water quality and algal communities in the Yangtze River mainstream and the driving mechanisms via Mann–Kendall trends, random forest modeling, and hierarchical partitioning. The results revealed significant long-term declines in TN, TP, ammonia nitrogen, and permanganate indices, with a rising water quality index (WQI), reflecting effective basin-wide pollution control.
