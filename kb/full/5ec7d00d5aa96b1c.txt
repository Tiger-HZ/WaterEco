ABSTRACT Fatalities occur each year at low‐head dams (LHDs) due to the powerful churning of submerged hydraulic jumps. LHDs are small structures that allow water to flow over the crest continuously from bank to bank and are ubiquitous in the United States. They are often used to increase the water depth in a river for irrigation and water supply or to produce hydropower. The purpose of this paper is to increase awareness, promote river safety, and inform remediation efforts at LHDs through an updated database and website documenting fatalities at these structures. The website and database are found at www.lowheaddamfatalities.org . The approach used is based on the work of Kern et al. utilizes modern software.
