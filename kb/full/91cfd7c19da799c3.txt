Post-mining void represents critical environmental challenges due to water contamination, unstable slopes, and limited ecological recovery. These problems persist in many coal mining areas in Indonesia, threatening surrounding ecosystems and communities. Addressing these challenges requires an integrated reclamation approach that combines ecological and socio-economic functions. Therefore, this study aimed to develop an integrated reclamation model using Eichhornia crassipes (water hyacinth) as a phytoremediation agent for sustainable post-mining void management. A qualitative literature synthesis was conducted using Scopus, Web of Science (WoS), and Garuda databases, focusing on peer-reviewed articles published between 2017 and 2025.
