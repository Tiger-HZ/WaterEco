Background As survival into adulthood improves for patients with congenital heart disease (CHD), quality of that survival is paramount. We assessed health‐related quality of life (HRQL) in a large adult CHD cohort. Methods We studied 868 participants from a multicenter registry with complete Pediatric Quality of Life Inventory 4.0 Generic Core Scale and covariate data. Analyses followed 3 stages: CHD complexity across HRQL score bands, predictors of worse or better HRQL, and predictors of HRQL in severe CHD. Results Pediatric Quality of Life Inventory total scores were high (median 76.1, interquartile range 64.1–87.0), highest in Social functioning, with longer lower tails for Emotional and Psychosocial scales.
