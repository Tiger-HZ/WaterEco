SOFTWARE TO ENCOURAGE POSITIVE ORAL 
HEALTH BEHAVIOUR IN CHILDREN
Hello Pearl, a global leader in AI-powered 
dental diagnostics, has introduced Second 
Opinion for Kids, a paediatric-focused 
version of its clinically validated AI-assisted 
radiologic detection platform designed to 
enhance patient communication, improve 
engagement and support earlier intervention 
in children’s oral healthcare.
Building on the existing paediatric 
capabilities of the Second Opinion platform, 
the new software analyses dental radiographs 
in real time to identify radiographic findings 
consistent with dental pathology, including 
early carious lesions. While maintaining 
the platform’s established AI-driven 
diagnostic support, Second Opinion for Kids 
incorporates child-friendly visual overlays 
using animated ‘Sugar Bugs’ characters to help 
clinicians communicate radiographic findings 
in a way that is accessible to younger patients.
The platform has been developed to 
address one of paediatric dentistry’s ongoing 
challenges: effectively communicating clinical 
findings to children while 
reducing dental anxiety and 
improving cooperation during 
examination and treatment 
planning. By presenting 
radiographic findings in an 
engaging and age-appropriate 
format, the software aims to 
facilitate shared understanding 
between clinicians, children and 
their parents or carers.
Second Opinion for Kids 
represents a further evolution 
of Hello Pearl’s AI-assisted diagnostic 
technology, which supports clinicians in 
identifying pathology and monitoring areas 
requiring ongoing review in paediatric 
patients from as young as four years of age. 
Earlier identification of developing disease 
enables timely preventive intervention and 
minimally invasive treatment strategies, 
supporting improved long-term oral health 
outcomes.
By combining AI-assisted radiographic 
interpretation with child-centred patient 
communication, Second Opinion for Kids 
provides clinicians with a tool to support 
diagnosis, enhance patient education and 
improve acceptance of preventive treatment 
recommendations. The software is intended 
to strengthen communication, reduce 
treatment-related anxiety and encourage 
positive oral health behaviours from an 
early age.

ASSURED WATER QUALITY
Join the Dentisan Clean Water Club for a 
more efficient way of managing your dental 
unit water lines (DUWLs).
The process is simple: use Bioclear 
Biological Dip Slides to identify microbial 
contamination, upload an image of the tested 
dip slide to the Clean Water Club portal, and 
await a report from Dentisan’s in-house lab 
technicians. 
Supporting ease of use and ensuring 
excellent infection control outcomes, 
members of the Clean Water Club can stay 
compliant, with dip slide deliveries serving as 
a reminder to conduct DUWL testing. Pass or 
fail reports are issued by the Dentisan team 
to keep practices on top of their 
paperwork too. 
A free service that also offers 
up to 30% off Dentisan waterline 
contamination solutions, the 
Clean Water Club is a reliable 
way to maintain the water quality 
of your dental units and better 
protect patients and fellow members of the 
dental team – join today.

KEEP WORKING 
HANDS CLEAN AND 
HEALTHY
Achieve correct hand 
hygiene outcomes with 
the help of Dentisan and 
its comprehensive product 
range that’s designed to 
keep working hands clean 
and healthy. 
To reduce the risk of 
infection transmission, 
DentiGel is a ready-
to-use disinfectant that should be used 
following hand washing. Adding an extra 
layer of protection from pathogens in the 
dental practice, including coronaviruses, 
its effective formula contains emollients 
and skin conditioners that moisturise the 
hands, preventing soreness and irritation. 
An excellent ally during a busy 
workflow, DentiGel is quick drying and 
easy to dispense. It also comes with the 
Dentisan stamp of approval: DentiGel 
complies with BS EN 1500 (European hand 
sanitiser standards) and is HTM 01-05 
compliant. 
Available in 500 ml pump dispensers, 
DentiGel can also be bought in 800 ml 
pouches for use with a touchless dispenser 
– this is an effective way to ensure the 
correct amount of DentiGel is released.
