Purpose This study aims to investigate the effect of service quality on customer satisfaction in fast-food restaurant services. Design/methodology/approach A cross-sectional, online, self-administered survey was conducted among 300 fast-food customers in Bangkok, Thailand. The questionnaire was based on the DINESERV framework, which classifies service quality into five dimensions: tangibles, reliability, responsiveness, assurance and empathy. The constructs were validated using confirmatory factor analysis before multiple linear regression analyses were performed.
