Vertical eddy diffusion coefficients (K z ) were determined for the surface waters at several sites in Lake Ontario and along the Upper St. Lawrence River using the water column distribution patterns of hydrogen peroxide. Values of K z ranged from 0.45 × 10 -3 to 23 × 10 -3 m 2 ·s -1 in Lake Ontario and from 0.75 × 10 -3 to 2.1 × 10 -3 m 2 ·s -1 along the St. Lawrence River. The residence time for bacterioplankton and phytoplankton in the surface waters was then determined from the K z values and incorporated into a spectral model to determine the continuous biologically effective exposure to ultraviolet radiation (E* UVR ).
