Abstract Anthropogenic modifications have disrupted aquatic ecosystems by modification of the flow regime and transport of sediment. The impacts of dams are believed to be very large, but, despite the abundance of dams in monsoonal Asia, great scientific uncertainty still exists about the effect of dams on macroinvertebrates. Therefore, we studied macroinvertebrate assemblages at three reaches (downstream of a dam, downstream of a confluence and on a tributary) of the Yahagi River, central Japan, to confirm the impact of long‐term impoundment on the relationships between macroinvertebrate assemblages and biotic and abiotic environmental factors and also the role of a major tributary in community shift.
