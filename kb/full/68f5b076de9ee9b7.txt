The Iraqi banking sector suffers from various problems, particularly borrowers' defaults on their financial obligations, which has negatively impacted the quality of banking assets. This is due to numerous reasons and variables, particularly low per capita income, unemployment, inflation rates, economic growth, and other variables that affect asset quality. Therefore, the main problem of the study revolves around whether some macroeconomic variables have an impact on asset quality, and whether the Iraqi economy suffers from slow economic growth and high unemployment and inflation rates. The study relied on a descriptive, analytical, and quantitative approach using the ARDL model (autoregressive distributed lag model) and the 12Eviews program.
