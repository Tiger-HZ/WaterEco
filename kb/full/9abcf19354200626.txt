Background: Ultra-low-dose (ULD) chest CT can reduce radiation exposure but may compromise image quality. We evaluated image-domain generative adversarial network (GAN)-based denoising at low-dose (LD) and ULD levels, focusing on ULD-AiR versus LD-ADMIRE S3. Methods: In this single-center retrospective paired study, 262 participants underwent LD and ULD chest CT on the same scanner. Images reconstructed with Advanced Modeled Iterative Reconstruction at strength 3 (ADMIRE S3) were post-processed with AiR Denoising v1.0, yielding four series. Objective metrics, including signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and contrast-to-noise ratio (CNR), and 5-point subjective ratings were compared within dose levels and between ULD-AiR and LD-ADMIRE S3.
