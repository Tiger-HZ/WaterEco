This study assessed ambient air quality using selected gaseous and particulate pollutants across twelve sampling locations within the investigated environment. The parameters evaluated included nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), sulphur dioxide (SO₂), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), particulate matter (PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀), methane (CH₄), carbon dioxide (CO₂), carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen sulphide (H₂S), and ozone (O₃). The measurements were undertaken to characterize the spatial distribution of atmospheric pollutants and evaluate the general environmental quality of the study area. The results showed generally low concentrations of NO₂, VOCs, PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ across most locations.
