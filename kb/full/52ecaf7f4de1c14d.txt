Abstract Fipronil and imidacloprid are widely used urban insecticides that enter municipal wastewater through down-the-drain pathways and can occur in treated effluent above aquatic toxicity thresholds, yet the capacity of receiving streams to buffer them remains poorly understood. We conducted a screening-level ecological risk assessment for 160 wastewater treatment plants discharging to California surface waters by integrating effluent monitoring data with modeled receiving-stream dilution across four flow regimes.
