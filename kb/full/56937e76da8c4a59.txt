This study presents long-term background concentration estimates for hydrochemical parameters in the Shu River at the Kaynar transboundary section for 2002–2025 and assesses chemical mass transport across this section. Annual mean values for 31 hydrochemical parameters were analyzed; 533 annual values were processed, and 498 remained after statistical extremes were excluded from background-concentration calculations. Background concentrations were estimated as one-sided confidence limits of the mean at a 0.95 confidence level. The Shu River water is characterized by a bicarbonate-sulfate composition and moderately elevated mineralization.
