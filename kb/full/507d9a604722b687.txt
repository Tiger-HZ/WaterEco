Onion (Allium cepa L.) is an economically significant vegetable crop with persistent year-round demand. Supply constraints arise due to the limited shelf life of the rabi-season crop. To address this challenge, an experiment was conducted in eastern India using the cultivar ‘Bhima Dark Red’. The investigation aimed to identify the optimal sowing date between 15 June and 15 August 2023 to maximise yield and the benefit-cost (B : C) ratio. Results demonstrated that onion bulbs produced from latest sowing date (15 August; D5) exhibited superior bulb morphometric characteristics, including polar diameter (5.93 cm), equatorial diameter (5.55 cm) and the thinnest neck (0.86 cm). The D5 treatment produced an average bulb weight of 82.76 g, resulting in a maximum total bulb yield of 204.82 q ha-1.
