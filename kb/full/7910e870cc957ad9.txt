Abstract Background Despite the critical importance of groundwater as a source of drinking water, for agriculture, and industry, its ecological condition is rarely addressed in public discourse. Groundwater is inhabited by a myriad of organisms, from animals to microbes. The fauna living in groundwater contributes significantly to maintaining groundwater quality. However, how groundwater as alive and intact ecosystems are perceived by relevant stakeholders and what their attitudes are toward their conservation remains largely unknown. We conducted a qualitative interview study with key stakeholders in the groundwater sector to investigate these aspects.
