Background/Aim: Land use changes alter soil physical and chemical properties, with significant consequences for soil quality and ecosystem sustainability. This study aimed to determine the effects of different land use types (pasture, meadow, field, orchard-garden, coniferous forest, oak forest, recreation, and wetland) on soil quality indicators in and around Mount Erciyes.Methods: Soil samples were collected from depths of 0-10 cm and 10-20 cm. Texture, organic matter, pH, electrical conductivity (EC), lime (CaCO₃), available phosphorus (P₂O₅), aggregate stability, exchangeable cations (Ca, Mg, K, and Na), and cation exchange capacity (CEC) were analyzed.Results: Land use type had statistically significant effects on most of the soil properties examined (p≤0.01).
