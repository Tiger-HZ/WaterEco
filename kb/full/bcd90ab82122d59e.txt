To determine the direct effects of microcystin on the fitness of herbivorous zooplankton, we experimentally added microcystin‐LR to freeze‐dried cells of palatable Chlorella and fed these compound‐treated cells to two clones of Daphnia pulicaria that had shown differing responses to a diet containing a strain of Microcystis aeruginosa that produces microcystin. The Daphnia that performed better on a diet containing live Microcystis showed reduced population growth when exposed to microcystin‐LR‐treated Chlorella , whereas the Daphnia that performed poorly on the diet containing live Microcystis was not affected by the experimental diet containing microcystin‐LR.
