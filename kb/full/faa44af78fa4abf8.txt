In arid and semi-arid regions, water pollution poses a significant threat to environmental sustainability and public health due to the limited availability and high vulnerability of water resources. Traditional methods for monitoring water quality often struggle to address the unique challenges of these regions, including sparse data collection and delayed response times. This review explores the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies in enhancing water pollution monitoring in these environments. By leveraging advanced data analytics, real-time processing, and predictive modelling, AI and ML offer promising solutions to overcome the limitations of conventional monitoring techniques.
