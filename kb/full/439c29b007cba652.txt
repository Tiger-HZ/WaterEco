Abstract This study aimed to develop and validate a practical nomogram to screen poor sleep quality among night-shift nurses, providing an effective screening tool for managing their occupational health. A cross-sectional study was conducted involving 595 night-shift nurses selected between January and March 2026. The nurses were randomly divided into two groups in a ratio of 7:3. Data were collected using a general information questionnaire, the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, the Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale, and the Fatigue scale-14. Univariate and multivariate logistic regression analyses were conducted to develop a nomogram model. The performance was evaluated by discrimination, calibration, and clinical utility.
