Introduction
Thegenus ArrenurusDugès,1834isthemostspecies-richgenusofthewatermiteswithmore
than1000speciesknown(Smit2020a). Thegenushasacosmopolitandistribution,andoccurs
oneverycontinentexceptAntarctica. FromAustralia63speciesareknown(Cook1986;Halík
1940,1941;Harvey1998;Lundblad1941,1947;Smit1992,1997,1999,2002,2003a,b,2007,
2010, 2021; Smith & Harvey 1989; K. Viets 1955; K.O. Viets 1975, 1981, 1984). Within
Australia, Arrenurusspecies are known from almost all states and territories, only from the
Australian Capital Territory no species are known. Most species are known from tropical
Australia(inbracketsthenumberofspecies): Queensland(27),WesternAustralia(25),Northern
Territory (17), New SouthWales (14), Tasmania (11), Victoria (9), SouthAustralia (4) and
AustralianCapitalTerritory(0).
Currently,11subgeneraareknownwithinthegenus(Smit2020b). Thefollowingsubgenera
occur inAustralia: Arrenuruss.s., BrevicaudaturusSmit, 1997,DividuracarusSmit, 1997,
MegaluracarusK.Viets,1911, MicruracarusK.Viets,1991, RhinophoracarusK.Viets,1916
and TruncaturusThor, 1901. The subgenusDividuracarus is only known fromAustralia.
However, the subgeneric classification is artificial. Cook (1974) stated that “the subgenera
are poorly defined, undoubtedly polyphyletic and are almost meaningless in indicating true
relationshipsformanyofthespeciesgroups”. Manyauthorsstillusethesubgenericsubdivision
tosubdividethislargegenusinsmallerunits.
Most species of the subgenusTruncaturusare small and with a yellowish or brownish
idiosomacolour,notonlyinAustraliabutalsoinotherbioregionsoftheworld. Hereanunusual
newspeciesisdescribed,whichisnotonlyrelativelylarge,butalsodarkred,acombinationnot
foundinanyother Truncaturusspecies.

Remarks
The new species is the sixthTruncaturusspecies fromAustralia. Species of the subgenus
Truncaturusare usually small and coloured yellowish or brownish. The new species is an
exception,itisrelativelylargeandthecolourisdarkred. ThreeotherAustralian Truncaturus
arealsolarge,i.e. A. haswelli Cook,1986, A. indentatus Smit,2010and A. novaecambricus
Smit, 2010.Arrenurus haswelli and A. indentatus differfromthenewspeciesinhavingthe
posterioridiosomamarginwithamediancleft. Themaleof Arrenurus novaecambricusislarger
(850-907),theposterioridiosomamarginisconcave,thegenitalplatesareshorterandslightly
bowedandtheidiosomacolourisyellowishbrown.
