Coal mining in South Kalimantan has created numerous pit lakes that local communities use as water sources, although their quality has not been comprehensively assessed. This study presents an integrated evaluation of pit lake water quality by combining regulatory compliance with STORET standards (STOrage and RETrieval), contamination factor (CF), and pollution load index (PLI), while also examining the effect of water depth on quality variation. Water samples were collected using grab sampling and analysed for temperature, total suspended solids (TSS), total dissolved solids (TDS), pH, biological oxygen demand (BOD), chemical oxygen demand (COD), dissolved oxygen (DO), Fe, Pb, and Mn in accordance with Government Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia Number 22 of 2021.
