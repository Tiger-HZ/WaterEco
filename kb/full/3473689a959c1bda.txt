Background Chronic insomnia impairs daytime functioning and increases the risk of cardiovascular and psychiatric disorders. Although cognitive behavioral therapy and pharmacotherapy are conventional treatments, their use may be limited by implementation barriers and adverse effects. This study systematically evaluated the efficacy of acupuncture compared with sham acupuncture for chronic insomnia. Methods Nine English- and Chinese-language databases and clinical trial registries were searched from inception to July 1, 2026, for randomized controlled trials (RCTs). Eligible studies included patients aged 18–65 years with a formal diagnosis of chronic insomnia.
