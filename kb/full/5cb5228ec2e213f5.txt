The hypothesis that negative effects of Microcystis on Daphnia growth and reproduction can be explained from the presence of microcystin in the Microcystis cells was tested by comparing the effects on Daphnia life history characteristics of a microcystin‐free mutant strain and microcystin‐containing strain of the cyanobacterium Microcystis aeruginosa PCC7806. To avoid nutritional deficiency, Microcystis was offered to Daphnia alone and in various mixtures with the high‐quality green alga Scenedesmus obliquus . In contrast to expectation, growth of Daphnia on microcystin‐free cells was not much better than growth on microcystin‐containing cells.
