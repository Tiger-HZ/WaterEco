Oilfield-wetland zones are ecologically fragile landscapes where petroleum extraction, traffic, and agricultural activities may jointly affect soil metal accumulation. In this study, surface soils from oilfield-wetland patches in the Yellow River Delta were analyzed for Cr, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn, Cd, and Pb, together with selected soil properties. The concentrations of Cr, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn, Cd, and Pb ranged from 47.11 to 106.72, 11.01 to 23.53, 27.61 to 64.28, 6.41 to 60.45, 10.41 to 190.95, ND to 0.37, and 16.53 to 104.58 mg/kg, respectively, and were below the Chinese agricultural soil risk-screening values.
