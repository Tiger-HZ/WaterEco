Background/Objectives: Hyperglycemia can compromise anticancer treatment, leading to treatment delay or discontinuation. Identifying factors associated with hyperglycemia is therefore important. We aimed to examine associations between hyperglycemia and dietary patterns, food-group and nutrient intakes, and clinical and behavioral factors in patients undergoing chemotherapy. Methods: This cross-sectional survey assessed 176 patients with cancer undergoing chemotherapy at a university hospital in South Korea. Data were collected from April 2024 to March 2025 and analyzed using descriptive statistics, chi-square or Fisher’s exact tests, t-tests or Mann–Whitney U tests, and logistic regression.
