The current research looks at how Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) disclosure affects real earnings management (REM) and how audit quality (AQ) moderates this relationship. The study sample consists of 292 firm-year observations for the period 2022–2025 from 73 non-financial firms in highly polluting industries listed on the Saudi Stock Exchange (Tadawul). Panel regression analyses and an alternative estimator (pooled OLS with robust standard errors) are used to assess the robustness of the results. The findings show that REM is negatively associated with ESG disclosure, suggesting that firms with higher ESG disclosure have lower REM.
