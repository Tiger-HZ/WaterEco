The discharge of nonionic surfactants into aquatic environments poses a significant environmental challenge due to their persistence and potential ecological impact. In this work, porous PPO/ZnO composite fibers were fabricated through a simple shear-spinning process and evaluated for the photocatalytic degradation of Triton X-100 under UV irradiation. Structural and morphological characterization confirmed the successful incorporation of ZnO particles into the porous PPO matrix while preserving their crystalline wurtzite structure. Nitrogen adsorption analysis revealed that the composite retained a highly porous mesostructure, providing a suitable support for the immobilized photocatalyst.
