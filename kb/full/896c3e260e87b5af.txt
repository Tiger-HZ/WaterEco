This study assessed the chemical composition, mineral and sensory properties of (stiff dough) fufu produced from yam and millet flour blends. Analysis was done using standard methods. Fufu was prepared from blends of yam and millet flour, with yam substituted from 10-50% in the following ratio (100:0, 90:10, 80:20, 70:30, 60:40 and 50:50) respectively. Data obtained was subjected to analysis of variance (ANOVA) and was separated using Duncan's multiple range test. Results showed that the Proximate composition revealed that the Moisture content of the (Stiff dough) fufu samples ranged from 67.73 to 71.88%, crude protein 1.38 to 4.99%, fat 0.66 to 1.31%, ash 0.66 to 2.71%, crude fiber 1.71 to 4.97% and carbohydrate 20.41 to 24.98%.
