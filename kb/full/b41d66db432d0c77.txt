Evaluating water quality is fundamental to ensuring that freshwater ecosystems support the well-being of all life on Earth. The present study uses physicochemical parameters to assess groundwater in Dili, Timor-Leste, from 2011 to 2018. Water quality was determined using the conventional Water Quality Index (WQI), aggregated via a weighted sum function aligned with the World Health Organization (WHO). The study aims to predict the WQI and water quality status, expressed as Water Quality Classification (WQC), to support sustainable water resource management. In low-resource domains, it is crucial to address challenges related to small dataset size and class imbalance in the sample observations.
