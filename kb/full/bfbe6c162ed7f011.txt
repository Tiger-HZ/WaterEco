Huanglongbing (HLB) compromises citrus root function, nutrient uptake, and productivity, making fertilizer management critical for sustaining tree performance in affected groves. This study evaluated conventional and controlled-release fertilizer (CRF) programs in mature HLB-affected ‘Valencia’ sweet orange trees grown on sandy soil in Florida. Treatments consisted of a conventional standard fertilizer program (Standard) and two CRF programs, CRF1 and CRF2. Leaf nutrient concentrations, fruit yield, juice quality, partial factor productivity (PFP), and multivariate response profiles were assessed.
