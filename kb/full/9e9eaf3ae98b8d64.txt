The Emeishan Large Igneous Province (ELIP) contains high-Mg volcanic rocks, evolved basalts, and Fe-Ti-V-oxide-bearing layered intrusions, providing a natural system for investigating the coupled effects of H2O and the redox state on silicate crystallization. We modeled three representative starting melts, namely Melt-6 (basaltic), Melt-8 (meimechitic), and Melt-10 (komatiitic), at 5 kbar under prescribed fO2 conditions spanning ΔFMQ = −3 to + 3 using rhyolite-MELTS. The simulations show that the starting melt composition sets the liquidus temperature and controls whether olivine saturates first.
