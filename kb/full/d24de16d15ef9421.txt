From hospital readmission risk to patient stratification based on biomarkers, predictive analytics systems have become integral to the healthcare and life-science value chain. These systems are traditionally judged by their predictive accuracy with the tacit assumption that their input data is clean, complete and stable. In practice, operational data pipelines have measurable defects in several dimensions of data quality - incompleteness, inconsistency, inaccuracy and staleness - but none of these dimensions is standardized with a method of measuring how sensitive a predictive system is to it. Consequently, two models that perform the same accuracy on the benchmark data could fail very differently when applied to imperfect data and this fragility would not be apparent until the model fails
