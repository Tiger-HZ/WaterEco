Abstract Topographic illumination effects, NDVI saturation, and inconsistent ecological orientations of annual PCA components can compromise ecological quality assessment in plateau–mountain regions. Based on Landsat observations from 1996 to 2025, this study developed a Modified Remote Sensing Ecological Index (MRSEI) by integrating SCS+C topographic correction, kNDVI, and ecologically direction–constrained PCA. Spatial autocorrelation analysis, XGBoost–SHAP, and PLUS were further employed to investigate spatiotemporal variations in ecological quality, identify driving factors, and project ecological changes to 2034.
