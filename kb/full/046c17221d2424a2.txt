The professional nail industry in the United States supports more than 375,000 licensed nail technicians and generated approximately $12.9 billion in revenue in 2024. Despite this scale, sanitation and occupational health standards remain fragmented across state regulatory frameworks, creating gaps that expose both clients and practitioners to preventable risks. This article examines current evidence on infection prevention, chemical exposure control, and nail health protection in professional nail settings, drawing on peer-reviewed literature, federal regulatory guidance from OSHA and NIOSH, and the author's structured career methodology developed for practice in the U.S. beauty industry.
