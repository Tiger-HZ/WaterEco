Abstract Poly(oxindole biphenylene) ion-solvating membranes (POBP-ISMs) have emerged as promising candidate membrane materials for use in hydrogen production through alkaline water electrolysis (AWE), owing to their exceptional alkaline stability. However, their dense architecture leads to insufficient exposure of functional groups, resulting in low electrolyte uptake and substantial ion transport resistance, which collectively limit ionic conductivity and compromise the durable, high-efficiency performance of AWE. To overcome this limitation, we prepared an asymmetric porous POBP (AP-POBP) membrane using a two-step nonsolvent-induced phase separation (NIPS) method.
