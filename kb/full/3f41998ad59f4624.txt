Effective crop production depends on a sufficient supply of nutrients through fertilizer application to achieve optimal yield. However, farmers must properly manage soil nutrients to meet the fertility needs of crops without negatively impacting the quality of precious water resources. This study evaluated nitrate and phosphate levels in water and soil in Barangays Mangayang and Gabut, Dupax del Sur, Nueva Vizcaya, to understand the impact of fertilizer and agrochemical use in agriculture. The study revealed that nitrate concentrations in irrigation water were low, with mean values of 0.20275 mg/L in Mangayang and 0.41250 mg/L in Gabut, both within the acceptable limit of 7 mg/L, and showed no significant differences (p &gt; 0.05).
