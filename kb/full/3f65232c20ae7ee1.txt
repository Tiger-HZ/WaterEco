Eutrophication is a nutrient-driven shift in trophic status characterized by the appearance of green or blue green algae and declining water quality. Non-point source (NPS) pollutants are diffuse pollutants originating from multiple sources like agriculture runoff, urban waste, animal or human activities, etc. are major drivers of trophic transitions and algal proliferation. However, their seasonal influences on nutrient balance and trophic state remain understudied. This study provides an integrated framework combining water quality parameters, Carlson trophic state index (CTSI), Redfield ratio, and normalized differentiated chlorophyll index (NDCI) to understand the effect of NPS on nutrient loading and trophic transition across three major wetlands in Guwahati city, Deepor Beel, Dighali
