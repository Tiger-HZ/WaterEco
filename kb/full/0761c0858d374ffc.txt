Abstract Background Increasingly, individuals are seeking treatment for sexual health concerns through direct-to-consumer (DTC) telehealth platforms, and there is ongoing debate about whether such platforms can deliver safe and effective care. Objective The objective of this study was to examine how men who sought sexual health treatment through a large national DTC telehealth platform viewed the quality and safety of their care, and how these perceptions differed from those of men who sought other types of treatment through the platform. Methods We conducted a retrospective analysis of data gathered from an online survey sent to individuals who accessed treatment through the DTC telehealth platform.
