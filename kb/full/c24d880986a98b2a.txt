Abstract Waste from pharmaceutical production processes has the potential to pollute aquatic environments, especially the occurrence of eutrophication events. Environmental indicators that usually affect eutrophication include Phosphate, Sulfate, and Ammonia, which exceed the limits set by regulations. This study focuses on investigating the fluctuations in the quantity of wastewater contaminant parameters from pharmaceutical production facilities before and after treatment using ultrasonic methods with variations in time and pH and testing the effectiveness of the ultrasonic process on water quality using predetermined parameters.
