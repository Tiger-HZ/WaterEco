Annual flooding in the Krueng Keureuto Watershed causes significant losses to the surrounding community. One flood-control measure is the use of an oxbowlake, which is cyclical and lake that stores excess water when river discharge increases, provides clean water, and supports irrigation. However, the lake’s mainproblem is sedimentation, which can reduce its capacity and shorten its service life. Therefore, this study analyse the sedimentation rate and estimate the lake’s service life. The sedimentation analysis was conducted using the modified universal soil loss equation (MUSLE) method.
