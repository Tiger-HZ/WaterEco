Abstract Accurate identification of dissolved reactive phosphorus (DRP) sources and quantification of its loading into aquatic environments are critical for mitigating eutrophication and improving environmental management strategies. DRP, the most bioavailable form of phosphorus, stimulates algal proliferation directly, serving as a major driver of eutrophication, thereby threatening water quality degradation and freshwater sustainability. However, quantifying DRP loads and tracing their sources remains challenging due to landscape heterogeneity and the dynamic nature of precipitation events.
