Understanding how dams restructure fish diversity is critical for biodiversity conservation and reservoir management. Here, we integrated species, taxonomic, and functional diversity within a unified framework to assess fish community responses to the Datengxia Water Conservancy Project impoundment on the Hongshui River (Pearl River basin). Based on surveys before (2013) and after (2020–2022) impoundment across upstream, downstream, and tributary reaches, we recorded 162 species, including four nationally protected, four endangered, two critically endangered, and 19 exotic taxa.
