Abstract Background Prior evidence suggests that promotion visit frequency is qualitatively associated in individual studies with effectiveness on diarrhoea of handwashing with soap (HWWS) and chlorination by household water treatment (HWT). However, this relationship has not been explored quantitatively, nor beyond diarrhoea. We aimed to assess whether intensity of household promotion modifies effectiveness of HWWS and HWT on diarrhoea and acute respiratory infections. We conducted a re-review of randomised controlled trials evaluating domestic HWWS and HWT interventions included in two recent systematic reviews.
