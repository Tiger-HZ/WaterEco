Seed priming is used to improve the speed, uniformity, and physiological performance of germinating seeds, but responses can depend on the priming agent and genotype. This study evaluated two field-pea varieties (KPMR-522 and Rachana) under six seed-treatment levels: control, hydropriming, KNO₃ at 3%, GA₃ at 0.01%, NAA at 0.01%, and Panchagavya at 5% during the 2023-24 and 2024-25 rabi seasons. The treatment structure was factorial (two varieties × six treatments), and germination-based laboratory observations were made with four replications of 100 seeds per treatment combination.
