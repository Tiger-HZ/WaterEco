Massive open online courses (MOOCs) are increasingly positioned as infrastructure for continuing professional development, yet the mechanisms through which educational personnel accept and use them remain incompletely specified. Drawing on the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology (UTAUT), the information systems success framework, and the trust&amp;ndash;risk perspective, this study develops and tests an extended acceptance model that incorporates perceived content quality, trust, perceived risk, self-regulated learning, and digital&amp;ndash;AI literacy. We collected survey data from 290 educational personnel in Thailand and screened for insufficient-effort responding, yielding an analytical sample of 270.
