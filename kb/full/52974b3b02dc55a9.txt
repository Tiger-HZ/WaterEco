ABSTRACT This study examines whether firm‐level water management efficiency is associated with corporate carbon emissions, and whether this association is moderated by corporate social responsibility (CSR) performance, environmental regulatory stringency, and industry water intensity. Using an international panel of 2318 firms across 22 countries (32,448 firm‐year observations) over 2010–2023, we estimate two‐way (firm and year) fixed‐effects regressions with standard errors clustered at the firm level. Water management efficiency is negatively and significantly associated with both total carbon emissions and carbon intensity. This association is significantly stronger among firms with above‐median CSR performance and, for total emissions, in highly water‐intensive industries. EnviroMANUSC
