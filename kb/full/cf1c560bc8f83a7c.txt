Abstract Abundance and species composition of benthic invertebrate communities were investigated in two rivers of contrasting channel form, the Tukipo and Waipawa, in the Southern Hawke's Bay, New Zealand. Monthly collections between December 1995 and December 1996 yielded a total of 89 taxa from the two sites; Deleatidium spp. numerically dominating the braided Waipawa whereas Pycnocentrodes spp., Pycnocentria funerea, Aoteapsyche spp., and Elmidae were most common in the constrained Tukipo. Although disturbance frequency was similar between rivers, the streambed substrates were smaller, and hence less stable in the Waipawa.
