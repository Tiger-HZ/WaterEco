The valorization of mineral wastes into functional catalysts provides a promising route for integrating solid waste management with advanced water purification. However, the catalytic potential of waste-derived materials is still commonly interpreted from elemental composition or preparation-induced performance enhancement, leaving the fundamental link between waste mineralogy and catalytic reactivity largely unresolved. This review introduces the concept of a mineralogical catalytic fingerprint (MCF) to establish a mechanistic framework connecting mineral origin, structural reconstruction, active-site evolution, and PMS/PDS activation behavior.
