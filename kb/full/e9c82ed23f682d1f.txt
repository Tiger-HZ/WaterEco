This study assessed the impact of refinery effluent discharge on the water quality of Ubeji River, Delta State, Nigeria. Water samples were collected over a six-month period from five points along the river (discharge point, upstream and downstream) and one control station. Physicochemical parameters (pH, temperature, TDS, turbidity, DO, BOD, COD, nutrients, THC) and heavy metals (Fe, Zn, Cu, Pb, Cd, Cr, Ni) were analyzed using standard methods. Results revealed that most parameters were within the permissible standard limits of the Federal Ministry of Environment (FMEnv), however, significant spatial variations (p&lt;0.05) were observed.
