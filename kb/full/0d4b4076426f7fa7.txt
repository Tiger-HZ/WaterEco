Patient-reported service evaluations provide a basis for improving inpatient care. This analytical cross-sectional study examined associations between five SERVQUAL dimensions and patient satisfaction at Stella Maris General Hospital, South Nias Regency, Indonesia. A purposive sample of 115 respondents completed a structured questionnaire. Associations were assessed using Pearson’s chi-square tests and exploratory Firth penalized logistic regression. Overall, 59 respondents (51.3%) were satisfied. All five dimensions were associated with satisfaction in unadjusted analyses (all p &lt; 0.001).
