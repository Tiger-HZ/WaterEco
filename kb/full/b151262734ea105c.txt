The planktonic food chain phytoplankton - zooplankton - young-of-the-year roach (Rutilus rutilus) was studied in 19 lakes in southern Sweden to investigate the bioaccumulation of polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs). The ΣPCB concentrations did not steadily increase with increasing trophic level. The ΣPCB concentrations in zooplankton (400 ng·g lipid -1 ) were lower than in both phytoplankton (660 ng·g lipid -1 ) and fish (890 ng·g lipid -1 ), which did not differ significantly. Lipid content explained 40% of the total variation in dry weight normalised ΣPCB concentrations in the samples. The PCBs were differentially partitioned between the trophic levels.
