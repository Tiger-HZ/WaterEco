Abstract M50 bearing steel is extensively utilised in aerospace bearings due to its elevated hardness and wear resistance. Nevertheless, during conventional grinding(CG) processes, the high grinding forces and temperatures involved render it highly susceptible to a range of surface damage issues. The utilisation of low-temperature cold air processing has been demonstrated to exert a suppressive effect on tool wear, concomitantly enhancing the quality of the material's surface. Consequently, a novel machining method combining longitudinal torsional ultrasonic-assisted grinding(LTUAG) with low-temperature cold air processing has been proposed to enhance the machining quality of M50 bearing steel.
