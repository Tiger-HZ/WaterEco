Food recovery programs are often described by pounds collected or distributed, but weight alone does not capture the nutritional value of recovered foods or the local infrastructure needed to move nutrient-dense surplus foods before they become waste. This community case study describes the early implementation period of the Waste Not, Want Not Kentucky Feeding Network (WiN-WiN), a USDA Sustainable Agriculture Research and Education-funded program in Jessamine County, Kentucky. WiN-WiN was designed as a connector among existing food sources, donation drop-off sites, and community feeding organizations.
