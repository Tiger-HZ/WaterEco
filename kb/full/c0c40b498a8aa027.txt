ABSTRACT The growing global demand for food necessitates an increase in the supply of fertilizers that enable greater crop productivity in a sustainable manner, minimizing environmental impacts. The increasing availability of fertilizer products and formulations has raised concerns about compositional quality, which cannot always be adequately verified. Generally, fertilizer quality evaluation is rarely incorporated into agronomic management, which tends to focus on soil analysis, irrigation water analysis, and foliar analysis for determining nutrient dosages. For this reason, the quality of solid inorganic fertilizers was evaluated using energy‐dispersive X‐ray fluorescence (XRF) for the determination of elements from Mg to Fe.
