Abstract Frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) laser sources are core components of high-precision coherent light detection and ranging (LiDAR) systems. However, the inherent frequency sweep nonlinearity induced by internal thermal effects and carrier dynamics within semiconductor lasers severely limits the system’s ranging accuracy. To mitigate this limitation, an adaptive segmented-gain iterative learning control (ASG-ILC) algorithm is proposed to achieve high-precision linearization of the laser frequency sweep.
