The title Br/Cl-substituted Schiff base, C 15 H 12 BrCl 2 NO 2 , was synthesized via condensation of 2,4-dichloroaniline with 3-bromo-4,5-dimethoxybenzaldehyde under mild acidic conditions. Its molecular and crystal structures were determined by single-crystal X-ray diffraction (monoclinic space group C 2/ c , Z = 8). Structurally, the molecule adopts a twisted conformation, with an angle of approximately 41.6 (4)° between the planes of the 3-bromo-4,5-dimethoxyphenyl and 2,4-dichlorophenyl moieties, a value significantly greater than those reported for related halogen-substituted Schiff bases in the literature, indicating a substantial disruption of the π-conjugated system across the azomethine bridge.
